## Answer

Covered. No documented automatic retry or recovery policy.

Source statement: "A parent agent can invoke multiple subagents concurrently." (https://antigravity.google/docs/subagents/)

Research detail: A parent agent starts subagents with invoke_subagent in inherit, branch (worktree) or share modes, and they run concurrently with running, idle and killed states. Users or the parent can stop subagents, sessions can be resumed or forked, and scheduled tasks send recurring messages to agents.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Antigravity subagents, scheduled tasks, CLI resume and fork

## Gaps

Automatic retry and failure recovery policies are not documented. A misspelled tool name can hang a subagent (known issue).
