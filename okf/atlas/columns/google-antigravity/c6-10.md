## Answer

Partly covered. Residency applies to first-party Gemini models only. Local conversation and knowledge retention and deletion rules are not documented; in-country regions unsupported.

Source statement: "Regional data residency constraints apply to first-party Gemini models" (https://antigravity.google/docs/enterprise)

Research detail: Enterprise sessions run in the customer's Google Cloud project with us and eu multi-region data residency and ML processing support, and Google states prompts, responses, code and telemetry are not stored outside private environments. Compliance logs stay in the organisation's project and are not used for training.

## Products

Gemini Enterprise data residency, Antigravity in Gemini Enterprise

## Gaps
