## Answer

Not covered. No central gateway for model calls with per-agent or per-user routing, quotas and rate limits, and no prompt injection screening. Admin model enablement and pooled quotas fall short.

Research detail: Admins authorise which models users can select, third-party models need opt-in, and pooled quotas, overages and a monthly spend limit cap consumption. The individual product also enforces weekly and five-hour usage limits.
