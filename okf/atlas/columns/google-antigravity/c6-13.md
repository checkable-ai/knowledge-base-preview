## Answer

Not covered. Google states SOC, ISO 27001, ISO 42001 and FedRAMP are not supported. IRAP list does not name Antigravity or Gemini Enterprise.

Research detail: Google states that Antigravity in Gemini Enterprise does not support SOC 1, 2 or 3, ISO 27001 or ISO 42001, FedRAMP Moderate or High, IL4 or IL5, ITAR or Access Transparency. Google Cloud holds an IRAP assessment at PROTECTED, but its products-in-scope list does not name Antigravity or Gemini Enterprise.
