## Answer

Not covered. Admin toggle and IAM roles control human access only. Custom agents, skills and hooks are local files with no controlled create, change or retire process.

Research detail: Admins turn AI developer tools on or off per subscription, assign the Gemini Enterprise User role, and can build custom IAM roles without businessaicode permissions to withhold access. Custom subagents, skills, hooks and MCP configs are local files users create without an admin workflow.
