## Answer

Covered. The user runs /learn and approves the proposed learning_proposal.md. Retained knowledge is saved as rules or skills in workspace files (AGENTS.md, GEMINI.md, .agents/rules, .agents/skills) or global files under ~/.gemini/config. Agents do not otherwise remember conversations.

Source statement: "The agent reviews the recent conversation, identifies what to retain, and proposes rules or skills." (https://antigravity.google/docs/commands/learn/)

Research detail: Rules in AGENTS.md, GEMINI.md and .agents/rules persist instructions across sessions at global and workspace scope. The local app data directory holds artifacts and knowledge items, and permissions granted in a project can persist.
