## Answer

Not covered. Digital Wallet shows Flex Credit usage, but no measurement of labour cost, return or token-level cost against delivered value.

Research detail: Usage is metered in actions, prompts or conversations and converted to Flex Credits using the Agentforce Rate Card, with metered usage visible in Digital Wallet. A usage data model is available for agents. Labour cost savings, output increase and profit are not measured natively.
