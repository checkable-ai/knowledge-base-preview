## Answer

Not covered. Tool poisoning scoring at registration only; no look-alike name or compromised package detection, or ongoing monitoring.

Research detail: Third-party MCP servers are scanned at registration for tool poisoning attacks: exfiltration, privilege escalation, steering and instruction overriding, scored low, medium or high per tool. Pre-packaged servers can be installed from AgentExchange. Detection of name squatting or package compromise after registration is not documented.

## Gaps
