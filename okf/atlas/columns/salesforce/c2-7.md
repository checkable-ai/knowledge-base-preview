## Answer

Not covered. No limits on an agent's files, processes or network. The Kill Switch policy only blocks a quarantined agent's access to the model proxy, and native deactivation stops only new sessions.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Agentforce agents run on Salesforce's hosted platform, and the earlier finding found no customer-controlled execution host or sandbox. Whether an administrator can deactivate an agent or revoke its access with immediate effect could not be established in this run, because the Salesforce Help and developer pages needed did not render or returned 403 without a browser.
