## Answer

Covered. Gated pre-send approval is documented for Engagement (SDR) agent emails with Require Manual Approval on; other agents rely on escalation to humans and user feedback.

Source statement: "If Require Manual Approval is on, the agent adds each email to a queue for approval by the prospect owner or their manager." (https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=sales.sales_agent_sdr_lead_process.htm&language=en_US&type=5)

Research detail: Agents escalate conversations to human reps or queues through the Escalation subagent and Omni-Channel flows. Salesforce recommends a human check of model responses for most use cases, and feedback data records accept, modify and decline actions on generated responses. No pre-delivery review queue for autonomous agent output is documented.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Escalation subagent, Omni-Channel, Einstein feedback data

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Human review is by escalation or by the user, not a gated review step.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Design subagents to draft rather than send for sensitive outputs and route to a rep queue.
