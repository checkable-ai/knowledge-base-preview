## Answer

Not covered. Orchestration and human handoff are documented, but retry, cancellation and recovery of steps are not.

Research detail: The Atlas reasoning engine routes messages through a starting subagent, resolves subagent instructions deterministically, then uses the LLM to pick actions. Multi-Agent Orchestration lets an orchestrator delegate to connected subagents, and an Escalation subagent transfers conversations to humans. Cancellation, retry and recovery behaviour is not described in the pages reviewed.
