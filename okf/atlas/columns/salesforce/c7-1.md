## Answer

Not covered. Agentforce session tracing and the Setup Audit Trail record agent activity, but Salesforce does not document write-once or integrity-validated storage for these records, nor tamper detection. Nearby features (Event Monitoring, Field Audit Trail retention) provide retention, not tamper evidence. No documented export of the agent action record to immutable storage was found.

Research detail: Session tracing records turn-by-turn interactions, reasoning engine executions, actions, prompt and gateway inputs and outputs, errors and responses under a session ID. The audit trail stores masked and unmasked prompts, responses, model details and safety scores in Data 360. The docs do not claim the records are tamper-proof, and audit data can be deleted by removing the data lake object.
