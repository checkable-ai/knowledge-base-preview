## Answer

Not covered. No gated lifecycle from proposal through approval, testing, production, change and retirement. Agent versioning and Testing Center cover build, test and activation only.

Research detail: Agents move through draft, test in preview, activation, new version and deactivation or deletion. Activated versions are locked. Salesforce provides planning guidance for use cases and recommends sandbox testing. Proposal and retirement approval stages are not native.
