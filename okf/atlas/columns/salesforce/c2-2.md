## Answer

Covered. Zero data retention applies under the Einstein Trust Layer; geo-aware routing with in-region-only option and provider toggles per Salesforce documentation. Deprecated models may be rerouted to a newer model.

Source statement: "the prompts and generated responses are never stored or used to train the underlying third-party large language models." (https://www.salesforce.com/artificial-intelligence/trusted-ai/?bc=DB)

Research detail: Salesforce publishes supported models with provider, version string, usage type and regions. Admins can turn model providers on or off, requests use geo-aware routing with an in-region only option, and external providers are under zero data retention agreements. Deprecated models may be rerouted to a newer model in the same family.
