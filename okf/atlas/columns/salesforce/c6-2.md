## Answer

Not covered. No documented submission, registration and approval workflow with a separate approver for new models, agents or tools. Anypoint Exchange scanners and admin enablement switches discover and register assets but are not an approval process.

Research detail: Registering an MCP server requires Manage AI Agents and an explicit tool allowlist step informed by risk scores. Model providers are enabled or disabled by admins. No submission and approval workflow for agents, models or tools is documented.
