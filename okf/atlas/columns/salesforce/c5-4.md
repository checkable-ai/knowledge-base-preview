## Answer

Covered. Health alerts arrive 3 to 7 minutes after detection. Agent Platform Tracing is not available in Government Cloud.

Source statement: "Trust, RAG, health and quality metrics that previously lived in separate dashboards are now consolidated in one view." (https://www.salesforce.com/blog/agentforce-observability/?bc=DB)

Research detail: Agent Health Monitoring detects spiking error rates and high latency in near real time with email alerts, built on session tracing. Agent Platform Tracing captures spans across Apex, flows, Prompt Builder, planner, AI Gateway and LLM Gateway for bottleneck analysis.
