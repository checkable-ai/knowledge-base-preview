## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Managed Agents vaults are in beta (header managed-agents-2026-04-01). Not eligible for zero data retention or HIPAA. The customer creates a per-end-user vault and passes it at session creation. The downstream service must honour that user's token and permissions.

Source statement: "Vaults and credentials are authentication primitives that let you register credentials for third-party services once and reference them by ID at session creation." (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/managed-agents/vaults)

Last checked 18 November 2026.

Research detail: Source statement: MCP connections can use OAuth credentials attached through vaults.

Assessment: Credential attachment does not establish universal on-behalf-of semantics for every downstream API.
