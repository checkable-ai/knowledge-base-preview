## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Managed Agents is in beta and needs the managed-agents-2026-04-01 beta header. It is offered in beta on the Claude API, Claude Platform on AWS and Claude Models on Google Cloud (Anthropic managed). It is not eligible for Zero Data Retention (except self-hosted Managed Agents) or HIPAA BAA coverage.

Source statement: "Claude Managed Agents is stateful by design: sessions are long-running, resume cleanly after pauses, and store conversation history, sandbox state, and outputs server-side." (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/managed-agents/overview)
