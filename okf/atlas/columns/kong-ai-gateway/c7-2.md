## Answer

Not covered. Logging policies route logs to external systems, but there are no documented security detections, session-to-person linkage, or named response owner. Sanitizer logs can include original sensitive text, and payload logging warns it may expose sensitive data.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Konnect audit logs go to a SIEM webhook in CEF or JSON, or by pull. AI Gateway logs carry consumer identity and guardrail fields such as guardrail_triggered, and logging policies forward them to aggregators. Blocked content is logged only when log_blocked_content is enabled.

## Products

## Gaps

No detections or response ownership. Redaction of logs depends on PII Sanitizer placement.

## Options
