## Answer

Not covered. No detections or named response ownership. Log export (e.g. Kafka Log, audit log to SIEM) is transport only; redaction depends on PII Sanitizer placement.

Research detail: Konnect audit logs go to a SIEM webhook in CEF or JSON, or by pull. AI Gateway logs carry consumer identity and guardrail fields such as guardrail_triggered, and logging policies forward them to aggregators. Blocked content is logged only when log_blocked_content is enabled.
