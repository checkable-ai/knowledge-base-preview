## Answer

Not covered. No approval gate, versioned releases or rollback for models, prompts, tools, permissions and knowledge. kongctl declarative mode previews and applies configuration changes but is stateless and documents no approval or rollback.

Research detail: kongctl plan and diff preview gateway changes, and plans can be saved as JSON artefacts for CI review before apply or sync. Model aliases let upstream models change without client changes, and Catalog MCP servers carry semver versions.
