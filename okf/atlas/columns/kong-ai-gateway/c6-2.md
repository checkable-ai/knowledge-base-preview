## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Requests are made by Team Managers and Individual Users and approved by an Org Admin in Konnect AI Management. Agent requests ask for a display name, a type (A2A or HTTP) and a URL. AI Management requests and approvals are available through Konnect and are incompatible with on-prem.

Source statement: "An Org Admin decides whether to approve each request." (https://developer.konghq.com/ai-management/roles-and-permissions/)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: MCP servers are registered and published to a registry through the UI or API, and the announcement says only approved MCP servers can be discovered. Konnect has a Registration Approver role for application registration to APIs.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

MCP Registry, Konnect Catalog roles

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The registry docs describe no separate approval step before publishing. No approval workflow for models or agents.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Restrict publish rights through roles and run approval outside Konnect.
