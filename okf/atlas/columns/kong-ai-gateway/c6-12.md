## Answer

Not covered. No scheduled reassessment of external models, tools or services and no governed retirement lifecycle. The nearest feature is Org Admin approval of requested provider models and MCP servers in Konnect AI Management, with admin-only disable and delete. That is an access-approval step, not supplier governance. The Konnect Catalog only lists models by provider.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Catalog lists AI models by provider so teams can see which providers receive data, and the MCP Registry is positioned as a system of record for approved internal and external tools. Unused servers can be identified for retirement from usage data.
