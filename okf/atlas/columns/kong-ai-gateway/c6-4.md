## Answer

Not covered. No documented process for who writes, owns, versions, publishes or reviews AI policies. Konnect roles give write access to a gateway, and the UI, API and kongctl give authoring, but that is access control, not policy governance.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Enforcement policies are AI Policy entities attached to models, consumers, groups, agents or MCP servers, and can be kept as declarative YAML in source control through kongctl.
