## Answer

Covered. Model and provider matches need partition_by set to true. Cost limits are reflected only on the next request. Some guardrail policies require Enterprise tier.

Source statement: "The cost is only reflected during the next request." (https://developer.konghq.com/ai-gateway/policies/ai-rate-limiting-advanced/)

Research detail: AI Models route per consumer with ACLs and load balancing. AI Rate Limiting Advanced limits by tokens or cost per AI Consumer, consumer group, model or provider, including monthly calendar windows. Prompt screening comes from AI Prompt Guard, AI Semantic Prompt Guard and vendor guardrails (AWS, Azure, GCP Model Armor, Lakera, NeMo), and AI PII Sanitizer redacts prompts.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Model, AI Rate Limiting Advanced, AI Semantic Prompt Guard, AI Semantic Response Guard, cloud guardrail policies, AI PII Sanitizer

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Cost limits apply to the next request, so one request can overrun. Model and provider matches need partition_by true or are not enforced. Semantic Prompt Guard is an allow and deny list, not a named injection detector.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use Redis counters for shared limits and add a vendor guardrail for injection detection, then test with known jailbreak prompts.
