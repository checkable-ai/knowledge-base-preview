## Answer

Not covered. Cost analytics cover model spend and tokens per consumer. They do not cover infrastructure cost, staff time or return delivered.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Every request gets a cost figure from token counts, cache, context-window and service-tier pricing. Prometheus cost and token metrics carry consumer and model labels, and requests appear per consumer in the AI Gateway dashboard. The product page claims showback and chargeback.
