## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The sanitizer needs the separately deployed AI PII Anonymizer Service, run as a Docker container with at least 600MB of free memory. Retrieval filtering covers only the gateway's RAG Injector collections.

Source statement: "helps protect sensitive information in client request bodies before they reach upstream AI providers or tools" (https://developer.konghq.com/ai-gateway/policies/ai-sanitizer/)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: AI PII Sanitizer masks PII in requests and responses through a separate PII service, with placeholder or synthetic replacement and custom regex patterns. AI RAG Injector limits which retrieval collections a consumer or consumer group can reach, with per-collection allow and deny lists and metadata filters.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI PII Sanitizer, AI RAG Injector collection ACLs

## Gaps

Retrieval control covers only the gateway's own RAG Injector vector store. The RAG page gives conflicting examples on default ACL behaviour. PII service must be deployed separately.
