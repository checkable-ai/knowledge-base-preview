## Answer

Covered. PII Sanitizer needs a separately deployed AI PII Anonymizer Service (Docker). Retrieval filtering covers only the gateway's RAG Injector collections.

Source statement: "helps protect sensitive information in client request bodies before they reach upstream AI providers or tools." (https://developer.konghq.com/ai-gateway/policies/ai-sanitizer/)

Research detail: AI PII Sanitizer masks PII in requests and responses through a separate PII service, with placeholder or synthetic replacement and custom regex patterns. AI RAG Injector limits which retrieval collections a consumer or consumer group can reach, with per-collection allow and deny lists and metadata filters.

## Products
