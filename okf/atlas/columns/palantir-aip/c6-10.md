## Answer

Not covered. Retention of chat sessions and agent memory is not documented. Provider non-retention and regional endpoints fall short.

Research detail: Third-party LLM providers do not retain prompts or completions or train on them, and regional endpoints are used where available. Retention policies delete dataset data, and session logging guidance tells teams to set a retention period before export.
