## Answer

Not covered. No long-term cross-session memory, retention or access control is documented. Stored sessions fall short.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Chat sessions persist and can be listed and retrieved through the Agents API, so past conversations stay available. No cross-session memory that feeds earlier conversations into new ones is documented for chatbots.
