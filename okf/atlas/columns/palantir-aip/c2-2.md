## Answer

Covered. Model availability differs by enrollment and region. Provider data terms depend on the customer agreement.

Source statement: "AIP is designed to take advantage of regional endpoints wherever available and possible." (https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/aip/aip-security/)

Research detail: AIP lists supported LLM versions from several providers, and administrators control which model families are enabled. Chatbot builders choose a model from those enabled and set temperature. Third-party providers do not retain or train on prompts, regional endpoints are used where available, and TPM and RPM limits apply per model.
