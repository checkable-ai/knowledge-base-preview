## Answer

Covered. Limits apply at enrollment, project and user level per model. No per-agent quota, cost budget or prompt injection screening documented.

Source statement: "The standard unit of measure across the industry is tokens per minute (TPM) and requests per minute (RPM)." (https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/aip/llm-capacity-management/)

Research detail: All AIP LLM traffic is capped per model at enrollment, project and user level in tokens and requests per minute, with reserved capacity and group overrides. Administrators enable models per enrollment or user group, and a Markings policy rejects requests from sessions carrying markings outside an allowlist.
