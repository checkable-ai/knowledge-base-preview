## Answer

Not covered. No prompt or response DLP. The Markings policy checks session markings, not content.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Markings are mandatory controls that propagate to derived data. An enrollment policy limits which markings may be present in sessions sending requests to AIP models, and users can pick a scoped session. Sensitive Data Scanner can apply markings, create issues or obfuscate matched data.
