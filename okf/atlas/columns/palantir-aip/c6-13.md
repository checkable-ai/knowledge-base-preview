## Answer

Covered. Palantir's SafeBase Trust Center holds pen test reports, compliance information and SOC and ISO certifications. Non-public reports go to existing and prospective customers under NDA on request. Palantir announced IRAP PROTECTED for Foundry and AIP on Palantir Platform Australia (Australian AWS regions) on 20 November 2025, but its public material does not give the assessed service scope or ISM version. The public docs do not confirm ISO/IEC 42001 or privacy impact assessment material.

Source statement: "pen test reports, compliance information, certifications (such as SOC and ISO)" (https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/security/overview/)

Research detail: Palantir states it achieved IRAP PROTECTED on 20 November 2025, assessed by an independent IRAP assessor, and that Palantir Platform Australia delivers Foundry and AIP hosted in Australian AWS regions. Palantir's security overview says a SafeBase trust centre holds pen test reports and certifications such as SOC and ISO for customers under NDA. The release is Palantir's own statement and the IRAP report itself was not seen.
