## Answer

Not covered. No workflow sequencing, handoff between agents or retry semantics. The Tasks extension (opt-in) and cancellation (cooperative) fall short of the full outcome.

Research detail: The protocol defines request cancellation, multi round-trip requests for interim input, and the Tasks extension gives durable task handles with working, input_required, completed, failed and cancelled states. It does not sequence workflows or delegate between agents.
