## Answer

Not covered. Registry versions are unique and immutable, but there is no approval workflow, rollback, or coverage of models, prompts or permissions. Pinning is guidance.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Registry versions MUST be unique and cannot be changed once published. The guide recommends pinning versions and choosing clients that re-prompt when tool definitions change, which the protocol does not require.
