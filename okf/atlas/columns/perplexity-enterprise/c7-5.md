## Answer

Not covered. Admins can allowlist or blocklist Comet extensions and gate custom connectors, but no SBOM, scanning or review of connectors, skills or Comet is documented.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Admins can allow, block or force-install Comet extensions through MDM policies, require an acknowledgement and admin share for custom MCP connectors, and restrict sandbox egress with the firewall policy. Remote MCP URLs must use HTTPS.
