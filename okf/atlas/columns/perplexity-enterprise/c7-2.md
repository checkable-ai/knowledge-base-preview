## Answer

Not covered. Audit events stream to a webhook with Splunk formatting, but there are no built-in detections, redaction or response ownership.

Research detail: The audit stream can authenticate to a webhook with a bearer or custom header and is wrapped in Splunk HEC format when the header starts with Splunk. Session IDs link queries and answers, and events carry user and IP. Comet Telemetry can send scheduled alerts on conditions.

## Products
