## Answer

Not covered. No versioning or rollback of skills, connectors or permissions, and model changes are made by Perplexity without customer approval.

Research detail: Settings changes are audited with old and new values, role and group changes are audited, and skill installs can require admin approval. Firewall changes apply to new tasks only.
