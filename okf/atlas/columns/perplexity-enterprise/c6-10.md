## Answer

Not covered. Retention windows, Incognito enforcement and memory deletion exist, but data residency choices are not documented and Comet keeps page context for up to 30 days.

Research detail: Admins set session retention windows or enforce Incognito, files attached to sessions are deleted after seven days, and turning off Memory deletes memories. Enterprise data, connector files, memory and Computer sandbox contents are excluded from model training, and provider agreements prohibit training.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Data Retention, Enforce Incognito Mode, Memory controls, no-training commitment

## Gaps

Data residency choices are not documented apart from some US-hosted models. Custom retention needs 50 seats or one Max seat. Comet keeps page context for up to 30 days.
