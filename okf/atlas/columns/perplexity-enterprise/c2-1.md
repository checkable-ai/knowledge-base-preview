## Answer

Not covered. Ordering and delegation exist for Computer, but retry, recovery and cancellation behaviour are not documented.

Research detail: Computer runs multi-step tasks asynchronously, delegates to domain sub-agents and coordination agents, orchestrates multiple tools, and runs scheduled tasks. Tasks pause when credits run out and resume when credits return. Scheduled tasks carry their own model and fall back to the default model when theirs is unavailable.
