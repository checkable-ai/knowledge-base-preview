## Answer

Not covered. Computer Analytics reports credit cost by model, user and connector, but does not measure labour savings or value delivered.

Research detail: Computer Analytics shows credit use by day, model and credit source, top users, connectors, skills, projects and task duration, with export and an analytics API. Insights shows users, active users and query volumes. Admins see per-member credit use and pool balances.
