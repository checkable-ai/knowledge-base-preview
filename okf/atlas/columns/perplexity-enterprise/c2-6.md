## Answer

Covered. Cleared memories may be logged up to 30 days.

Source statement: "If an organization admin disables either setting at the organization level, it is also disabled for all users in the org." (https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13654357-memory-for-enterprise-organizations)

Research detail: Enterprise Memory stores preferences and past searches across sessions. Admins can turn off search history references and saved memories for the organisation, and turning memories off deletes existing ones. Memories belong to the organisation. Brain, in research preview for Enterprise Max, keeps a per-user context graph for Computer and can be disabled by admins.
