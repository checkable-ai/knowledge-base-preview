## Answer

Not covered. No agent lifecycle process. Role permissions, master switches and skill approvals exist, but there is no provisioning, versioning or retirement of agents.

Research detail: Admins control who can use Computer and Comet through role permissions and organisation master switches, and who can create organisation skills. SCIM syncs members and admin or member groups from the IdP. The Skills Marketplace lets admins decide whether skill installation requests are approved automatically. Role, group and membership changes are written to the audit log.

## Products

Roles and permissions, Computer access, Manage organization skills, Skills Marketplace

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

There are no agent identities to provision. Custom roles and groups depend on a sales-assisted plan. Skill versioning and retirement are not described beyond deletion.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Grant Computer and skill creation to named groups and review role events in the audit stream.
