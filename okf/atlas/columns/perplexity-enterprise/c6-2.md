## Answer

Not covered. Admins gate custom connectors and skills with toggles, but there is no submission, registration and recorded approval workflow for models, agents or tools.

Research detail: Since September 2026 admins control who can create organisation skills and whether skill installation requests are approved automatically. Only admins can share custom connectors organisation-wide, and members cannot add custom connectors unless admins allow it.

## Products

Skills Marketplace approvals, Manage organization skills, custom connector sharing

## Gaps

No submission and approval workflow for models or agents. Connector approval is a toggle and admin action, not a recorded review.

## Options
