## Answer

Covered. Custom retention needs an Enterprise organisation with 50 seats or one Enterprise Max user.

Source statement: "Data Retention is measured from a session's last activity, not its creation date." (https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11652701-data-retention-for-enterprise)

Research detail: Sessions (threads) keep conversation state and can be resumed from the library. Admins set a session retention window measured from last activity, or enforce Incognito so sessions are kept for 24 hours and cannot be shared. Sharing is limited to the organisation unless public sharing is allowed.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Sessions, Data Retention, Enforce Incognito Mode, sharing controls

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Custom retention needs 50 seats or one Enterprise Max seat. Sessions created before a member joined are not covered.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set a retention window and disable public sharing.
