## Answer

Not covered. Admins can restrict providers and models and enterprise data is not used for training, but the exact model version per output, endpoint and hosting region are not pinned or documented.

Research detail: Enterprise members can pick named models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Moonshot, NVIDIA and Perplexity, or leave Best to choose. Admins can restrict model providers or individual models, and Computer skips models the organisation has disabled. Enterprise data is never used for training and provider agreements prohibit training and retention.
