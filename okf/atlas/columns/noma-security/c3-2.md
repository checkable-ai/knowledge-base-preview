## Answer

Not covered. The AI-DR page documents per-agent and per-tool policies for MCP connections, tool access and actions, and real-time blocking of unauthorised actions. Noma does not document input-format or schema enforcement on tool calls, rate limits or quotas, or control of internet egress. It also has no gateway of its own: enforcement runs through third-party MCP gateways, hooks, SDKs or APIs.

Research detail: Noma enforces per-user, per-agent allowed MCP servers and tools, blocks out-of-scope and destructive actions before they run, and inspects tool responses for embedded prompt injection, exfiltration patterns and cross-server shadowing. Enforcement runs through MCP gateways, hooks, SDKs or APIs. Schema validation, quotas, rates and internet egress control are not documented.
