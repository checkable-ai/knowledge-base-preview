## Answer

Not covered. No documented controlled process to create or onboard an agent, change its configuration, or retire it. The live registry and Approved, Requires Review and Blocked states govern discovery and connections, but they are not a lifecycle workflow.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: The AI Registry, populated from discovery, assigns agents, MCP servers and skills a status of approved, needs review or blocked, and new connections are flagged before data flows. Noma does not create, change or retire agent identities or configurations in the source platforms.
