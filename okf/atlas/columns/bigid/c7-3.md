## Answer

Not covered. No model file inspection, lineage attestation or AI bill of materials.

Research detail: BigID detects and catalogues managed models on Azure OpenAI, Hugging Face and OpenAI, flags unmanaged LLM files in unstructured stores and code repositories, and alerts when sensitive data was used in training. It does not inspect model files for malicious content or produce an AI bill of materials.
