## Answer

Not covered. Per-query retrieval enforcement and gateway deployment not documented.

Research detail: Prompt Protection detects personal, health, payment and credential data in prompts and responses, masks or redacts it, and applies policies by app, group, user, access rights or data type. RAG security classifies retrieval sources and vector environments and maps identities to the data they can reach. Per-query retrieval enforcement is not documented.
