## Answer

Not covered. BigID discovers agent and non-human identities but does not issue them or bind them to a runtime and release.

Research detail: BigID discovers AI agents, service accounts and other non-human identities and maps them to the data they can reach. It does not issue or manage agent or workload identities, or bind them to a runtime and release.
