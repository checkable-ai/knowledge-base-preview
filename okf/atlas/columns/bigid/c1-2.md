## Answer

Not covered. BigID maps and prioritises over-permissioned agents and supports remediation, but documents no per-call enforcement of what an agent may do and no control of non-data actions.

Research detail: BigID maps what agents can reach across SaaS, cloud, APIs and data sources, compares provisioned access with observed access and surfaces over-permissioned agents with remediation paths. Remediation reduces access through workflows, but no per-call enforcement of agent scopes is described. Coverage is limited to data access, not arbitrary actions.
