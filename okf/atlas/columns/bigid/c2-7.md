## Answer

Not covered. No sandbox, egress control or kill switch. Access remediation falls short of immediate revocation.

Research detail: BigID does not run agent code, so it offers no sandbox, filesystem or process policy, or egress control. Its agent and non-human identity pages describe finding and reducing excessive access through remediation, with no stop control for a running agent and no immediate revocation mechanism.
