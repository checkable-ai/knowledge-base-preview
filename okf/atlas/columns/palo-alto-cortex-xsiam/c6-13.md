## Answer

Covered. FedRAMP High and Moderate authorisation applies to US federal use. IRAP PROTECTED assessment is portfolio-level and AgentiX is not named in it. No ISO 42001 or AI red-team attestation found.

Source statement: "Cortex XSIAM is FedRAMP High- and Moderate-authorized for U.S. federal agencies and regulated industries." (https://cortex-docs.paloaltonetworks.com/cortex-xsiam/onboard-cortex-xsiam/deployment-steps/fedramp-overview/cortex-xsiam-federal-compliance)

Research detail: Palo Alto Networks publishes an IRAP assessment at PROTECTED level whose current service list includes Cortex XSIAM, Cortex XDR, Cortex XSOAR and Cortex Data Lake. The page gives no assessment date or ISM version, and the 2022 announcement covered five services against the December 2021 ISM. Cortex XSIAM is FedRAMP High and Moderate authorised, and the July 2026 blog says AgentiX holds FedRAMP Moderate and High. The Trust Center lists SOC 2+ and ISO certifications without product scope.
