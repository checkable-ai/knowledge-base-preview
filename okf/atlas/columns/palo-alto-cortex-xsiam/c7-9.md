Source statement: "Provides a unified inventory and visibility into deployed agents, mitigating against "shadow AI" risks." (https://cortex-docs.paloaltonetworks.com/cortex-xsiam/cloud-security/cortex-cloud-saas-security/saas-ai-agent-security)

Cloud AI Security discovers AI assets on AWS, Azure, GCP and self managed models. SaaS AI Agent Security inventories agents on platforms such as Microsoft 365 Copilot, ChatGPT Enterprise and Cursor Enterprise.
