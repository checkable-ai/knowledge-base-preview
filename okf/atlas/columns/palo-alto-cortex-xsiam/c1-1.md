Source statement: "intercepts actions that violate guardrails or custom policies before they execute" (https://www.paloaltonetworks.com/blog/security-operations/cortex-xdr-6-0/)

The release notes describe AES as visibility, discovery and risk assessment for AI plugins, components and binaries, and require an additional licence.
