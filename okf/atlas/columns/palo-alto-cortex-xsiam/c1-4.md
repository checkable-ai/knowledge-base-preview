## Answer

Not covered. RBAC governs who can create, edit and disable agents in the Hub, but no controlled process or approval step for setup, change and retirement is documented.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: RBAC controls who can view, create, edit, delete, enable and disable agents and actions in the Agentic Assistant Hub. System agents can be enabled and disabled, custom actions can be edited or deleted, and knowledge source changes are logged in the management audit dataset.
