## Answer

Not covered. No documented process for who writes, owns, versions, publishes or reviews policies. The Policy plugin lets operators author policy.jsonc with scoped overlays and records attestation hashes, but its checks observe config conformance only.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Policy is authored in policy.jsonc, with sections for channels, MCP servers, model providers, network, gateway, sandbox, data handling, secrets and exec approvals, scoped overlays for named agents and an attestation hash. Versioning, ownership and review of the file are left to the customer.

## Products

Policy plugin, policy.jsonc, scoped overlays

## Gaps

Policy does not enforce tool calls. Publication and review cycles are not part of the plugin.

## Options
