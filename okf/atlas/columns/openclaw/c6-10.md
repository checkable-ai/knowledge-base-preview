## Answer

Partly covered. No single retention and permitted-use policy across inputs, outputs and memory. Memory forget does not remove transcripts.

Source statement: "Records expire after 30 days and the ledger is capped at 100,000 rows." (https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/config-observability.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: State, memory and credentials live on the operator's hardware. Session maintenance prunes sessions on a schedule, audit records expire after 30 days, memory forget removes tracked artifacts, and telemetry beyond a daily version check is opt-in. There is no single retention and permitted use policy across inputs, outputs and memory.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Session maintenance, audit expiry, memory forget, telemetry controls, uninstall

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Forget does not remove transcripts. Prompts go to the providers configured, so provider terms set their residency and retention.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set session and audit retention to policy and choose providers by region and terms.
