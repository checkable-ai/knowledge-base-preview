## Answer

Partly covered. No single register across agents, models and tools, and no registry of models or component records.

Source statement: "Provider-status labels include optional account display names beside account IDs." (https://docs.openclaw.ai/cli/agents.md)

Research detail: CLI inventories cover agents, bindings, skills, plugins, MCP servers, devices and nodes. The Policy plugin can require governed tools to declare risk and owner metadata in AGENTS.md. There is no single registry across all of these.
