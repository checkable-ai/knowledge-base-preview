## Answer

Not covered. No assessment of use cases. ClawHub risk levels (Low, Medium, High) rate skill and plugin releases, and the security audit grades configuration findings, but neither rates a deployment use case or matches it to controls.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: ClawHub rates each skill or plugin release with a risk level (Low, Medium or High) for its blast radius, and the security audit grades findings by severity. There is no assessment of use cases for the deployment.
