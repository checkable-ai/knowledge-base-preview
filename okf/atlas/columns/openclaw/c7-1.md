## Answer

Not covered. The Gateway audit ledger is metadata-only, capped at 100,000 rows, pruned after 30 days and best-effort. The vendor says it is not a lossless compliance archive. No write-once or integrity-validated storage and no tamper detection is documented, and the HMAC key sits in the same state database.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The Gateway keeps a metadata-only audit ledger of agent runs and tool actions, with optional execution identity and operator decision receipts. It never stores prompts, tool arguments or results. Records expire after 30 days, the ledger is capped and writing is best effort.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Audit history, execution identity, decision receipts, openclaw audit

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Not tamper-proof and not lossless. No inputs, outputs or reasoning are stored. Audit records are not authorisation evidence.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Ship audit and OTel data to an immutable external store and capture content under a retention decision.
