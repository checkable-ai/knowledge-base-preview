## Answer

Partly covered. No approval flow for prompt, tool or permission changes, and prompts and knowledge are not versioned. Update rollback is conditional and can be refused.

Source statement: "Saves retain their snapshot-hash compare-and-set check; a stale hash requires reloading and reapplying the edit." (https://docs.openclaw.ai/cli/approvals.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Plugin and skill installs can be pinned to a version and updates are tracked, config writes use snapshot hash compare-and-set, policy drift can be watched, and updates keep a durable run ledger with repair and rollback paths. Prompts and knowledge changes are not versioned by the platform.
