## Answer

Covered. Lobster is a separately installed plugin (@openclaw/lobster) that is not installed or enabled by default, and must be added to the tool allowlist. It is disabled in sandboxed tool contexts.

Source statement: "side effects (send, post, delete) halt the workflow until explicitly approved." (https://docs.openclaw.ai/tools/lobster)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Pending exec, plugin and system-agent approvals are listed for an operator to review, and Ask me mode puts a human on exec escalations. Sessions can require a human decision before a message is sent as peer input. There is no general review queue for agent output.

## Products

Approvals, Ask me permission mode, session send policy

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Review covers actions and peer messages. Review of generated content before use is left to the operator.

## Options
