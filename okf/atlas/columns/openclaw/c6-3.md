## Answer

Partly covered. No fleet-level view of control coverage, open risks or governance status across deployments is documented. The policy checks observe configuration conformance only.

Source statement: "A clean policy check emits policy, evidence, findings, and attestation hashes that operators can record for audit." (https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/reference/policy)

Research detail: The security audit and policy check report drift, findings and severity, and policy findings feed doctor lint. Policy state can be accepted and watched for drift with an attestation hash.
