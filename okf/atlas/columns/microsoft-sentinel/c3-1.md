## Answer

Not covered. No model call gateway, quotas or prompt injection screening. MCP service limits protect the service only.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Sentinel does not route model traffic or enforce per-agent quotas or injection screening on model calls. The MCP server applies its own fixed quotas, such as a 120-second streaming limit, which protect the service rather than govern agent model use.
