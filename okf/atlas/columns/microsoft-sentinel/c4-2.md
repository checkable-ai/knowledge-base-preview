## Answer

Not covered. No brokering, rotation or revocation of agent credentials. Playbook connections serve Sentinel's own automation.

Research detail: Checked roles, playbook and MCP documentation. Sentinel does not broker credentials to agents. Playbooks run under a Sentinel service account and Logic Apps connections, which serves Sentinel's own automation, not agent secret delivery.
