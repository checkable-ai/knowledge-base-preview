## Answer

Not covered. Sentinel lists only saved custom MCP tools in the Advanced hunting Tools tab. It has no register of the models, agents and tools in use with their components. Broader AI inventories sit in Defender and Agent 365.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Sentinel does not maintain an inventory of models, agents or tools. Agent inventory with owner and platform is in the Defender AgentsInfo table, and that table is not among the Defender XDR tables listed for streaming into Sentinel.
