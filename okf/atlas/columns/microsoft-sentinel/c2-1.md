## Answer

Not covered. No agent orchestration runtime with handoff, cancellation, retry or recovery. Playbooks and automation rules sequence SOC response steps only.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Sentinel automation rules and Logic Apps playbooks sequence SOC response steps, but they do not orchestrate agent workflows with delegation, cancellation or recovery of agent tasks. This sits outside the product's category.
