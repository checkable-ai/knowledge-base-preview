## Answer

Not covered. No documented agent telemetry pipeline with redaction of sensitive data, linking to sessions and people, and a named response owner. The Global Secure Access MCP workbook is in preview and comes from a separate product (Microsoft Entra Global Secure Access). It visualises traffic but does not meet the full outcome.

Research detail: Sentinel is a SIEM that ingests agent telemetry through the Agent 365 connector, Copilot audit logs through the Microsoft Copilot connector into CopilotActivity, and Defender XDR tables such as CloudAppEvents, AlertInfo and AlertEvidence. Analytics rules group alerts into incidents with entities, UEBA adds context, playbooks automate response, and transformations can redact sensitive columns. Agent-specific detections are generated by Defender and synchronised into the shared incident queue.

## Products

Microsoft Sentinel SIEM, Agent 365 connector, Microsoft Copilot connector, Defender XDR connector, UEBA, playbooks

## Gaps

The Agent 365 connector entry lists no tables or setup steps. CopilotActivity is lake-only ingestion. Sentinel documents few agent-specific analytics rules of its own.

## Options
