## Answer

Not covered. No documented agent-specific telemetry pipeline with redaction, session and person linking, and a named response owner. Generic Defender XDR incident and alert sync falls short.

Research detail: Sentinel is a SIEM that ingests agent telemetry through the Agent 365 connector, Copilot audit logs through the Microsoft Copilot connector into CopilotActivity, and Defender XDR tables such as CloudAppEvents, AlertInfo and AlertEvidence. Analytics rules group alerts into incidents with entities, UEBA adds context, playbooks automate response, and transformations can redact sensitive columns. Agent-specific detections are generated by Defender and synchronised into the shared incident queue.
