## Answer

Not covered. UEBA and analytics rules are not documented as profiling AI agents. There is no task training or continuous validation of agent behaviour.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: UEBA builds machine-learning baselines for users, hosts, IP addresses and applications and scores deviations, and analytics rules flag unusual behaviour. Agent behaviour reaches Sentinel through the Agent 365 and Microsoft Copilot connectors and Defender XDR tables. Agent-specific detections such as jailbreak and indirect prompt injection are produced by Microsoft Defender, not by Sentinel.

## Products

UEBA, analytics rules, Agent 365 and Microsoft Copilot connectors

## Gaps

UEBA documentation does not list AI agents as profiled entities. No task training or continuous validation of agents. Defender agent detections are in preview.
