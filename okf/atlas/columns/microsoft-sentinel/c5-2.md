## Answer

Not covered. UEBA does not list AI agents as profiled entities; no task training or continuous validation. Agent-specific detections come from Defender.

Research detail: UEBA builds machine-learning baselines for users, hosts, IP addresses and applications and scores deviations, and analytics rules flag unusual behaviour. Agent behaviour reaches Sentinel through the Agent 365 and Microsoft Copilot connectors and Defender XDR tables. Agent-specific detections such as jailbreak and indirect prompt injection are produced by Microsoft Defender, not by Sentinel.
