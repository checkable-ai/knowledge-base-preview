## Answer

Not covered. Graph blast radius is an incident investigation view, not a standing per-agent map of reach and permitted actions.

Research detail: Sentinel graph models assets, identities and activities as nodes and edges and powers blast radius analysis in the Defender incident graph, which shows paths from a compromised entity to critical assets. Defender uses this to trace the blast radius of AI agent threats. It is an investigation view, not a standing map of each agent's reach and action scopes.
