## Answer

Not covered. No per-agent retrieval filtering or DLP. Llama Guard classifies content only.

Research detail: Llama Guard 4 classifies prompts and responses against hazard categories including Privacy. ClassifyIt labels Google Drive files for sensitivity using a Llama Stack model. Meta's Llama security guide advises propagating ACLs to RAG stores and output DLP, but these are guidance, not shipped features.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Llama Guard 4, SensitiveDocClassification (ClassifyIt)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No per-agent or per-user retrieval enforcement or DLP in the Model API. ClassifyIt labels files and does not gate retrieval.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use a third-party data gateway or vector store with document ACLs, and add Llama Guard as an output filter.
