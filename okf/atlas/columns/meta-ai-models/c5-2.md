## Answer

Research detail: CyberSecEval benchmarks measure insecure code, prompt injection susceptibility, attack compliance and false refusal for any model endpoint. LlamaFirewall AlignmentCheck audits agent reasoning in real time for goal hijacking. Meta documents fine-tuning and RL for Muse Glimmer.
