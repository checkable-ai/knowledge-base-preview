## Answer

Not covered. Agent definitions are hand-edited JSON or Markdown files, or a per-run --agents flag. Validation only rejects malformed definitions. There is no approval step, registry, or documented change and retire process.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: API keys are created, named and deleted in the dashboard and are shared by the team. Businesses use managed accounts tied to a work email and SSO, and only team admins manage payment and business details. No documented roles, key permissions or agent configuration lifecycle.
