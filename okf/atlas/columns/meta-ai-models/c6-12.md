## Answer

Not covered. No approval, scheduled reassessment or retirement process for external models, tools and services. Plugin approval is per user only.

Research detail: Muse Code lets users approve or reject third-party plugins and their agents. The terms disclaim third-party features and note that calls through hosted platforms follow that platform's terms. Llama licences add a 700 million MAU clause and attribution duties, and Llama is not offered on the Model API.
