## Answer

Not covered. No documented retention periods, processing region or deprecation policy for prompt data. Versioned model IDs and rate limits fall short.

Research detail: Models have explicit versioned IDs such as muse-spark-1.3, served at api.meta.ai/v1 with per-team RPM and TPM limits by tier. Standard tier content is not used for training, contributor tier content is, and ZDR is available to qualified organisations on request. Retention periods, processing region and a deprecation policy are not stated.

## Products

## Gaps

No deprecation or retirement policy. Section 4.4 of the terms gives no retention periods. Terms describe a limited preview that Meta may change without notice. Press reports US-only access at launch, while the geographic policy lists restricted territories only.
