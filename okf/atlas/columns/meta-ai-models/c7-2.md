## Answer

Not covered. No documented SIEM or OpenTelemetry export, detections, redaction or response ownership. Customer-built hooks fall short.

Research detail: Muse Code session logs carry session IDs, approval records and timestamps, exports can be redacted, and hooks fire on events such as PreToolUse, PostLLMCall and SessionEnd that can forward data. No native SIEM export or detections.
