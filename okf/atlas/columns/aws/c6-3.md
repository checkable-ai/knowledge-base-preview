## Answer

Not covered. AWS does not document a product feature that reports AI control coverage, policy breaches, open risks and governance status. The nearest features fall short. The Audit Manager generative AI framework collects evidence but does not verify compliance, and Audit Manager is closed to new customers. AI Risk Intelligence (AIRI) appears only in a blog post as a solution from the AWS Generative AI Innovation Center, which you get by contacting AWS or booking a demo. AWS does not document it as a generally available product feature.
