## Answer

Covered. A runtime or gateway is configured with either SigV4 or OAuth. With OAuth a wildcard principal is required and access is restricted by condition keys.

Source statement: "Resource-based policies in Amazon Bedrock AgentCore allow you to control which principals (AWS accounts, IAM users, or IAM roles) can invoke and manage your Amazon Bedrock AgentCore resources (supported for Runtime, Gateway and Memory)." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/resource-based-policies.html)
