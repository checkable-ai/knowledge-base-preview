## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Applies to traffic through AgentCore Gateway with a policy engine attached. Guardrail scoring is probabilistic, and sensitive-information guardrails suppress the whole response rather than redacting fields. Guardrail categories and Region availability are listed on the Guardrails in policies page. Sensitive-content checks use the separately charged Amazon Bedrock Guardrails service.

Source statement: "Suppress the entire claim status response when it contains a social security number, so the caller receives nothing rather than the sensitive value." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/example-policies-guardrails.html)
