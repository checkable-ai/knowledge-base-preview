## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Approval governs discoverability and does not itself authorise runtime actions. Models use a separate SageMaker approval status. Registry semantic search may process inputs and outputs in any commercial AWS Region while storing registry data in its primary Region.

Source statement: "AWS Agent Registry is a fully managed discovery service that provides a centralized catalog for organizing, curating, and discovering resources across your organization." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/registry.html)

Research detail: Source statement: Registry documents publisher submission and curator approval.
