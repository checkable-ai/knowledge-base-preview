## Answer

Covered. Applies to agents, MCP servers, tools, skills and custom records. Approval governs discoverability and does not itself authorise runtime actions. Models use a separate SageMaker approval status.

Source statement: "A curator (or the administrator) reviews records pending approval and approves or rejects them; They also deprecate records no longer in use." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/registry.html)

Research detail: Source statement: Registry documents publisher submission and curator approval.

Assessment: Discoverability approval does not by itself authorise every runtime action.
