## Answer

Covered. Reports are available to customers through AWS Artifact. The AWS Top Secret cloud for Australia is not open to general buyers.

Source statement: "For more information, you can also go to AWS Artifact to access the IRAP PROTECTED pack from the most recent assessment." (https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/irap/)

Research detail: An ASD-certified IRAP assessor completed the 2026 Phase 1a assessment of AWS in June 2026, bringing 167 services to PROTECTED, including Amazon Bedrock AgentCore. The IRAP services list includes Amazon Bedrock, excluding Bedrock Marketplace. AWS holds ISO/IEC 42001 certification from Schellman for a listed set of AI services, and SOC 1 and SOC 2 reports. Reports and the IRAP pack, with a consumer guide and PROTECTED reference architecture, are in AWS Artifact. The Australian Government announced a Top Secret cloud partnership between ASD and AWS in July 2024 for the National Intelligence Community and Defence.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

IRAP PROTECTED (167 services), ISO/IEC 42001, SOC 1, SOC 2, AWS Artifact, IRAP PROTECTED reference architecture, AWS Top Secret cloud for Australia

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The ISO 42001 service list is only in AWS Artifact. The Top Secret cloud is described by the government, not by AWS documentation, and its assurance and availability are not public. It is not open to general buyers.

## Options
