## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Agents must be instrumented with the OpenTelemetry or OpenInference libraries, and Strands and LangGraph are named as supported frameworks. Online evaluation must be configured with session sampling rules. Online evaluation must also be configured with built-in or custom evaluators. Scores come from LLM-as-a-Judge evaluators. Anomalies surface as low evaluator scores, not through a separate anomaly detector. Evaluation inference can be processed in another region within your geography, though data stays stored in the originating region.

Source statement: "AgentCore Evaluations integrates with popular agent frameworks including Strands and LangGraph with OpenTelemetry and OpenInference instrumentation libraries." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/evaluations.html)

Research detail: Source statement: Evaluations scores instrumented agent traces with built-in or custom judges.

Assessment: Judge scores need calibration against human-labelled failure cases.
