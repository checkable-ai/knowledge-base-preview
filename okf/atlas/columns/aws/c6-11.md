## Answer

Covered, with conditions. AWS Audit Manager is no longer open to new customers; existing customers can continue to use it. Assessments and evidence sources must be configured. Audit Manager does not assess compliance itself. Assessments must run in the accounts and Regions hosting the AI workloads. Audit Manager is restricted to existing configured accounts and Regions following its April 30, 2026 maintenance-mode change.

Source statement: "Then, it's attached to the relevant controls to help you demonstrate compliance in security, change management, business continuity, and software licensing." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/audit-manager/latest/userguide/what-is.html)

Last checked 17 November 2026.
