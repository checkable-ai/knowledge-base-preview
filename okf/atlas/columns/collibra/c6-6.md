## Answer

Covered. Owner resource role must be assigned.

Source statement: "At least one user or user group is assigned the Owner resource role on the community or domain of the asset." (https://productresources.collibra.com/docs/collibra/latest/Content/AICommandCenter/ta_aicc-edit-trust-score.htm)

Research detail: Every registered use case, model and agent has an Owner, shown in the registry. The Trust Score documentation theme checks that an Owner role is assigned, and assessment owners and assignees receive notifications.
