## Answer

Covered. New lifecycle tracker in AI Command Center; legacy tracker in AI Governance.

Source statement: "You cannot advance to the next status in the Core phase if there are required activities that have not been completed." (https://productresources.collibra.com/docs/collibra/2026.08/Content/Assets/LifecycleMgmt/co_lifecycle-tracker.htm)

Research detail: Lifecycle management is on by default for AI Agent, AI Agent Version, AI Base Model, AI Model Version and AI Use Case. The Lifecycle tracker shows stages, required activities such as assessments and sign-offs, and blocks advancement until they are complete, with a Retirement phase.
