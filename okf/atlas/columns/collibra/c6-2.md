## Answer

Covered. Needs lifecycle management and required activities configured; tool registration not covered.

Source statement: "You cannot advance to the next status in the Core phase if there are required activities that have not been completed." (https://productresources.collibra.com/docs/collibra/2026.08/Content/Assets/LifecycleMgmt/co_lifecycle-tracker.htm)

Research detail: Use cases, models and agents are registered under dedicated permissions, and admins can attach a custom workflow to the register button. Submitted assessments can trigger the Assessments Approval workflow to a Business Steward, and lifecycle stages can require sign-off activities before advancing.
