## Answer

Not covered. The Model Registry records model provenance and assessments, but there is no model file inspection and no AI bill of materials.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: The Model Registry records model versions with provider, model type, owner, status and version, harvested through Edge from platforms such as Bedrock, SageMaker, Azure AI Foundry, Databricks and Anthropic. Model assessments cover model information and data collection, and lineage links models to training data.
