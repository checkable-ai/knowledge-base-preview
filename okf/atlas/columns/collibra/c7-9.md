## Answer

Not covered. The Unauthorized view lists models with status Rejected or Archived, and Unused lists models not linked to a use case. Neither discovers AI never approved or never connected. Edge only harvests platforms an admin has connected. SaaS AI use and local agents are not detected.

Research detail: Edge integrations harvest model and agent metadata from connected platforms, including Claude Managed Agents from Anthropic, and the Model Registry offers Unauthorized and Unused views of models. This finds AI on platforms an administrator has connected.
