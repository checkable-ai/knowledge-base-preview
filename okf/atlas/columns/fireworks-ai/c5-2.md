## Answer

Not covered. No continuous validation of running agent behaviour and no anomaly detection. The agentic RL cookbook and fine-tuning train models on tasks, and observability covers deployment performance, not agent behaviour.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Fireworks offers supervised, DPO and reinforcement fine-tuning, and evaluation of trained checkpoints and promoted models by sampling with a customer-written grader.
