## Answer

Covered. Responses API stores conversations by default and they can be continued, retrieved, listed and deleted; store=false avoids storage. Access is account-wide, with no per-user or per-session isolation documented.

Source statement: "All stored conversation data is automatically deleted after 30 days." (https://docs.fireworks.ai/guides/security_compliance/data_handling.md)

Research detail: Responses API conversations are stored by default and can be continued with previous_response_id, retrieved, listed and deleted. Stored conversation data is deleted automatically after 30 days, and store=false avoids storage.
