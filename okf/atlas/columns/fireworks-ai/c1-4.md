## Answer

Not covered. No agent configuration lifecycle or documented retirement; service account roles and SCIM for human users fall short.

Research detail: Admins create service accounts with a role through firectl and change roles with firectl user update. SCIM provisions and deprovisions human users and groups from Okta, Entra ID or Google Workspace.

## Products

## Gaps
