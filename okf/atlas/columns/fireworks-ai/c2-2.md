## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Data residency is available on Enterprise accounts only. The setting is account-wide and does not move existing traffic or deployments. BYOC deployments run in the customer's own cloud, so Fireworks cannot enforce where they run. Serverless training is blocked, and outside the US Nexus Auto is rejected, while a region is set. Training checkpoint and output storage location is not enforced. Closed-model turns use the customer's Anthropic, OpenAI or Amazon Bedrock account and that provider's data policies.

Source statement: "This setting enforces all requests to be in the set region, and rejects the ones that are not." (https://docs.fireworks.ai/accounts/data-residency.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Serverless, on-demand and reserved deployments run named models with validated deployment shapes, region placement, autoscaling and documented per-account, per-model rate limits. Data residency restricts inference to a US endpoint, and ZDR rejects requests that would store content.
