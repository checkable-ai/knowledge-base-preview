## Answer

Covered. Data residency and ZDR are Enterprise features; data residency excludes FireRouter and training.

Source statement: "Data residency restricts inference on your account to a single region." (https://docs.fireworks.ai/accounts/data-residency.md)

Research detail: Serverless, on-demand and reserved deployments run named models with validated deployment shapes, region placement, autoscaling and documented per-account, per-model rate limits. Data residency restricts inference to a US endpoint, and ZDR rejects requests that would store content.
