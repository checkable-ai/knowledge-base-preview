## Answer

Not covered. Service accounts are account-level non-human identities with roles; they are not per-agent identities tied to an owner, runtime or release.

Research detail: Service accounts give applications and automated systems a non-human identity that acts through API keys and is kept separate from human activity in audit logs. Each service account has a role such as user, contributor or inference-user.

## Products

Service accounts

## Gaps

No agent identity linked to an owner, runtime or release. Service accounts are account-level workload identities, not per-agent records.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Create one service account per agent and record owner and release in an external registry or key annotations.
