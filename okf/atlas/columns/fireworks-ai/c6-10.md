## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Zero Data Retention policy can be turned on by Enterprise account admins. Data residency is available on Enterprise accounts only. Zero data retention does not apply to Batch API or to Responses API requests with store=True (the default). Stored Responses API data is cached and can be evicted earlier than 30 days. Residency enforcement blocks Serverless Training and BYOC, and Nexus Auto outside the US.

Source statement: "Stored conversation data is kept for up to 30 days and is automatically deleted no later than that." (https://docs.fireworks.ai/guides/security_compliance/data_handling.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026. This platform is checked on demand, so this answer has not been rechecked since.

Research detail: Open model prompts and outputs are not stored by default, stored Responses API data is deleted after 30 days, and Enterprise ZDR rejects any request or job that would store content. Data residency restricts inference to US endpoints, and BYOB and CMEK cover training data.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Zero Data Retention, Data residency, BYOB, CMEK

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

ZDR does not delete existing data, data sent to MCP servers or closed-model providers follows their policies, and residency blocks training and FireRouter.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable ZDR and residency on Enterprise accounts and delete existing stored data.
