## Answer

Covered. ZDR and data residency are Enterprise features; ZDR does not delete existing data; residency blocks training and FireRouter; MCP and closed-model providers follow own policies.

Source statement: "All stored conversation data is automatically deleted after 30 days." (https://docs.fireworks.ai/guides/security_compliance/data_handling.md)

Research detail: Open model prompts and outputs are not stored by default, stored Responses API data is deleted after 30 days, and Enterprise ZDR rejects any request or job that would store content. Data residency restricts inference to US endpoints, and BYOB and CMEK cover training data.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Zero Data Retention, Data residency, BYOB, CMEK

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

ZDR does not delete existing data, data sent to MCP servers or closed-model providers follows their policies, and residency blocks training and FireRouter.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable ZDR and residency on Enterprise accounts and delete existing stored data.
