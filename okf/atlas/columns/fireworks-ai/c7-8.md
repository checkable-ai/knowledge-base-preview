## Answer

Not covered. BYOB, CMEK and ZDR secure storage and retention but there is no governance or cleaning of data that agents read; CMEK excludes full-parameter training.

Research detail: Datasets and models can live in a customer bucket (GCS, S3 or Azure) through BYOB, and customer-managed keys cover LoRA SFT, DPO and RFT training. ZDR can reject dataset uploads and training jobs.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

BYOB, CMEK, Secure Training, ZDR

## Gaps

CMEK does not cover full-parameter training, and encryption of final weights is listed as coming soon. No data sanitisation features are documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options
