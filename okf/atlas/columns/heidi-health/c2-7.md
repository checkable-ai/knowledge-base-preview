## Answer

Not covered. No sandboxing, network egress control or computer-use isolation documented, and no way to revoke agent access or disable a Routine. Run stop and approval gating fall short.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Heidi II starts in Manual mode where anything that touches a chart, reaches a patient or leaves the system waits for review. A run can be stopped at any point. The launch post says some actions stay off limits in every mode, and that Heidi will not prescribe, delete a record or submit a claim.
