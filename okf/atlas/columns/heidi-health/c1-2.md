## Answer

Covered. Limits are fixed by Heidi and not customer-configurable. Risk classification and mandatory approvals apply in every mode.

Source statement: "Heidi will not prescribe, delete a record, or submit a claim." (https://www.heidihealth.com/en-au/blog/heidi-ii-is-here)

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Every agent action carries a risk classification. Prescribing, deleting records or destructive EHR actions, submitting claims and making a diagnosis always need approval, and Heidi cannot send drafted mail without the clinician approving the exact text. Admins cannot define their own per-system scopes in the documentation found.
