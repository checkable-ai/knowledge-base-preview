## Answer

Not covered. Heidi does not publish which model version and endpoint each agent uses, or the per-agent operating limits. No-training and regional data storage statements exist but do not cover the model, endpoint and prompt-handling outcome. The usage limits article for Heidi II covers allowances only.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Heidi runs its own hosted models and states that patient and customer data is not used to train models and that data is stored regionally. Model names, versions and endpoints are not published.
