## Answer

Covered. Approve for me mode skips lower-risk steps. High-risk Routine steps still wait for approval. The exact text is approved before any mail is sent.

Source statement: "Approving one action does not approve the next." (https://support.heidihealth.com/en/articles/17212197-approving-actions)

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Ask for approval mode stops at each step that needs it, and nothing reaches a chart, payer, patient or inbox until approved. Approving one action does not approve the next, high-risk steps in routines wait for approval, and every approval decision is recorded with its outcome. Approvals can be given or a run stopped from iOS.
