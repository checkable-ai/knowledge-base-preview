## Answer

Not covered. No admin-controlled process to set up, change or retire agents. Routines are created and edited per user without admin approval. Admins manage users and roles, but the admin article does not mention routines or agent features.

Research detail: Global admins manage users, roles, seat allocation, SSO and MFA, and can sign out members across all devices for offboarding. Routines can be shared so a team runs the same process. There is no separate provisioning of agent identities or permissions.
