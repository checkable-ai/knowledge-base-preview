## Answer

Covered. GPU dashboards and metrics in VCF Operations; Private AI Services Prometheus metrics need a customer-configured observability platform such as Grafana.

Source statement: "This is important so that we can get early warnings of GPU overheating conditions." (https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2024/05/07/whats-new-in-the-vmware-private-ai-foundation-with-nvidia-ga-release/)

Research detail: Prometheus inside Private AI Services collects DCGM GPU, vLLM, inference gateway, indexing and Kubernetes metrics for Grafana. VCF Operations shows GPU and vGPU metrics for VMs, hosts and clusters.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Private AI Services observability, VCF Operations GPU metrics

## Gaps

Alerting and anomaly detection are not described on the metrics page. Grafana must be deployed by the customer.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options
