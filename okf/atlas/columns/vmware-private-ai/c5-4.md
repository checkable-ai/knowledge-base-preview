## Answer

Covered. GPU dashboards are in VCF Operations. Private AI Services Prometheus metrics need a customer-configured observability platform such as Grafana.

Source statement: "VCF Operations now shows you a dashboard summary of the GPU-equipped clusters in your environment." (https://blogs.vmware.com/cloud-foundation/2024/05/07/whats-new-in-the-vmware-private-ai-foundation-with-nvidia-ga-release/)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Prometheus inside Private AI Services collects DCGM GPU, vLLM, inference gateway, indexing and Kubernetes metrics for Grafana. VCF Operations shows GPU and vGPU metrics for VMs, hosts and clusters.

## Products

Private AI Services observability, VCF Operations GPU metrics
