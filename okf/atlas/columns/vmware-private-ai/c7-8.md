## Answer

Not covered. Scheduled refresh and removal of deleted documents only; no sanitisation or document-level access control.

Research detail: Data Indexing and Retrieval indexes documents into a PostgreSQL pgvector database, refreshes on a schedule, and removes embeddings for deleted or unlinked documents on the next run. Data sources can be scoped by space, folder or prefix.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Data Indexing and Retrieval, PostgreSQL with pgvector, VMware Data Services Manager

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No sanitisation of indexed content and no document-level access control are documented. A known Google Drive issue can remove files when permissions change.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Clean source content before indexing and size the vector database at five to ten times the source data, as the design notes suggest.
