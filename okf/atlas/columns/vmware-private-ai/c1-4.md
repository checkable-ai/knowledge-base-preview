## Answer

Not covered. No approval, review or retirement workflow for agents; self-service creation is not a controlled lifecycle.

Research detail: Organisation administrators enable Private AI Services in namespaces from VCF Automation, and users with access to the namespace create, configure and delete agents in Agent Builder. Direct Supervisor use needs the Namespaces Edit role.
