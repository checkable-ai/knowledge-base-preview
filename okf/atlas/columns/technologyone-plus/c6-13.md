## Answer

Covered. Reports such as SOC are released on request to contracting customers. Whether Plus and Guide are in IRAP scope is not stated.

Source statement: "TechnologyOne is subject to independent penetration testing as part of maintaining ISO 27001 certification." (https://www.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-14/services/886485371920512)

Research detail: TechnologyOne states its SaaS completed an IRAP assessment up to and including PROTECTED and that SaaS+ is hosted in IRAP-assessed environments. It publishes ISO 27001, 27017, 27018 and ISO 42001:2023 certificates, offers SOC 1, SOC 2 (with HIPAA) and SOC 3 reports to customers on request, and runs external penetration testing and Privacy Impact Assessments. The 2026A release says the ISO 42001 certification covers its AI-enabled capabilities, including Plus.
