## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Certifications are held at company level. TechnologyOne says Plus is supported by its ISO 42001:2023 certification, and says its SaaS platform has IRAP assessment up to PROTECTED, ISO 27001 and SOC 2. SOC 2 reports are available on request to existing SaaS customers. I found no explicit statement that Guide is within IRAP scope.

Source statement: "Leveraging its deep industry expertise and existing compliance program, TechnologyOne attained the certification in just five months." (https://www.technology1.com/resources/media-releases/technologyone-builds-trust-at-scale-with-global-ai-management-standard-iso-42001)

Last checked 10 November 2026.

Research detail: TechnologyOne states its SaaS completed an IRAP assessment up to and including PROTECTED and that SaaS+ is hosted in IRAP-assessed environments. It publishes ISO 27001, 27017, 27018 and ISO 42001:2023 certificates, offers SOC 1, SOC 2 (with HIPAA) and SOC 3 reports to customers on request, and runs external penetration testing and Privacy Impact Assessments. The 2026A release says the ISO 42001 certification covers its AI-enabled capabilities, including Plus.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

IRAP assessment, ISO 27001, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, ISO 42001, SOC 1, SOC 2, SOC 3, Essential Eight maturity assessment

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The IRAP date and whether Plus and Guide are inside the IRAP scope are not stated. The Essential Eight assessment is dated 2019. Reports are released only to contracting customers through the Customer Community.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Request the IRAP report and SOC 2 report, and confirm in writing that Plus and Guide are in the assessed boundary before an authority-to-operate decision.
