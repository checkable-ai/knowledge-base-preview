## Answer

Research detail: TechnologyOne says Plus runs on the customer's own tenant 'with the same security and governance controls as what you currently have', and that its role-based security lets customers grant or restrict user access to functions, reports and data. Plus permissions are configured automatically when the feature flag is enabled.
