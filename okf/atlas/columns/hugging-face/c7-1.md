## Answer

Not covered. Hugging Face Audit Logs record organisation administrative events only (membership, settings, repository changes). They do not record per-agent tool calls, data access or actions by session, and the documentation describes no write-once or integrity-validated storage or tamper-evidence for such a record.

Research detail: Team and Enterprise audit logs record who did what and when, with location and anonymised IP, across organisation settings, membership, repositories, secrets, resource groups, jobs and billing. Logs export as JSON from settings or the API. Inference Endpoints keeps its own audit log of endpoint changes.
