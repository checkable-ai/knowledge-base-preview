## Answer

Not covered. No submission, registration and approval workflow. Token approval and gating approve access only.

Research detail: Admins can require approval of fine-grained tokens before they reach organisation resources, gate repositories behind manual approval, and require approval for new users with a matching email domain. A Private only visibility setting stops members publishing new repositories publicly.
