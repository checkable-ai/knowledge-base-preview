## Answer

Not covered. Service account and token administration only. No agent lifecycle process for configuration or permission changes.

Research detail: Admins create service accounts and create, update, rotate and delete their tokens. SCIM provisions and deprovisions users and links IdP groups to resource groups. Token policies can require admin approval before a fine-grained token reaches organisation resources.

## Products

## Gaps

Provisioning covers human and service identities and Hub permissions, not agent configurations. Service accounts are not provisioned through SCIM.

## Options
