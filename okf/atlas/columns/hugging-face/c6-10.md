## Answer

Not covered. Storage regions set repository residency only. No retention or deletion rules for agent inputs, outputs or memory.

Research detail: Team and Enterprise organisations can store models, datasets, Spaces and buckets in the US or EU region. Inference Providers routing does not store request or response bodies, and debug logs are kept up to 30 days. The privacy policy keeps data as long as needed to deliver the service.

## Products

Storage Regions, Inference Providers data handling, Privacy Policy

## Gaps

No configurable retention or deletion schedule. Partner providers apply their own data policies. The privacy policy gives no retention periods.

## Options

Choose EU storage where needed and select providers whose terms meet retention requirements.
