## Answer

Not covered. Gating and resource groups control access, but no data cleaning, classification or governance of data stores.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Datasets are hosted as repositories with private visibility, gating with manual approval, resource group access control and US or EU storage regions, and pass malware and secrets scanning.
