## Answer

Not covered. Provider routing and org spending limits exist, but no per-agent or per-workload rate limits or quotas and no prompt injection screening.

Research detail: Inference Providers acts as a proxy router with fastest, cheapest or preferred provider policies. Team and Enterprise admins can set a spending limit and disable providers. Enterprise resource groups add per-group monthly spend limits and can restrict who may bill Inference Providers to the organisation.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Inference Providers router, organisation provider settings, Resource Group spend limits and granular feature access

## Gaps
