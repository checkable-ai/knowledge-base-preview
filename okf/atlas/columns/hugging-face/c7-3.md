## Answer

Not covered. Malware, pickle and third-party scanning exist, but no AI bill of materials, signed provenance or compliance evidence. Model card lineage is self-declared.

Research detail: Every repository file under 2 GB is scanned with ClamAV at each commit, pickle files have their imports listed with suspicious imports flagged, and Protect AI and JFrog scanners report on model files. Model cards record base_model lineage, training datasets and licence, and GPG-signed commits show as verified.

## Products

Malware Scanning, Pickle Import scanning, Protect AI Guardian, JFrog scanner, model card metadata, GPG commit signing

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No AI BoM or signed provenance attestation. Pickle scanning is stated as not foolproof. The Protect AI page says it scans public repositories. Lineage metadata is self-declared.

## Options

Prefer safetensors, require verified commits for internal models, and rescan downloaded weights before deployment.
