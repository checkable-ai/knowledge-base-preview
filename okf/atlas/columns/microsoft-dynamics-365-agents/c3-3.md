## Answer

Not covered. Outbound email governance only, fail-open, and personal data removal may miss instances. No general data access gateway or DLP.

Research detail: Quality Management Agent governance intercepts outbound email bodies and attachments from Case Management Agent and representatives and logs or blocks those that breach natural-language policies. Customer Knowledge Management Agent removes personal data before drafting articles. Sellers see agent output only within their roles.
