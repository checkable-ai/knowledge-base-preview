## Answer

Not covered. Content moderation screens requests for injection, but there is no gateway with routing, quotas or rate limits per agent or user. Credit capacity checks only.

Research detail: Customer Service and Contact Center agents apply content moderation to all generative AI requests, including jailbreak, prompt injection and prompt exfiltration attempts. Dynamics checks Copilot Studio credit capacity and disables AI features when the quota is depleted.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Content moderation for service agents, Copilot Studio credit capacity

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No per-agent or per-user model routing or rate settings in Dynamics.

## Options
