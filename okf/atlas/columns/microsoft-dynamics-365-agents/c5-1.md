Covered. Review is optional in autonomous modes. Applies to AI-assisted case creation, knowledge article review, handed-over leads and AI-assisted quality evaluations.

Source statement: "Service representatives can then review the predicted values and make any necessary changes before saving the case." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/customer-service/administer/set-up-autonomous-case-agents)

Research detail: Sellers work leads handed over or disqualified by the agent in dedicated views, and representatives review predicted case fields and agent-drafted knowledge articles. Quality evaluations can run in AI-assisted mode where a supervisor reviews results. Representative feedback on AI suggestions can be recorded.
