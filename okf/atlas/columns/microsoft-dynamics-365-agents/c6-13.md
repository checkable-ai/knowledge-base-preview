## Answer

Covered. IRAP assessment (PROTECTED) covers Dynamics 365 as a platform; reports are downloaded from the Service Trust Portal. Microsoft also lists Copilot Studio in ISO/IEC 42001 scope. The most recent Dynamics IRAP date stated on the public page is December 2020, and the page does not break scope down to individual agent features. Customers must check the STP scope appendices and engage their own assessor for their deployment.

Source statement: "If your organization requires or seeks an approval to operate in line with the ISM, you can use the IRAP security assessments of Azure, Dynamics 365, Microsoft Managed Desktop, and Office 365 in your risk assessment." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/regulatory/offering-IRAP-Australia)

Research detail: Microsoft lists Dynamics 365 among its IRAP-assessed platforms at PROTECTED, with an incremental Azure and Dynamics assessment completed in December 2020 under the post-CCSL cloud security guidance, and states agencies can use the reports in their own authorisation to operate. Assessment reports are on the Service Trust Portal. Microsoft Copilot Studio, which the Dynamics 365 agents depend on, is in scope for ISO/IEC 42001 certification. Microsoft points to Service Trust Portal appendices for which Dynamics 365 services are in scope of its SOC, ISO/IEC and IRAP audits.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Microsoft IRAP assessments, ISO/IEC 42001 certification for Copilot Studio, Service Trust Portal

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The public pages do not say which Dynamics 365 agent features are in the IRAP scope. The latest Dynamics IRAP date stated publicly is December 2020, which is more than 24 months old. ISO 42001 scope names Copilot Studio, not the Dynamics 365 agents themselves.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Download the current Azure and Dynamics IRAP report from the Service Trust Portal and confirm the agent features and the Australian geography are in scope before an authorisation decision.
