## Answer

Not covered. Review of drafts and predicted case fields before saving is not approval of an exact action. Autonomous modes act without per-action approval, and denial and replay handling is not documented.

Research detail: Several agents offer a supervised mode. The Sales Qualification Agent in research-only mode drafts outreach for sellers to send, representatives review predicted case fields before saving, and Knowledge Management Agent articles can default to Pending until a reviewer marks them compliant. These are review steps on drafts, not approvals bound to an exact action with replay handling.
