## Answer

Not covered. ServiceNow product documentation does not publish IRAP, SOC 2, ISO 27001 or ISO 42001 evidence for these AI features. Reports sit behind the CORE Compliance Portal login and docs note some AI features are unavailable in IRAP-Protected environments.

Research detail: ServiceNow documentation refers to Australia IRAP-Protected data centres and states that some AI products and features are unavailable to customers there, in FedRAMP and NSC DOD IL5 environments, for self-hosted customers and in other restricted environments. It also states AI processing may move data to a centralised ServiceNow environment in a different data centre region or to a third-party cloud provider such as Microsoft Azure. Compliance policies and reports are published through the CORE Compliance Portal, which needs a login.
