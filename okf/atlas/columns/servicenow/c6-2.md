## Answer

Covered. Approvals are off by default and must be enabled.

Source statement: "Activating AI systems, all the AI skills and agents need approval by an AI steward before being deployed." (https://www.servicenow.com/docs/r/intelligent-experiences/ai-control-tower/explore-approvals.html)

Research detail: AI steward approval can gate deployment of AI systems, models and MCP servers, and playbooks create approval requests automatically when active (Brazil). AI intake request forms submit systems, models and datasets for evaluation, and stewards mark agents publishable.
