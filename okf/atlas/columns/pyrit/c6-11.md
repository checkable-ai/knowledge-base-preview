## Answer

Not covered. Findings can be logged and tracked in Foundry, and CoPyRIT exports conversation evidence, but nothing maps evidence to control requirements or audits. The vendor states that use of the product does not guarantee compliance.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Foundry states red teaming findings can be logged, monitored and tracked over time to support compliance, and run output items can be exported as JSON. PyRIT memory persists conversations and scores in SQLite or Azure SQL, and CoPyRIT exports conversations as Markdown, JSON or HTML. This provides red team evidence but no mapping to requirements or audits.
