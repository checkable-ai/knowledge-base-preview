## Answer

Out of scope. Setting and enforcing what an agent may do in connected systems is a runtime control. PyRIT and the Foundry AI Red Teaming Agent test whether an agent breaches a user-supplied prohibited-actions policy and score the result. Kept as out-of-scope.
