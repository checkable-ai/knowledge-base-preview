## Answer

Not covered. CoPyRIT's History view and findings, and the Foundry scorecard, report red team attack outcomes only. There is no view of AI control coverage, policy breaches across the estate, open-risk register or governance status.

Last checked 16 November 2026. This platform is checked on demand, so this answer has not been rechecked since.

Research detail: Foundry's AI Red Teaming Agent produces a scorecard by attack technique and risk category with Attack Success Rate, and findings can be logged and tracked over time in Foundry. PyRIT aggregates outcomes into a ScenarioResult. This reports adversarial test results, not overall control coverage or policy breaches.
