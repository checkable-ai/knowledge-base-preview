## Answer

Not covered. The scorecard reports attack success rates by technique and risk category only. It does not show control coverage, policy breaches across the estate, open risks or governance status.

Research detail: Foundry's AI Red Teaming Agent produces a scorecard by attack technique and risk category with Attack Success Rate, and findings can be logged and tracked over time in Foundry. PyRIT aggregates outcomes into a ScenarioResult. This reports adversarial test results, not overall control coverage or policy breaches.
