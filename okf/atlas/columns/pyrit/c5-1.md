## Answer

Not covered. No review of production agent output. CoPyRIT review of test conversations and Foundry advice to review results fall short.

Research detail: CoPyRIT, the PyRIT web GUI, lets human operators run interactive attacks, review responses, branch conversations and filter attack history by outcome, operator and labels. Foundry advises reviewing red teaming results before acting because ASR scoring is model-based and non-deterministic. This is human review of red team output, not review of production agent output.
