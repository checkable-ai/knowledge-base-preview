## Answer

Partly covered. Validation results beyond confidence scores are not published, and anomaly detection is for customer threats, not for the agents themselves.

Source statement: "allowing portal users to view reasoning for each agent, verdicts, and confidence scores." (https://docs.arcticwolf.com/en/arctic-wolf-unified-portal/unified-portal/organization-profile/admin-settings)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The vendor describes validation of every agent decision by a Swarm Judge, reinforcement learning with human review and a measure called Mean Time to Trusted Action. Customers can switch on a view of each agent's reasoning, verdicts and confidence scores. Its ML models support anomaly detection for threats.
