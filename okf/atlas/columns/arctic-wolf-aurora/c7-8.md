## Answer

Not covered. No governance, cleaning or securing of the data stores that agents draw on. The open data pipeline, tenant isolation and masking apply to Arctic Wolf's own telemetry handling, not customer agent data stores.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The open data pipeline parses, normalises and enriches customer telemetry, tenant data is isolated and data masking policies exist. Data storage regions and the storage providers are listed in the data location matrix and subprocessor list.
