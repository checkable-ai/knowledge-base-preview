## Answer

Partly covered. Customers cannot create, change permissions of or retire agents. Agent lifecycle is vendor-controlled and no change or retirement process is documented.

Source statement: "New agents are battle-tested in the Aurora Agentic SOC before deployment" (https://arcticwolf.com/aurora-platform/)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Arctic Wolf says new agents are tested in the Agentic SOC before deployment. Customers can switch the Aurora Security Assistant off for the whole organisation and can turn on Agentic SOC transparency, but cannot create, change or retire agents.
