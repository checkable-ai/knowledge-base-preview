## Answer

Covered. Response actions in connected systems depend on which Active Response integration the customer connects and enables.

Source statement: "Each AI agent operates within clearly defined boundaries and least-privilege controls." (https://arcticwolf.com/aurora-platform/)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Customers choose which Active Response integrations to connect and enable, and the documented response actions cover hosts, accounts, email, firewalls and URLs. Arctic Wolf says its internal generative AI tools have bounded autonomy and can only support response actions in their designated area of expertise.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Active Response integrations and response actions, bounded agent autonomy

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Per-action scopes for the AI agents themselves are not shown to customers. The scope is set by which integrations the customer enables.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable only the response actions you accept and review them after each new integration.
