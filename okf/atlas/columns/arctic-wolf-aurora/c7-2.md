## Answer

Not covered. No documented monitoring of customers' AI agents: no agent telemetry linked to sessions and people, no redaction of sensitive agent data, and no agent-specific detections. MDR 24x7 monitoring covers networks, endpoints and cloud generally.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The core service is 24x7 monitoring that collects telemetry from networks, endpoints and cloud, enriches it, investigates, alerts and responds. Escalation rules say who is contacted for which incident type and priority, and Data Explorer lets customers search analyzed logs.
