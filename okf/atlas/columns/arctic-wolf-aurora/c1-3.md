## Answer

Partly covered. The approver is an Arctic Wolf analyst rather than a customer-authorised person. No binding of approval to the exact proposed action, and no handling of refusals or repeat attempts, is documented.

Source statement: "Humans retain authority over irreversible, high-impact, or low-confidence actions." (https://docs.arcticwolf.com/en/additional-information-about-products-and-services/supplemental-agreement-documentation-and-other-information/supplemental-agreement-documentation/artificial-intelligence)

Research detail: Arctic Wolf says humans retain authority over irreversible, high-impact or low-confidence actions, and agents escalate to human analysts when unsure. About one third of complex cases get final human verification. Customers can set escalation rules for who is contacted.
