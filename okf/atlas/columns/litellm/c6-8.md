## Answer

Not covered. No approval step or rollback for models, tools, permissions or knowledge. Prompt versioning (beta) and Enterprise audit logs fall short.

Research detail: Prompt Management (beta) versions prompts on each save and lets calls pin a prompt_version. Audit logs record changes to keys, teams, users and models with before and after values. Tool pinning detects tool changes.
