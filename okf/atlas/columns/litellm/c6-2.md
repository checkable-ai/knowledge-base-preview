## Answer

Not covered. The submission and approval workflow covers MCP servers only. No submission or approval flow is documented for models or agents; admins add them directly.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Team members submit MCP servers with a team-scoped key and admins approve or reject them with review notes through the UI or API. Models and agents are added directly by admins without a submission step.

## Gaps

## Options
