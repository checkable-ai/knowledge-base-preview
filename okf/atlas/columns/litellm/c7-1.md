## Answer

Not covered. LiteLLM's Enterprise audit logs and spend logs record who changed what and when, and an S3 audit callback can export them. The docs make no write-once, immutability, Object Lock or integrity-validation claim for the stored record. Admins can also disable audit logs. Spend logs do not capture agent tool calls or data access as a protected record.

Research detail: Audit logs (Enterprise) record create, update, delete and regenerate actions on keys, teams, users and models with actor, hashed key, before and after values, and can be exported to S3. Spend logs record each request with key, user and team, and can store prompts and responses. The docs make no tamper-resistance claim.

## Products

Audit Logs (Enterprise), Spend Logs

## Gaps

No immutability or integrity checks. Before v1.84.0 the LiteLLM-Changed-By header could rewrite attribution. Admins can turn audit logs off. Reasoning is not captured.

## Options

Export audit logs to write-once S3 storage and confirm allow_litellm_changed_by_header is off.
