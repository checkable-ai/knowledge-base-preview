## Answer

Not covered. Spend tracking and custom A2A agent pricing cover usage cost only. No infrastructure cost, staff time or value delivered measures.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Spend is tracked automatically per key, user, team, tag, customer, model and agent, with tokens and cost in spend logs, Prometheus and the Usage UI. Spend reports by team, customer or key (Enterprise) support chargeback. Soft budget alerts and weekly or monthly spend reports go to Slack or Teams.
