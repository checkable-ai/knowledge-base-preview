## Answer

Not covered. No policy authoring ownership, versioning, publication or review workflow for AI policies. Guardrail Policies are technical enforcement objects, not AI policy lifecycle management.

Research detail: Guardrail Policies (beta) group guardrails, inherit from parent policies and attach globally or to teams, keys, models or tags, managed in config.yaml or the dashboard. Team and key attachments need an Enterprise licence.
