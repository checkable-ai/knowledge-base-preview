## Answer

Covered. Region outage alerting is Enterprise.

Source statement: "The state of the deployment: 0 = healthy, 1 = partial outage, 2 = complete outage." (https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/proxy/prometheus)

Research detail: Prometheus metrics cover end-to-end and provider latency, time to first token, in-flight requests, failures, deployment health state, cooldowns and fallbacks, with maintained Grafana dashboards. Alerting reports hanging, slow and failed calls, model outages and database failures. Region outage alerting is an Enterprise feature.
