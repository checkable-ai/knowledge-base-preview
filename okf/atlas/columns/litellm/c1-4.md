## Answer

Covered. Fine-grained RBAC needs Enterprise licence; config.yaml-defined agents need a restart to change and cannot be edited in the UI.

Source statement: "You can add A2A-compatible agents through the LiteLLM Admin UI." (https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/a2a)

Research detail: Keys, teams, users, agents, MCP servers and models are created, updated, blocked and deleted through the Admin UI, API or config.yaml. key_generation_settings, upperbound parameters and custom key policy hooks restrict who can create keys and with what limits. Organisation and team admin roles and team member permissions are Premium features under an Enterprise licence.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Virtual keys, Agent Gateway, MCP registry, RBAC (Enterprise)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No approval step for agent creation. Fine-grained RBAC needs an Enterprise licence. Config-defined agents need a restart to change.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Manage agents and keys through config.yaml or Terraform under change control, and enable audit logs to record changes.
