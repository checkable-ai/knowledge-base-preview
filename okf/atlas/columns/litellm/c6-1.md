## Answer

Covered. Covers assets registered in or seen by the gateway only. No software component records.

Source statement: "Tool Policies is a registry of every tool the proxy has seen in traffic." (https://docs.litellm.ai/docs/proxy/tool_policies)

Research detail: The gateway keeps registries of models, A2A agents, MCP servers and observed tools, with model_info metadata and agent card fields. AI Hub publishes selected models, agents and MCP servers to a discovery page and API.
