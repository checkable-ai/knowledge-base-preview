## Answer

Not covered. Spend log retention deletion is the only deletion rule. Nothing is deleted by default, and there are no rules for memory or residency beyond region routing. Provider retention is outside LiteLLM.

Research detail: Spend log retention is set by maximum_spend_logs_retention_period with a cleanup job, without an Enterprise licence. Message logging can be turned off or redacted, and Presidio can mask logged data only. region_name filtering limits routing to a region.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Spend Logs Deletion, Logging redaction, Router region filtering

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

With no retention setting nothing is deleted. Retention at providers and logging destinations is outside LiteLLM.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retention, turn off message logging where not needed, and align callback destination retention.
