## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The manifest page notes that some of its information relates to a prereleased product that may be substantially modified before commercial release. The tool-calling runtime is hosted and managed by Microsoft as part of the Security Copilot service.

Source statement: "The tools perform specific tasks and are called by the agent to fulfill its objectives." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/security/developer/agent-manifest)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: A Microsoft managed orchestrator grounds prompts, calls plugins and post-processes responses, and custom agents are defined by a YAML manifest listing tools, triggers and boundaries. Owners control plugin availability but not the orchestrator itself.
