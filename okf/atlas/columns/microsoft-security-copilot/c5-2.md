## Answer

Partly covered. No customer-facing continuous validation or anomaly detection of agent behaviour. Evaluation and red teaming are done by Microsoft, and validation of outputs is left to analysts.

Source statement: "Owners and Contributors can provide feedback to the agent to help it improve." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/security/agents-manage)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Triage agents learn from analyst feedback that is approved before it is saved to memory, and Microsoft reports evaluation, red teaming and operational monitoring of the service. There is no customer facing anomaly detection of agent behaviour.
