## Answer

Not covered. The product governs only its own stores (Workspace geo, AES-256 encryption at rest, retention schedules). It does not govern, clean or secure the external data stores agents read from. RBAC on-behalf-of access and the Microsoft 365 data access toggle fall short of that.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Customer data is stored in the Workspace geo chosen at creation and cannot be moved, encrypted at rest with AES-256, and deleted on defined schedules. This governs the product's own stores and not customer data lakes.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Workspace geo, encryption at rest, retention and deletion schedules

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Outside the category for customer feature stores. Data sharing opt in can store prompts outside the geo.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Choose the workspace geo carefully at creation and leave data sharing off.
