## Answer

Partly covered. Controls cover which plugins are available and who may add them, not per-call policy such as input formats, rate limits or blocking dangerous actions. No internet access control is documented.

Source statement: "As an administrator, you have full control over which plugins are available across your organization." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/security/manage-plugins)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Owners decide whether contributors may add or publish custom plugins and can limit preinstalled plugins to owners. Agent required plugins switch on only for that agent. Plugin data stays subject to each service's RBAC. No schema, rate or dangerous-action filtering is documented.
