## Answer

Escalated for a person to settle. The agent checks did not agree on this finding.

Research detail: Capacity is set per workspace through provisioned and overage SCUs with an optional cap on overage, and Microsoft applies optional classifiers for jailbreak and indirect prompt injection. There is no per-user or per-agent routing or quota.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

SCU provisioned and overage capacity, content classifiers

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps
