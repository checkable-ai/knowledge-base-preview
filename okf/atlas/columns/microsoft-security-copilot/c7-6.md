## Answer

Not covered. No scanning of third-party plugins or agents for look-alike names, poisoned descriptions or compromised packages. Global Administrator approval for partner agents and owner controls on custom plugins are access controls, not scanning.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Partner agents that need Microsoft product data need Global Administrator approval and owners control custom plugins. Microsoft says there is no plugin marketplace and partners publish through the commercial marketplace. No scanning for squatting or tool poisoning is documented.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Global Administrator approval, plugin settings, commercial marketplace publishing

## Gaps

No automated scanning of third-party plugins is documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Vet plugins at user scope first and approve partner agents through change control.
