## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Workspace data storage location is chosen at creation and cannot be changed afterwards. Prompts can be evaluated in Australia (ANZ), Europe (EU), United Kingdom (UK) or United States (US), or anywhere in the world if the customer opts in. If data sharing is opted in, prompts and responses can be stored outside the chosen Workspace geo, and previously shared data is kept up to 180 days after opt-out. Purview stores audit data in the region where Microsoft 365 data is stored. Defaults differ for Microsoft 365 E5 and E7 customers. GCC customers on the Commercial SKU have data stored in the US geo. Not supported in Government and Microsoft Sovereign Public/Private Cloud environments.

Source statement: "Data is stored in the location you select during Workspace creation." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/security/privacy-data-security)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Data is stored in the Workspace geo chosen at creation and prompts can be evaluated in Australia, EU, UK or US. Session data is deleted after 180 days of inactivity and within 180 days of deleting capacity, deletion requests are met within 30 days, and shared data is held 90 days. Audit logs default to 180 days.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Workspace geo, prompt evaluation location, retention and deletion, data sharing settings

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Data sharing opt in can store prompts outside the chosen geo, and anywhere evaluation is optional. Retained data for opted in sharing is up to 180 days after opt-out.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Choose the Australia geo and evaluation location and keep sharing off.
