## Answer

Not covered. The Copilot owner role is accountable for platform settings, and agents run under an assigned identity, but there is no accountable owner field per agent, model or tool.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The Copilot owner role is accountable for platform settings and the service keeps at least two owners at all times. Sessions and usage are attributed to the initiating user. No owner is assigned per agent or decision.
