## Answer

Partly covered. No configurable limits on an agent's files, processes or network access, and no single control that cuts all access at once.

Source statement: "You can disable the agent at any time, giving you complete control over its operation." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/security/manage-plugins)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Owners can pause an agent, switch off plugins for all users immediately and remove roles or identities. When SCU capacity is used up, requests stop. No sandbox, filesystem or egress policy is configurable.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent pause, plugin restriction, role removal, capacity limits

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No single documented revoke-everything control. Capacity changes take up to 30 minutes to apply.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Define a runbook to pause the agent, restrict plugins and remove identity permissions.
