## Answer

Covered. Requires Kiro Enterprise model access management; applies organisation-wide.

Source statement: "New models are not available until an administrator selects them and saves the list." (https://kiro.dev/docs/enterprise/governance/model/)

Research detail: The models page lists each named model version, context window, credit multiplier and inference geography, and states that some models use global cross-region routing. Admins can restrict models to an approved list with a default, and new models stay unavailable until approved. Data protection documents per-model retention for abuse detection.

## Products

Models, Model governance, Data protection

## Gaps

The Auto router picks the model per task. Model governance is organisation-wide only and does not apply to some Kiro Web settings.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Remove Auto and globally routed models from the approved list where residency matters.
