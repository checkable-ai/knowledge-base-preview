## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Needs administrator of the AWS account that owns the Kiro profile, with the EnterpriseManagement IAM actions allowed. Applies to the Kiro Enterprise console. Kiro Enterprise Management is in preview.

Source statement: "Every Publish makes a numbered revision" (https://kiro.dev/docs/enterprise/enterprise-management/getting-started/)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins define permission policies in managed-settings.json and publish them to devices through MDM. Team steering files carrying standards can also be pushed by MDM or Group Policy.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Permission policies, team steering

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No policy ownership, versioning or review features in the product.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Keep policy and steering files in a version-controlled repository with review.
