## Answer

Covered. Rules are vendor-set and differ by surface; no admin-configurable organisation retention period is documented. Kiro Web sessions expire 90 days after last activity and can be deleted earlier with Delete Session. Cloud sessions run in us-east-1 only, which matters for Australian residency. Enterprise content is stored in the profile's region; Free Tier and individual content is stored in us-east-1; model inference may occur in other regions. Enterprise content is not used for service improvement; Free Tier and individual users are opted in by default and can opt out. Free Tier inputs may be kept up to 60 days for abuse detection, and that opt-out does not stop it. Model-specific abuse-detection retention of up to 30 days applies to Claude Fable 5.1 traffic and classifier-flagged OpenAI GPT traffic, and may apply to enterprise users. Crew history is deleted from disk at 365 days and episodic memory is capped at 10,000 entries. IDE and CLI memories are stored locally and can be deleted by the user, with no documented expiry. Account data is permanently deleted 30 days after account deletion if the account is not restored; the page does not mention enterprise accounts.

Source statement: "Sessions expire 90 days after their last activity." (https://kiro.dev/docs/web/using-the-agent/)

Research detail: Data protection documents storage Regions, per-model abuse retention, no service-improvement use for enterprise users, and customer managed KMS keys. Users can delete CLI sessions and Kiro Web memories, and prompt log retention follows the customer's S3 bucket.
