## Answer

Not covered. No DLP and no per-user or per-agent retrieval filtering. Path deny rules fall short.

Research detail: File read and write rules can deny paths by pattern, and some paths always prompt. Kiro Crew blocks reads of sensitive paths such as .aws, .ssh and .env and redacts credential patterns from tool output before it reaches chat.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Permission rules on fs_read and fs_write, Crew sensitive-path blocks and output redaction

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No DLP for personal or business data, no retrieval controls per user or agent, and redaction is documented for Crew only.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Exclude sensitive repositories and paths with admin deny rules and use external DLP on egress.
