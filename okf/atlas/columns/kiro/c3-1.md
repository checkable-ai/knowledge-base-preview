## Answer

Not covered. No gateway for model calls. The approved model list and default model give no per-user or per-agent routing, rate limits or prompt injection screening.

Research detail: Admins restrict models to an approved list with a default model, and overage beyond plan credits is off by default with a profile-wide cap set through Service Quotas. The Auto router chooses models per task. All inference goes through Kiro's service rather than a customer gateway.
