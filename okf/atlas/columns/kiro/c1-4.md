## Answer

Not covered. No controlled process to create, approve, change permissions of or retire agents. Subscription management covers human users, and agent profiles are user-editable files.

Research detail: Admins assign, change and deactivate subscriptions per user or group, and removal from IAM Identity Center or a subscribed group cancels the subscription at month end. Admin settings and managed-settings.json control what clients may use. Agent profiles themselves are files users create and change.

## Gaps

No controlled creation, approval or retirement of agent profiles, hooks or MCP configurations beyond the MCP registry.

## Options
