## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Applies to Kiro Crew bots, the open-source agent that runs on the customer's own machine.

Source statement: "Permissions don't change until you confirm." (https://kiro.dev/docs/crew/bots/manage-bots/)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins assign, change and deactivate subscriptions per user or group, and removal from IAM Identity Center or a subscribed group cancels the subscription at month end. Admin settings and managed-settings.json control what clients may use. Agent profiles themselves are files users create and change.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Kiro console Users and Groups, subscription management, managed settings

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No controlled creation, approval or retirement of agent profiles, hooks or MCP configurations beyond the MCP registry.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Distribute approved agent profiles through source control or MDM and block local changes with permission policies on .kiro paths.
