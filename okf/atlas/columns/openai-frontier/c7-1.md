## Answer

Not covered. Tamper protection is not documented. The vendor says agent actions are auditable and that built-in monitoring and detailed logs give traceability, but gives no write-once or integrity-validated storage and no documented export of the record to such storage. Log contents, retention and export are also undocumented.
