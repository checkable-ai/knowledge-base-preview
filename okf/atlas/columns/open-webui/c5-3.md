## Answer

Partly covered. No money cost, staff time, savings or return figures; tokens only.

Source statement: "Cost allocation - Attribute token consumption to business units." (https://docs.openwebui.com/features/administration/analytics)

Research detail: The admin Analytics dashboard reports token usage by model, user and group over time, which supports cost allocation by team. Users also see their own usage.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Analytics dashboard

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Tokens are counted, but money cost, labour cost, savings and yield are not. Analytics depends on stored message history.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Combine token counts with provider billing and business metrics.
