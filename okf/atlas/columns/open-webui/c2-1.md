## Answer

Escalated for a person to settle. The agent checks did not agree on this finding.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Sub-agents (off by default) run delegated tasks in parallel with admin limits on concurrency, background count, iterations and output size. Automations run prompts on a schedule with pause and resume and an execution history. Users can stop a reply.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Sub-agents, Automations

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Retry and recovery semantics are not documented. There is no workflow engine for sequencing agents beyond model-driven delegation.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Limit sub-agent settings during a pilot and add an external workflow engine where retry and recovery are needed.
