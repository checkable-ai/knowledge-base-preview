## Answer

With our team for a decision. The agent checks did not agree on this finding.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Chats are stored per user in the database and are readable by their owner and people the chat is shared with. Temporary Chat avoids saving history. Analytics data follows the instance's chat retention policy.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Chat history, Temporary Chat, sharing permissions

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Session resumption and a built-in retention schedule for chats are not described on the pages read. Retention is mainly an operator decision on the database.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retention at the database and enforce Temporary Chat for sensitive groups.
