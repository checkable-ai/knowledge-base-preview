## Answer

Partly covered. No built-in retention scheduler or residency setting. Memory and outputs are not covered by one lifecycle policy.

Source statement: "Temporary Chat reduces saved chat history." (https://docs.openwebui.com/security/chat-data-privacy-and-encryption)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The operator owns the database and decides retention and residency. Temporary Chat avoids saved history and can be enforced. Permissions can switch off chat deletion, export and sharing.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Temporary Chat, deployment residency, retention in the operator's database

## Gaps

No built-in retention scheduler or residency setting is described. Memory and outputs are not covered by one lifecycle policy.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retention and deletion jobs at the database and enforce Temporary Chat where needed.
