## Answer

Partly covered. Ratings are feedback after the fact, not a gate, and there is no reviewer queue; the person reviewing is the acting user.

Source statement: "During chats, leave a thumbs up if you like a response, or a thumbs down if you don't." (https://docs.openwebui.com/features/administration/evaluation)

Research detail: Users can rate responses with thumbs up or down, and ratings feed an admin leaderboard. Tool approval gives a human decision before a tool runs. There is no reviewer queue for outputs.
