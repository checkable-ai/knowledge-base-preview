## Answer

Covered. Penetration test reports are available only on request via the Dynatrace account executive. IRAP, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 cover the Dynatrace SaaS platform, and the AI Observability app is not named in scope. ISO 42001 certifies Dynatrace's AI management system, not an individual product.

Source statement: "For penetration test reports, security audits, and other sensitive documents, contact your Dynatrace account executive." (https://www.dynatrace.com/company/trust-center/)

Research detail: Dynatrace published an IRAP assessment at PROTECTED for Dynatrace SaaS on Microsoft Azure (post dated 7 April 2025, data in Sydney) and an earlier PROTECTED assessment for its AWS-hosted platform. The Trust Center also lists SOC 2 Type II, SOC 1 Type II, ISO 27001:2022, ISO 27017, FedRAMP Moderate and ISO 42001:2023. The ISO 42001 certificate was issued by Schellman, effective 2 September 2026 to 1 September 2029, for the AI management system supporting the platform.

## Products

IRAP PROTECTED (Azure and AWS hosted SaaS), SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001.

## Gaps

Penetration test reports are available under NDA from the account executive. The IRAP evidence is older than 18 months for Azure and may exceed 24 months for AWS.
