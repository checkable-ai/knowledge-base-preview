## Answer

Not covered. No documented write-once or integrity-validated storage for agent action records. Grail audit logs record Dynatrace settings and configuration changes, not agent tool calls or data access. AI Observability traces and spans in Grail are retained by bucket policy but are not documented as tamper-evident, and no export to WORM storage is documented for this purpose.

Research detail: Dynatrace says it can track every input and output with lineage from prompt to response, stored and queryable. Grail audit logs record read and write operations on Dynatrace resources for one year and cannot be disabled. Tamper resistance of either record is not documented.
