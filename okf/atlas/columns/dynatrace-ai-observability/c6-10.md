## Answer

Not covered. Telemetry retention only. Does not set rules for agent inputs, outputs and memory.

Research detail: Telemetry in Grail, including captured prompts and responses, has per-bucket retention up to 10 years, regional hosting, hard deletion of records, encryption and masking at capture or ingest. dt-evals masks PII before calling the judge and excludes prompt content from results by default. This governs Dynatrace's copy of the data, not the agent's own inputs, outputs or memory.

## Gaps

## Options
