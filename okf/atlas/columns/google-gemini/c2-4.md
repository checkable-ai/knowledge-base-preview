## Answer

Covered. Interactions API. Paid tier keeps interactions for 55 days and free tier for 1 day. store=false prevents resuming.

Source statement: "The system retains interactions for 55 days." (https://aistudio.google.com/docs/interactions-overview)

Research detail: The Interactions API stores each interaction and resumes a session through previous_interaction_id. Paid projects retain interactions for 55 days, adjustable to 7, 14 or 28, free projects for 1 day, and interactions can be deleted by ID or opted out with store=false. Gemini CLI saves sessions per project, supports resume and deletes them after 30 days by default.

## Products

Interactions API, Gemini CLI session management

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Access control is at the project or local user level, not per end user.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use store=false for sensitive workloads and set short retention windows.
