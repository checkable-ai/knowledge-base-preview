## Answer

Not covered. Owners exist only for Gemini Enterprise custom agents (one owner, admin-transferable). No owner records for models or tools, and no ownership review to keep the record current.

Last checked 18 November 2026.

Research detail: Gemini Enterprise agents have owners, and admins can transfer ownership of shared agents.
