## Answer

Not covered. The Agents page manages agent state (update, test, share, enable, disable, delete). There is no proposal or intake stage, and no lifecycle for models or use cases.

Research detail: Models move through experimental, preview, stable and deprecated stages with notice periods. Gemini Enterprise agents move between private, enabled, suspended and disabled states.
