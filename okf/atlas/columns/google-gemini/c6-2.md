## Answer

Covered. Gemini Enterprise agents. An app administrator must turn off the 'Enable agent sharing without admin approval' feature control, because sharing without approval is the default. Custom agent registration is admin-only.

Source statement: "After the app administrator approves the request, the chat agent is shared with the users in the access list." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/workflow-builder/share-chat-agent)

Research detail: Admins can require review before employee-made agents or skills are shared, and approve or deny each share request. Custom agents can only be registered by holders of the Gemini Enterprise Admin role.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini Enterprise share approval, feature controls

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Sharing without approval is the default. Custom agent registration has no separate approval step.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Turn off sharing without admin approval and keep an external approval record for custom agents.
