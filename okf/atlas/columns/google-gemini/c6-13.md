## Answer

Covered. Applies to Gemini Enterprise and Gemini Notebook Enterprise. Antigravity in Gemini Enterprise is excluded. ISO 42001 is documented for Google Cloud and the Gemini app, but is not in the main Gemini Enterprise list. IRAP scope is not named, and no assessment is documented for the Gemini API, CLI or Jules.

Source statement: "have many of the following certifications" (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/compliance-security-controls)

Research detail: Gemini Enterprise lists ISO 27001, 27017, 27018, 27701, SOC 1, 2 and 3, PCI DSS, BSI C5, HIPAA and FedRAMP, and Google lists Gemini Enterprise, the Gemini app and Gemini Notebook Enterprise as ISO/IEC 42001:2023 certified. The Google Cloud IRAP page states Google Cloud and Workspace were assessed against OFFICIAL and PROTECTED ISM controls, and its in-scope list names Gemini for Google Cloud, Gemini for Workspace and Generative AI on Vertex AI, but not Gemini Enterprise, the Gemini API, Gemini CLI or Jules by those names. Reports are requested through Compliance Reports Manager.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini Enterprise certifications, Google Cloud IRAP and ISO/IEC 42001 programs

## Gaps

No IRAP scope statement for Gemini Enterprise or the Gemini API by name. No assessment date on the IRAP page. No third-party assessment found for the Gemini API, Gemini CLI or Jules. Antigravity in Gemini Enterprise is excluded from ISO and SOC scope.

## Options

Request the IRAP report and ISO certificates through Compliance Reports Manager and confirm whether Gemini Enterprise falls under a listed service, such as Discovery Solutions.
