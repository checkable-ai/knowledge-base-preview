<!-- chunk: answer -->
## Answer

Covered. Applies to the Gemini Enterprise assistant, Workflow Builder agents and Google-made agents with Model Armor and Sensitive Data Protection enabled. Custom ADK, A2A and Dialogflow agents are not screened.

Source statement: "Interactions with custom agents from your organization, such as ADK, A2A, and Dialogflow, are not screened." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/enable-model-armor)

Research detail: Gemini Enterprise carries source ACLs into retrieval, applies resource-level IAM to apps and data stores, and restricts which external sources can be added. Model Armor blocks assistant responses that trigger Sensitive Data Protection detectors. VPC Service Controls is supported.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini Enterprise connectors and IAM, Model Armor, VPC Service Controls

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Knowledge files on a shared agent are visible to all agent users regardless of file permissions. DLP blocks rather than redacts, and does not apply to custom agents.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Prefer connectors over uploaded knowledge files for sensitive content and add DLP inside custom agents.
