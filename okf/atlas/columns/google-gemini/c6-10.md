## Answer

Covered. Gemini API Interactions: paid tier retains 55 days, free tier 1 day; store=false opts out of storage; stored interactions can be deleted. Gemini Enterprise data residency is limited to listed regions.

Source statement: "After the retention period expires, your data will be deleted automatically." (https://aistudio.google.com/docs/interactions-overview)

Research detail: The Gemini API terms state that paid data is not used for training and abuse logs are time-limited. API logs and stored interactions have 7 to 55 day retention and delete methods. Gemini Enterprise supports data residency and CMEK in US and EU multi-regions, user memory deletion, and deletes user-requested data within 60 days. Gemini CLI deletes sessions after 30 days by default.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini API terms and retention settings, Gemini Enterprise residency and memory controls, Gemini CLI session retention

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Unpaid services may train on data. Paid API data may be cached in any country. Gemini Enterprise memory retention is not stated.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use paid projects only, and Gemini Enterprise regional apps with CMEK for residency needs.
