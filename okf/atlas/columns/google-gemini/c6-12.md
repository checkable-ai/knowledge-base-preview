## Answer

Not covered. No scheduled reassessment or retirement workflow. The allowedDataSources and mcp.allowed policies are approval lists only.

Research detail: Admins can restrict which third-party data sources may be connected in Gemini Enterprise with an org policy, and restrict which MCP servers Gemini CLI runs.
