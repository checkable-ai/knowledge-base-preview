## Answer

Covered. Preview models may have more restrictive rate limits, and experimental models are typically not suitable for production. Data handling differs between paid and unpaid tiers.

Source statement: "Stable models usually don't change." (https://aistudio.google.com/docs/models)

Research detail: The Gemini API exposes stable, preview, experimental and latest model names, with two weeks' notice before a latest alias or preview model changes. Data handling differs by paid and unpaid tier and is set in the terms. Rate limits and spend limits are published per tier.
