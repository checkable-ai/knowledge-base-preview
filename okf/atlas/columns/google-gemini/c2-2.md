## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Preview and experimental models may have more restrictive rate limits. Data handling differs between paid and free tiers. Services are available only in supported regions.

Source statement: "Gemini models are available in either stable, preview, latest, or experimental versions." (https://aistudio.google.com/docs/models)

Research detail: The Gemini API exposes stable, preview, experimental and latest model names, with two weeks' notice before a latest alias or preview model changes. Data handling differs by paid and unpaid tier and is set in the terms. Rate limits and spend limits are published per tier.
