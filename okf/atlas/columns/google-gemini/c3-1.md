## Answer

Not covered. No gateway enforcing routing, quotas or cost limits per agent or user, and no prompt screening layer for model calls. Limits are per project only.

Research detail: Gemini API rate limits and spend limits apply per project, not per key or user, and an experimental per-project monthly spend cap exists. Configurable safety filters apply per request. Gemini Enterprise applies Model Armor to assistant prompts and responses, including prompt injection screening, with fail-closed as the default.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini API rate limits and spend caps, safety settings, Model Armor in Gemini Enterprise

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No per-user or per-agent routing or quota. Model Armor does not cover custom ADK, A2A or Dialogflow agents.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Separate workloads into projects for quota, or front the API with an external gateway.
