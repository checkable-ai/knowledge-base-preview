## Answer

Partly covered. Content policies do not cover older data stores (Cloud Storage, BigQuery, websites) or the Business edition, so screening of those stores falls to the customer. No documented data-quality or poisoned-content cleaning across all stores agents read.

Source statement: "Content policies don't apply to the older type of data store, such as data from Cloud Storage or BigQuery or websites." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/protect-sensitive-data)

Research detail: Gemini Enterprise data stores support ingestion or federation, ACL and identity sync, resource-level IAM, CMEK and data residency in US and EU multi-regions, and an org policy on allowed sources.
