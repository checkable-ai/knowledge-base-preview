## Answer

Not covered. No register of models, agents and tools with their components. The Gemini Enterprise Agents page lists agents and their state only, and Gemini CLI lists tools and extensions per CLI.

Last checked 18 November 2026.

Research detail: The Gemini Enterprise Agents page and Agent Gallery list Google-made, employee-made and custom agents, and A2A agents are registered with an agent card that includes skills and input and output modes.
