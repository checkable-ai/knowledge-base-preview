## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Vercel Connect is available on all plans and is subject to the Vercel Connect terms. Token requests are billed per request: Hobby includes 500 per month, Pro is billed at $3.00 per 1,000. Code authenticates to Connect with a Vercel OIDC token or a Vercel access token.

Source statement: "Give your agents and services short-lived access to Slack, GitHub, Microsoft, Snowflake, and other OAuth or API-key services, without storing provider secrets in your environment variables." (https://vercel.com/docs/connect)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: OIDC federation gives deployments short-lived tokens signed by Vercel that cloud providers exchange for short-lived credentials, with function tokens living two hours. Secret environment variables are write-only after saving and are redacted from build logs. Vercel Agent can read environment variables without approval and is told not to repeat secrets.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

OIDC federation, Secret environment variables, build log redaction

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The agent reading environment variables without approval means secrets can enter model context. Rotation and revocation of Secret values are manual.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use OIDC federation instead of stored cloud keys and keep long-lived secrets out of projects the agent can read.
