## Answer

Not covered. No documented retention, deletion or residency rules for Vercel Agent inputs, outputs or memory. Compute region selection, the GDPR commitments and 90-day audit log export fall short, and Vercel may transfer data to the United States and elsewhere.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Teams choose compute regions, including Sydney, and Vercel publishes a GDPR commitment and data processing addendum. Audit logs export for 90 days. Retention and deletion of Vercel Agent chat content are pointed to privacy guidance that was not opened.
