## Answer

Not covered. No lifecycle for models, agents or use cases from proposal through testing, production and retirement. Deployment Checks and Instant Rollback govern software releases only.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Deployments move from preview to production through Deployment Checks, and Instant Rollback restores a previous production deployment. This covers software releases and not proposal intake, model testing or retirement of AI use cases.
