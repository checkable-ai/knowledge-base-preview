## Answer

Not covered. No owner field or accountable-owner record for each agent, model or tool. Team roles such as Owner and Access Groups define who manages a team or project, and Activity Log events name the user who started an agent session.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Team roles define who owns and manages a team, including the Owner role with the highest level of control, and Access Groups assign project roles. Agent sessions and approvals name the user. Accountable owners for individual agents or decisions are not recorded as fields.
