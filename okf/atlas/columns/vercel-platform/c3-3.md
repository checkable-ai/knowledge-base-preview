## Answer

Not covered. No content filtering or data loss prevention for what Vercel Agent retrieves is documented. Build log redaction, Deployment Protection and team scoping fall short of per-agent or per-user retrieval filtering.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Secret values are redacted from build logs, Vercel Agent is told not to repeat secrets and is limited to the selected team, and Deployment Protection controls who can view preview and production URLs. There is no content DLP for agent prompts or retrieval.

## Products

Build log redaction, Deployment Protection, Agent scope

## Gaps

Redaction covers secrets of 32 or more characters in build logs. No retrieval policy or content inspection for Agent chat is documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Keep secrets in environment variables and add upstream DLP for anything pasted into chat.
