## Answer

Partly covered. No continuous validation or anomaly detection of the agent's own behaviour is documented. Alerts detect anomalies in application usage and errors, and Alerts need Observability Plus.

Source statement: "Code Review then generates patches, runs them in secure sandboxes, and executes your real builds, tests, and linters." (https://vercel.com/docs/agent/pr-review)

Research detail: Alerts raise usage and error anomalies against a baseline, and Vercel Agent investigates them. Custom monitors need Observability Plus. These detect application behaviour anomalies and do not validate agent task behaviour.
