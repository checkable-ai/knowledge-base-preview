## Answer

Not covered. No write-once or integrity-validated storage claim for audit records, and no record of agent tool calls, data access or transcripts. The Activity Log records only Vercel Agent session start, elevated-permission request and plan approval events. Audit Log Drains can export events to S3, Splunk, Datadog or Panther, but the docs do not say that storage is tamper-evident.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Audit logs (Enterprise) record actor, action, location, user agent, request ID and the previous and next state, export to CSV for 90 days and drain to external storage. The Activity Log records Vercel Agent session and approval events. Tamper proofing and capture of agent input, output and reasoning are not described.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Audit Logs, Activity Log

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Retention in Vercel is 90 days for export. No immutability claim and no agent transcript in the log.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Drain audit logs to write-once storage and keep chat transcripts under your own retention rules.
