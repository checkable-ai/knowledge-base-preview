## Answer

Covered. A live network policy update applies to an eligible attached session first and is then saved, and the two steps are not atomic.

Source statement: "Sandboxes can use three distinct modes, which can be updated at runtime, without restarting the process." (https://vercel.com/docs/sandbox/concepts/firewall)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Permissions expire when a plan completes or is cancelled, owners can disable Agent features, budgets can pause production deployments, Instant Rollback restores a previous deployment, and WAF rules and Attack Mode can deny traffic. Sandbox egress and process policy for the agent are not documented.
