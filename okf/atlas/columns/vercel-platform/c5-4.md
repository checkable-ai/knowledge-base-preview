## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Alerts are available on Enterprise and Pro plans with Observability Plus. Custom monitors are available only with Observability Plus.

Source statement: "Alerts let you know when something's wrong with your Vercel projects, like a spike in failed function invocations or unusual usage patterns." (https://vercel.com/docs/alerts)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Alerts detect error and usage anomalies on routes using baselines, and the firewall dashboard and Query give traffic views. Drains forward logs and traces to external tools. Custom monitors and full alerting need Observability Plus.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Alerts, Observability, Drains

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Load testing is not offered. Alerts need Observability Plus on Pro and Enterprise.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable Observability Plus on production projects and drain data to your monitoring tool.
