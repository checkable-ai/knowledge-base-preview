## Answer

Partly covered. No documented redaction of agent content in the telemetry, no built-in detections on agent events, and no named owner for the response.

Source statement: "Audit Log Drains forward team activity events to external endpoints for storage, compliance review, and analysis." (https://vercel.com/docs/drains/reference/audit-logs)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Audit Log Drains send events to S3, Splunk, Datadog and Panther, and log, trace and other drains feed external tools. Records name the actor and request. Alerts add detections. Response ownership and redaction beyond build log secrets are not described.
