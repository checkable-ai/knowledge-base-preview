## Answer

Not covered. No per-agent or per-user quotas or cost limits. Model policies give routing and failover only; the watsonx.ai model gateway is beta with tenant, provider or model rate limits.

Research detail: The watsonx.ai model gateway proxies multiple LLM providers with access policies (action, resource, subject) and request and token rate limits per tenant, provider or model. Orchestrate model policies load-balance and fail over between virtual models, and controls can apply content and jailbreak guardrails to named models.
