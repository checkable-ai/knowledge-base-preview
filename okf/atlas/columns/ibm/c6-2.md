## Answer

Covered. Requires the Model Risk Governance solution in watsonx.governance with workflows configured; approval is not enforced at Orchestrate deployment.

Source statement: "The AI Agent Onboarding workflow takes an agent through an approval process." (https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/watsonx/w-and-w/2.4.x?topic=solution-model-risk-governance-workflows)

Research detail: The Governance console includes an AI Agent onboarding workflow and an AI Agent Onboarding Risk Identification questionnaire, plus model onboarding and use case questionnaires. Use Case Review records capture reviewer approvals, and workflows are configurable.

## Products

watsonx.governance Governance console (MRG workflows, questionnaires)

## Gaps

The governed agentic catalog itself has no approval step. Approval is not enforced in Orchestrate deployment.

## Options
