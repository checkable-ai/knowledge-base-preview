## Answer

Covered. Human review must be configured as a user activity step in an agentic workflow; no production sampling queue.

Source statement: "After the recipient completes the task, the workflow resumes." (https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/watsonx/watson-orchestrate/base?topic=workflows-adding-user-activities)

Research detail: Agentic workflows can include user activity steps where a person reviews or supplies data before the flow continues. watsonx.governance supports validator and risk officer review of models and prompts before deployment.
