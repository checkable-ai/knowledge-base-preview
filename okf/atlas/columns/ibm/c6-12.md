## Answer

Not covered. Catalog inventories external agents and tools, but no approval, scheduled reassessment or retirement process for suppliers.

Research detail: The default inventory stores third-party models, and the governed agentic catalog registers agents and tools deployed outside watsonx.ai by endpoint. Orchestrate's AI Gateway brings external agents into one inventory and lets teams retire duplicates.
