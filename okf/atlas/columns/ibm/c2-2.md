## Answer

Covered. watsonx.ai foundation models; third-party virtual models follow provider terms.

Source statement: "The foundation models are hosted in IBM Cloud or AWS; your prompts are not sent to third-party platforms." (https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/watsonx/saas?topic=watsonx-security-foundation-models)

Research detail: watsonx.ai hosts foundation models in IBM Cloud or AWS and states that prompts and outputs are not stored unless saved, are not logged by IBM, are not used to improve IBM models and are encrypted in transit. Orchestrate agents reference explicit provider and model IDs, including external providers registered as virtual models.
