How [Lovable](profile.md) addresses the [Inference](../../controls/c2-2.md) control. Researcher verdict: covered. Verification state: verified. Agents may retrieve this page until 2027-02-15 on the confirmation of 2026-11-17: two model families agreed and code found the quote on the vendor's page.

## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Zero-data-retention models by default, the opt-in to 30-day retention models, and EU inference are Enterprise only. With EU inference on, requests fail rather than run in another region, and fewer models and features are available.

Source statement: "Enterprise workspaces use only zero-data-retention models by default." (https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/lovable-for-enterprise.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Enterprise workspaces use zero-data-retention models by default, and admins can opt in to models retained by the provider for 30 days. EU inference keeps AI requests on EU endpoints and fails rather than falling back to another region. Lovable picks and updates the build models itself.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

EU inference, extended-retention models setting

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No model name or version pinning for the build agent. Lovable updates models for everyone automatically. EU inference excludes some models and features.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable EU inference where residency matters and record the extended-retention decision.
