## Answer

Not covered. Lovable has no built-in MCP registry or curated catalogue, and documents no scanning of registry tools, plugins or skills for look-alike names, poisoned descriptions or compromised packages. The dependency audit checks npm dependencies for known vulnerabilities only.

Last checked 17 November 2026.
