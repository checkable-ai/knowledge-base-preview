## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Zero-data-retention models by default, the 30-day retention opt-in and EU inference are Enterprise only. Business and Enterprise workspace data is excluded from AI model training by default. Abandoned project auto-delete is Enterprise only and off by default. The default data hosting region is available on Business and Enterprise, with Americas, Europe and Asia Pacific regions. Country-specific regions on Enterprise are subject to capacity.

Source statement: "Enterprise workspaces use only zero-data-retention models by default." (https://docs.lovable.dev/introduction/lovable-for-enterprise.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Business and Enterprise data is excluded from model training by default. Enterprise adds zero-data-retention models by default, EU inference, a data hosting region, 13 week audit logs, abandoned project auto-delete and code download limits. Project and workspace deletion are documented.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Training exclusion, EU inference, data hosting region, extended-retention models, abandoned project cleanup

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No configurable retention for chat or memory is documented. Free and Pro data may be used for training unless the user opts out.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set region and EU inference and confirm retention terms in the DPA.
