## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The Data Processing Agreement is included on Business and Enterprise plans. A custom DPA is available to Enterprise customers only.

Source statement: "As of August 13, 2025, Lovable is officially SOC 2 Type 2 compliant." (https://lovable.dev/blog/lovable-security)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Lovable lists SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001:2022, AIUC-1 and GDPR with a Data Processing Agreement included on Business and Enterprise, and Cloud data can be hosted in Asia Pacific. It says it is not HIPAA compliant.
