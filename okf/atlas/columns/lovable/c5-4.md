## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The Database settings cards apply to the Lovable Cloud database. They are shown on all plans, while instance resizing depends on the plan. Larger instances need a paid plan.

Source statement: "On all plans, Database settings opens with three cards: CPU, Memory, and Disk." (https://docs.lovable.dev/features/database-performance.md)
