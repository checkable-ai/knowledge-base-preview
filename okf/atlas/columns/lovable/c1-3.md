## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Chat connectors are enabled by default on all plans. Workspace policy approval of publishing and the built-in backend is on the Enterprise plan, and requests expire as denied after 30 days.

Source statement: "Before any other tool runs, Lovable shows an approval card." (https://docs.lovable.dev/integrations/chat-connectors)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Enterprise workspace policies can require an admin or owner to approve publishing or enabling the built-in backend. Every admin and owner is notified, a denial takes a reason, requests expire as denied after 30 days, and approvals and denials are logged. This covers publish and backend actions, not individual agent tool calls.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Workspace policies, Action center

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Approvers must be admins or owners and cannot be chosen per request. An approval covers the requester's later updates until something the policy checks changes. Per-action approval of agent steps is not documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Turn on Require approval for external publishing and connector apps, and review requests in the Action center.
