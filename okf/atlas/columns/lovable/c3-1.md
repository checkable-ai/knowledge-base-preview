## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Creating usage limits needs a paid plan. Group and access-token limits are on Business and Enterprise plans. EU inference is an Enterprise setting.

Source statement: "Access-token limits apply per token." (https://docs.lovable.dev/features/usage-limits-and-alerts.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Credit limits can be set for the workspace, projects, members, groups and access tokens, with notify or block actions. EU inference routes model requests by region. Credits report AI gateway usage separately.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Usage limits and alerts, EU inference

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No per-user model routing rules and no prompt injection screening are documented. Quotas are in credits, not tokens or request rates.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set member and group limits and use an outside gateway for injection screening.
