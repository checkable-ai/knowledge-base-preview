## Answer

Not covered. No documented way to write, own, version, publish or review AI policies. Workspace policies, Privacy and security settings and workspace instructions enforce technical rules but have no policy ownership, versioning or review cycle.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Enterprise workspace policies and Privacy and security settings define and publish workspace rules that apply to all projects, and changes are recorded in audit logs. Owners and admins maintain workspace instructions.
