## Answer

Partly covered. No registration workflow for models or agents. Policies are not customisable per approver and cover publishing of apps and connector use, not registration of new tools.

Source statement: "Every workspace admin and owner receives each request in Lovable and by email." (https://docs.lovable.dev/features/workspace-policies.md)

Research detail: Enterprise policies can require admin or owner approval before an app that uses connectors goes live, before public publishing and before a project enables the built-in backend. MCP registries and per-connector availability give a vetting step for tools.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Workspace policies, connector availability, MCP registries

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Policies cannot be customised or sent to chosen approvers. Models and agents have no registration workflow.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable approval for connector apps and external publishing.
