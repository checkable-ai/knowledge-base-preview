## Answer

Covered. Enterprise retention caps (Indefinite or 90 days); US-only residency by enrolment; BYOK and some models fall outside zero data retention.

Source statement: "The available windows are Indefinite and 90 days." (https://cursor.com/docs/cloud-agent/security-network)

Research detail: Privacy Mode can be enforced org-wide, most models run under zero data retention, US-only residency covers inference, processing and storage, and Cloud Agent repository copies are deleted after the run. CMEK is available for Cloud Agent data.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Privacy Mode, Zero data retention, Data residency, CMEK

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

General retention periods and deletion of chats are not documented. Only US residency is available. BYOK, some Anthropic models and Cyber-enabled models fall outside ZDR.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enforce Privacy Mode, restrict BYOK and data-retaining models, and confirm retention terms contractually.
