## Answer

Not covered. Cloud Agent tokens name an owner, but there is no owner field on service accounts, models or tools and no accountability record kept current.

Research detail: OIDC tokens name the owning user or service account, and automations record a Creator or service account owner. Service accounts decouple automations from individual staff.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Cloud Agent OIDC owner claims, Automations Run as, Service accounts

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Service accounts have no documented owner field. No accountability assignment for tools or decisions.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Record owners for service accounts and automations in an external register.
