## Answer

Covered. ZDR applies to most models, not BYOK or some models. US-only residency is opt-in for Enterprise. Auto mode lets Cursor choose the model.

Source statement: "Most models run under Cursor's ZDR agreements, so providers don't store inputs or outputs or train on your data" (https://cursor.com/docs/enterprise/privacy-and-data-governance)

Research detail: Models are listed with explicit versions across Cursor, Anthropic, Google, OpenAI and others, with context limits and pricing. Most run under zero data retention agreements, and US-only residency covers inference, processing and storage for eligible models. Admins enable or disable providers and models.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Model access, Zero data retention, Data residency, Cursor Router

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Models are hosted by the provider, a partner or Cursor without a per-model endpoint list. Auto mode routes to a model chosen by Cursor Router. BYOK and some models fall outside ZDR.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pin allowed models through the Admin API model access endpoints and disable BYOK.
