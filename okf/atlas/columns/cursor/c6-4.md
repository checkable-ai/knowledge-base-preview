## Answer

Not covered. Team Rules can be published and enforced, but no policy ownership, versioning or review cycle; Cursor describes rules as suggestions.

Last checked 8 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins publish Team Rules from the dashboard and can enforce them so members cannot disable them. Run mode, sandbox and hook policies are set centrally and override local settings.
