## Answer

Not covered. Admins can allowlist MCP servers and models, but there is no submission, registration and approval workflow for agents or automations.

Research detail: Admins approve MCP servers and models, and models that retain data need admin approval. Cursor approves marketplace plugins and updates before listing, and admins can limit who publishes personal skills.
