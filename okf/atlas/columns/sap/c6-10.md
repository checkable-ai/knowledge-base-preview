## Answer

Not covered. Retention changes need a support ticket; no self-service residency or memory retention controls.

Research detail: Joule interaction data is retained one year by default, changed only by support ticket, and personal data is anonymised before use for improvement. Users can delete conversations and memories, and data centres are listed per region. SAP AI Core acts as data processor and leaves deletion of object store data to the customer.
