## Answer

Not covered. Data masking handles personal data in prompts only, with no guarantee of full detection. No per-user or per-agent retrieval filtering is documented.

Last checked 12 November 2026. This platform is checked on demand, so this answer has not been rechecked since.

Research detail: The data masking module anonymises or pseudonymises personal data in prompts across listed entity types, with custom regular expression entities. SAP warns that detection is automated and cannot guarantee all personal data is masked. Grounding pipelines provide retrieval but per-user retrieval enforcement was not found in the pages read.
