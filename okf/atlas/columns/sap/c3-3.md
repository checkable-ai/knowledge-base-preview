## Answer

Not covered. Data masking handles personal data in prompts only, with no guarantee of full detection. No per-user or per-agent retrieval filtering is documented.

Research detail: The data masking module anonymises or pseudonymises personal data in prompts across listed entity types, with custom regular expression entities.
