## Answer

Covered. Generative AI hub in SAP AI Core; pinned or automatic upgrades; content filtering and masking are optional modules.

Source statement: "Model versions have deprecation dates." (https://github.com/SAP-docs/sap-artificial-intelligence/blob/main/docs/sap-ai-core/generativeAI/model-lifecycle-313fe25.md)

Research detail: The generative AI hub serves named models and versions with deprecation dates and a choice of automatic or pinned upgrades. Per-model request limits apply per tenant and resource group, and Joule agents declare base, advanced and backup models. SAP AI Core acts as data processor, and content filtering and data masking are optional modules.
