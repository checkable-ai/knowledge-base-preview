## Answer

Not covered. No tamper-evidence; agent reasoning and tool calls not logged for customers.

Research detail: SAP AI Core writes audit log events for secrets, resource groups, deployments, executions and repositories, plus access denials. Joule stores conversation logs with user ID, input, response and feedback, retained one year by default and retrievable by ticket or SAP Data Privacy Integration. Feature enablement shows who enabled it and when.

## Products

## Gaps
