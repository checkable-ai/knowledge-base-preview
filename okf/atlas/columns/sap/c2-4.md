## Answer

Not covered. Session lifetime exists but access control and resumption of session state are not detailed.

Research detail: A Joule conversation session ends after 24 hours or 8 hours of inactivity, or when the user deletes it. Remote agents can keep multi-turn state through context and task IDs that Joule stores in runtime variables. Users must not share browser sessions, and single logout through SAP Identity Authentication ends Joule sessions.
