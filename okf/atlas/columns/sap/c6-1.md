Source statement: "standardized fact sheets capturing identity, ownership, capabilities, technology stack, and governance status" (https://architecture.learning.sap.com/docs/ref-arch/jfhsd9)

Research detail: SAP AI Agent Hub keeps a registry of agents, MCP servers and LLMs as fact sheets with identity, ownership, technology stack and governance status. Prompt templates and orchestration configs are stored and versioned in the prompt registry. Joule Admin Center lists deployed assistants and scenarios.
