## Answer

Covered. Multi-agent Apps pattern. Caller service principal needs CAN_USE on the target app, applied manually after deployment.

Source statement: "App-to-app calls require OAuth." (https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/agents/custom-agents/multi-agent-apps)

Research detail: Orchestrator apps call subagent apps over OAuth, and the orchestrator's service principal needs CAN_USE on the target app or CAN_QUERY on a serving endpoint. Supervisor Agent only routes end users to subagents they hold grants on.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Databricks Apps permissions, Model Serving endpoint permissions, Supervisor Agent

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No A2A protocol support is documented. Multi-agent pages do not describe user identity passthrough between agents or trust boundaries across workspaces.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use one service principal per agent and grant CAN_USE only for intended pairs, then review grants from audit logs.
