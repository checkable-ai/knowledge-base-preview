## Answer

Not covered. No configurable retention periods or expiry for agent memory and sessions, and no unified rules across inputs, outputs and memory. Deleting a session leaves memory-store content intact.

Research detail: Designated services keep data in its Geo unless cross-Geo processing is enabled. Trace tables have a 90 day default TTL.
