## Answer

Not covered. No retention period, expiry or TTL is documented for sessions. Access is store-level only: actor_id and metadata group and filter sessions but do not grant or restrict access. Managed sessions (Beta) persist history and resume by session ID, which falls short of the full outcome.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Managed agent sessions store conversation items in a Lakebase-backed session store keyed by actor_id and session_id, with resume, fork, undo and clear operations. Access is granted at the store level.

## Products

Managed agent sessions

## Gaps

actor_id does not restrict access, so any principal with store access sees all sessions. No retention or TTL is documented. Beta.

## Options

Use one store per trust boundary and add scheduled deletion.
