## Answer

Not covered. Managed sessions (Beta) persist and resume history, but actor_id does not restrict access and no retention or TTL is documented.

Research detail: Managed agent sessions store conversation items in a Lakebase-backed session store keyed by actor_id and session_id, with resume, fork, undo and clear operations. Access is granted at the store level.

## Gaps
