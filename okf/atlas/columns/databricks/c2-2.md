## Answer

Covered. Some endpoints are unavailable in certain regions; partner providers apply their own data policies; default limits apply.

Source statement: "Model Serving imposes default limits to ensure reliable performance." (https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/machine-learning/model-serving/)

Research detail: Model Serving exposes custom models, Databricks-hosted foundation models and external models as endpoints, with Unity Catalog model versions and aliases. The docs state data handling: encryption at rest and in transit, no training on paid account inputs, and Foundation Model APIs may keep inputs and outputs up to 30 days in region for abuse detection. Limits pages list payload, QPS and timeout limits.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Model Serving, Foundation Model APIs, Unity Gateway model services

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Partner providers keep data under their own policies. Several AWS regions (Jakarta, Paris, us-west-1) lack Model Serving and some Geos need cross-Geo processing.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Check region and Geo tables before deployment and disable cross-Geo processing where residency matters.
