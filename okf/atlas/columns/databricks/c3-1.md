## Answer

Covered. Budget enforcement is approximate. In-flight requests are not interrupted. Some budget options are Beta.

Source statement: "Block usage thresholds are enforced approximately." (https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/ai-gateway/budgets)

Research detail: Unity Gateway routes model traffic with traffic splitting and fallbacks, enforces QPM and TPM rate limits at service, user, group and individual principal level, and applies monthly budgets with per-user thresholds and block mode. Built-in service policies screen for jailbreak, unsafe content and sensitive data.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Unity Gateway model services, rate limits, budgets, service policies

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Rate limit and budget enforcement is approximate. Budgets skip provisioned throughput and traffic sent to endpoints outside the gateway. Service policies are Beta and skip model provider passthrough requests.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Force all clients through model services and remove direct endpoint grants.
