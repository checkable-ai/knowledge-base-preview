## Answer

Covered, with conditions. ABAC row filter and column mask policies are in Beta. Data classification tagging can lag by up to 24 hours. AI Search does not apply row and column permissions. Pipeline expectations require the Lakeflow pipelines Advanced edition.

Source statement: "Manage who can access what using privileges, attribute-based policies, row and column filters, and workspace bindings." (https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/data-governance/unity-catalog/)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Unity Catalog governs tables, features and AI Search indexes with grants, ABAC row filters and column masks, lineage and LLM based data classification that tags sensitive columns. AI Search supports customer managed keys.
