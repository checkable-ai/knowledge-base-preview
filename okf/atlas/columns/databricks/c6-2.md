## Answer

Not covered. Deployment job approval is a Public Preview, experimental, model-only step. There is no submit, register and approve workflow for agents or tools.

Research detail: Deployment jobs have an approval task that blocks deployment until an approval tag is set, and governed tags limit who can approve. GRANT policies only grant EXECUTE on MCP and model provider services tagged as approved.

## Products

MLflow deployment jobs, GRANT policies with governed tags
