## Answer

Covered. Beta. An admin must enable Issue Detection from Previews. Traces must be in Unity Catalog. Scoring is sampled. Alerting needs a separate SQL alert.

Source statement: "MLflow then runs the scorer online on a sample of new traces to track the issue rate in production." (https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/mlflow3/genai/tracing/observe-with-traces/analyze-traces)

Research detail: Production monitoring runs registered MLflow scorers and LLM judges on a sample of production traces and attaches results as feedback, with monitoring dashboards. mlflow.genai.evaluate compares app versions against datasets.
