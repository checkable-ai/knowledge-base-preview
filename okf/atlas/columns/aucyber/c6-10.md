## Answer

Not covered. Residency is documented (onshore data, Australian AI hosting). No retention, deletion or permitted-use rules for agent inputs, outputs or memory. Deletion is a customer responsibility.

Research detail: Residency is documented: all customer data, metadata, support, administrative and backup data stays onshore, offshore access is blocked unless permitted, and AI hosting is in Australian data centres. Secure deletion of data in the environment is a customer responsibility and the CRISP sets no retention periods.
