## Answer

Covered. Published IRAP letter is from December 2022 (ISM June 2022 PROTECTED) and Managed AI Services is not named in its scope. No SOC 2 or ISO/IEC 42001 found. IRAP report and SSP on request.

Source statement: "AUCyber is ISO/IEC 27001 certified having received certification in January 2020." (https://docs.aucyber.com.au/latest/Platform_Overview/Security/security_and_governance/)

Research detail: The December 2022 IRAP letter from PCG Cyber records an assessment against ISM June 2022 PROTECTED controls covering the AUCloud portal and infrastructure plus IaaS, BaaS, DRaaS, SOCaaS, M365 BaaS and DaaS. AUCyber holds a DTA Hosting Certification Framework Certified Strategic certificate for facilities EC1, EC2, EC3, F1 and F2 and is on the DTA list, plus a 2025 ISO 27001 certificate. The IRAP report and SSP are available to customer security teams on request.
