## Answer

Covered. Applies to use cases moving through configurable workflow stages. Approver sign-off detail is not public.

Source statement: "Workflow stages define the governance pipeline that use cases progress through." (https://docs.sdk.credo.ai/core-concepts/workflow/)

Research detail: Use cases are submitted through intake and move through workflow stages, each with assessment, evidence collection and clear steps, and can be rejected as a terminal state. Intermediate stages such as a security review are configurable. The product page lists governance workflows with approval gates, and GAIA drafts intake records.
