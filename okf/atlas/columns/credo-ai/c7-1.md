## Answer

Not covered. Credo AI documents agent registries and audit trails of governance actions, but no write-once or integrity-validated storage of per-agent action records (tool calls, data access, changes) tied to user or session, and no documented export of such records to tamper-evident storage.

Research detail: The product page lists automated evidence generation and audit trails, and the Agent Registry post says it captures audit trails for agent decisions. The audit scope appears to be governance activity. No tamper-proof ledger of agent inputs, outputs and reasoning is documented.
