## Answer

Not covered. Only control assignment on use cases is documented. No owner for agents, models or tools, and no reassignment.

Research detail: The SDK accepts an owner value when a use case is created and returns a creator id. Controls attached to a use case can be assigned to users and teams with a due date. No owner field is documented for models, vendors or agents, and the update schema has no owner, so reassignment is not shown.
