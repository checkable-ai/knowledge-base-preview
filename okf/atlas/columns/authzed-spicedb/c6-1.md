## Answer

Not covered. No inventory or registry of models, agents or tools with components. SpiceDB can store agents and tools as objects, but only as a modelling pattern.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: SpiceDB can store objects such as agents, tools and tasks and the links between them, but it is not an inventory or registry of models, agents or tools and records no schemas or component data for them.
