Research detail: SpiceDB can answer what a subject can do and who can reach a resource. ExperimentalReflectSchema and bulk checks report permissions on a resource, and the Access Control Audit page describes auditing which permissions users hold.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

ExperimentalReflectSchema, BulkCheckPermission, LookupResources and LookupSubjects

## Gaps

No documented human readable view of an agent's network reach. The FAQ says there is no single API that returns all permissions for a subject on a resource. The data reflects only what is modelled in SpiceDB.
