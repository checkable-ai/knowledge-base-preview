## Answer

Not covered. A SpiceDB schema is an access model, not an AI policy document, and the vendor documents no ownership, publication or review of AI policies. Restricted API Access roles only govern who may call the SpiceDB API.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: A SpiceDB schema is the written definition of who can do what, with a validation and testing workflow and a Playground. Restricted API Access roles define rules for who may call the SpiceDB API.
