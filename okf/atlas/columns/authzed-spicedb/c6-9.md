## Answer

Not covered. No documented way to record, approve or periodically review policy exceptions or accepted risks, and no risk register or exception workflow. Expiring relationships (SpiceDB v1.4.0 or later, enabled with `use expiration` in the schema) and caveats only end or condition individual permission grants. The vendor does not describe them as exception or accepted-risk management.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Relationships can carry an expiry time, so a temporary grant ends automatically. Caveats can require conditions at check time.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Expiring relationships, caveats

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

This is the grant mechanism only. Recording, approval and periodic review of exceptions or accepted risks are not documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Record exceptions in a risk register and write them to SpiceDB as expiring relationships.
