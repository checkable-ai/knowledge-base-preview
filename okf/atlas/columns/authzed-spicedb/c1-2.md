## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The application remains the enforcement point and must call SpiceDB before acting. SpiceDB is available as open source, AuthZed Cloud, AuthZed Dedicated and SpiceDB Enterprise. Provided by Open Agent Primitives (OAP), which runs self-hosted in your own Kubernetes cluster.

Source statement: "It calls SpiceDB before releasing data or performing the action." (https://authzed.com/blog/spicedb-secures-authorization-for-ai)

Research detail: SpiceDB is built to decide what a subject may do on a resource, and AuthZed positions it for agent tool calls, per-document RAG access and MCP scopes. Permissions come from a typed schema and relationship graph, with caveats and expiry for context and time limits.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

SpiceDB, AuthZed Cloud, AuthZed Dedicated

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

SpiceDB is the decision point only. The application must call it before releasing data or acting, and nothing in SpiceDB stops an agent that skips the check.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Put the check in a gateway or tool wrapper that the agent cannot bypass.
