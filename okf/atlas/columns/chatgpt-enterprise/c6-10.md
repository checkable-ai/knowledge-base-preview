## Answer

Covered. Configurable retention applies to Enterprise and Edu only, and Business keeps chats indefinitely. Residency covers only eligible content and supported workloads, regions and configurations, in new Enterprise and Edu workspaces. Australia has data residency at rest but no inference residency. Local computer access with Work Cloud does not provide strict zero data retention. Memories, Library files and conversations are deleted separately. EKM covers supported stored content in eligible workspaces.

Source statement: "Data residency and inference residency cover only eligible content and supported workloads, regions, and configurations." (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/admin-setup)

Research detail: Enterprise admins set conversation retention and deleted chats are removed within 30 days. New Enterprise and Edu workspaces can keep customer content at rest in Australia and other regions. EKM encrypts content with customer keys. Business admins can also set retention.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Workspace retention, data residency, EKM

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Residency applies to new workspaces and named features only. Australia has no inference residency. Local work sync has no zero data retention. Memories, Library files and conversations are deleted separately.

## Options

Choose residency at workspace creation and confirm Work and Codex coverage before enabling.
