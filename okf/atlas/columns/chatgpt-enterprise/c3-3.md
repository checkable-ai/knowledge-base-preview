## Answer

Not covered. The readable OpenAI SharePoint page documents only an admin allowlist of approved site collections. No readable page documents sensitivity-label exclusion or other DLP on the retrieval path. Compliance Platform DLP integrations act after the event.

Research detail: Enterprise admins can restrict live app content: Google Drive shared drives, SharePoint site collections and sensitivity labels, Teams by group and label, and Outlook by label. Enterprise can also restrict connections that use the company email domain to authorised workspaces. The Compliance Platform feeds DLP tools.

## Gaps
