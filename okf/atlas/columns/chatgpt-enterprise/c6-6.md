## Answer

Not covered. Workspace agents have an owner who manages access, but no documented accountable owner record for every agent, model and tool, with reassignment to keep it current.

Last checked 13 November 2026.

Research detail: Each workspace agent has an owner who alone controls sharing, distribution and deletion, and the owner grants chat or edit access. Admins can review and transfer GPT ownership. Service accounts have shared-account roles for managers.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Workspace agent owner, GPT ownership transfer, service account Manager role

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Owner is the creator, not a recorded accountable business owner. Ownership of agent decisions is not tracked.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Transfer GPT and agent ownership when staff leave.
