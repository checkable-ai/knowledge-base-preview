## Answer

Not covered. Ownership is the creator. No readable page documents admin assignment or transfer of an agent, GPT or app owner. Service-account manager roles are not agent owners.

Research detail: Each workspace agent has an owner who alone controls sharing, distribution and deletion, and the owner grants chat or edit access. Admins can review and transfer GPT ownership. Service accounts have shared-account roles for managers.

## Products

Workspace agent owner, GPT ownership transfer, service account Manager role

## Gaps
