## Answer

Not covered. No documented way to train agents on their tasks, validate behaviour continuously or define anomaly detection. The nearby vendor-run safety monitoring, which pauses or ends conversations and sends a workspace alert webhook, is applied by OpenAI, covers some models only and is asynchronous.

Research detail: With Astra models, a monitoring system pauses or ends conversations in ChatGPT and Codex when an agent may be misreading instructions around sensitive data or destructive changes. Workspace owners can subscribe a webhook to safety.org_alert.created to track these alerts centrally. ChatGPT agent had prompt injection monitoring and watch mode before its retirement.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Astra safety monitoring, workspace safety webhooks

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Monitoring is asynchronous and may pause after the triggering action. It applies to Astra models only. No customer-defined anomaly rules.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Route safety.org_alert.created to the SOC and add usage-spike alerts from exported logs.
