## Answer

Not covered. Retention, data residency and EKM secure the stores, but nothing cleans or sanitises stored knowledge content. Governance of the data agents read is not documented as a feature.

Research detail: Library, project and GPT knowledge files follow workspace retention, are stored in-region under data residency, and can be encrypted with customer keys through EKM. Library files are available to Library-specific Compliance API endpoints while active.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Library, projects, GPT knowledge, EKM, data residency

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No sanitisation of stored content. Synced indexes and connected-app copies follow separate rules.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options
