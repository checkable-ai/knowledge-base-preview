## Answer

Not covered. Admins can make apps available by role and remove them, but no scheduled reassessment or expiry of app approvals is documented. The only scheduled check is a daily update check for marketplaces.

Research detail: Admins enable, restrict by role and disable third-party apps, limit which account domains can connect, and review Microsoft Graph permissions before granting them. A global admin sets which external applications may use Sign in with ChatGPT. The admin quickstart directs regular audits of enabled apps and domains.
