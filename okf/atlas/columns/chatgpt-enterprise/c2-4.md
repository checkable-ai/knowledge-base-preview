## Answer

Covered. Session state is the conversation or project. Enterprise and Edu workspaces apply a configurable retention policy, while Business workspaces keep chats, files and canvas documents indefinitely with no retention window. When a member leaves, their projects and GPTs are reassigned, but their private conversations are not.

Source statement: "For Enterprise and Edu workspaces, chats, files, and canvas documents follow the configured workspace retention policy." (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/user-lifecycle)

Research detail: Chats and projects persist until the user deletes them or the workspace retention policy removes them. Enterprise admins set retention, and owners control project creation and sharing through roles. Deleted chats are scheduled for deletion within 30 days.
