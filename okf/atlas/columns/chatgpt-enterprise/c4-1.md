## Answer

Not covered. No per-agent identity tied to an owner, runtime and release. Codex service accounts give automation an attributed identity but document no owner field, runtime binding or release binding. Workspace agents and GPTs have no identity separate from their human owner.

Research detail: Codex service accounts give each CI runner, scheduled job or shared integration its own non-human ChatGPT workspace identity with its own roles, groups, plugins and access tokens, and runs are attributed to the account. They can be SCIM provisioned with userType ServiceAccount. Workspace agents carry a named owner, editors and version history. Service accounts are limited to pay-as-you-go plans.

## Gaps
