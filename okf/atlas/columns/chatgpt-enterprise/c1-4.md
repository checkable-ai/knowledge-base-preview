## Answer

Covered. RBAC controls who builds and publishes agents. Service accounts can be disabled or deleted. Creation by permitted members needs no admin approval.

Source statement: "Both actions revoke all active tokens." (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/service-accounts)

Research detail: RBAC permissions control who can build, publish and publish with agent-owned connections for workspace agents. Only admins and owners publish custom apps. Service accounts are created, disabled and deleted in the workspace or Admin API, and deletion revokes their tokens. GPT ownership can be transferred or GPTs deleted from Workspace settings.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

RBAC agent permissions, custom app publishing, service accounts, GPT management

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Agent creation by permitted members needs no admin approval. Workspace agents are off by default at launch in Enterprise.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Limit agent publishing to a builder group and review the agents directory on a schedule.
