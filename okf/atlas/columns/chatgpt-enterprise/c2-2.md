## Answer

Covered. Model access is set at workspace and role level. Separate starting defaults apply for Chat and for Work and Codex, and some models (such as Astra and GPT-6 Luna) need administrator enablement in Enterprise and Edu. Inference residency is available only for eligible content and supported regions (Europe, United States, UAE). Australia has no inference residency, and Work is not supported with UAE inference residency.

Source statement: "Workspace owners can enable Astra for the workspace or specific roles across Chat, Work, and Codex." (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/workspace-model-availability)

Research detail: Owners control which models each role can use, and Model Test shows a member's effective model access. Business data is not used for training by default. Enterprise and Edu workspaces can add inference residency in Europe, the United States or the UAE.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Models page and Model Test, RBAC model access, inference residency

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Australia is a data residency region but not an inference residency region. Explicit model version pinning is not documented beyond the picker and legacy model access.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Record the approved models per role and check them with Model Test after each model launch.
