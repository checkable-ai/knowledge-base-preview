## Answer

Partly covered. No complete per-action ledger. The vendor states records do not establish a complete record of every local command or file operation, and do not fully cover approvals or external actions. Reasoning is not logged.

Source statement: "a security team can stream immutable compliance events into its SIEM for investigations" (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/compliance-api)

Research detail: The Compliance Logs Platform gives Enterprise and Edu an immutable, append-only log of conversations, Work prompts and responses, Codex use, app calls, authentication and audit events, kept 30 days. OpenAI states these records do not form a complete trail of hosted file operations, shell commands, browser steps, tool calls or approvals. Business has no Compliance Platform.

## Products

Compliance Logs Platform, workspace audit logs, tenant audit logs

## Gaps

No complete per-action ledger. Reasoning is not logged. 30-day source retention. Enterprise and Edu only.
