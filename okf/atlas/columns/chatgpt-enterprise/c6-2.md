## Answer

Covered. Enterprise and Edu disable new plugins and apps by default. Business enables apps by default. Publishing a workspace agent needs a permission but no admin approval.

Source statement: "Use Installation policy to choose Available or Installed for each eligible role." (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/plugin-management)

Research detail: Custom MCP connections are tested privately and only admins and owners can publish them. App templates produce a draft app that admins review and publish. In Enterprise and Edu new plugins and apps are disabled by default. Agent publishing is a role permission.
