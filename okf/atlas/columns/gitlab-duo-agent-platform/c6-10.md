## Answer

Not covered. Session retention is fixed at 30 days, but GitLab.com residency and AI audit event retention are not documented, and retention varies by provider.

Research detail: Sessions are deleted 30 days after last activity. GitLab states zero retention with Fireworks AI and no model training on customer data, and prompt caching can be turned off. Self-hosted deployments keep prompts and responses in the customer network.
