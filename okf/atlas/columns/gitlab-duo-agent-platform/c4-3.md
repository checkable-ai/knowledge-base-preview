## Answer

Covered. Flows and agents on runners only. Agentic Chat in the UI and IDE acts directly as the user. Effective access is limited to the more restrictive of the user's role and the service account's Developer role.

Source statement: "This identity has a combination of the user's role and the service account's Developer role, whichever is more restrictive." (https://docs.gitlab.com/user/duo_agent_platform/composite_identity/)

Research detail: Composite identity combines the service account and the initiating human into one token, with the human's user ID carried in a dynamic scope. Effective access is the more restrictive of the user's role and the service account's Developer role, limited to projects both can reach. Flow-created merge requests are attributed to the triggering user.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Composite identity

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Does not apply to Agentic Chat in the UI or IDE, which act directly as the user. Propagation to third-party services beyond GitLab is not described.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use flows rather than chat where delegated attribution matters. Check downstream systems receive user identity through ID token claims if needed.
