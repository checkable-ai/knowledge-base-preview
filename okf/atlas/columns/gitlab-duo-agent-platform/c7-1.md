## Answer

Not covered. The vendor documents no tamper evidence, integrity protection or immutability for AI audit events. Storage is off by default and the feature is in Beta. Some flows store only session metadata, with no prompts, responses or tool content. The audit events are the nearest feature, and they fall short of a tamper-evident record of inputs, outputs and reasoning.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: AI audit events record each agent session with metadata and a chronological event list, attributed to the service account with the initiating human recorded. Changes to catalog confinement are recorded in the audit log.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI audit events, GitLab audit log

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Beta. Storage is off by default. Tamper resistance, retention and capture of inputs, outputs or reasoning are not documented. Self-managed gateways must enable forwarding.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable storage with a group lock and stream events externally for immutability.
