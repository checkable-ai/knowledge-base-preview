## Answer

Not covered. Restrictions on MCP servers, external agents and models exist, but no scheduled reassessment or retirement workflow.

Research detail: MCP servers are restricted to vetted or partner servers added by administrators, external agents can be turned off at instance or group level, Agentic Chat can be restricted to a model list, and the catalog can be confined to a group hierarchy.
