## Answer

Covered. SOC 2 Type 2, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, 27017 and 27018 cover GitLab.com and GitLab Dedicated only, not Self-Managed. ISO 42001 certificate and penetration test summary are listed on the Trust Center (trust.gitlab.com), which is script rendered. No IRAP assessment is published, and Duo is not named in any certification scope.

Source statement: "GitLab maintains a SOC 2 Type 2 report for the Security, Confidentiality and Availability Trust Services Criteria" (https://about.gitlab.com/security/)

Research detail: GitLab's security page lists a SOC 2 Type 2 report (GitLab.com and Dedicated), ISO/IEC 27001:2022, 27017 and 27018 for those two SaaS offerings. Its Trust Center lists an ISO/IEC 42001 certificate for AI governance, a penetration test executive summary, a PCI DSS attestation and a sub-processor list. Neither page mentions IRAP, and GitLab is not on the Hosting Certification Framework list.

## Products

SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001, penetration test summary via Trust Center

## Gaps

No IRAP assessment is published, so an agency would need to rely on its own risk assessment. The ISO 42001 certificate scope is not stated on the Trust Center page and GitLab Duo is not named in any certification scope. The Trust Center is script rendered and the reports themselves were not opened. The security page does not list ISO 42001 while the Trust Center does.
