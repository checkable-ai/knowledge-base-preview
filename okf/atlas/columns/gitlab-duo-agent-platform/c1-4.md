## Answer

Covered. Maintainer or Owner role in the managing project to edit. Catalog confinement is GitLab.com only.

Source statement: "Members of the managing project who have the Maintainer or Owner role can edit the item." (https://docs.gitlab.com/user/duo_agent_platform/ai_catalog/)

Research detail: Custom agents and flows are created, enabled, disabled, edited, hidden and deleted through the AI Catalog with role requirements (Maintainer or Owner to create, administrators to delete). Enabling a flow provisions its service account in the top-level group with the Developer role. Owners or administrators can turn custom and external agents on or off.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Catalog, composite identity service accounts, Agent Platform on/off settings

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Service account retirement when a flow is disabled is not described. No approval step before an agent is created or enabled.
