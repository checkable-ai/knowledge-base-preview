## Answer

Not covered. Flows sequence steps and sessions can be cancelled, but retry and recovery are not documented and cancelled sessions cannot be resumed.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Flows sequence agent, tool, AI task and Human Input components defined in YAML, and foundational flows cover code review, CI fixes and vulnerability resolution. Running or waiting sessions can be cancelled. A cancelled session cannot be resumed or restarted.
