## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Requires the Premium or Ultimate tier. Offered on GitLab.com, GitLab Self-Managed and GitLab Dedicated. Viewing project sessions needs the Developer, Maintainer or Owner role. Retention is fixed at 30 days after last activity.

Source statement: "Sessions are automatically deleted 30 days after the last activity." (https://docs.gitlab.com/user/duo_agent_platform/sessions/)

Last checked 12 November 2026. This platform is checked on demand, so this answer has not been rechecked since.

Research detail: Sessions hold status, execution data and logs per agent or flow run, visible to Developer and above on the project and to the triggering user. Sessions are deleted 30 days after last activity and can be deleted earlier in the IDE.

## Gaps

Resumption is limited (cancelled sessions cannot resume). Retention is fixed rather than configurable. Stored fields beyond logs are not listed.
