## Answer

Covered. Retention is fixed at 30 days. Cancelled sessions cannot be resumed.

Source statement: "Sessions are automatically deleted 30 days after the last activity." (https://docs.gitlab.com/user/duo_agent_platform/sessions/)

Research detail: Sessions hold status, execution data and logs per agent or flow run, visible to Developer and above on the project and to the triggering user. Sessions are deleted 30 days after last activity and can be deleted earlier in the IDE.

## Gaps
