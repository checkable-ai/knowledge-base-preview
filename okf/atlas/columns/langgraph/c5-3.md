## Answer

Not covered. Token cost tracking covers model cost only. No staff time or return measures.

Research detail: LangSmith calculates token costs per run and trace from a model pricing map and accepts custom costs for tool and retrieval runs. Dashboards and cost alerts are available. Labour savings, output and profit are not tracked.

## Products

LangSmith cost tracking, dashboards, cost alerts

## Gaps
