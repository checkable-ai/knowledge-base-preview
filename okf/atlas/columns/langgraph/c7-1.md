## Answer

Not covered. No write-once or integrity-validated store for agent actions. LangSmith audit logs record administrative actions only, not agent tool calls or data access. LangSmith traces capture agent steps but can be deleted, masked, and expire under retention settings, and no documented export to immutable storage is provided for them.

Research detail: LangSmith audit logs (Enterprise) keep a tamper-resistant record of administrative and configuration actions with actor, operation and resources, for up to 400 days. Traces record agent inputs, outputs and steps, but they can be masked, deleted and expire under retention settings.

## Products

LangSmith audit logs, LangSmith tracing

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Audit logs cover mostly write actions, not agent runtime actions. The tamper-resistance mechanism is not described. Traces are not an immutable ledger.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Forward audit logs and traces to a write-once store.
