## Answer

Covered. Annotation queues review after the fact; in-flow review needs interrupts.

Source statement: "Single-run queues present one item at a time and let reviewers submit any rubric feedback you configure." (https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/annotation-queues)

Research detail: LangSmith annotation queues route runs and threads to human reviewers with rubrics, reviewer assignment and pairwise comparison. Interrupts let a person review and edit agent state or output before the graph continues.
