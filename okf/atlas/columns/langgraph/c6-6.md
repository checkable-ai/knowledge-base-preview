## Answer

Not covered. No accountable owner field for agents. Resource ownership is for data access and prompt owners fall short.

Research detail: Prompts have named owners who can be the only people allowed to tag and promote them. Custom auth can stamp an owner on threads, assistants and crons. Agents and deployments have no accountable owner field.

## Products

LangSmith prompt owners, Agent Server resource ownership metadata

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Resource ownership means data access ownership, not accountability. No owner review or reassignment process.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Record accountable owners in assistant metadata and an external register.
