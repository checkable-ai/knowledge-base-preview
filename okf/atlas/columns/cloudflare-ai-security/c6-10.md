## Answer

Not covered. No documented rules for retention, deletion or residency of agent inputs, outputs and memory. Zero Data Retention applies only to Unified Billing traffic, and gateway logging is turned off separately. The page makes no data residency statement.

Research detail: AI Gateway logging can be turned off per gateway or per request, and request and response bodies can be dropped while keeping metadata. Retention follows Workers Logs for new gateways, Logpush exports bodies encrypted with a customer key, and portal resources are fetched live rather than stored.
