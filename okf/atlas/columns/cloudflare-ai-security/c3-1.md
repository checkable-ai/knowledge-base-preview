## Answer

Covered. Per-user budgets need the gateway behind Cloudflare Access or a custom metadata user identifier; service-token requests carry no user ID. Spend limits are eventually consistent and cost tracking is best-effort for models with known pricing. Maximum 20 spend limit rules per gateway.

Source statement: "Spend limits let you set cost-based budgets on your AI Gateway." (https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-gateway/features/spend-limits/)

Research detail: AI Gateway sits between clients and model providers with dynamic routing, rate limiting, and spend limits that can be scoped per user through cf.user_id or custom metadata. Guardrails screen prompts and responses for harmful content and DLP scans both for sensitive data, with flag or block actions. Access policies decide who can call a gateway.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Gateway (dynamic routing, rate limiting, spend limits, Guardrails, DLP, Access)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Rate limiting is per gateway, not per user. Spend limits are eventually consistent and best effort. Guardrails target harmful content categories, and prompt injection detection is documented in AI Security for Apps (inbound app traffic) rather than AI Gateway.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use per-user spend limits and dynamic route rate nodes. Put AI Security for Apps in front of public LLM endpoints for injection screening.
