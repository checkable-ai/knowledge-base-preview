## Answer

Covered. The SOC 2 report is available only under NDA. New features enter scope on the annual audit cycle. IRAP PROTECTED applies to Cloudflare for Government Australia. ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 were not confirmed.

Source statement: "All Cloudflare plans are now in-scope for Cloudflare's SOC 2 report." (https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/compliance-resources/soc-2/)

Research detail: Cloudflare for Government Australia was assessed at IRAP PROTECTED in March 2025, covering WAF, Secure Web Gateway, CASB, Workers and almost every other product, with a boundary of US and Australian facilities. Cloudflare issues an annual SOC 2 Type II report covering all plans, available under NDA through the dashboard.
