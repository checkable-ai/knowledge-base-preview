## Answer

Not covered. No submission or approval workflow; approval status does not block access by itself.

Research detail: Admins mark SaaS and AI apps as Approved, Unapproved, In review or Unreviewed, and Gateway policies can act on that status. MCP servers become available only when an admin adds them to a portal. There is no submission or approval workflow.
