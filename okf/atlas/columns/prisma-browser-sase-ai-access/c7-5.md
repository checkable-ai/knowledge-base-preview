## Answer

Not covered. Advanced Extension Security risk-rates browser extensions only. No SBOMs or scanning of MCP servers, CLIs or skills, and no coverage of external services.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: The AXS engine classifies each browser extension version as malicious, high, medium or low risk using inline analysis and threat intelligence. Extensions can be allowed, blocked or limited by permission and host access. Coverage is browser extensions only, not MCP servers, CLIs or skills.
