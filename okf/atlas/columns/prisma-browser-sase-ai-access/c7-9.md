## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Needs a Strata Logging Service licence plus one of: an AI Access Security licence, CASB-PA, CASB-X, or a Prisma Browser standalone licence. Runs on NGFW and Prisma Access (managed by Panorama or Strata Cloud Manager), or on Prisma Browser. Discovery covers GenAI apps from an app dictionary; agent and MCP discovery is not documented.

Source statement: "a comprehensive dictionary of over 500 GenAI apps" (https://docs.paloaltonetworks.com/ai-access-security/getting-started/introducing-ai-access-security)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: AI Access Security uses a dictionary of GenAI apps to identify sanctioned and shadow AI use, with a dashboard by app, use case, user and data incidents. Prisma Browser flags GenAI apps and accounts in its events and dashboards.
