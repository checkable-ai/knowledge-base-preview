## Answer

Not covered. No approval flow for connectors or tools, and no approval step for models beyond workspace opt-in. Workflow publication approval is the nearby feature.

Research detail: Workflow builders request publication approval and admins have a central oversight dashboard. Custom agents and workflows need admin approval at the sharing levels the firm selects, with email notifications for approvals. New models are enabled per workspace by opt-in. Tools and connectors have no documented approval flow.
