## Answer

Not covered. No named owner field for agents or workflows. Vault ownership and transfer apply to vaults, which are data stores, not agents.

Research detail: Vault projects have an owner who always holds manage access, and ownership can be transferred when responsibilities change. Agent and workflow ownership is not described publicly.
