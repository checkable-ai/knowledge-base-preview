## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Processing can be in the EU and Switzerland, or Australia. Model providers are required to apply zero data retention. Customers set retention policies and can delete data at any time. Default retention periods and memory retention are not stated. Anthropic processes in the US and Mistral in the EU.

Source statement: "we offer processing in the EU and Switzerland or Australia." (https://www.harvey.ai/security)

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Customers set retention policies and can delete data at any time. Admins set Vault retention per client matter, deleted vaults sit in a 30-day recycle bin, and users can delete memories. Processing can be localised to the EU and Switzerland, Australia or the US, Harvey does not train on customer data by default, and model providers are held to zero data retention.

## Products

Retention settings, Vault retention, recycle bin, regional hosting
