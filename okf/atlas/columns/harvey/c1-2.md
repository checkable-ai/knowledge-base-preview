## Answer

Not covered. No per-agent or per-connector action scopes for what an agent may do in connected systems. Per-user MCP permission checks and sharing levels govern who can use content, not what an agent may write.

Research detail: MCP tools check per-user permissions, for example ask_about_vault requires the VAULT permission. Vault owners can grant access levels and block view-only users from downloading or duplicating content, and ethical walls from the firm's walls provider restrict access to walled content. Public pages do not describe per-agent scopes for write actions in connected systems.
