## Answer

Not covered. No customer approval, scheduled reassessment or retirement of providers. The subprocessor list and model opt-in fall short.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Harvey publishes its subprocessors with purpose and location, passes its security obligations to them and bars model providers from training on customer data. Workspaces opt in to some new models. Customers cannot approve or retire individual providers themselves.
