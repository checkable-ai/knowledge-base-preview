## Answer

Not covered. Approval is of the plan before work begins, not of each exact action. No per-action approval for external writes, and no documented refusal or replay handling.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Users can preview an agent's plan, change its scope and approve it before work begins, and agents nudge users when input is needed. The LexisNexis motion to dismiss workflow requires the user to approve the research plan before drafting. Approval is of a plan, not bound to each exact downstream action, and denial or replay handling is not described.
