## Answer

Covered. Covers saved user preferences applied across the web app, Outlook, the Word Add-In and playbook runs. Admins grant Memory by role.

Source statement: "Set your preferences upfront or as you work, so every answer and work product comes back the way you want, every time." (https://www.harvey.ai/platform/memory)

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Memory saves a user's working preferences and applies them across sessions in the web app, Outlook, Word and playbook runs. Users can review, edit or delete saved memories, and admins grant Memory by role. Harvey says memories are not used to train models.
