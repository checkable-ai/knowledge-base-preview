## Answer

Not covered. No documented retirement of agents or process for changing an agent's permissions. Publication approval for workflows and admin export of agents fall short of a full lifecycle process.

Research detail: SCIM syncs users, roles and groups from the identity provider. Workflow builders request publication approval, and since October 2026 custom agents and workflows are usable on save with admin approval only at the sharing levels the firm selects. Agent Builder Admins can export workspace agents with metadata.
