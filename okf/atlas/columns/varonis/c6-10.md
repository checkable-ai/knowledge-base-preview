## Answer

Not covered. No retention, deletion or residency rules for AI inputs, outputs or memory; sensitivity labelling falls short.

Research detail: Varonis for Microsoft 365 Copilot applies sensitivity labels to Copilot-created data and detects Copilot access to stale data. Atlas keeps AI activity logs in customer-owned telemetry. Retention, deletion and residency of AI inputs, outputs and memory are not governed by Varonis.
