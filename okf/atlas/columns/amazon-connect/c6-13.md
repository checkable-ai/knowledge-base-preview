## Answer

Covered. The IRAP assessment applies to Amazon Connect as a whole, and the AI agent features are not itemised separately. Reports are obtained through AWS Artifact. SOC 2, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 were not verified for Connect.

Source statement: "This service is currently in scope and is reflected in current reports." (https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/services-in-scope/IRAP/)

Research detail: AWS lists Amazon Connect as assessed at the IRAP PROTECTED level on its services in scope page, last updated 2 July 2026. The latest IRAP report is available through AWS Artifact, and AWS says an ASD certified assessor completed the assessment in June 2026. The Connect documentation says Connect Customer and Amazon Bedrock are in scope for many AWS compliance programs.
