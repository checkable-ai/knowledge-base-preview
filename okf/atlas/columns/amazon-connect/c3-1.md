## Answer

Not covered. No model-call gateway with routing, quotas, cost or rate limits per agent. Bedrock guardrails screen inputs and outputs only.

Research detail: AI guardrails use Amazon Bedrock guardrails, with up to three custom guardrails per instance. Agentic CX designer input guardrails can detect prompt injection with regex, keyword or LLM judge rules.
