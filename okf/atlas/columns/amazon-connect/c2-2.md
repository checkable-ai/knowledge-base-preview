## Answer

Covered. For many managed features Connect selects and changes the model itself. Cross-region inference may process data in other listed Regions.

Source statement: "The models available on Amazon Bedrock never store, log, or share customer prompts (input) and responses (output), and never use this data to train these models." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/connect/latest/adminguide/ai-in-connect.html)

Research detail: AI features use Amazon Bedrock models, and AWS states Bedrock models do not store or train on prompts and responses. Each AI prompt names its model, Connect notifies before deprecation and redirects to a supported model afterwards, and the docs list the inference Regions each instance Region may use under cross-region inference.
