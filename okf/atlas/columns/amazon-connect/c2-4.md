## Answer

Not covered. Context variables in agentic CX designer are session-scoped and persist for the conversation session, and Allow frontend updates controls what can write to them. The vendor does not document who can read session memory, how a session resumes after a break, or how long session data is kept.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Each contact has an AI agent session, and custom data can be added with UpdateSessionData for use in AI prompts. Agentic CX designer keeps conversation state across turns.
