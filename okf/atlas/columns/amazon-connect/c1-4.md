## Answer

Not covered. No approval step or documented retirement process for AI agents. Nearby: separately grantable admin permissions for AI Agents, Prompts and Guardrails and CloudTrail logging.

Research detail: AI Agents, AI Prompts and AI Guardrails each have an All Access admin permission in security profiles. AI agents are created in the console or API, and Connect API calls are logged in CloudTrail.
