## Answer

Not covered. The Trust Center AI Agents Inventory (Preview) lists Cortex Agents only. Models and tools are not in it, and non-Cortex agents are not listed.

Research detail: Agents, Cortex Search services, semantic views, external MCP servers and External Agents are schema objects listable with SHOW commands. Cortex base models appear as objects in SNOWFLAKE.MODELS, and the Model Registry holds customer models with versions and tags.
