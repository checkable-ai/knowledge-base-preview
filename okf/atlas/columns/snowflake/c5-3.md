## Answer

Not covered. Usage history and budgets show credit cost only. There is no labour cost, savings or output measure set against return.

Research detail: CORTEX_AI_FUNCTIONS_USAGE_HISTORY reports credits by function, model, user, role and query. AI_GATEWAY_USAGE_HISTORY gives per-request gateway, user, model and token counts for showback, and the gateway console splits activity and cost. Team spend can be grouped by tag in shared resource budgets.
