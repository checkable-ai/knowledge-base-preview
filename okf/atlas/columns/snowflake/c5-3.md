## Answer

Not covered. No accounting of staff time or of value delivered against cost. Usage views report credits and tokens only.

Research detail: CORTEX_AI_FUNCTIONS_USAGE_HISTORY reports credits by function, model, user, role and query. AI_GATEWAY_USAGE_HISTORY gives per-request gateway, user, model and token counts for showback, and the gateway console splits activity and cost. Team spend can be grouped by tag in shared resource budgets.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

CORTEX_AI_FUNCTIONS_USAGE_HISTORY, AI_GATEWAY_USAGE_HISTORY, resource budgets, per-user quotas

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Gateway attribution is per user, with no documented per-agent breakdown. Labour cost, savings and output measures are not covered.

## Options
