## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Model availability varies by region and account, per the Model availability page. Individual models can be in preview or have lifecycle restrictions. Web search must be enabled at the account level. OpenAI GPT-5.6 and later models can require account eligibility under model-provider requirements.

Source statement: "you can choose the language model that powers it or let Snowflake select one automatically." (https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-agents)

Last checked 18 November 2026.

Research detail: Agents and AI functions name a model or use auto, and SHOW CORTEX BASE MODELS reports lifecycle status, legacy and end-of-life dates. CORTEX_ENABLED_CROSS_REGION sets where inference may run, payloads are processed transiently and not persisted in the processing region, and context window limits are documented per model and region.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Cortex AI Functions, Cortex Agents, cross-region inference

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

With auto, the model chosen can change. Rate limits are not documented for AI functions, and Cortex Analyst picks models from a ranked list the customer cannot set directly.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pin models in production agents, set CORTEX_ENABLED_CROSS_REGION explicitly, and restrict models with RBAC.
