## Answer

Covered. Sensitive data classification requires Enterprise Edition or higher. Masking policies must be assigned directly or via tags.

Source statement: "You can combine tagging and masking policies to automatically mask data that is classified as sensitive." (https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/classify-intro)

Research detail: Horizon sensitive data classification tags columns by semantic and privacy category and can apply tag-based masking automatically. Cortex Search requires change tracking on source tables and suspends after repeated refresh failures. AI_REDACT can sanitise text before indexing.

## Products

## Gaps
