## Answer

Not covered. No documented pipeline of agent telemetry linked to sessions and people, with redaction, detections and a named response owner. Trust Center notification integrations push findings outbound but fall short.

Research detail: Trust Center Threat Intelligence and AI Security packages raise detections and violations, such as unusual login IPs and sensitive data accessed by agents without policies, with email and programmatic notifications. Guardrail flags are recorded in CORTEX_AI_GUARDRAILS_USAGE_HISTORY, and observability events carry user and session attributes with redaction by privilege.
