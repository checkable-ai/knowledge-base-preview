## Answer

Covered. The compliance page does not confirm that Cortex AI features are inside the IRAP boundary.

Source statement: "Snowflake underwent a rigorous, independent third-party audit conducted by an accredited certification body" (https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/ISO-IEC-42001-AI-certification/)

Research detail: Snowflake lists IRAP (Protected) for Australia alongside SOC 1 Type II, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, 27017, 27018, CSA STAR Level 1 and FedRAMP Moderate and High. Its region list marks AWS ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) and Azure australiaeast as supporting IRAP Protected compliance, and a 27 May 2026 blog reports a PROTECTED assessment of the Google Cloud Melbourne region by ASD-endorsed assessors. The same blog refers to Snowflake's ISO 42001 certification.
