## Answer

Not covered. Ownership is a role, not a named accountable person with a maintained record.

Research detail: Every agent and MCP server object has an OWNERSHIP role, transferable with GRANT OWNERSHIP. Contacts record approver, security, steward and support contacts for tables, schemas and databases.

## Products

OWNERSHIP privilege, contacts

## Gaps

Ownership is a role, not a named person. Contacts are not listed as supported on agent objects.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options
