## Answer

Not covered. No scheduled reassessment or retirement workflow for external models and tools.

Research detail: Third-party MCP connectors require admin-created objects and can be disabled, which revokes all user tokens. Web search must be enabled by an account admin, and third-party models are governed by model RBAC and lifecycle dates.
