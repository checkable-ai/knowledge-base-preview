## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Needs the SNOWFLAKE.TRUST_CENTER_VIEWER or SNOWFLAKE.TRUST_CENTER_ADMIN application role. The CIS Benchmarks scanner package must be enabled. Snowflake reader accounts aren't supported. Evidence is account-level configuration findings, not AI-specific. Account posture reporting and PDF posture reports are in open preview.

Source statement: "generate a PDF report for audit and review" (https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/trust-center/overview)
