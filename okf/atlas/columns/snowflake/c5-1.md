## Answer

Covered. Reviewers need monitoring privileges on the agent, plus the privilege to see unredacted content. Review is after the fact, with no pre-release approval gate.

Source statement: "Observability shows live conversation history and execution traces: planning, tool calls, responses, and user feedback." (https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-agents-monitor)

Research detail: End users can rate responses and leave comments, stored as CORTEX_AGENT_FEEDBACK observability events, and owners can review threads and traces in the Observability pane. Snowflake advises reviewing agent answers before showing them to users.
