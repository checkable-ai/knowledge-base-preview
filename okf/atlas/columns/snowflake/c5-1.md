## Answer

Covered. Reviewers need OWNERSHIP or MONITOR on the agent plus the CORTEX_USER database role. Unredacted content needs the READ UNREDACTED AI OBSERVABILITY EVENTS TABLE privilege. Review is after the fact.

Source statement: "To view Cortex Agent logs, users must have the following privileges:" (https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/cortex-agents-monitor)

Research detail: End users can rate responses and leave comments, stored as CORTEX_AGENT_FEEDBACK observability events, and owners can review threads and traces in the Observability pane. Snowflake advises reviewing agent answers before showing them to users.
