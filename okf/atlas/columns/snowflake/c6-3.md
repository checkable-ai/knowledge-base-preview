## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Scanner packages are deactivated by default, except Security Essentials. Snowflake can automatically enable the AI Security scanner package for Business Critical Edition or Virtual Private Snowflake accounts with a capacity contract. The Cortex AI Guardrails scanner requires Enterprise Edition or higher. Reader accounts are not supported. Access needs the SNOWFLAKE.TRUST_CENTER_VIEWER or an admin application role. The organization-level view needs an organization account and does not show detection findings. Account posture reporting and the AI agents inventory are in Open Preview.

Source statement: "For accounts on Business Critical Edition or Virtual Private Snowflake (VPS) with a capacity contract, Snowflake can automatically enable the AI Security scanner package." (https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/trust-center/overview)

Last checked 18 November 2026.

Research detail: Trust Center scans accounts against Security Essentials, CIS Benchmarks, Threat Intelligence and an AI Security package, recording persistent violations and one-time detections with organisation-level views of violations. AI scanners cover privileged Cortex Search owners, unrestricted Cortex Code tokens, unmasked sensitive data used by agents and a disabled prompt injection guardrail.
