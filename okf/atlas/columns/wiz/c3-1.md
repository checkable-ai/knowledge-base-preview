## Answer

Not covered. No model gateway, routing, quotas, rate limits or inline prompt screening. AI-SPM only checks that provider guardrails are enabled, and Wiz is not in the model call path.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Wiz has no model gateway. It verifies that provider guardrails such as Bedrock Guardrails are enabled on inputs and outputs, and its Cloudflare integration flags AI endpoints not protected by Cloudflare. Cloudflare, not Wiz, enforces at the edge.
