## Answer

Not covered. No assessment, risk tiering or control matching for use cases. Attack-path-based asset risk prioritisation is a different thing from use case risk rating.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Wiz ranks AI risks by attack path impact using vulnerabilities, identities, exposure, data and secrets, and recommends remediation such as enforcing model guardrails. This classifies technical risk on assets, not use cases, and does not select controls for a use case.
