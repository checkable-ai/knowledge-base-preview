## Answer

Not covered. No tamper-evident ledger of agent actions and no reasoning capture. Log ingestion in Wiz Defend falls short of a tamper-proof per-action record.

Research detail: Wiz Defend ingests AI invocation logs that record what is sent to and received from models, and the Claude integration ingests Claude Enterprise activity logs through Anthropic's Compliance API, which Wiz says helps audit reporting. Wiz consumes provider logs rather than keeping its own tamper-proof ledger, and agent reasoning is not captured.
