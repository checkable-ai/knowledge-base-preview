## Answer

Covered. AI components must be mapped into Services and ownership policies configured. Ownership quality depends on tags and Backstage or API-synced catalogue data.

Source statement: "Service Posture Policies give teams a way to define and enforce standards for service configuration and ownership." (https://www.wiz.io/blog/wiz-service-catalog-updates)

Research detail: The AI inventory groups AI components into Services that show ownership, attack surface mapping ties AI endpoints to owners, and the Green Agent suggests the best suited owner for an issue. Service Catalog ownership can be synced from Backstage or through the Wiz API. Ownership of decisions made by agents is not covered.
