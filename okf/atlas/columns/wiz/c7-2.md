## Answer

Not covered. No documented redaction of sensitive content in agent telemetry, linkage to sessions and people, or named response owner. Wiz Defend detections and Workflows fall short.

Research detail: Wiz Defend ingests cloud control plane logs, SaaS logs, AI invocation logs and sensor events, and detects AI threats including prompt injection, model exfiltration and MCP server attacks. Detections map to MITRE ATT&CK, the Blue Agent triages them with a verdict, and Wiz Workflows routes response with ownership context. Redaction of prompt content is not documented.
