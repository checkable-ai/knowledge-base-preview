## Answer

Not covered. No credential brokering, short-lived scoped tokens, rotation or revocation for agents, and no keeping secrets out of model context. Secret detection in AI-SPM and Wiz Code falls short.

Research detail: Wiz does not broker credentials to agents. It detects exposed secrets, for example OpenAI API keys in a vulnerable container or embedded credentials in AI code, and recommends rotation. That is detection of secret exposure, not scoped delivery or revocation.
