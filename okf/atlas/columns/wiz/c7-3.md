## Answer

Not covered. Model origin lineage, model signing and compliance evidence are not documented. Nearby features, model scanning for unsafe formats and the AI-BOM, cover inspection and inventory only.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Wiz detects self-hosted PyTorch and TensorFlow models on VMs and in buckets and flags unsafe formats such as pickle files that run commands or connect out. The 2026 AI risk post says Wiz scans files of hosted models, agents and MCP servers for malicious payloads, and the AI-BOM lists models, frameworks and libraries.
