## Answer

Not covered. Wiz publicly documents nothing for training agents on their tasks or for continuously checking how well agents do those tasks. The nearest feature is the Wiz Runtime Sensor, with Wiz Defend. Its runtime behavioural baselines tie process activity to specific AI agents, MCPs and models and flag security anomalies such as rogue agents and prompt injection attempts. That is security anomaly detection only. Wiz does not document the baselining method or which agent frameworks it supports, and no Wiz page says the feature meets part of the outcome.

Research detail: The Runtime Sensor attributes process activity on AI workloads to specific agents, MCPs and models and detects anomalies against a known baseline, including rogue agents. Wiz Defend applies behavioural analytics and flags suspicious model input such as prompt injection attempts from AI invocation logs. Task training and continuous quality validation are not covered.
