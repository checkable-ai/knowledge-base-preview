## Answer

Not covered. No gateway enforcing routing, quotas, cost or rate limits per agent, user or workload, and no prompt-injection screening at the model boundary. Chat middleware and Purview chat policy middleware inspect model calls but do not enforce quotas or routing.

Research detail: Chat middleware can inspect every model request, and Purview chat middleware evaluates the full prepared request and response against DLP policies on each model call. The SDK has no per-agent or per-user quota, cost or rate enforcement and says rate limiting is the developer's job.

## Products

## Gaps

No quota, cost cap, rate limit or prompt-injection screening at the model boundary.

## Options
