## Answer

Not covered. No write-once or integrity-validated store of each agent action by user, agent and session. The FIDES audit log covers policy events only, Purview Audit is a Microsoft 365 service rather than a product-level action ledger, and OpenTelemetry traces are ordinary telemetry with no tamper-evidence.

Research detail: FIDES records every blocked or approval-gated call in a per-session audit log. Purview middleware logs AI interactions to Purview Audit. OpenTelemetry spans capture agent, model and tool calls, with content only when sensitive data capture is enabled.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

FIDES audit log, Purview Audit, OpenTelemetry traces

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No tamper-evident ledger in the SDK. FIDES only logs policy events and is Python-only.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Export traces and audit records to immutable storage, or rely on Purview Audit retention.
