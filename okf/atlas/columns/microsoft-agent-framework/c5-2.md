## Answer

Not covered. No training of agents on tasks, continuous validation or anomaly detection. The evaluation framework runs on demand and observability is passive telemetry; continuous evaluation sits in the separate Foundry service.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: The evaluation framework runs local checks and Foundry LLM-as-judge evaluators covering task adherence, tool call accuracy and safety categories, with repetitions to detect non-deterministic behaviour and per-agent workflow breakdowns.
