## Answer

Not covered. No submission and approval workflow. Model registry registration and catalog source toggles are not approval.

Research detail: Users with registry access can register models and versions. Administrators enable or disable catalog sources and allow or disallow models, and a Developer Preview lets administrators edit MCP catalog entries.
