## Answer

Not covered. No gateway filtering what each agent may retrieve. NeMo Guardrails PII and secret detection and OGX ABAC are nearby but are not a retrieval control point.

Research detail: NeMo Guardrails detects personal information with Presidio and secrets or keywords with regex rails on input and output. OGX filters stored resources by tenant and owner. Feature Store instances have role-based access control and OIDC sign-in.
