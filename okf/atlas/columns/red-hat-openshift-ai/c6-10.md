## Answer

Not covered. No unified retention, deletion and residency rules across inputs, outputs and memory. MLflow trace retention and conversation deletion cover parts only.

Research detail: MLflow trace archival sets retention per server and per experiment. OGX conversations can be deleted. The platform runs in the customer cluster, including disconnected installs, so residency follows cluster placement, and usage data collection can be turned off.

## Products

MLflow trace archival, OGX Conversations API, usage data collection settings

## Gaps
