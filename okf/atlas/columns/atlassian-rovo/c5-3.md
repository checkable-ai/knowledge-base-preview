## Answer

Not covered. No per-agent cost accounting across model use, infrastructure and staff time, and no measure of value or return delivered. Rovo credit usage tracking in Atlassian Administration, with a CSV export including agent, shows consumption only and is not a cost-versus-return view.

Last checked 11 November 2026.

Research detail: Rovo trends shows users with AI enabled, active users, interactions, active agents, agent runs and agents created, with CSV export. Credit consumption is under Platform usage, and Rovo Dev has credit allowances and caps.
