## Answer

Not covered. Atlassian-hosted LLMs option (Cloud Enterprise, by request) keeps data in the cloud boundary, but no per-agent model version or endpoint is exposed.

Research detail: Rovo routes dynamically across Atlassian-hosted open models and OpenAI, Anthropic and Google models under zero data retention agreements. Cloud Enterprise customers can request Atlassian-hosted LLMs only, and Rovo Dev CLI takes a modelId setting.

## Products

## Gaps

No per-request model version or endpoint is exposed to customers. The hosted-only option has no self-serve control and costs more credits.
