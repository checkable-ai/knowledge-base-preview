## Answer

Not covered. Retention is fixed at 30 days and not customer-configurable. Residency applies to some data and Rovo Dev lacks data residency.

Research detail: Chat and agent data is kept 30 days, LLM providers have zero retention, connector data is deleted within 30 days of disconnect, and chat logs, agent configuration and ingested content can be pinned by data residency.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Rovo data handling, data residency, Atlassian-hosted LLMs

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Retention is not configurable in the docs. Rovo Dev does not support data residency.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable residency and hosted-only LLMs where needed.
