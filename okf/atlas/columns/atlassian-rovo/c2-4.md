## Answer

Not covered. Retention is fixed at 30 days, and no documented admin control over session contents access or resumption. Live conversation review is the nearby feature.

Research detail: Chat and agent inputs and outputs are kept for 30 days. Agent creators and managers can read live conversation transcripts once an org or Studio admin enables review, and Rovo Dev stores sessions in a configurable directory.
