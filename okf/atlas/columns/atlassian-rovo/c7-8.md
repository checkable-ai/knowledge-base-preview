## Answer

Not covered. No sanitisation or cleaning of ingested data; permission sync and blocklists fall short.

Research detail: Rovo indexes connected third-party workspaces with permission sync, supports blocklists for some connectors, deletes indexed content within 30 days of disconnect, and can pin ingested third-party content to a residency region.

## Products

Teamwork Graph connectors, Rovo data residency

## Gaps

No sanitisation of ingested content against prompt injection documented. Indexed data cannot be purged early per the docs.

## Options
