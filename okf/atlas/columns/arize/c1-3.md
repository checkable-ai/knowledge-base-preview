## Answer

Not covered. No approval gate for actions run by customers' agents. PXI approval cards and diffs cover only Arize's own Phoenix assistant, a Bypass Approval setting applies edits without asking, and refusal and repeat-attempt handling are not documented.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Arize offers no approval step for actions proposed by customer agents. Its own assistant Alyx presents proposed changes to Arize assets for review before applying them, with optional Auto Accept per change type, but this governs Alyx only.
