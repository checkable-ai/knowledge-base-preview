## Answer

Covered. Customer must configure monitors and online evaluators. Automatic thresholds use historical data and a sensitivity setting.

Source statement: "Arize sets a threshold based on your historical data." (https://arize.com/docs/ax/observe/production-monitoring.md)

Research detail: AX online evaluators score production spans, traces or sessions continuously with LLM-as-a-judge or code evaluators, with sampling, rate limiting and retries. Tracing project monitors alert on eval labels, latency or custom metrics using automatic thresholds derived from history.
