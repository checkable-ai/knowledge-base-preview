## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Monitors and online evaluation tasks must be configured on a live project. Prompt Learning optimises prompts, not model weights. Self-hosted Arize AX requires AX Enterprise. AX Enterprise has custom span and retention limits.

Source statement: "Monitors watch your metrics continuously and alert you when something crosses a threshold." (https://arize.com/docs/ax/observe/production-monitoring)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: AX online evaluators score production spans, traces or sessions continuously with LLM-as-a-judge or code evaluators, with sampling, rate limiting and retries. Tracing project monitors alert on eval labels, latency or custom metrics using automatic thresholds derived from history.
