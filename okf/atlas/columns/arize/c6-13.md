## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Certifications cover Arize AX SaaS, not self-hosted deployments. SOC2 reports are included with AX Enterprise.

Source statement: "Arize is independently audited so you can run Arize AX on production data" (https://arize.com/docs/ax/security-and-settings/compliance.md)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: The Arize AX docs list SOC 2 Type II, PCI DSS 4.0, HIPAA compliance and CSA STAR Level 1 for Arize. The Arize Trust Center also shows ISO/IEC 27001 certification by A-LIGN and GDPR compliance. Neither page states an IRAP assessment, ISO/IEC 42001 certification or a published penetration test attestation.
