## Answer

Covered. Applies to data stored in Arize (traces holding agent inputs and outputs). Phoenix has per-project retention policies and AX has configurable retention and deletion by time range or span ID. Residency via self-hosted AX in the customer VPC. Agent memory held outside Arize is not governed.

Source statement: "Arize AX automatically deletes data based on your configured retention policies." (https://arize.com/docs/ax/security-and-settings/compliance/delete-traces-with-sensitive-data.md)

Research detail: Phoenix keeps data indefinitely by default and supports per-project retention policies by days or trace count on a CRON schedule, plus manual purge and deletion of resources. AX deletes data under configured retention policies and supports deletion by time range or span ID. Self-hosted AX keeps data in the customer VPC.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Phoenix retention policies, AX deleteData API, self-hosted AX

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Governs Arize telemetry only, not agent inputs, outputs or memory held elsewhere. AX retention settings are not described in detail.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retention to match the data policy and redact before export.
