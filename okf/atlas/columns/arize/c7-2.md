## Answer

Not covered. No documented agent security detections, delivery of security telemetry to a security operations team, or named response owner. Monitors, notifications to email, Slack, PagerDuty and webhooks, and masking are general observability features.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Traces carry session IDs and span attributes, and client-side masking or Presidio-based processors can redact PII before export. Guard failures can be tracked as a metric and alerted to PagerDuty, Slack or email.
