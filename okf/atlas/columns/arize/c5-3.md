## Answer

Not covered. Cost tracking (AX track-costs, Phoenix cost tracking) covers token cost per span and trace only. No infrastructure cost, staff time or measures of what agents deliver. AX cost is not retroactive.

Research detail: AX and Phoenix compute token cost per LLM span from built-in or custom per-million-token rates and roll it up to trace level. Phoenix also aggregates by session, experiment and project. AX monitors and dashboards can track cost over time.

## Gaps
