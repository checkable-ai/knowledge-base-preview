## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Applies to Arize managed agents (Agent Studio) via agent presets in the ag CLI. Deleting a preset does not affect existing sessions, which must be retired separately. Managed agents require AX Enterprise.

Source statement: "Change one or more fields on a preset." (https://arize.com/docs/api-clients/ag/presets)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Arize does not create, change or retire agent identities or permissions. AX and Phoenix provision human users through SAML JIT, OAuth2 or LDAP role mapping for the Arize platform only.
