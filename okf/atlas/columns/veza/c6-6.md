## Answer

Covered. Applies to agents and non-human identities discovered through supported integrations.

Source statement: "Ownership can be assigned in bulk from the Agents view, or systematically using enrichment rules." (https://docs.veza.com/4yItIzMvkpAvMVFAamTf/features/ai-agent-security.md)

Research detail: Owners can be assigned to AI agents in bulk or by enrichment rules from an identity provider or HR system. Owners are notified when they are included in access reviews, and rules can flag agents deployed without an owner.
