## Answer

Not covered. No record of customers' agent actions (tool calls, data access, changes) per agent and session. The Audit Log records calls to Veza APIs and its tamper resistance is not documented. The Remediation Log is append-only but records only Veza remediation actions.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Veza's Audit Log records every call to Veza APIs by humans and machines, including reads, and is exportable. The Remediation Log records who triggered each action, what it targeted, when and the outcome, and logged entries cannot be modified by an operator.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Audit Log, Remediation Log

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

These record activity in the Veza tenant and its remediation actions. They are not a ledger of customers' agent inputs, outputs or reasoning. Tamper-proofing of the Audit Log is not documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Collect agent action logs from the agent platforms and keep them with the Veza logs.
