## Answer

Not covered. No sandboxing of files, processes or network, and no direct stop of a running agent. Remediation can disable or suspend accounts at an identity provider and revoke direct grants, and NHI disable is Early Access and limited to Active Directory service accounts, but these are not documented as an agent emergency stop.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Veza can revoke an agent's access through the identity layer. Remediation actions disable or suspend accounts, revoke direct access and remove users from groups at an identity provider, and each action is logged. NHI Security can disable Active Directory service accounts in bulk with guardrails.
