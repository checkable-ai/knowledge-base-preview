## Answer

Partly covered. Veza does not issue the agent identity. It records agents discovered on hosting platforms as non-human identities and lets owners be assigned. Linkage to where the agent runs and to a specific release is not documented.

Source statement: "owners can be assigned to agents for governance accountability" (https://docs.veza.com/4yItIzMvkpAvMVFAamTf/features/ai-agent-security/supported-entities.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Veza discovers AI agents on named platforms and records them as identities in the Access Graph with their models, access paths and an assigned owner. It does not issue or manage the agent identity itself. The identity comes from the platform that hosts the agent.
