## Answer

Not covered. Rules and Alerts and Activity Monitoring detect changes in permissions and configuration, and dormant or overprovisioned access on a few platforms. They do not validate agent behaviour or detect task drift.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The rules engine alerts when authorisation conditions change. Suggested AI rules include agents with no owner, agents using legacy models and agents with write access to production databases. Activity Monitoring flags dormant and overprovisioned access on a few platforms.
