## Answer

Covered. Agent coverage requires at least one supported integration. The MCP Servers view is enabled for a tenant by Veza Support.

Source statement: "Veza traces these multi-hop paths to show the agent's effective permissions across downstream resources." (https://docs.veza.com/4yItIzMvkpAvMVFAamTf/features/ai-agent-security)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Veza traces an agent's multi-hop access, such as an assumed IAM role reaching storage and databases, to show effective permissions on connected systems. Remediation can then revoke direct access, remove users from groups or disable accounts at the identity provider, and access reviews can certify agent permissions.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Agent Security, Remediation, Access Reviews

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Veza finds and reduces excess permission but does not sit in the call path to enforce scopes at runtime. Remediation actions cover listed identity providers and the NHI disable feature covers Active Directory service accounts.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use Veza findings to cut agent roles and enforce scopes at a gateway.
