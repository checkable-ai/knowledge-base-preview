## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Needs at least one supported platform integration to be configured. The MCP Servers card is Early Access and Veza Support enables it for the tenant. Graph Investigation is described as a read-only preview.

Source statement: "Veza traces these multi-hop paths to show the agent's effective permissions across downstream resources." (https://docs.veza.com/4yItIzMvkpAvMVFAamTf/features/ai-agent-security.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: AI Agent Security gives a readable view of what an agent can reach. Prebuilt graph cards show an agent's resources, the users who can use it, its models and its MCP servers, and access path analysis shows blast radius through assumed roles and service principals.
