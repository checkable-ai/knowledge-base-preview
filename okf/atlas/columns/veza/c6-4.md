## Answer

Not covered. No authoring, ownership, versioning, publishing or review of AI policies. Access request and lifecycle policies are identity governance policies.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Access Request Policies define approval workflows and access duration, and Lifecycle Management policies define rules and actions for managing identities.
