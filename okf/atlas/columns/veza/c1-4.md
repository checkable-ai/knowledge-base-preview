## Answer

Not covered. Lifecycle Management policies automate onboarding, role changes and offboarding for identities. Creating, changing configuration of or retiring an agent is not documented. Veza discovers agents and assigns owners only.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Lifecycle Management policies automate onboarding, role changes and offboarding for identities, and Access Request Policies set time limits on granted access. AI agents are discovered and given owners, and permissions can be reduced through remediation.
