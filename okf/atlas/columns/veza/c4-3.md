## Answer

Not covered. Veza models ambient and delegated agent permissions in the Access Graph for analysis, but does not make called systems see the person or enforce only their permissions. Identity propagation is done by the hosting platform.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Veza models two access patterns for an AI agent. Ambient permissions belong to the agent itself and delegated permissions are inherited from the invoking user. Both appear in the graph so a reviewer can ask what a user can reach through an agent that they cannot reach directly.
