## Answer

Partly covered. Veza does not clean, sanitise or assess the quality of the data. It governs and shows access to data stores only.

Source statement: "Veza traces these multi-hop paths to show the agent's effective permissions across downstream resources." (https://docs.veza.com/4yItIzMvkpAvMVFAamTf/features/ai-agent-security)

Research detail: Veza discovers data stores that agents use, such as OpenAI vector stores and Snowflake Cortex Search services, and shows who and what can access them in the graph. Access reviews and alerts can then cover that access.
