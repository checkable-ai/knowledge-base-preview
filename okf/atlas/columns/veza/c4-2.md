## Answer

Not covered. No issuing, short-lived scoping, rotation or revocation of agent credentials, and no protection of secrets from the model. NHI Secrets only reports last-rotated and last-used metadata and lets queries find stale or unrotated secrets, which is visibility, not credential handling.

Research detail: NHI Secrets discovers tokens, keys, passwords and certificates used by non-human identities and reports metadata such as last rotation and last use. Queries can find credentials unused for 90 days or not rotated to policy. Veza does not deliver, rotate or hide credentials for agents.

## Gaps

Visibility and hygiene only. No scoped credential delivery or protection from model context is documented. Rotation tracking applies to listed secret types only.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use a secrets broker for delivery and rotation and use Veza to find stale or unowned credentials.
