## Answer

Not covered. Prompts are versioned with rollback, but no approval workflow spans tools, permissions or knowledge.

Research detail: Prompts are versioned as commits with tags, promoted to Staging and Production with an ordered history and rollback, and owners-only mode limits who can promote. Assistant edits create versions that can be promoted or rolled back.

## Gaps
