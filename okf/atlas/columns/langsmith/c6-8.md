## Answer

Not covered. Prompt versioning, environments and rollback exist, but there is no approval workflow and no versioning of models, tools, permissions or knowledge.

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Prompts are versioned as commits with tags, promoted to Staging and Production with an ordered history and rollback, and owners-only mode limits who can promote. Assistant edits create versions that can be promoted or rolled back.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Prompt Hub, assistants versioning, deployment revisions

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No approval workflow for changes to tools, permissions or knowledge. Audit logs record admin changes after the fact.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use prompt commit webhooks to trigger CI review and gate production tags with owners-only mode.
