## Answer

Not covered. Audit logs cover administrative actions only, with no integrity validation or immutable storage. Traces are deletable and not described as tamper-evident.

Research detail: Enterprise audit logs record administrative and configuration actions with timestamp, actor, operation, resources and outcome, described as tamper-resistant and kept up to 400 days. The LLM Gateway logs each proxied call with caller identity and matched policy IDs. Traces separately hold agent inputs, outputs and tool calls.
