## Answer

Covered, with conditions. At most 100 runs or threads can be added to an annotation queue in one action. Review is after the fact and does not gate delivery. The default annotation layout and Classic Trace View selector require LangSmith Cloud or self-hosted LangSmith v0.18 or later. earlier self-hosted versions use the classic layout.

Source statement: "Annotation queues give human reviewers a focused workflow for attaching feedback to specific runs or threads." (https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/annotation-queues)

Research detail: Annotation queues give reviewers a workflow to attach rubric feedback to runs or threads, with reviewer assignment, reservations and pairwise comparison queues.
