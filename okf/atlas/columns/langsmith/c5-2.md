## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Engine must be turned on per tracing project or agent environment. Usage is billed in LangChain Standard Units (LSUs) with an org-wide spend cap. Fix validation and Red Teaming are beta and LangSmith Cloud only. Self-hosted LangSmith and BYOC require Enterprise. BYOC deployments use their own model providers.

Source statement: "Scan your tracing projects on a schedule to surface, prioritize, and diagnose recurring issues" (https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/engine)

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Online evaluators score production runs with LLM-as-judge or code, with filters and sampling. Alerts fire on error rate, latency, feedback scores and cost. Insights clusters traces into behaviour patterns and Engine surfaces recurring issues from traces and feedback.

## Products

Online evaluators, alerts, Insights, Engine

## Gaps

Detection quality depends on evaluator design. Alerts are threshold based over 5 or 15 minute windows.

## Options
