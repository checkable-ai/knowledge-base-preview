## Answer

Covered, with conditions. TTL must be configured. Threads and store items do not expire by default. Store TTL applies only to items created after it is deployed. The delete strategy does not apply retroactively to existing threads. Data location follows the chosen regional hosting, as an organisation cannot span regions. Custom workspace-level extended trace retention requires Enterprise. Self-hosted Enterprise deployments are not subject to that SaaS cap.

Source statement: "If omitted, threads do not expire by default." (https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/configure-ttl)

Research detail: Trace retention is set per workspace or project with base (14 days) or extended (up to 180 days) tiers, and traces and examples can be deleted through the API. Thread checkpoints and memory store items have TTL policies. Data can be hosted in a regional instance, and SDK masking limits what is stored.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Data retention, trace deletes, TTL, regions, masking

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Trace deletes run as a weekend batch with no confirmation. An organisation cannot have workspaces in different regions.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retention per project, configure TTLs and verify deletes by re-querying.
