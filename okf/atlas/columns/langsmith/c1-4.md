## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Applies to Fleet agents. Custom models are available for enterprise deployments. Self-hosted Fleet RBAC requires LangSmith 0.17.44 or a later v0.17 patch and the FLEET_RBAC_PERMISSIONS_ENABLED deployment flag.

Source statement: "Agents can be private to the creator, shared with specific people, or shared with your entire LangSmith workspace." (https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/fleet/manage-agent-settings)

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Agents are created through four routes and Fleet agents can be shared, paused or deleted. Service keys are scoped to workspaces or the organisation with an expiry and, on Enterprise, a workspace role. Assistant configurations are versioned.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

LangSmith agents, Fleet, service keys, assistants

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No approval step for creating an agent or granting it permissions. SCIM provisioning covers human users, not agents.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Gate agent creation through CI with Terraform or the API and review service key roles.
