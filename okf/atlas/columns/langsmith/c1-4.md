## Answer

Covered. Fleet agents; no separate approval workflow for creation or permission grants.

Source statement: "will permanently delete the agent, all threads linked to the agent, and unlink any attached channels." (https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith/fleet/manage-agent-settings)

Research detail: Agents are created through four routes and Fleet agents can be shared, paused or deleted. Service keys are scoped to workspaces or the organisation with an expiry and, on Enterprise, a workspace role. Assistant configurations are versioned.

## Products

LangSmith agents, Fleet, service keys, assistants

## Gaps

No approval step for creating an agent or granting it permissions. SCIM provisioning covers human users, not agents.

## Options
