## Answer

Not covered. The Platform Consumption Console shows credit usage and low-balance alerts (the cost side), but there is no documented report of what agents deliver, such as ROI, labour savings or staff-time value.

Research detail: The Platform Consumption Console shows agent usage, API requests and Flex Credit consumption, with notifications at 80, 90 and 100 percent of entitlement. ASOR provides session, skill interaction and unique user analytics, and the datasheet describes ROI tracking. Analytics cover 30 days and several reports apply to Workday-built agents only.
