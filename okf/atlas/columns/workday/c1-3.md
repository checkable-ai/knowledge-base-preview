## Answer

Not covered. Approval is feature-specific (Sana inbox, some agents); no admin-attachable approval bound to the exact action, and no denial or replay handling.

Research detail: Sana prompts users to review and confirm actions that need human-in-the-loop approval and keeps pending approvals in the Sana Inbox. Some agents add their own steps, for example Payroll Agent asks to Approve or Reject large exports, and Revenue Contract Agent output is routed through the Customer Contract Event business process. Binding of approval to the exact action and replay handling are not documented.

## Gaps

## Options
