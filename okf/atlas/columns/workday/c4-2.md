## Answer

Not covered. Access tokens are time-limited (60 minutes first-party, 4 hours external) and the private key sits in the credential store, but no automatic rotation is documented, third-party token expiry cannot be customised, external platforms hold their own secrets, and Workday cannot detect compromised credentials.

Research detail: ASOR generates the ASU OAuth client and key pair, stores the private key in the Credential Store and keeps only a reference ID. Access tokens last up to 60 minutes for first-party agents and 4 hours for third-party agents, refresh tokens 24 hours. Admins can generate a new client secret, and Workday says to disable the OAuth client if compromise is suspected.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent System of Record Credential Store, ASU OAuth clients, Credentials tab for external agents

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Third-party token expiry cannot be customised yet. External agent platforms hold the client secret or x509 key themselves. No documented automatic rotation, and Workday states it cannot detect compromised credentials.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Keep external agent secrets in the agent platform's own vault. Script periodic secret regeneration and test revocation by disabling the OAuth client.
