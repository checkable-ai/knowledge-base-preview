## Answer

Not covered. No short-lived scoped agent credentials, rotation or revocation. External secrets (Enterprise) store secrets in a vault and resolve only in credential fields, but this does not meet the outcome.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Credentials are stored by n8n and used by nodes at runtime. With External secrets (Enterprise) credential fields can reference values in AWS, Azure, GCP, HashiCorp Vault, 1Password or Infisical, and secrets resolve only in credential fields, not general expressions. Vaults can be scoped to a project.

## Gaps

## Options
