## Answer

Covered.

Source statement: "You can require human approval before an AI Agent executes a specific tool." (https://docs.n8n.io/build/integrate-ai/ai-examples/human-in-the-loop-for-tools)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Human review can be required before an AI Agent runs all or selected tools. The workflow pauses, the reviewer sees the tool name and parameters through n8n Chat, Slack, Teams, email or other channels, and approves or denies. On denial the tool does not run and the agent is told.
