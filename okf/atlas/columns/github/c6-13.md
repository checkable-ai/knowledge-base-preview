## Answer

Covered. SOC reports cover Copilot Business and Enterprise only, available via the GitHub Enterprise Trust Center. No IRAP assessment, ISO 42001, privacy impact material or penetration test attestation confirmed for Copilot; customer must address those if required.

Source statement: "We are pleased to announce that our most recent SOC reports (1, 2, and 3) are available now and include GitHub Enterprise Cloud for github.com with all new regions like the EU, as well as Copilot Business and Enterprise." (https://github.blog/changelog/2024-12-06-the-latest-github-and-github-copilot-soc-reports-are-now-available/)

Research detail: Organisation owners can download SOC 1 Type 2, SOC 2 Type 2, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, CSA STAR Level 2, CSA CAIQ, PCI DSS attestation and quarterly bug bounty reports from organisation settings. GitHub's December 2024 changelog states its SOC 1, 2 and 3 reports include Copilot Business and Enterprise for April to September 2024. GitHub states the Copilot model hosts are FedRAMP Moderate authorised and that Copilot itself is not yet authorised. GitHub's own pages do not state an IRAP assessment.

GitHub compliance reports in organisation settings, GitHub Copilot Trust Center

## Gaps

No IRAP assessment found. ISO/IEC 42001 is claimed by third-party summaries of the Trust Center but not confirmed on a page that rendered. The compliance reports page does not say whether Copilot is in scope of each report. No privacy impact material or agency authority-to-operate support found for Australia.

## Options
