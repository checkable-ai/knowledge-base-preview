## Answer

Not covered. View effective role shows per-member capabilities and connectors (custom-role members only), not a map of what each agent can reach downstream.

Research detail: On Enterprise, View effective role shows each member's capabilities, admin permissions and connectors with the level granted and the granting role. Usage analytics count connector users and read and write actions. The plugin and skill Inventory lists capabilities and audience.
