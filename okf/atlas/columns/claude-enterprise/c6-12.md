## Answer

Not covered. Approval, blocking and removal of connectors and extensions are documented, but there is no scheduled reassessment workflow.

Research detail: Owners choose which directory connectors, custom connectors and desktop extensions are available and can decline and block requested connectors. Enterprise can stop verified-domain services being connected to outside Claude accounts. Removing a connector or extension from the allowlist retires it.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Connector enablement and requests, desktop extension allowlist, verified-domain connector restriction

## Gaps
