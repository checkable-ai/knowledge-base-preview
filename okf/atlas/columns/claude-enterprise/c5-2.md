## Answer

Not covered. Injection screening of untrusted content is the nearest feature, but there is no customer-facing anomaly detection on agent or member behaviour and no task training for agents.

Research detail: Content classifiers scan untrusted content for prompt injection, and in auto mode each Cowork and Chrome action is screened for exfiltration or injection before it runs. Anthropic reports attack success under 0.08 per cent in its internal Chrome testing and runs ongoing red teaming. Admins can turn auto mode off.
