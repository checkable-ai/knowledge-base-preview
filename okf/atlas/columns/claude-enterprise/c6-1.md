## Answer

Not covered. The Plugins and skills Inventory and connectors list cover tools only; there is no register of models or agents.

Research detail: The Plugins and skills Inventory lists every skill and plugin the organisation governs, including user-created ones, with source, version, capabilities, audience and 30-day usage. Connectors are listed in Organization settings > Connectors. The Compliance API lists users, roles, groups and effective settings.
