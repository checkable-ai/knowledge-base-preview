## Answer

Partly covered. Look-alike or squatted names and compromised packages are not checked. MCP servers, and skills shared through connected MCP servers, are not scanned.

Source statement: "Claude reviews the contents, looks for signs of malicious behavior, and returns one of three results: pass, warn, or fail." (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15927065-get-started-with-skill-and-plugin-scanning)

Research detail: Third-party skills and plugins are scanned at upload for content built to misuse access, with pass, warn or fail results and fails blocked (Enterprise). Publishing review flags possible duplicates. The desktop extension allowlist limits installs to the sanctioned registry.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Skill and plugin scanning, publishing review duplicate detection, desktop extension allowlist

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Skills shared through MCP servers and the MCP servers themselves are not scanned. Squatting checks are limited to duplicate tags in review.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Allow connectors only from the directory or vetted internal servers.
