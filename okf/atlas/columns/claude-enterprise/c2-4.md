<!-- chunk: answer -->
## Answer

Covered. Team and Enterprise; owner must enable memory. Users manage their own memory; owners cannot view it. Not available in some surfaces (Managed Agents, cloud Claude Code). Retention configurable on Enterprise.

Source statement: "Claude saves memory as a set of individual topics as you chat, rather than summarizing conversations after they end." (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11817273-use-claude-s-chat-search-and-memory-to-build-on-previous-context)

Research detail: Chats and projects are stored per member with private, shared or organisation visibility, and owners control project sharing (by role on Enterprise). Enterprise owners set retention for chats and projects with a 30 day minimum. Cowork cloud sessions are saved to the member's account and can be resumed across devices.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Chats, projects, project sharing controls, custom data retention

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Retention does not apply to Claude Design, Claude Tag, Managed Agents or Claude Code cloud features. Local Cowork history sits on the device and cannot be centrally deleted.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retention to policy and restrict project sharing for sensitive groups.
