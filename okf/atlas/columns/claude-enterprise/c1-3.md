## Answer

Covered. Needs approval grants on Enterprise custom roles; Chrome and Cowork permission modes; approval is by the acting user and members may skip approvals in Chrome unless restricted.

Source statement: "Claude pauses and asks for approval before each action." (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12902446-claude-in-chrome-permissions-guide)

Research detail: Connector tools set to Needs approval require the member to confirm each call. In Cowork, the organisation setting that lets members always allow write-capable connector tools is off by default, and owners can remove Automatically approve mode. Claude in Chrome Manually approve mode asks Allow or Deny before each action. Cowork OpenTelemetry records each approve, reject or automatic decision.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Needs approval connector grants, Cowork permission settings, Claude in Chrome permission modes, Cowork OTel approval events

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Replay handling and cryptographic binding of an approval to the exact action are not documented. Members can choose Skip all approvals in Chrome unless restricted. Approval is by the acting user, not a separate approver.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Keep the Cowork always-allow setting off, turn off Automatically approve where needed and monitor approval events in the SIEM.
