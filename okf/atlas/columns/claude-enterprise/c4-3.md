## Answer

Covered. Team and Enterprise; per-user authentication connectors and Enterprise-managed auth. Shared-credential connectors are an exception.

Source statement: "Claude inherits each person's permissions from the connected service." (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities)

Research detail: Connectors run with each member's own authorisation, so Claude can only reach data that user can see in the source system. Enterprise-managed auth passes the member's IdP identity, groups and roles to the connector, and owners choose the scopes Claude can request. Generally available on Team and Enterprise.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Connectors with per-user authentication, Enterprise-managed auth

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Scopes and downstream access are governed by the IdP and connected service. Shared-credential connectors are an exception to per-user auth.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Prefer managed auth with narrow scopes per role and avoid shared-credential connectors.
