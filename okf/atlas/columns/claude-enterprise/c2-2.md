## Answer

Covered. Enterprise plan organisations; Primary Owners, Owners and custom roles with Identity & Access permission. Effort caps per model. US-only inference option on usage-based Enterprise at higher rates; no Australian region.

Source statement: "Model access settings are available for Enterprise plan organizations." (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15694740-manage-model-access-for-your-organization)

Research detail: Enterprise owners enable or disable each model for the organisation and per custom role, cap effort per model and set default models (Haiku cannot be disabled). US-only inference keeps inference in the US on usage-based Enterprise plans at 1.1x usage rates. Data is stored in the US and Team and Enterprise data is not used for training.

## Products

Model access settings, default model, US-only inference
