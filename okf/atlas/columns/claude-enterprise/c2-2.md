## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Model access settings are available for Enterprise plan organizations. Primary Owners, Owners and members whose custom role grants Identity & Access can manage them. US-only inference needs a usage-based Enterprise plan and bills at 1.1x standard API rates for Claude Opus 4.6, Claude Sonnet 4.6 and later models. With US-only inference off, inference may be routed to other regions. Claude Code execution can be local, Anthropic-hosted or routed to a self-hosted environment.

Source statement: "Model access settings are available for Enterprise plan organizations." (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15694740-manage-model-access-for-your-organization)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Enterprise owners enable or disable each model for the organisation and per custom role, cap effort per model and set default models (Haiku cannot be disabled). US-only inference keeps inference in the US on usage-based Enterprise plans at 1.1x usage rates. Data is stored in the US and Team and Enterprise data is not used for training.
