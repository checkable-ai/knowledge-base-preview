## Answer

Covered. Org-wide action limits for Owners on Team and Enterprise; per-tool role grants Enterprise only. Users cannot override. Does not govern connectors run locally.

Source statement: "Owners on Team and Enterprise plans can limit which actions a connected service can take across your organization." (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11176164-use-connectors-to-extend-claude-s-capabilities)

Research detail: Owners on Team and Enterprise can restrict connector actions organisation-wide, for example read but not write. On Enterprise, custom roles set each connector or each tool to Always allow, Needs approval or Blocked, and the organisation-wide tool policy is a ceiling roles cannot widen. The same policy also applies to Claude Code once connector permissions are enabled.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Connector tool permissions, custom role Connectors tab, organisation-wide tool policy

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Per-tool role grants are Enterprise only. Grants are additive across roles, so one role cannot remove access granted by another. New roles default to Needs approval, existing roles were seeded with Always allow.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set a restrictive organisation-wide tool policy first, then grant per role. Check effective roles after changes.
