## Answer

Not covered. No workflow covering models, agents or use cases from proposal through testing, production, change and retirement. The skills library review, versioning and removal workflow covers skills only.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: For skills and plugins, the lifecycle runs from submission and review through publishing, versioned updates that are re-reviewed, and retirement by setting a plugin to Not available. Version history is kept per item.
