## Answer

Not covered. No workflow from proposal through testing, production, change and retirement for models, agents or use cases; model toggles and skill/plugin review cover only parts.

Research detail: For skills and plugins, the lifecycle runs from submission and review through publishing, versioned updates that are re-reviewed, and retirement by setting a plugin to Not available. Version history is kept per item.
