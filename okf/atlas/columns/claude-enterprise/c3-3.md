## Answer

Covered. Inference hooks Enterprise only; deny only with no redaction; image-only content not inspected. Tenant Restrictions and IP allowlisting on Enterprise.

Source statement: "Forward the transcript to your DLP scanner and deny prompts that carry regulated or classified material." (https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/inference-hooks)

Research detail: Inference hooks (Enterprise) let a DLP scanner deny prompts carrying regulated material before inference, with extracted attachment text included. Tenant Restrictions block personal or other organisations' Claude accounts on the corporate network, IP allowlisting (Enterprise, set up through Anthropic support) limits access to approved networks, and verified-domain connector restriction stops work accounts being connected to personal Claude accounts.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Inference hooks, Tenant Restrictions, verified-domain connector restriction

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Hooks see extracted text only, so image-only content is not inspected, and verdicts are allow or deny with no redaction. The connector restriction does not stop deliberate data movement.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Connect hooks to the existing DLP engine and enable Tenant Restrictions on the proxy.
