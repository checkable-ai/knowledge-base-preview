## Answer

Not covered. Workflow ordering, sub-agent handoff and execution limits exist, but retry, cancellation and recovery of steps are not documented beyond A2A task cancel.

Research detail: The agent builder supports workflow and auto mode agents, sub-agents with separate memory, external A2A sub-agents and schedule or event triggers. Scheduled runs have a limit of about 30 minutes and a hard cap on tool calls per run.
