## Answer

Not covered. No single control point that every model call passes through. Model exclusion and Auto routing control model availability, and usage limits cap consumption, but there are no per-agent or per-user routing, rate or quota policies.

Research detail: Admins can exclude models, block them for Assistant or Agents, and restrict models to departments or IdP groups. Protect+ AI security screens prompts for injection and jailbreaks. Agent runs consume FlexCredits.
