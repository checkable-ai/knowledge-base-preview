## Answer

Covered. Retention is set organisation-wide by admins (Off, 30 days, 90 days, 6 months or 1 year).

Source statement: "Glean automatically and permanently deletes chats that go longer than the selected retention period without an update." (https://docs.glean.com/administration/assistant/configuration/chat-history)

Research detail: Each agent step's output is stored for the run, and builders choose whether a step sees the previous step, all steps or none. Conversations and agent runs are saved under an org retention setting and users manage their own chats. Sandbox state may be kept only for pause and resume.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent step memory, chat history

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

With history off, memory is still kept for up to 2 hours for follow-up tools. Per-user scoping of run memory is not stated beyond chat ownership.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set chat history retention to policy and check behaviour with history off.
