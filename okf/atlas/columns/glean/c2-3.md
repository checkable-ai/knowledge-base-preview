## Answer

Covered. Egress allowlist is not customer controlled.

Source statement: "Each sandbox is dedicated to a single customer environment and a single session." (https://docs.glean.com/security/agent-sandbox-ptc)

Research detail: The Agent Sandbox serves one customer environment and one session, cannot reach other sandboxes, and is ephemeral. Outbound internet is off by default with an allowlist of approved domains, ingress is limited to authenticated internal systems, and the sandbox has no IAM credentials or secrets.
