## Answer

Not covered. No registration and approval flow for agents or models, and custom MCPs have no documented review step. Nearby features: Device Trust app accept and reject states, SaaS Manager approvals.

Research detail: Device Trust lets admins mark each discovered AI app Discovered, Accepted, Rejected or Ignored. SaaS Manager approvals can require up to two approvers before an app account is provisioned.

## Gaps
