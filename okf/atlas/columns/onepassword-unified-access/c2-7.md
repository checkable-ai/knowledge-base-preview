## Answer

Not covered. Sandbox, process and network egress policy belong to NVIDIA OpenShell, not 1Password. The sandbox keeps running after credential expiry, and no control to stop a running agent is documented. Nearby feature: time-windowed credential cutoff.

Research detail: 1Password covers the credential side of containment. With NVIDIA OpenShell the agent only holds placeholders, and the gateway stops resolving them when the time window expires or the Environment is removed. Service accounts take an optional expiry and are revoked and replaced, and Privileged Access says access is revoked when a session ends or risk changes, or before an action that drifts from stated intent.

## Products

Environments with NVIDIA OpenShell, service accounts, Privileged Access (Apono Agentic Gateway, intent-based access control)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The sandbox, process policy and network egress policy belong to NVIDIA OpenShell, not 1Password. The docs say the sandbox keeps running after expiry and do not say whether service account revocation is immediate. No control to stop a running agent is described.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pair the credential controls with a sandbox and egress policy, and time how long a revoked service account token or removed Environment takes to fail.
