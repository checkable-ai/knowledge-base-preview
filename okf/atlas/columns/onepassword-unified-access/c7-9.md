## Answer

Covered. Device Trust AI Insights requires Device Trust Connect or Unified Access Pro; SaaS Manager also discovers shadow AI.

Source statement: "Find the AI apps Device Trust has discovered being used across your organization." (https://support.1password.com/device-trust-insights/)

Research detail: SaaS Manager discovers SaaS and AI tools from identity providers, finance systems and its browser extension, including shadow AI used in the browser. Device Trust AI Insights finds AI apps and MCP integrations on endpoints, lets admins mark apps accepted or rejected, and can block rejected apps.
