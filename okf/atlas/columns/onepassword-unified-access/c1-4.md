## Answer

Not covered. No documented edit, disable or retire actions for Apono agent identities and no approval step for creating them. Service accounts have expiry and revoke-and-replace only.

Research detail: Apono creates agent identities automatically on first gateway connection. Service accounts are created with fixed vault, Environment and permission scope and an optional expiry, and are revoked and replaced to change. Agent access is granted through access flows and removed at session end.
