## Answer

Not covered. No public documentation of write-once or integrity-validated storage for agent action records, or of an export of such a record to tamper-evident storage. The 1Password audit log and Apono AI Sessions record access grants and decisions, not each agent tool call, data access and change, and tamper resistance is not documented.

Research detail: The 1Password audit log keeps 12 months of sign-ins, item usage and admin actions including service accounts, and every Agentic Autofill grant is recorded in item usage history. Apono AI Sessions records each tool call with action category, policy decision and timestamp, plus access grants, tied to agent and user.
