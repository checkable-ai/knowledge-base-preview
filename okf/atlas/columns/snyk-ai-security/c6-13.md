## Answer

Not covered. Snyk's security page gives no formal certification; no IRAP, ISO 42001 or pen test attestation found; Evo scope in SOC 2 or ISO not stated.

Research detail: Snyk states an integrated ISO 27001 and ISO 27017 management system reviewed externally each year, and a SOC 2 Type II assessed by Schellman each year with the report available through the account team.
