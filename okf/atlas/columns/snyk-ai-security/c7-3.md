## Answer

Not covered. No documented inspection of model files for malicious payloads. AI-BOM base model and dataset data only where the Snyk Risk Database has a record.

Research detail: The AI-BOM scan and the snyk aibom CLI produce a CycloneDX 1.6 AI-BOM of models, datasets, agents, tools and MCP components. Where the Snyk Risk Database has a record, Evo adds base models and training datasets and gives license and model card metadata. Risk intelligence assigns each model a Model Risk Score from Snyk's adversarial testing.
