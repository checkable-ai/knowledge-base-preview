## Answer

Covered. Targets (LLM, OpenAI-compatible or REST endpoints) must be registered in the Netskope UI. Retrieval and runtime boundary testing are not described.

Source statement: "To utilize the AI Red Teaming service, you must create and register your AI targets in the Netskope UI." (https://docs.netskope.com/en/creating-a-target)

Research detail: AI Red Teaming sends adversarial prompts covering data leakage, prompt injection and jailbreaking, inappropriate content and 23 languages to registered targets, and reports attack success rate by technique and OWASP LLM category.
