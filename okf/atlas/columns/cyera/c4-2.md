## Answer

Not covered. Agentic Access Management documents ephemeral least-privilege identities, and Cyera Identity documents secret rotation, but no explicit revocation and no keeping of secrets out of model context.

Research detail: Agentic Access Management runs each allowed request on an ephemeral, least-privilege identity with no long-lived tokens or hard-coded secrets, decommissioned when the task ends. Cyera Identity rotates secrets across existing vaults on demand or on schedule without storing them, and flags expired secrets still in use. The pages do not say how credentials are kept out of model context.

## Products

Oasis Agentic Access Management, Cyera Identity secrets and vault governance, Oasis Safe Secret Rotation

## Gaps

Delivery path of the short-lived credential to the agent and its isolation from the model are undocumented. Revocation is described only as decommissioning.
