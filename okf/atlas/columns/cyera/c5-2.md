## Answer

Not covered. Agent Guardian baselines and Access Trail anomaly detection exist, but no task training or continuous validation of agent behaviour.

Research detail: Agent Guardian measures runtime decisions against a continuous behavioural baseline of what the agent was designed to do and flags drift from its intended purpose. Oasis Scout detects anomalous NHI behaviour, leaked credentials and account takeover, and Access Trail detects abnormal access patterns in near real time. Task training and continuous quality validation are not offered.
