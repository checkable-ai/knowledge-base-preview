## Answer

Not covered. Access Trail logs access events with one year retention, but documents no tamper-proofing and no capture of model outputs or reasoning.

Research detail: Agentic Access Management produces a per-session chain of custody covering prompt, intent, policy, identity, activity and expiration. Access Trail ties every access event to data and identity, human or AI, and retains detailed logs for one year. AI Firewall keeps an audit trail of blocked prompts. Tamper-proofing and capture of model outputs and reasoning are not described.
