## Answer

Not covered. SIEM and ticketing integrations exist, but agent event schema, session correlation and redaction in exports are not documented.

Research detail: Cyera ships its own integrations to Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, CrowdStrike Next-Gen SIEM, AWS Security Hub and Cribl, and to Jira, ServiceNow, Slack and Teams for routing. Events are correlated to identity and data sensitivity, and Oasis Scout and Agent Guardian raise detections. Which agent events reach the SIEM, and any redaction of exported data, are not documented.
