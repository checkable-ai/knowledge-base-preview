## Answer

Not covered. Cyera Identity assigns owners to identities including agents, but no owners are documented for models or tools.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Cyera Identity maps each identity, including AI agents, to an owner, suggests owners and keeps ownership current through attestation campaigns. Agent Guardian knows the owner of the data an agent reaches. Named owners for models and tools are not described.
