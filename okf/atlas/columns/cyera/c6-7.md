## Answer

Not covered. No process to assess each AI use case, assign a risk level and match it to required controls. Per-asset technical risk severity falls short because it rates assets, not use cases.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Risk is assigned per asset through severity labels driven by the sensitivity of data each agent can reach and by toxic combinations of access, autonomy and identity. Agent Guardian release notes describe risk assessment to show which agents create meaningful risk. Use case assessment and control selection are not described.
