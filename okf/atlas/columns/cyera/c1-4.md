## Answer

Covered. Cyera Identity; lifecycle covers agent identities and permissions, not agent platform configuration.

Source statement: "Manage every identity from creation to retirement" (https://www.cyera.com/platform/identity)

Research detail: Cyera Identity applies lifecycle policies from creation to retirement: identities are provisioned with an owner, justification and approval, re-certified as access drifts and decommissioned, with restore during a grace period. This covers agent identities and permissions. Provisioning or change control of agent configurations is not covered.
