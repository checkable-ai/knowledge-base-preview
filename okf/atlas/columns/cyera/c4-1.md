## Answer

Not covered. Cyera Identity inventories agents as non-human identities with owner and data reach, but documents no per-agent identity tied to where it runs or which release it is.

Research detail: Cyera Identity discovers AI agents alongside service accounts, keys and tokens as non-human identities and maps each to its users, applications, data reach and owner. Agentic Access Management binds each prompt to a unique identity and issues a short-lived identity per session. Neither page links an agent identity to a runtime or release version.
