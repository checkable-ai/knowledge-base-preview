## Answer

Last checked 12 November 2026. This platform is checked on demand, so this answer has not been rechecked since.

Oasis Context-Aware PAM Escalation applies time-bound, policy-driven privilege elevation, and the product view shows an admin approval step for a high-privilege database request.
