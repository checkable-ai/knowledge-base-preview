## Answer

Not covered. No documented on-behalf-of or token exchange so downstream systems see the person's own permissions. User approval and per-prompt identity binding fall short of this.

Research detail: Agentic Access Management records a chain of custody starting with the human (Human, Agent, Prompt, Intent, Policy, Identity, Actions, Results) and evaluates policy for a named user's agent. Cyera Identity requires plain-language user approval before granting agent access. The mechanism for propagating the user's identity or delegated authority to downstream services is not described.

## Gaps

No documented token exchange or on-behalf-of flow. Oasis names delegated OAuth tokens as a risk to govern rather than a feature it provides.

## Options
