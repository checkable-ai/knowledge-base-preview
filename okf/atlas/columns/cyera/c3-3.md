## Answer

Covered. Omni DLP blocking depends on underlying DLP vendor.

Source statement: "Define your data protection policies once and enforce them across DLP, browsers, copilots, and custom-built agents." (https://www.cyera.com/platform/ai-runtime-protection)

Research detail: AI Runtime Protection applies one data policy set across Omni DLP, Browser Shield and AI Firewall, using Cyera classification and identity context to alert on or block prompts carrying source code, PII, PHI or IP. Agent Guardian turns agent data flows into allow, redact or block decisions and redacts sensitive records before they reach unauthorised destinations. Omni DLP orchestrates existing DLP tools from Zscaler, Netskope, Palo Alto, Microsoft Purview and Proofpoint.
