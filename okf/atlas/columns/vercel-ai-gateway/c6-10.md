## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Zero data retention of prompts and responses is the default on Vercel's side and needs no action. Enforcing ZDR so only ZDR providers serve a request, per request or team-wide, requires a paid account: a Pro or Enterprise plan or purchased AI Gateway credits. Team-wide ZDR costs $0.10 per 1,000 requests. Per-request region pinning is on all plans. Team-wide region pinning is on Pro and Enterprise (not Pro trial) and costs $0.10 per 1,000 requests. Inference regions are us, eu, au and jp, and support varies by model. Regional hosting (receiving requests in-region) is in beta, covers the EU and US only, and costs 5% of inference cost on Pro and Enterprise. In Australia and Japan only inference can be pinned. BYOK keys are skipped under ZDR unless marked ZDR-compliant. Tools that AI Gateway or the provider runs, such as web search, fall outside ZDR and region pinning.

Source statement: "AI Gateway has a ZDR policy and does not retain the prompts or responses in your requests." (https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/zdr)

Research detail: Zero data retention is the default and deletes prompts and responses once requests complete. Teams or single requests can require ZDR providers or providers that do not train on prompts. Inference can be pinned to the US, EU, Australia or Japan and the request fails if the region cannot be honoured.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

ZDR, disallow prompt training, regional inference, regional hosting

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

BYOK keys sit outside the ZDR and prompt training filters unless marked, and tools that AI Gateway runs are an exception. In Australia only inference can be pinned and not request receipt. Agent memory is not addressed.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Turn on team-wide ZDR and region pinning, and test with the models you use.
