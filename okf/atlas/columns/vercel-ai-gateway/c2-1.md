## Answer

Not covered. No ordering, hand-off, cancellation or recovery of an agent's task steps. Model fallbacks retry a single model request on another model or provider and do not manage multi-step work.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The gateway tries fallback models and providers in order when a call fails, supports per-provider timeouts for fast failover, and records every routing attempt. It does not sequence workflows, delegate work or cancel runs.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Model fallbacks, provider routing, provider timeouts

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Retry and recovery are at the model request level. Workflow orchestration sits outside the product.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Keep orchestration in the agent framework and use fallbacks for provider outages.
