## Answer

Not covered. No authoring, ownership, versioning, publication or review of AI policies. Team settings, routing rules and virtual models enforce technical settings but are not written policy management.

Research detail: Team settings hold the technical policy: ZDR, prompt training, allowlists and routing rules, with an optional reason and description on each rule. Virtual models let a team share one approved configuration across apps and agents.
