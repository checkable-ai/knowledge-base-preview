## Answer

Not covered. No control over what an agent may do in connected systems such as tools or APIs. The model and provider allowlists and routing rules only restrict which models and providers a team can use, team-wide and not per agent. Budgets limit spend only.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Team owners can restrict which models and which providers may serve requests, and routing rules can deny named models. Budgets can be set per team, project, API key and member. These limit what a team can reach in model providers and nothing in the pages covers tools or other connected systems.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Model allowlist, provider allowlist, routing rules, budgets

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Allowlists are team-wide and not per agent or user. Allowlists need Pro or Enterprise and carry a surcharge of 0.10 dollars per 1,000 billed requests.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use a key per agent with a project budget and a team allowlist of approved models and providers.
