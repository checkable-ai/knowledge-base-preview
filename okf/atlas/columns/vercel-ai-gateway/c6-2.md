## Answer

Partly covered. Covers models and providers only. No submission workflow, recorded approver or evidence trail, and no registration or approval of agents or tools.

Source statement: "A common reason to enable this is governance: your team has approved a specific set of models, and you want to guarantee that AI Gateway never serves one you haven't reviewed." (https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/model-allowlist)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: With the model allowlist on, models that Vercel adds later are disabled until a team owner decides and saves, and the provider allowlist records providers the team has reviewed. This gives an owner approval step for models and providers.

## Products

Model allowlist, provider allowlist

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No submission workflow, recorded approver or evidence trail. Agents and tools are not covered. Allowlists need Pro or Enterprise.

## Options

Record each allowlist decision in a change ticket.
