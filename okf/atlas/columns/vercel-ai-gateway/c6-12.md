## Answer

Partly covered. No scheduled reassessment or retirement workflow for suppliers. Tools and external services other than model providers are not covered.

Source statement: "A common reason to enable this is compliance: your team has reviewed a specific set of providers, and you want to guarantee that AI Gateway never routes to one you haven't approved." (https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/security-and-compliance/provider-allowlist)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Team owners approve which providers and models may be used, and the headquarters filter limits routing to providers whose parent company is in chosen countries.

## Products

Provider allowlist, model allowlist, provider headquarters filter
