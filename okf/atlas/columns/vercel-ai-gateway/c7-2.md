## Answer

Not covered. Trace drains forward OpenTelemetry traces to any OTLP/HTTP endpoint, but Vercel presents them for latency, token usage and failover. No detections, no redaction controls, and no named response owner are documented.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: AI Gateway produces an OpenTelemetry trace per request and a trace drain forwards it to any OTLP/HTTP endpoint or marketplace integration. Audit log drains replace the older custom SIEM streaming.
