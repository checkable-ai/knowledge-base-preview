## Answer

Not covered. No write-once or integrity-validated storage, and no export to such storage. Request logs keep routing details for 30 days and hold no tool calls or data-access actions. Team audit logs (Enterprise, CSV for 90 days) list Vercel team activity, not AI Gateway agent actions. Prompts and responses are deleted after requests complete.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Request logs list every request with status, model, provider, usage, cost and the credential used. Vercel team audit logs track members' activity and can be exported as CSV for the last 90 days on Enterprise. Prompts and responses are deleted after requests complete by default, so content is not kept.

AI Gateway request logs, team audit logs

## Gaps
