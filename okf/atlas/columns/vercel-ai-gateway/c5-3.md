## Answer

Partly covered. Only model-use cost through the gateway is covered. Infrastructure cost, staff time and what agents deliver are not measured.

Source statement: "You can break down costs and token consumption by model, user, tag, provider, or credential type to understand exactly where your AI spend is going." (https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/observability-and-spend/custom-reporting)

Research detail: Each request records cost and the dashboard breaks spend down by model, user, tag and provider, with budgets and spend alerts. Tokens carry no markup and the service can be bought through AWS Marketplace private offers against cloud commitments. Labour cost, savings and output gains are not covered.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Request logs, Custom Reporting API, budgets, AWS Marketplace

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Cost covers gateway-billed tokens only. BYOK spend is metered separately and does not count toward budgets. No yield or profit measures.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Tag requests by feature and team and join cost to outcomes in a separate report.
