## Answer

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Routing rules apply team-wide and can rewrite one model to another or deny a model. Allowlists restrict models and providers, and budgets cap spend per team, project, API key or member. The gateway does not rate limit the paid tier and applies lower per-model limits on the free tier.
