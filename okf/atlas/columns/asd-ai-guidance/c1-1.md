## Answer

Covered. ISM September 2026 release. Local file and process scopes are not listed separately from tools, functions and permissions.

Source statement: "Agentic AI applications are restricted to the minimum set of tools, functions and permissions required for their intended purpose." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/main/ISM_catalog.yaml)

Research detail: ISM-2156 requires agentic AI applications to be restricted to the minimum set of tools, functions and permissions for their purpose. The Agentic AI harnesses advisory recommends an organisation-controlled hardened environment with only the tools and permissions the task needs, and restricted access to production systems and network services. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends prohibiting agents from modifying their own privileges.
