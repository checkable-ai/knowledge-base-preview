## Answer

Not covered. No ISM control or ASD page was verified that sets limits on an agent's files, processes and network access together with a way to cut its access at once. Least privilege (ISM-2156) is nearby but is not a sandbox or emergency stop.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends fail-safe defaults and containment mechanisms that limit blast radius, isolation of agents into enclaves, live monitoring with interruption during task execution, anomaly detection that triggers alerts or pauses, and trigger-action protocols that automatically restrict agent permissions. Deploying AI systems securely recommends sandboxed containers or VMs with allow-listed firewalls. ISM-2091 requires resource limits for AI models.
