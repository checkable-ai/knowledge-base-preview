## Answer

Not covered. ISM-2159 central logging is nearby, but no feed to security operations, redaction of sensitive data, detections or named response owner is documented.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: ISM-2159 requires central logging of agent tool calls and outputs for incident investigations, and the ISM security monitoring guidelines require centralised event logging. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends logging identity and privilege changes, runtime anomaly alerts, and developing and testing incident response procedures to contain agent compromise. The harness advisory asks for integration with incident response processes.
