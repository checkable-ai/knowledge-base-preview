## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites ISM-2159 and the harness advisory, but the pages could not be fetched. No ledger format is set.

Research detail: ISM-2159 requires all tool invocations, external requests and outputs of agentic AI applications to be centrally logged in enough detail for incident investigation. The harness advisory recommends recording prompts, responses, tool invocations, approvals, actions, security events and configuration changes, and protecting and independently monitoring the logs. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends isolating agents with no write access to logs and monitoring internal reasoning.
