## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites ISM-1570 and ISM-1793, but the pages could not be fetched. Retirement of a third-party service is not addressed.

Research detail: ISM-1570 requires outsourced cloud service providers to undergo an IRAP assessment against the latest ISM at least every 24 months, and ISM-1793 does the same for managed services. The ISM says an AI application or service may be a managed service, a cloud service, both or neither. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends regular third-party reviews of privileged architectures, and Engaging with artificial intelligence asks whether a provider retrains on customer inputs.
