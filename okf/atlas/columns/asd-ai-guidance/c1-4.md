## Answer

Covered. ISM September 2026 release. No ISM control requires explicit approval before an agent is created. Retirement relies on the register context and ISM-2146 revocation.

Source statement: "An AI agent register is developed, implemented, maintained and regularly verified." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/main/ISM_catalog.yaml)

Research detail: ISM-2134 requires an AI agent register to be developed, maintained and regularly verified, and ISM-2135 lists its contents including credentials, tools, permissions and data repositories. The ISM context says agent identities should be managed like service accounts and disabled or removed when no longer required. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends periodically reconciling the registry against live agents and logging identity and privilege changes.
