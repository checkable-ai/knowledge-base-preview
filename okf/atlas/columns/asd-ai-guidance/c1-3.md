## Answer

Covered. ISM September 2026 release, section 'Artificial intelligence applications'. Applies to systems assessed against the ISM. Handling of refusals and repeat attempts is not specified, and showing the exact action is control context guidance.

Source statement: "AI applications are configured to require human approval before executing sensitive or high-impact actions." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/main/ISM_catalog.yaml)

Research detail: ISM-2113 requires AI applications to require human approval before executing sensitive or high-impact actions, and its context says the application should show the proposed action, target, data disclosed and expected consequences, with dual approval considered for very consequential actions. Careful adoption of agentic AI services says designers or operators, not the agent, decide when approval is needed, and that requests to delete logs should be quarantined until approved. Replay handling is covered only through recommendations to sign authorised commands.
