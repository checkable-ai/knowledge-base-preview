## Answer

Covered. ISM September 2026 release. Per-agent retrieval policy comes from ISM-2157 rather than a control written for data stores.

Source statement: "Content filtering is implemented by AI applications to detect and block sensitive data exposure and improper output." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/main/ISM_catalog.yaml)

Research detail: ISM-2094 requires AI applications to implement content filtering to detect and block sensitive data exposure, and ISM-2093 requires role-based access controls to restrict AI application access to sensitive data. ISM-2112 disables direct external data access for AI applications handling classified data. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends DLP controls tuned to AI agent behaviours.
