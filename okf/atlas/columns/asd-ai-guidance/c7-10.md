## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites ISM-2118 and ISM-2119, but the pages could not be fetched.

Research detail: ISM-2118 requires vulnerability assessments and penetration tests before deployment, before significant changes and at least every six months, and ISM-2119 requires suitable AI models to augment them. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends red teaming in sandboxes, multi-agent red teaming and chaos testing, regular tests of an agent's ability to bypass guardrails and human approval, and penetration testing that targets agentic behaviours.
