## Answer

Not covered. ISM-2123 covers deletion of session prompts and outputs only. Nothing covers who can read session memory or how a session resumes.

Research detail: ISM-2123 requires all prompts and outputs of a chat session to be securely deleted when the session is removed from an AI application. The harness advisory says the memory and session store may preserve sensitive, stale or manipulated content and recommends deleting stale content and starting fresh sessions. Access control and resumption of sessions are not specified.
