## Answer

Covered. ISM September 2026 release. No protocol or token format for passing the user's identity is named.

Source statement: "Tools invoked by agentic AI applications are subject to both the access controls of the invoking user and agent-specific, task-scoped authorisation, with effective permissions limited to the minimum permitted by both." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/main/ISM_catalog.yaml)

Research detail: ISM-2157 requires tools invoked by agentic AI applications to be subject to both the invoking user's access controls and agent-specific task-scoped authorisation, with effective permissions limited to the minimum of both. The ISM-2133 context says agent identities let actions be told apart from those of the user on whose behalf the agent acts. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends prohibiting delegation without expiry timers and recorded grant chains.
