## Answer

Not covered. ISM-2141 to ISM-2143 and ISM-2146 cover short-lived, unshared, managed and revocable credentials. No control requires secrets to be kept out of the model's context, which the outcome states.

Research detail: ISM-2141 requires applications and workloads to use short-lived dynamically issued credentials in preference to static ones, ISM-2142 requires static credentials to be held in a secrets management solution, and ISM-2143 forbids sharing credentials across workloads or environments. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends just-in-time credentials for high-impact actions and ephemeral credentials that expire when the job is complete. Keeping secrets out of model context is not stated as a separate control.
