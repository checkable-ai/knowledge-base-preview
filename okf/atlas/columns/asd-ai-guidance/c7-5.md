## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites ISM-0402, ISM-1730 and ISM-2054, but the pages could not be fetched. MCP servers, CLIs and skills are not named in an ISM control.

Research detail: ISM-1730 requires a software bill of materials to be produced for consumers, ISM-2054 requires SBOMs of imported components to be checked for known vulnerabilities, and ISM-0402 requires SAST, DAST and SCA testing before each release. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends verifying third-party components come from trusted sources and restricting tools to an approved, regularly verified allow list. MCP servers and skills are named only as connectors.
