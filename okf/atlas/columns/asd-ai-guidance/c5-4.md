## Answer

Not covered. ISM-2089 monitors AI model performance and anomalies, and ISM-2091 sets resource limits. Neither requires monitoring of load or performance of the infrastructure agents run on.

Research detail: ISM-2089 requires AI model performance metrics to be monitored and anomalies investigated, and ISM-2091 requires resource limits. Careful adoption of agentic AI services says degraded or unusual performance can indicate compromise and recommends rate limits that interrupt long-running tasks. Load performance targets are not set.
