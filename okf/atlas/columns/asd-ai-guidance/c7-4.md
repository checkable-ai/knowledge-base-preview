## Answer

Research detail: Deploying AI systems securely recommends running ML models in hardened containers or VMs and isolating weight storage in a protected vault or HSM. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends cryptographic attestation that agents run expected, unmodified code and integrity checks for task definitions. The wider ISM hardening guidelines apply to the hosts.
