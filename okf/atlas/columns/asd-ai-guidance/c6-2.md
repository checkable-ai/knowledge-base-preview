## Answer

Not covered. No documented process for submitting, registering and approving a new model, agent or tool. The agent register (ISM-2134) is nearby but records agents rather than approving them.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: The ISM framework requires system owners to register each system with its authorising officer (ISM-1525) and to obtain an authorisation to operate. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends verifying third-party components before inclusion and only incorporating components whose risks the owner accepts. There is no approval workflow for individual agents, models or tools.
