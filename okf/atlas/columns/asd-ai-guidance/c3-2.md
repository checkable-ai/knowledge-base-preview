## Answer

Covered. ISM September 2026 release. Applies to systems assessed against the ISM. Schema enforcement and per-tool quotas are not ISM requirements. ISM-2158 and ISM-2159 add untrusted content handling and central logging.

Source statement: "Agentic AI applications are restricted to the minimum set of tools, functions and permissions required for their intended purpose." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/main/ISM_catalog.yaml)

Research detail: ISM-2156 and ISM-2157 restrict agentic AI applications to a minimum tool set under combined user and agent authorisation, ISM-2158 requires retrieved external content to be treated as untrusted and kept from overriding tool permissions or approval rules, and ISM-2159 requires all tool invocations and external requests to be centrally logged. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends an approved allow list of tools and versions, validation of tool responses and a centralised policy decision point for each request.
