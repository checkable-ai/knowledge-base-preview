## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites ISM-2103 and ISM-2123, but the pages could not be fetched. Residency is a consideration, not an ISM control.

Research detail: ISM-2103 forbids using organisational data from AI applications for training or fine-tuning without informed, explicit consent, and ISM-2123 requires prompts and outputs to be securely deleted with the chat session. Engaging with artificial intelligence asks organisations to confirm an AI system meets their data residency or sovereignty obligations. The ISM points to the National Archives of Australia for retention of records created with AI.
