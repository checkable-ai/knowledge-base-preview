## Answer

Not covered. ISM-2090 (rate limiting) and ISM-1924 (prompt evaluation) are separate controls. No control requires a single gateway for all model calls or per-agent, user or workload routing, quotas and cost limits.

Research detail: ISM-2090 requires rate limiting of inference queries and ISM-1924 requires generative AI applications to evaluate user prompts for adversarial inputs. The harness advisory recommends cost controls on APIs to detect runaway loops and denial-of-wallet attacks. Per-agent or per-user routing and quotas are not specified.
