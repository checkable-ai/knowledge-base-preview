## Answer

Research detail: The harness advisory says agent output and actions should be reviewed, tested and formally accepted before use in an operational setting, and that accountability stays with a person. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends validating outputs against multiple sources and prioritising human review of cases where guardrails trigger. ISM-2113 covers approval before high-impact actions.
