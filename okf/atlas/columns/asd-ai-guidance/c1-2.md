## Answer

Covered. ISM September 2026 release. No standard format for expressing remote scopes.

Source statement: "Access control policies are implemented to enforce fine-grained permissions for AI applications." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/main/ISM_catalog.yaml)

Research detail: ISM-2092 requires access control policies with fine-grained permissions for AI applications and ISM-2093 requires role-based access controls to restrict access to sensitive data. ISM-2157 limits tool permissions to the minimum of user and agent authorisation, and ISM-2112 disables direct access to external public data sources for AI applications processing classified data. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends a centralised policy decision point for each request.
