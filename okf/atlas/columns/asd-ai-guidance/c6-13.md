## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites the IRAP page, but it could not be fetched. SOC 2, ISO 27001 and ISO 42001 are outside ASD scope.

Research detail: The ISM framework has authorising officers grant an authorisation to operate, and requires IRAP assessments of cloud and managed services at least every 24 months. IRAP gives access to ASD-endorsed assessors for systems up to SECRET. ASD notes that IRAP assessors do not accredit systems, that assessments generally do not cover every ISM control, and that buyers should read the report to see what was tested.
