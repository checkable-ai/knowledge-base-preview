## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites ISM-2135 and GOV-08, but the pages could not be fetched. Owners for models and tools are not required.

Research detail: ISM-2135 requires an owner and business purpose for each AI agent, and principle GOV-08 makes the board or executive committee accountable for AI. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends defining legal accountability and risk ownership in policy and clearly assigning responsibility for errors caused by the system. The harness advisory says accountability for results stays with a person.
