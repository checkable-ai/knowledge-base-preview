## Answer

Not covered. No verified quote. Research cites ISM-2072, ISM-2084 and ISM-2086, but the pages could not be fetched.

Research detail: ISM-2086 requires the source and integrity of AI models, structures and weights to be verified, ISM-2072 requires models to be stored in a non-executable format, and ISM-2084 requires model cards and system cards. Deploying AI systems securely recommends digital signatures and checksums to confirm each artefact's origin. Careful adoption of agentic AI services points to SBOM guidance when procuring agentic AI systems.
