## Answer

Not covered. ISM-2133 gives each agent a unique identity and ISM-2135 ties it to an owner in the register. Nothing requires the identity to be bound to where the agent runs or to its release (model or agent version). Recording versions is only suggested in the register context.

Research detail: ISM-2133 requires each AI agent to have a unique identity distinct from personnel accounts and other agents. ISM-2135 requires the AI agent register to record each agent's identifier, owner, business purpose and assigned identities, and the register context suggests recording model versions to support change management. Careful adoption of agentic AI services recommends building each agent as a distinct principal with its own keys or certificates.
