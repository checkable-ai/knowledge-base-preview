## Answer

Covered. DeepSeek Harness only, developer preview. Agent Teams experimental. Hosted API is stateless.

Source statement: "Stop and release this activation and its owned descendants; rejects on teardown failure." (https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness/blob/master/docs/subsystems/subagent.md)

Research detail: DeepSeek Harness delegates work to subagents in their own context, can run them as background jobs collected or stopped with job tools, and offers send_message, interrupt_agent and list_agents controls. The tools/execute waterfall wraps timeout and retry, and resume closes interrupted turns from the persisted log after a crash.
