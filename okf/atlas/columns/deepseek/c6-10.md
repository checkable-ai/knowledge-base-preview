## Answer

Not covered. No region choice, configurable retention or self-service training opt-out. Data stored in PRC. Privacy policy deletion by request falls short.

Research detail: The privacy policy states personal data is stored in the People's Republic of China, kept while the account exists and longer for legal or business reasons, and that inputs may be used to train models with an opt-out requested by email. Chat history can be deleted in settings and deletion can be requested by email. API context caches expire within hours to days.
