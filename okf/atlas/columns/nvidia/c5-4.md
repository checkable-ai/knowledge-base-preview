## Answer

Covered. NIM Operator on Kubernetes. Anomaly detection is not built in, so alerting rules sit on the collected metrics.

Source statement: "You can collect and visualize latency and throughput metrics to optimize model performance at the NIM Operator level." (https://docs.nvidia.com/nim-operator/latest/index.html)

Research detail: NIM Operator collects latency and throughput metrics, creates service monitors and autoscales on DCGM or NIM metrics. The profiler reports latency by concurrency level and flags concurrency spikes.
