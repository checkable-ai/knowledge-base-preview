## Answer

Not covered. No documented rate limits, no internet egress control and no single gateway for tool calls. The nearby feature, NeMo Guardrails tool-calling rails, blocks undeclared tools and schema-invalid arguments and fails closed. However, it is opt-in and experimental on the IORails engine, does not execute tools, and does not limit or control internet access.

Research detail: Execution rails validate tool inputs and outputs. Guardrails middleware applies NeMo Guardrails at function boundaries. MCP include and exclude lists restrict allowed tools, and auth is configured per server.
