## Answer

Not covered. NGC model signing (OpenSSF Model Signing) and checksums give provenance and integrity, but there is no model file content inspection and no AI bill of materials for the model. The SBOM covers the container and needs an AI Enterprise entitlement.

Research detail: NGC models are signed with the OpenSSF Model Signing standard and can be verified with the model-signing package. NIM also validates checksums on download. Each NIM ships a machine-readable SBOM.
