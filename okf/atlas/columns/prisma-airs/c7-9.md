## Answer

Covered. Limited to onboarded cloud accounts and supported platforms (AWS Bedrock agents with configuration and runtime discovery, Azure AI Foundry agents and OpenAI assistants with configuration discovery only).

Source statement: "API results show all agents, models, knowledge bases, and tools divided into Protected and Unprotected lists." (https://docs.paloaltonetworks.com/prisma-airs/ai-inventory/agent-discovery)

Research detail: Agent Discovery finds Bedrock, Foundry and OpenAI Assistants agents and flags unprotected ones. AI traffic visibility maps AI traffic in onboarded cloud accounts. Cortex AISPM discovery inventories AI assets across AWS, Azure and GCP.

AI Agent Discovery, AI Inventory, AI Discovery with Cortex AISPM
