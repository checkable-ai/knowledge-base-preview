## Answer

Covered. AI Gateway, Americas region only. Requires a Prisma AIRS licence and Strata Cloud Manager access. The Hybrid data plane (customer-hosted Kubernetes) keeps payloads in the customer environment.

Source statement: "request and response payloads never leave your environment" (https://docs.paloaltonetworks.com/prisma-airs/ai-gateway/ai-gateway-overview)

Research detail: AI Gateway proxies requests to over 1,600 LLMs across 50+ providers through one endpoint, with routing configs for fallback, weighted load balancing, retries and conditional routing. Hybrid deployment keeps payloads in the customer's Kubernetes cluster. Palo Alto Networks does not run the models.
