## Answer

Not covered. Security Command Center AI Discovery inventories AI assets on Google Cloud but does not detect whether their use was approved. SaaS and endpoint shadow AI are not covered.

Research detail: AI Protection discovers AI assets from declared resources, inferred assets and Model-as-a-Service API calls, including endpoints, models, agents and MCP servers in Agent Registry. AI Discovery populates the AI inventory.
