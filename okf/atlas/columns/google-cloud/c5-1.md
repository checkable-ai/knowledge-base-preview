## Answer

Covered. Feedback service is preview and needs API or SDK integration.

Source statement: "The service stores user reactions and links them to the corresponding conversation event timelines." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/scale/feedback-service)

Research detail: The Feedback service collects end-user thumbs up or down, labels such as hallucination or tool_error, free text and optional transcript context, linked to a session and event, and shows it beside traces in the console. Evaluation results and failure clusters can be reviewed in the console.
