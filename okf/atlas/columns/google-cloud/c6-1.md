## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Supported workloads register automatically only in the governance project unless a management boundary is set. Other components and external endpoints are registered manually as Service resources. Standalone skills are Preview. Cross-cloud automatic registration is Limited Preview. Gemini model discovery through AI Discovery requires Premium or Enterprise. Enterprise is deprecated and shuts down on 21 May 2027.

Source statement: "By consolidating these components into a single registry, you can reduce fragmented tool access, isolated data, and redundant integrations across your AI deployments." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/agent-registry/overview)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Agent Registry catalogues agents, MCP servers with tool specs, endpoints and skills, registering supported runtimes automatically. Model Registry tracks custom model versions, and AI Protection maintains an AI inventory.
