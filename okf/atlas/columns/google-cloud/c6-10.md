## Answer

Covered. Applies to Memory Bank; ML processing location depends on the regional availability of the underlying model.

Source statement: "Set a time to live (TTL) on memories to ensure stale information is automatically deleted." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/scale/memory-bank)

Research detail: Memory Bank supports TTL, revision expiry and the gcp.resourceLocations constraint against cross-border access. Sessions can be deleted, sandboxes have TTLs, and data residency and CMEK apply to GA models.
