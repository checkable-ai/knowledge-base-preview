## Answer

Covered. Endpoints do not guarantee data residency unless multi-region endpoints are used. The global endpoint has separate quotas.

Source statement: "Endpoints don't guarantee data residency or in-region ML processing." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/resources/locations)

Research detail: Models are called by explicit versioned IDs, and the lifecycle tables list release and retirement dates with at least 45 days to migrate. Quotas are documented per project and region. Request-response logging to BigQuery is opt-in, prompt logging for partner and open models is off by default, and data residency, CMEK, VPC-SC and Access Transparency apply to GA models.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent Platform model serving, Model Garden, Provisioned Throughput

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Security controls are not supported for preview models. Quotas are per project, not per agent. The zero data retention page was not opened.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pin versioned model IDs and track retirement dates. Use regional endpoints where residency matters.
