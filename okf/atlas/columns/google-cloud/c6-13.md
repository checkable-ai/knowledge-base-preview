## Answer

Covered. Some resources require sign-in with a Google Cloud or Workspace account; confidential items are shared under agreement terms. IRAP scope lists older Vertex AI names.

Source statement: "Key resources include our latest ISO/IEC certificates, SOC reports, and self assessments." (https://cloud.google.com/security/compliance/compliance-reports-manager)

Research detail: Google states an independent assessor evaluated Google Cloud and Google Workspace against OFFICIAL and PROTECTED ISM controls and found them strongly aligned with PROTECTED requirements, with reports available through Compliance Reports Manager. The IRAP products in scope include Generative AI on Vertex AI, Vertex AI Platform, Gemini for Google Cloud and several Vertex AI components. Google Cloud, Workspace and the Gemini app are certified to ISO/IEC 42001:2023, with Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform and Generative AI on it in scope. SOC 2 Type II reports are issued quarterly and ISO/IEC 27001 covers the Agent Platform and Model Armor. The Australian Government Hosting Certification Framework list names Google Cloud Platform Australian Regions as a Strategic certified service.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Compliance Reports Manager, IRAP PROTECTED assessment, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001, SOC 2 Type II

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The IRAP page gives no assessment date and lists products under older Vertex AI names. Agent Runtime, Agent Gateway and Model Armor are not named in the IRAP scope list. No privacy impact material or penetration test attestation specific to Australian agencies was found on public pages.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Request the current IRAP report from Compliance Reports Manager and check its date, boundary and whether Agent Runtime and Model Armor are covered. Map the in-scope list to current Agent Platform product names before an authority-to-operate decision.
