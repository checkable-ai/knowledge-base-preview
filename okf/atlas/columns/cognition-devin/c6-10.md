## Answer

Not covered. No configurable retention periods.

Research detail: Data is kept for the customer relationship unless specified otherwise. Paid plans can opt out of training, enabling provider zero data retention, and Enterprise data is never trained on without written consent. Dedicated deployments keep data in the customer tenant, CMK covers session data and snapshots, and admins control memory and conversation sharing.

## Products

Data Controls opt-out, Customer Dedicated Deployment, Customer Managed Keys, memory and sharing controls

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No configurable retention periods. Feedback and interaction data are kept as long as Cognition decides.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Negotiate retention and deletion terms and choose dedicated or Enterprise Assured deployment for residency.
