## Answer

Not covered. Isolated VMs, single-tenant VPC and SOC 2 are documented, but no hardening or scanning of runtime images is described and no customer-verifiable attestation is offered.

Research detail: Each session runs in an isolated VM, dedicated deployments use a single-tenant VPC, and Enterprise Assured adds customer managed KMS keys for session data and snapshots. Cognition holds SOC 2 Type II and states FedRAMP High for its federal offering, with SOC 2 and pentest reports in the Trust Center.

## Products

## Gaps
