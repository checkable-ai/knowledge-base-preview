## Answer

Not covered. No organisational workflow to submit, register and approve new models, agents or tools. The plugin approval card only lets a user accept Devin's own proposed change into their personal scope. Organisation-scope installs are gated by the Manage plugins permission, which is access control, not an approval workflow.

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: PATs can require admin approval with requests lapsing after seven days. Plugins enter through admin-managed manifests and MCP servers through enforced registries.
