## Answer

Covered. Retry and recovery are left to the coordinator model.

Source statement: "Put child sessions to sleep or terminate them" (https://docs.devin.ai/work-with-devin/advanced-capabilities)

Research detail: A coordinator session launches managed child Devins with prompts, playbooks and ACU limits, messages them, is woken when they finish, and can sleep or terminate them. Automations add triggers, preflight checks, rate limits, concurrency queues and per-session ACU caps. CLI subagents can be cancelled, resumed and limited in nesting depth.

## Products

Managed Devins, Automations, CLI subagents, Sessions API

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Retry and recovery of failed child work is left to the coordinator model rather than a defined policy.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set ACU limits and queueing on automations and monitor child sessions through the sessions API.
