## Answer

Not covered. OIDC federation gives short-lived, audience-scoped tokens, but stored secrets have no rotation or expiry and the docs do not say secret values are withheld from model context. Per-provider blueprint setup is also needed.

Research detail: Secrets can be scoped to organisation, personal, repository or session, are encrypted at rest, and only admins can view or edit org secrets. Devin binds each secret to the commands that need it rather than exporting it to every shell. OIDC workload identity federation gives sessions short-lived, audience-scoped tokens for AWS, GCP, Vault, JFrog and custom services with nothing to rotate.

## Products

Secrets, repo-specific secrets, OIDC federation, PAT policy

## Gaps

Stored secrets are typed or set as environment variables inside the agent's VM, and the docs do not say values are withheld from model context. No rotation or expiry for stored secrets. OIDC needs blueprint setup per provider.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Prefer OIDC federation over stored secrets, give Devin dedicated low-privilege accounts, and test whether secret values appear in session transcripts.
