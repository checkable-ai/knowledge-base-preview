## Answer

Not covered. No documented per-session retention period or customer-set expiry.

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Sessions sleep when idle and resume on the next message, can be terminated through the API, and resolve their security profile again on each wake. View Sessions and Manage Sessions permissions control who can see or edit other users' sessions. Session data is kept for the length of the customer relationship.
