## Answer

Not covered. Knowledge Store selection and tags scope search, and Data Redaction covers logs and analytics, but there are no per-user retrieval permissions and no DLP on data sent to the model.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Knowledge use is set per AI Agent or job with a chosen Knowledge Store and source tags. Data Redaction detects PII by default and custom types for logs and analytics, and endpoint settings mask sensitive analytics and logging.
