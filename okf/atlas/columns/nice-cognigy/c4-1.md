## Answer

Not covered. AI Agents are named resources and Snapshots tie a release to Endpoints, but no owner field or workload identity is attached to an AI Agent, and nothing records where it runs.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: AI Agents are named resources in the AI Agent Management interface, created from scratch or hired from the Job Market, each with a persona, jobs and tools. Immutable Snapshots tie a set of AI Agents, Flows and LLM settings to a release.
