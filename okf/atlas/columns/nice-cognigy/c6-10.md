## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Contact Profile and conversation TTLs default to 30 days and are set through the Management UI or environment variables. Insights raw analytics expiry defaults to off until set. Endpoints can turn off profile, analytics and conversation collection. SaaS runs in regional clusters including Europe, US, UK and Australia.

Source statement: "The expiration of this data is controlled by environment variables or settings configured in the Management UI." (https://docs.cognigy.com/ai/agents/analyze/data-expiration)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: TTL settings control how long Contact Profiles, conversations and analytics are kept, with 30-day defaults for profiles and conversations. Endpoints can turn off profile, analytics and conversation collection and mask sensitive data. SaaS runs in regional clusters such as Europe.
