## Answer

Not covered. Snapshots give versioning and restore, and Audit Events record changes, but no approval gate before publishing or restoring a Snapshot.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Snapshots are immutable project versions used for production, backup and restore, with up to 10 per project. Changes to resources are recorded in Audit Events.
