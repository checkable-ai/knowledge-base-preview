## Answer

Not covered. Global LLMs and fallback give routing and failover, but no per-agent, user or workload quotas, cost or rate limits, and no prompt injection screening.

Research detail: Admins manage Global LLMs at organisation level and assign them to Projects, with per-model fallback. Endpoint input sanitisation removes harmful HTML from user input.
