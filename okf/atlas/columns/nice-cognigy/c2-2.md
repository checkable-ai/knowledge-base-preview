## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Customers connect their own LLM providers (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, Google, Mistral) with their own credentials and choose a standard or custom model by name. Fallback models can be in another region. Prompt data handling follows each provider's terms.

Source statement: "In Cognigy, you can add an LLM from the offered LLM providers as either a standard model or a custom model." (https://docs.cognigy.com/ai/agents/develop/gen-ai-and-llms/llms)

Research detail: Customers connect their own LLM providers, including OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, Google and Mistral, choosing a standard or custom model by name with their own credentials. Fallback models can sit in another region, and deprecated models are flagged in the interface.
