## Answer

Not covered. IAM target.model.id conditions and routing profiles restrict models and regions, but there is no gateway enforcing per-agent or per-user quotas, cost or rate limits, and guardrails are not applied by default to on-demand models.

Research detail: IAM policies with target.model.id conditions restrict a group's inference to approved models. Routing profiles restrict on-demand requests to selected regions. Guardrails detect prompt injection through ApplyGuardrails or in block or inform mode on dedicated endpoints and agent endpoints.

## Gaps
