## Answer

Covered. Retention is configured per project for responses and conversations.

Source statement: "Deleting a project deletes all associated artifacts (responses, conversations, files, and containers) created within that project." (https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/generative-ai/projects.htm)

Research detail: Inference inputs and outputs are not stored, fine-tuning data is deleted after training, and data is encrypted with optional customer keys. Projects set response and conversation retention, and deleting a project deletes its artifacts. Routing profiles keep requests in selected regions.
