## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Inference on a dedicated AI cluster stays on that cluster. On-demand Google Gemini and xAI Grok requests are sent to Google or xAI under Zero Data Retention agreements. Routing profiles can send requests to target regions that differ from the calling region. Fine-tuning is only in supported commercial (OC1) regions. Saving responses, conversations, files or memory is optional through an Enterprise AI Agents project.

Source statement: "Your prompts are processed by the model on your dedicated AI cluster and aren't sent to an external provider for inference." (https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/generative-ai/data-handling.htm)

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Models are called by explicit model ID in on-demand or dedicated mode, with dedicated AI cluster endpoints and private endpoints. Oracle states inputs and outputs are not stored and are not shared with third-party model providers. Models move through deprecated and retired states with notifications.

## Products

OCI Generative AI on-demand and dedicated AI clusters, private endpoints

## Gaps

On-demand throttling limits change dynamically and are not documented. Data handling for xAI-hosted models was not confirmed on the docs page.

## Options

Use dedicated clusters for fixed capacity and subscribe to retirement announcements.
