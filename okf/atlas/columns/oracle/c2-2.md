## Answer

Covered. Applies to OCI Generative AI inference; dedicated mode for dedicated endpoints.

Source statement: "Both input and output are not stored inside OCI Generative AI." (https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/generative-ai/data-handling.htm)

Research detail: Models are called by explicit model ID in on-demand or dedicated mode, with dedicated AI cluster endpoints and private endpoints. Oracle states inputs and outputs are not stored and are not shared with third-party model providers. Models move through deprecated and retired states with notifications.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

OCI Generative AI on-demand and dedicated AI clusters, private endpoints

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

On-demand throttling limits change dynamically and are not documented. Data handling for xAI-hosted models was not confirmed on the docs page.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use dedicated clusters for fixed capacity and subscribe to retirement announcements.
