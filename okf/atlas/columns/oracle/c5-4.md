## Answer

Not covered. Metrics gaps for on-demand and hosted deployments, and no anomaly detection beyond alarms.

Research detail: Generative AI publishes latency, call count, error and GPU utilisation metrics for dedicated clusters and endpoints, and Monitoring alarms can trigger on them. Hosted applications autoscale on concurrency, RPS, CPU or memory.

## Products

## Gaps

Fine-tuning clusters show no metrics. On-demand models and hosted deployments lack documented metrics. Anomaly detection is threshold alarms only.

## Options
