## Answer

Not covered. Agents share the genaiagent principal type. No documented link from an agent identity to an owner or release.

Research detail: Generative AI Agents act as a resource principal of type genaiagent, which IAM policies and dynamic groups match with request.principal.type or resource.type rules. Hosted agentic applications are each tied to a registered application in an OCI identity domain. Agents, endpoints and tools are OCI resources with OCIDs in a compartment.

## Products

## Gaps
