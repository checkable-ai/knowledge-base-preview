## Answer

Not covered. Public pages do not show Generative AI or Agents in IRAP scope, and no Australian region is listed for them.

Research detail: Oracle Cloud for Australian Government and Defence, a Canberra region isolated from commercial regions, is certified for IRAP at PROTECTED and for the Hosting Certification Framework at Certified Strategic. Oracle announced its IRAP assessment against ISM PROTECTED controls in August 2023, and the HCF list names Oracle Australia as a Certified Strategic cloud service. Oracle Cloud Applications completed an IRAP PROTECTED assessment in August 2023. The OCI Compliance Documents service gives SOC 3 reports, certificates, attestations and bridge letters, and Oracle's compliance page lists ISO/IEC 42001 among its frameworks.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

IRAP PROTECTED (Oracle Cloud for Australian Government and Defence), HCF Certified Strategic, OCI Compliance Documents, SOC reports

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The public pages do not list which services are in the IRAP scope or show OCI Generative AI or Generative AI Agents in it. Neither service lists an Australian region, so their use from Australia falls outside the Sydney, Melbourne and Canberra footprint. The IRAP statements date from 2023, older than 24 months. ISO 42001 scope by service is not public.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Get the IRAP report and service list from the Compliance Documents service, and confirm whether any Generative AI service is in scope before an authority-to-operate decision.
