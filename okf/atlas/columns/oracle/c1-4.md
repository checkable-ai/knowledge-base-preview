## Answer

Not covered. No approval workflow for creating or changing agents. IAM verbs and Audit are nearby but are not a controlled process.

Research detail: Agents, endpoints, tools, knowledge bases, hosted applications and deployments are IAM resource types with separate inspect, read, use and manage verbs for create, update and delete. Audit records these API calls.
