## Answer

Not covered. IAM allowlists, creation permissions and a deployment vulnerability scan exist, but there is no submission, registration and approval workflow for models, agents or tools.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: IAM target.model.id conditions enforce an allowlist of approved models, and the docs advise updating the allowlist when approved models change.
