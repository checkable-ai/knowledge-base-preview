## Answer

Not covered. No approval, reassessment or retirement workflow for external suppliers.

Research detail: Oracle states prompts, responses and training data are not shared with third-party model providers, and IAM conditions restrict use to approved models. Imported third-party models may carry separate terms that the customer must meet.
