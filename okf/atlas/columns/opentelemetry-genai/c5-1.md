## Answer

Not covered. The gen_ai.evaluation.result event records evaluation results but does not distinguish human review or define a review process.

Research detail: The gen_ai.evaluation.result event records an evaluation name, score value, score label and explanation, parented to the evaluated operation span or linked by gen_ai.response.id. A tool can use it to record review outcomes, including human ones.
