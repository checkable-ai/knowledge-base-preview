## Answer

Not covered. Only guidance on opt-in content capture; no retention, deletion or residency rules or attributes.

Research detail: For the telemetry copy of inputs, outputs, instructions, memory records and retrieved documents, the spec says instrumentations SHOULD NOT capture content by default, SHOULD offer opt-in, and recommends external storage with separate access controls in production where privacy rules apply.
