<!-- chunk: answer -->
## Answer

Covered. Grok Build; sandbox is off by default and child-network blocking is Linux only.

Source statement: "The sandbox limits what the agent process and its children can read, write, and reach on the network" (https://docs.x.ai/build/features/sandbox)

Research detail: Grok Build has ask, auto and always-approve modes, allow and deny rules per tool and pattern, and sandbox profiles using Landlock or Seatbelt. Admins can pin settings in /etc/grok/requirements.toml and lock always-approve off. Grok Bot admins can cap local execution to Ask every time or Never allow.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Grok Build permissions, sandbox, requirements.toml, Grok Bot Execution on Local Computer

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Sandbox is off by default. Child network blocking works on Linux only. Built-in profiles do not protect paths such as ~/.ssh. Hook failures are fail-open.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pin a strict sandbox profile and disable_bypass_permissions_mode in a root-owned requirements.toml.
