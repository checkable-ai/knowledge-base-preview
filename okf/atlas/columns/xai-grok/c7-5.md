## Answer

Not covered. No SBOMs or scanning of MCP servers, skills or extensions. Folder trust, network allowlists and permissions limit execution but do not check provenance or content.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Grok Build requires trust before project hooks, MCP and LSP servers run, and honours MCP allowlists and marketplace restrictions from managed settings. Grok Bot Enterprise restricts connectors with an MCP allowlist and egress with a destination allowlist.
