## Answer

Not covered. No approval primitive on the API, and binding of approval to exact payload and replay handling are not documented. Grok Build ask mode and Grok Bot approvals fall short.

Research detail: Grok Bot shows the proposed operation and its inputs, with Allow once, Always allow and Deny, and an Auto Review model can allow, require approval or deny risky actions. Grok Build prompts for any tool call not already allowed. The API's remote MCP does not support require_approval.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Grok Bot approvals and Auto Review, Grok Build ask mode

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Replay handling and binding of the approval to an exact payload are not documented. Auto Review does not review memory writes or most settings changes. No approval primitive on the API.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Implement approval in your own function-calling loop for API workloads. Enforce Auto-review on Grok Bot Enterprise.
