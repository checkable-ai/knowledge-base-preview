## Answer

Not covered. No ownership record for agents, models or tools beyond key creator.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Grok Bot Team Bots have one owner who controls publishing and deletion, plus managers who change setup. API keys are associated with their creating user, and teams have Admin and Member roles.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Grok Bot Team Bots, API key creator, team roles

## Gaps

If a user is removed, their API keys stay with the team without reassigning ownership. No ownership for models or tools.
