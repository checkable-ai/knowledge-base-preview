## Answer

Covered. Grok Bot on an eligible paid plan. No per-organisation retention policy is documented for Grok Bot memory.

Source statement: "A named Bot keeps its memory, files, browser sessions, and preferences across sessions instead of resetting on every task." (https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)

Research detail: A named Grok Bot keeps memory, files, browser sessions and preferences across sessions, and Team Bots have shared memory. Grok Business offers custom retention policies on the Enterprise tier. Grok Build keeps session history on disk.
