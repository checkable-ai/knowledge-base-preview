## Answer

Not covered. Admins can add and delete connectors, but there is no scheduled reassessment or retirement workflow for external models, tools and services.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins approve external services by provisioning connectors and custom MCP servers before members use them, and can remove them. Grok Bot advises contacting the account team when contracts restrict subprocessors.
