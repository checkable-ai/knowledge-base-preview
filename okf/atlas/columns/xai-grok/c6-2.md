## Answer

Covered. Grok Business and Enterprise. A team admin provisions connectors or custom MCP servers before members can use them.

Source statement: "Team admins can provision connectors from the catalog or add a custom MCP server." (https://docs.x.ai/grok/connector-management)

Research detail: On Grok Business and Enterprise, a team admin must add a connector or custom MCP server before members can use it. Grok Bot Team Bots are published by their owner, and admins choose who gets them.
