## Answer

Not covered. No documented sanitisation of data stores or per-document access control for Collections. Metadata constraints and management-key permissions do not govern or clean the data.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Collections stores documents with embeddings, metadata fields that can be required or unique, and configurable chunking. Collections data is not used for training, and existing collections can be viewed and deleted.
