## Answer

Covered. Applies to Grok Bot Team Bots, with owner, Manager and Collaborator roles.

Source statement: "Only the owner can delete a shared Bot." (https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/shared-bots)

Research detail: The Management API creates, updates, rotates and deletes API keys and their ACLs and limits. SCIM provisions users into roles mapped to teams, permissions and licences. Grok Bot Team Bots are created, published, unpublished and deleted by owners or admins, and those events reach Enterprise audit logs.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Management API, SCIM roles, Grok Bot Team Bots

## Gaps

No agent object on the API to provision. Grok Bot provisioning is administered in the Cursor dashboard.

## Options
