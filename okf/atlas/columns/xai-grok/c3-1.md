## Answer

Not covered. No per-user or per-agent routing and no injection screening.

Research detail: Each API key can be limited to models and endpoints and given its own QPS, QPM and TPM caps. Teams have postpaid spending limits and tiered per-model rate limits. mTLS can require a client certificate on an Enterprise endpoint. safety_identifier attributes abuse to end users.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

API key ACLs and limits, spending limits, mTLS endpoint

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No per-user or per-agent routing, and no customer-configurable injection screening. Grok Bot's model allowlist is not guaranteed to be enforced.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Place a third-party model gateway in front of the API for routing and screening.
