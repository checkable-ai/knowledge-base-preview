## Answer

Covered. ZDR is team-wide and optional; residency excludes Files, Collections and server-side tools.

Source statement: "SpaceXSI does not train on this data, and it is automatically deleted after 30 days." (https://docs.x.ai/developers/faq/security)

Research detail: API data is kept 30 days by default and not used for training. Team-level ZDR stops persistence of prompts and outputs. The US endpoint keeps retained data in the US. Grok Enterprise offers custom retention, and the Vault add-on offers a dedicated data plane with customer-managed keys.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

ZDR, US regional endpoint, Grok Enterprise retention, Enterprise Vault

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Vault and custom retention are documented only in an announcement and the user guide. Grok Bot has no per-organisation retention policy. Residency excludes tools and files.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Confirm Vault and retention terms in the Enterprise agreement.
