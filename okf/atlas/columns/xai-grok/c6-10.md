## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Default retention is 30 days. Zero Data Retention is optional and team-wide. The US regional endpoint https://us.api.x.ai/v1 serves only grok-4.7 and grok-4.6 at 10% higher token prices. Files, Collections and server-side tools are not covered by the US-handling commitment. The default endpoint guarantees no processing region. Grok Bot automated retention requires Enterprise and is off by default. API Zero Data Retention is team-wide and subject to environment availability or negotiated Enterprise terms. Grok.com custom retention requires Enterprise.

Source statement: "Delete Grok Bot data automatically once it passes a period you choose." (https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/teams-and-enterprises)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: API data is kept 30 days by default and not used for training. Team-level ZDR stops persistence of prompts and outputs. The US endpoint keeps retained data in the US. Grok Enterprise offers custom retention, and the Vault add-on offers a dedicated data plane with customer-managed keys.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

ZDR, US regional endpoint, Grok Enterprise retention, Enterprise Vault

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Vault and custom retention are documented only in an announcement and the user guide. Grok Bot has no per-organisation retention policy. Residency excludes tools and files.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Confirm Vault and retention terms in the Enterprise agreement.
