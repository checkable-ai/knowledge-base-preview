## Answer

Not covered. No documented cancellation, retry or recovery semantics for multi-agent work.

Research detail: The beta multi-agent model runs 4 or 16 agents with a leader that synthesises the answer. Grok Build spawns subagents and background tasks. Grok Bot runs routines on schedules or events, hands off between Bots and can delegate coding to Cursor Cloud Agents.
