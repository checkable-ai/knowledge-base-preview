## Answer

Covered. Grok Bot connected accounts; team-managed connectors may use team or service-account credentials instead.

Source statement: "A connected account always acts as the person the Bot is answering." (https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/shared-bots)

Research detail: Grok Business connectors authenticate each member by OAuth, and the Google Drive app respects the member's existing Drive permissions. Grok Bot acts as the signed-in member and can never hold more access than that person. Grok Build supports enterprise OIDC and team-pinned login.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Grok connectors, Grok Bot, Grok Build enterprise OIDC

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No token exchange or delegated-authority propagation for API calls or remote MCP. Grok Bot Team Bots may use the Bot's own shared credential instead of the user's.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use per-user OAuth connectors. For API workloads, pass user context to tools through your own gateway.
