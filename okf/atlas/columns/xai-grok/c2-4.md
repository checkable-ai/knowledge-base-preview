## Answer

Not covered. Responses API storage and previous_response_id resume exist, but who can read session memory and a clear retention outcome are not documented.

Research detail: The Responses API stores prompts, reasoning and responses for 30 days so a conversation can continue by previous_response_id, and store false disables this. Grok Build saves sessions locally under ~/.grok/sessions with resume, fork, rewind and delete.

## Products

## Gaps
