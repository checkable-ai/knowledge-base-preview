## Answer

Covered. US endpoint serves only grok-4.7 and grok-4.6; Files, Collections and server-side tools are outside the US guarantee; image, video and voice APIs are not served there.

Source statement: "The US guarantee covers API request handling, inference, moderation, and retained request data." (https://docs.x.ai/developers/advanced-api-usage/regions)

Research detail: Models have dated slugs for pinned versions plus aliases, published context and per-tier rate limits. The US endpoint keeps handling, inference, moderation and retained data in the US. API data is kept 30 days by default and not used for training, and ZDR can be enabled per team with a response header confirming it.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

xAI API, US regional endpoint, ZDR

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Retired slugs silently redirect to grok-4.3. The US guarantee excludes Files, Collections, server-side tools and image, video and voice APIs.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pin dated model slugs and check the x-zero-data-retention header in responses.
