## Answer

Not covered. Model lifecycle stages are documented for Mistral's serverless models and agents have versions, but there is no lifecycle for customer models, agents or use cases from proposal through testing and production to retirement.

Research detail: Mistral's own models move through Labs, Public Preview, GA, Deprecated and Retired stages with stated notice. Agents are versioned with switchable active versions.
