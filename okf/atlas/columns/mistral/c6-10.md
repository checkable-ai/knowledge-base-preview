## Answer

Covered. EU hosting by default, US endpoint optional. Zero data retention is on request and excludes some features such as agents, conversations, libraries and files.

Source statement: "By default, your data is hosted in the European Union." (https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347629-where-do-you-store-my-data-or-my-organization-s-data)

Research detail: Data is hosted in the EU by default, with a US endpoint option and organisation-level controls to switch off some features that transfer data outside the EU. ZDR is available on request for stateless API calls, Enterprise Vibe is opted out of training by default, conversations can be deleted or not stored, and traces are kept for 30 days.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Regional hosting, ZDR, training opt-out, store=False, Knowledge Base deletion

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

ZDR is discretionary and excludes Vibe, agents, conversations, libraries and files. Retention periods for API data and chats are not stated on the pages read.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Confirm retention periods in the DPA and use store=False and ZDR where eligible.
