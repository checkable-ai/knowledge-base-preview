## Answer

Covered. EU default hosting with US option; ZDR on request and excludes agents and conversations.

Source statement: "For precise control, pin your deployment to a specific major.minor version identifier." (https://docs.mistral.ai/inference/model-lifecycle)

Research detail: Models follow a lifecycle policy with fixed major.minor identifiers, moving aliases and stated deprecation notice (6 months for GA, 1 month for preview, Labs and third-party models). Data is hosted in the EU by default with a US endpoint option. Rate limits are set per model and ZDR is available for stateless API calls on request.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

La Plateforme API (Mistral AI Studio), model lifecycle policy, regional endpoints, ZDR

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Aliases can change behaviour and pricing silently. ZDR is discretionary and excludes agents, conversations, libraries and batch. API retention without ZDR is not stated on the page read.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pin major.minor identifiers. Request ZDR where eligible and confirm retention terms in the DPA.
