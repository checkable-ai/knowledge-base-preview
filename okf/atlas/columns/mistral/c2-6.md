## Answer

Covered. Vibe Work Knowledge Base; admins can disable; no retention period stated.

Source statement: "Knowledge Base saves what matters from your conversations so Vibe can recall it later." (https://docs.mistral.ai/vibe/work/knowledge)

Research detail: Knowledge Base, the successor to Memories, stores personal facts across Work conversations, saved explicitly or implicitly. Users can view, delete, export and turn it off, and workspace admins can disable it for the organisation. Sensitive categories such as health or religion are not stored.
