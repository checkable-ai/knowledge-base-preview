## Answer

Not covered. Workload identity federation gives short-lived tokens and credentials can be deleted, but it applies to service accounts authenticating to Mistral, not to agent credentials for downstream tools. Docs do not state connector secrets are kept out of model context or rotated and revoked per agent. Connectors are Public Preview.

Research detail: Connectors hold auth_data (OAuth2 client details or static headers) and per-user credential sets, and tools are called through Mistral's connector layer. Workload identity federation gives service accounts short-lived tokens from an OIDC or Kubernetes issuer with no stored secret. API keys support expiry, organisation expiry policies, rotation by replacement and deletion.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Connectors (auth_data, user credentials), workload identity federation, API key expiration policy

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Docs do not say how connector credentials are stored or confirm they are kept out of model context. Connectors are in Public Preview. Programmatic token passing for OAuth connectors is not supported.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Ask Mistral for connector credential storage and isolation details. Prefer federation over API keys and enforce an expiry policy.
