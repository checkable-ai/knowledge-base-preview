## Answer

Not covered. No customer-set retention or deletion for Gemini in Chrome history. Region choice limited to DLP scans.

Research detail: Most Admin console logs are kept for 6 months and personal data generally no longer than 18 months. DLP and malware scan data can be stored in a chosen region such as the US or Europe. For managed users, Gemini in Chrome interactions are not used for training outside the domain without permission.
