## Answer

Not covered. No cross-session memory controls in Chrome policy. History sits with Workspace Gemini settings.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Gemini in Chrome chats are kept in Gemini Apps Activity and sites visited by auto browse appear in Chrome history with an action icon.
