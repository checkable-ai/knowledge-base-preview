## Answer

Not covered. No admin visibility of model version, endpoint, region or limits per agent.

Research detail: Gemini in Chrome runs Google's Gemini models, and an on-device foundational model can be downloaded when GenAILocalFoundationalModelSettings allows. For managed users Google states interactions stay in the organisation and are not human reviewed or used for training outside the domain without permission.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini in Chrome, on-device generative AI model

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No model version, endpoint, region or operating limits are exposed to admins. Training terms come from Workspace agreements rather than the Chrome policy.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Read the Workspace generative AI terms for data handling, and block on-device model download where it is not wanted.
