## Answer

Covered. Requires Chrome Enterprise Premium. Masking needs the Secure Enterprise Browser extension.

Source statement: "Stops the user from completing the action, such as uploading a file." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/security/use-chrome-enterprise-premium-to-integrate-dlp-with-chrome)

Research detail: Premium DLP inspects uploads, downloads, paste, print and URL visits with predefined and custom detectors, and can audit, warn or block. Data masking redacts sensitive data in input to and output from generative AI sites. From Chrome 152 agent typing is scanned, and from Chrome 149 paste across management boundaries can be blocked.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Chrome Enterprise Premium DLP, data masking, DataControlsRules

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Premium licence required. Masking needs the Secure Enterprise Browser extension and does not support iframes through the console. No retrieval authorisation for RAG sources.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Start from the generative AI DLP templates in audit mode, then move to block.
