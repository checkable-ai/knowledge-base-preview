## Answer

Not covered. Only per-user stop, take over and resume. No admin orchestration, retry or recovery controls.

Research detail: Auto browse plans and runs multi-step web tasks. Users can stop a task, take over and resume it, and Gemini may try another approach after a stop. On Android a task can run in the background with status in a notification.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini in Chrome auto browse, Gemini Spark

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Orchestration is per user task. No admin view of task queues, retry policy or failure recovery is documented. Daily task limits apply by plan.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use for user-driven tasks and keep multi-agent workflows in a separate orchestrator.
