## Answer

Not covered. Policies only switch built-in Google agent features on or off. No creation, change or retirement process for distinct agents.

Research detail: Admins enable or disable Google's agent features per OU or group through policy: GeminiSettings, GeminiActOnWebSettings for auto browse, GeminiSparkSettings and GenAiDefaultSettings for defaults. Auto browse is off by default for managed users. Changes apply within minutes to 24 hours.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

GeminiSettings, GenAiDefaultSettings, GeminiActOnWebSettings, GeminiSparkSettings

## Gaps

## Options
