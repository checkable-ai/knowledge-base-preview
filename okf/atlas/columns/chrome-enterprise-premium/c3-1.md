## Answer

Not covered. No routing, quota, cost or rate enforcement per user or agent. Injection screening is built in, not admin-controlled.

Research detail: Admins allow or block Gemini features per OU or group with GeminiSettings and GenAiDefaultSettings, and Premium DLP and URL filtering can audit, warn or block generative AI sites by URL category. Google describes the User Alignment Critic as a built-in check on auto browse actions against the user's goal.
