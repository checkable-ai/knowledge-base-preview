## Answer

Not covered. Per-user consent for Password Manager sign-in only. No admin scoping, rotation, expiry or revocation of agent credentials.

Research detail: From Chrome 156 users can temporarily share saved Google Password Manager credentials with Gemini Spark to finish a task on a third-party site, with explicit consent and biometric or device unlock for each task. The auto browse help says Gemini can sign in with Password Manager or Sign in with Google with the user's permission and that passwords are not shared with Gemini. Admins can switch the feature off with GeminiSparkSettings, which is disabled by default.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Google Password Manager agentic sign-in, GeminiSparkSettings policy

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Consent and removal are per user. No admin scoping of which credentials or sites, and no rotation, expiry or revocation controls for agent use are documented. Covers Google's own agents only.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Keep GeminiSparkSettings disabled until tested, and check whether a managed profile's saved credentials can be limited before enabling it.
