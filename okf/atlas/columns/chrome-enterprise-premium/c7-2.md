## Answer

Covered. Data protection and extension telemetry events need Premium. No AI-agent-specific detections documented.

Source statement: "As an admin, you can use the Google Admin console to get Chrome to report events to third-party service providers." (https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/11375053)

Research detail: Reporting connectors send Chrome security and data protection events to Google SecOps, Splunk, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks and Pub/Sub. Premium adds Safe Browsing context and, from Chrome 152, risk scores and referrer chains for SecOps, and from Chrome 149 extension telemetry to third-party SIEMs.
