## Answer

Covered. Managed Chrome on Windows, Mac or Linux with signed-in managed users. Reports only a predefined list of Gen AI and SaaS sites. Does not discover desktop AI apps, agents or MCP servers. Needs Chrome administrator privilege to view.

Source statement: "monitor use of popular enterprise and Generative AI (Gen AI) apps and sites." (https://support.google.com/chrome/a/answer/17032872)

Research detail: The Gen AI and SaaS app usage report shows visits, unique profiles and browsers, and sensitive content transfers for a predefined list of about 130 sites, including ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. It rolled out in Chrome 151 and is on by default when browser reporting is on. Premium uses it to build DLP and access rules for unsanctioned AI.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gen AI and SaaS app usage report, Chrome Enterprise Premium security insights

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Limited to listed sites in managed Chrome on desktop. No discovery of desktop AI apps, agents or MCP servers. Advanced extension telemetry for agent visibility is Premium.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pair with endpoint or network discovery for AI use outside the browser.
