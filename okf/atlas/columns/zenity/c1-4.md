## Answer

Not covered. No provisioning, change or retirement workflow. AISPM pre-go-live policy checks are detective and corrective only.

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: Zenity does not create or retire agents. AISPM checks agent configuration, permissions and connectors against policy before go-live, and playbooks can alert, block or remediate violations. AI IAM can block an agent acting under a revoked identity.
