## Answer

Not covered. Relevance AI does not document a way for a customer organisation to submit, register and approve new models, agents or tools before they are used. The nearest features fall short. Marketplace submission is a vendor-run quality review of public listings, done by the Relevance team. It is not the customer's own governance approval. Admin model allow-listing only limits which models are available. Eval gating tests quality but is not a registration or approval step.
