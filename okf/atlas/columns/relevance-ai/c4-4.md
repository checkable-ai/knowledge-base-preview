## Answer

Not covered. Workforce edges connect agents inside the platform, and the permission check applies to the human user, not to the calling agent. No authentication of agent-to-agent calls or trust boundaries, and no external agent authentication.

Last checked 11 November 2026.

Research detail: Workforce edges connect one agent to another as an AI connection or forced handover, and running a workforce needs permission on each agent in it. Communication is one way.
