## Answer

Covered. Approval Required mode must be set; Let Agent Decide leaves the choice to the model.

Source statement: "The agent will now draft its intended action and wait for human approval before proceeding" (https://relevanceai.com/docs/build/workforces/workforce-features/approvals-and-escalations)

Research detail: Each tool or workforce edge can be set to Approval Required, where the agent drafts its intended action and waits. Reviewers approve, reject or give guidance in the task view, and unapproved tasks stay pending. Invent marks destructive actions as Always ask.
