## Answer

Not covered. Relevance AI documents streaming audit logs and execution traces (OTEL format) to a customer-owned S3 bucket on the Enterprise plan, but does not document write-once or integrity-validated storage, Object Lock, or any tamper-detection for that record. Immutability would depend on the customer's own bucket configuration, which is outside what the vendor documents.

Research detail: Event streaming sends audit logs and traces of agent, workforce and LLM calls, including inputs and outputs, to the customer's S3 bucket in OTEL format. RBAC gives views of audit and activity logs.
