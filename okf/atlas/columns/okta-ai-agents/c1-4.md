## Answer

Covered. Admin-driven and manual; issued tokens stay valid until expiry unless revoked.

Source statement: "After you've registered an AI agent in Okta, you can add owners, credentials, and a linked OIDC app for user assignment." (https://help.okta.com/oie/en-us/content/topics/ai-agents/ai-agent-register.htm)

Research detail: Admins register or import agents, add owners and credentials, and create, edit, deactivate or remove resource connections. Agents can be deactivated, and the kill switch combines deactivating the agent, its linked app and its authorisation server.
