## Answer

Not covered. Owners are optional for agents, models and tools are not owned records, and nothing keeps ownership current. Okta only recommends at least two owners per agent.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Owners are added to each registered agent and discovered agents are assigned a named human owner. Okta's product page states each agent gets a clear human owner.
