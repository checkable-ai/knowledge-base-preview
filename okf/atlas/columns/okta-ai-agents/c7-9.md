## Answer

Covered. ISPM is excluded from the Okta for AI Agents Core SKU, so a SKU that includes ISPM is needed. Browser shadow AI discovery uses the Secure Access Monitor plugin. Local agents and MCP servers depend on the CrowdStrike Falcon integration.

Source statement: "ISPM finds shadow AI agents, and lets you assess the risk each one poses." (https://help.okta.com/oie/en-us/content/topics/ai-agents/ai-agent-discover.htm)

Research detail: ISPM discovers shadow agents through the Secure Access Monitor browser plugin, CrowdStrike Falcon on endpoints, and builder platforms including Agentforce, Copilot Studio and Bedrock AgentCore. It also detects OAuth grants and MCP servers, and discovered agents can be registered or imported into Universal Directory.
