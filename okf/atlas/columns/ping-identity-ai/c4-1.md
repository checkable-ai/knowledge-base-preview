## Answer

Not covered. Agents get a distinct identity and an owner, but the agent record has no runtime location or release/version attribute. Custom attributes in Advanced Identity Cloud are free-form and owners there are symbolic.

Research detail: PingOne and Advanced Identity Cloud register each AI agent as a distinct OAuth 2.0 identity with its own credentials and lifecycle. Owners are assigned through the Application Owner role in PingOne, and Advanced Identity Cloud owners are symbolic with no functional permissions. The documentation does not link an agent identity to a runtime or a release version.

## Gaps
