## Answer

Covered. PingOne needs an attribute mapping to include the act claim.

Source statement: "the `sub` and `act` claims preserve who acted on whose behalf." (https://developer.pingidentity.com/identity-for-ai/identity/idai-token-exchange.html)

Research detail: Agents use OAuth 2.0 token exchange (RFC 8693) to trade the user's subject token plus their own actor token for a downscoped token. The act claim records the agent and nests across multi-agent chains while sub stays the human user. PingOne, Advanced Identity Cloud, PingFederate and PingAM are listed.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent IAM Core token exchange, PingGateway delegation token validation

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

PingOne does not include the act claim by default and needs an attribute mapping with an advanced expression.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Configure the act claim mapping and verify downstream resource servers check both user and agent.
