## Answer

Not covered. No documented per-user or document/row-level retrieval filtering inside data sources. AI Guard inspects traffic for exfiltration and PII but that is not retrieval filtering.

Research detail: Agent Privilege brokers agent access to data stores such as PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Elasticsearch and OpenSearch as agentic apps, and AI Guard checks traffic for data exfiltration and PII with sanitise or block actions. Retrieval filtering by user entitlement inside a data source is not described.
