## Answer

Covered. Owners in Advanced Identity Cloud are symbolic with no functional permissions; PingOne uses the Application Owner role.

Source statement: "such as excessive permissions, missing owners, or unmonitored agents" (https://docs.pingidentity.com/privilege/agent-privilege/use-cases/workload-agents.md)

Research detail: PingOne assigns agent owners through the Application Owner role, Advanced Identity Cloud supports symbolic owners, and Agent Privilege records a custodian and flags workload agents with missing owners. Owners on Advanced Identity Cloud carry no functional permissions.
