## Answer

Covered. availableModels must be set in managed or policy settings (v2.1.283 or later for availableModelsMatch). Organisation model controls do not fully reach third-party cloud providers.

Source statement: "Aliases point to the recommended version for your provider and update over time." (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config)

Research detail: Models are selected by alias or pinned ID, with per-alias pinning on Bedrock, Google Cloud and Foundry. Administrators restrict models with availableModels, enforceAvailableModels and deniedModels, cap effort, and restrict API providers with allowedProviders. Data handling and encryption at rest are documented per provider.

## Products

Model configuration, availableModels, allowedProviders, maxEffortLevel, provider choice

## Gaps

Aliases move to new model versions over time unless pinned. Organisation-level server-side model controls do not reach third-party cloud providers.

## Options
