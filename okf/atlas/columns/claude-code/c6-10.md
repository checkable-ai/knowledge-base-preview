## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Retention depends on account type: consumer plans (Free, Pro, Max, Ultra) 5 years or 30 days by training choice; commercial plans (Team, Enterprise, API) 30 days standard. Zero Data Retention is available to qualified accounts for Claude Code on Claude for Enterprise, enabled per organisation by the account team after eligibility checks. Local transcripts are kept 30 days by default, set with cleanupPeriodDays. Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud's Agent Platform and Microsoft Foundry follow their platform policies. Microsoft Foundry data handling differs between Hosted on Azure and Hosted on Anthropic deployments.

Source statement: "Standard: 30-day retention period" (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/data-usage)

Last checked 18 November 2026.

Research detail: Commercial data is not used for training, standard retention is 30 days, and Zero Data Retention is available to qualified Enterprise accounts. Local transcript retention is set by cleanupPeriodDays, cloud sessions can be deleted, and residency follows the chosen provider or a gateway that routes inference through the customer's cloud.

## Products

Data usage policy, ZDR, cleanupPeriodDays, Claude apps gateway

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Auto memory has no documented retention limit. Feedback transcripts are kept for 5 years when users submit them.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Disable feedback and nonessential traffic in managed settings, set retention, and use ZDR or a provider in the required region.
