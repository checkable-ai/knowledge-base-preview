## Answer

Covered. Zero Data Retention is optional. Batch files kept 30 days unless deleted earlier. Troubleshooting logs kept up to 30 days. Customer data stored in US GCP buckets only.

Source statement: "By default, Groq does not retain customer data for inference requests." (https://console.groq.com/docs/your-data)

Research detail: Inference data is not retained by default. Zero Data Retention can be enabled globally or per feature. Batch files are kept up to 30 days and temporary troubleshooting logs up to 30 days, with an opt-out. Customer data is kept in US GCP buckets.
