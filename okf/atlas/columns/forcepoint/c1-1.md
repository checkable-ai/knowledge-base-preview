## Answer

Not covered. No control over file, command or process actions on the agent's host. Endpoint shadow AI allow/block of tools is the nearest feature and falls short.

Research detail: The endpoint agent detects AI tools on devices, including browser extensions, vibe-coding tools and MCP clients, and policies can allow, restrict or block each tool and its upload or download activity. Control is data and application centric.
