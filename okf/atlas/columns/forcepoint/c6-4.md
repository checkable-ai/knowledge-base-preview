## Answer

Not covered. Policy Center covers data governance policies, not AI policies with ownership and versioning.

Research detail: DSPM Policy Center builds data governance policies from templates, and the Compliance Hub tracks who read and accepted each policy, sends annual review reminders and keeps an audit trail of edits and reviews. ARIA drafts AI policies in plain language for admin review.
