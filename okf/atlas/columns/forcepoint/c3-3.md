## Answer

Covered. DLP policies applied to connected AI platforms; DSPM for oversharing; AI channel features documented on product pages.

Source statement: "2,000+ out-of-the-box classifiers covering PII, source code, financial records and more" (https://www.forcepoint.com/use-case/dlp-for-ai)

Research detail: DLP for AI applies existing classification policies, with more than 2,000 classifiers, to prompts, responses and uploads, with notify, coach, restrict, block or SIEM escalation. DSPM scans SharePoint and OneDrive, remediates overshared permissions and revokes Copilot access to overshared files, and classification tags data before AI tools reach it.
