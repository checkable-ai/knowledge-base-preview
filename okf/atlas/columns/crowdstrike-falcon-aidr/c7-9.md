## Answer

Covered. Depends on browser and Falcon Endpoint collectors being deployed. Agent discovery on endpoints is announced only.

Source statement: "Helps uncover unsanctioned tools or traffic observed by browser collectors." (https://aidr-docs.crowdstrike.com/docs/aidr/visibility/data-flows-and-dashboards)

Research detail: The browser collector monitors use of web AI tools against a CrowdStrike-maintained Generative AI site collection with a Discovery mode, and Falcon Endpoint network inspection covers desktop apps and CLIs. The Sankey view is documented for employee AI usage and shadow AI. Falcon Guardian is announced to discover unsanctioned agents on endpoints.
