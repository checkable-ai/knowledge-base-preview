## Answer

Not covered. Per-agent identity is described only in press releases and announcements, not product documentation. AIDR records caller-supplied app_id and user_id only.

Research detail: The Agentic Identity Provider and Continuous Identity for AI Agents releases describe a cryptographically verifiable identity for each agent based on SPIFFE, registered automatically when Falcon Guardian discovers the agent, with actions tied to an accountable human or workload. AIDR itself only records app_id, user_id and collector metadata supplied by the caller.
