## Answer

Covered. Cross-geo and external models can process data outside the region. Transcripts are retained for a limited default period, adjustable in settings; Purview retention policies apply separately.

Source statement: "Microsoft Copilot Studio allows organizations to choose where their data is stored, providing flexibility to meet regional data residency requirements." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/geo-data-residency)

Research detail: Data stays in the environment's chosen geography, with EU Data Boundary support and an admin setting for cross-region data movement. Transcripts are deleted after 30 days in Dataverse by default and 28 days in Copilot Studio storage, memory after 28 days of inactivity, and Purview retention policies can retain user and agent text for zero days. Customer-managed keys are supported.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Geographic data residency, transcript retention, Purview retention policies, customer-managed keys

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Cross-geo and external models can process data outside the region. External threat detection providers may store data elsewhere.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Disable cross-region data movement and external models where residency is required.
