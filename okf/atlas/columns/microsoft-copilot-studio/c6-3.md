## Answer

Covered. Maker-facing view on the Agents page, for published agents only. There is no tenant-wide consolidated report in Copilot Studio itself.

Source statement: "By displaying the protection status for each agent as one of Protected, Needs review, or Unknown, makers can quickly determine when threat detection is active and their agents are protected." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/security-agent-runtime-view)

Last checked 13 November 2026.

Research detail: Each published agent shows a protection status of Protected, Needs review or Unknown across authentication, policies and content moderation, with blocked-message analytics. Data policy violations are listed per agent and admins can see inventory and credit status across the tenant.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent runtime protection status, security analytics, data policy violation reports

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Protection status is a maker view per agent. No tenant-wide control coverage report is documented in Copilot Studio.
