## Answer

Not covered. Built-in prompt injection blocking and monthly per-agent credit limits exist, but no gateway for model calls with routing or per-user or per-workload rate limits is documented.

Research detail: Built-in threat detection blocks user prompt injection and cross-domain prompt injection at runtime, with configurable content moderation. Admins can set a monthly Copilot Credit limit per agent with notifications and a hard stop, and allow or block preview, experimental and external models per environment.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Built-in UPIA and XPIA protection, content moderation, per-agent credit limits, model admin settings

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No per-user or per-workload model routing or rate limits are documented. Credit limits are monthly, not request rates.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use per-agent credit limits with hard stop and environment-level model restrictions.
