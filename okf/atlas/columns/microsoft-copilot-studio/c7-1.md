## Answer

Not covered. No tamper-evident, write-once or integrity-validated record of per-action inputs, outputs and reasoning. Purview audit covers authoring/admin events and CopilotInteraction metadata, and can be disabled or subject to retention deletion.

Last checked 13 November 2026.

Research detail: Purview logs maker and admin events such as BotCreate, BotPublish, BotShare and component changes, plus CopilotInteraction events for user use with a transcript thread ID. Collection cannot be disabled, and DSPM for AI retrieves the chat text. Entra logs agent identity sign-ins.

## Products

Microsoft Purview Audit, DSPM for AI, Entra sign-in logs

## Gaps

Audit records do not hold the transcript or reasoning. Some channels are excluded. FedRAMP tenants are excluded. Tamper evidence is not stated. Audit can be disabled in Purview tenant-wide.
