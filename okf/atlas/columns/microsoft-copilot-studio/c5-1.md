## Answer

Covered. Multistage approvals are preview, available only in agent flows on the standard harness, and need human stages configured.

Source statement: "The human review ensures that humans remain in control while still benefiting from faster processing of routine evaluations." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/flows-advanced-approvals)

Research detail: Multistage approvals let AI stage decisions and rationale go to human approvers in later stages before the flow continues. Admin-granted reviewers can read conversation transcripts, and compliance managers can view transcripts in Purview DSPM for AI.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Multistage approvals, conversation transcripts, Purview DSPM for AI

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No general review queue for agent outputs before delivery. Multistage approvals are preview.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use agent flows with manual stages for outputs that need sign-off, and sample transcripts for review.
