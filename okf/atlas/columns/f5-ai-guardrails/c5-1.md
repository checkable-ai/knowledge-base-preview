## Answer

Covered. After-the-fact review of prompt history, not an inline review queue.

Source statement: "View the details of each interaction-prompts, responses, outcomes, timestamps, and user info." (https://docs.aisecurity.f5.com/hubspot/prompt-logs.html)

Research detail: Prompt history lets reviewers inspect each interaction with prompt, response, outcome (blocked, passed or flagged), timestamp and user, filtered by project and provider. This is after-the-fact review, not a review step before output reaches users.
