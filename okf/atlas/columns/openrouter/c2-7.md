## Answer

Not covered. No sandbox, egress control or stop control for a running agent. Key disable and spend limits cut model access only.

Research detail: Keys can be disabled or deleted and key state is checked on every request, so access can be cut at once. Per-key and per-workspace spend limits cap damage.

## Products

## Gaps
