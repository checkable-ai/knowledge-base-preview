## Answer

Covered. Workload Identity Federation tokens last up to 15 minutes. Maximum key lifetime is set in privacy settings. Management key expiry is fixed at creation. No dedicated rotate endpoint. Not documented whether secrets stay out of model context.

Source statement: "Access tokens live for 15 minutes at most, and never longer than the JWT that was exchanged for them." (https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/overview/auth/workload-identity-federation.md)

Research detail: Management API keys create, update, disable and delete keys with expiry and spend limits. A maximum key lifetime can be enforced. Workload Identity Federation exchanges a workload JWT for an access token of up to 15 minutes, so no key sits in the workload environment. Disabling the target key cuts access at once.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Management API Keys, Security settings, Workload Identity Federation

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No dedicated rotate endpoint. Management key expiry cannot be changed after creation. Issued access tokens stay valid until expiry. Whether credentials stay out of model context was not tested.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use federation for workloads and a short maximum key lifetime.
