## Answer

Covered. Retention is opt-in. ZDR and data-collection deny are settings the customer must enable. EU and US regions only. Account, billing, authentication and abuse-monitoring data may be stored in the US. ZDR does not cover plugins or tools.

Source statement: "OpenRouter does not store your prompts or responses, unless you opt in to one or both of the following" (https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/privacy/data-collection)

Research detail: Prompt retention is opt-in and off by default. Using data for product improvement is a separate opt-in. ZDR can be enforced per request, per account and per guardrail. Regional routing keeps prompts in EU or US, and data_collection deny limits providers.

## Products

Data Collection, ZDR, In-Region Routing

## Gaps

Account, billing and abuse-monitoring data may stay in the US. ZDR does not cover plugins or tools. No Australian region is listed.

## Options
