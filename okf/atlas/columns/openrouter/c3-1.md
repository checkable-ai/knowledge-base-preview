## Answer

Covered. Guardrails must be assigned to members, keys or the workspace default. Prompt injection detection is regex-based.

Source statement: "Guardrail budgets are enforced per-user and per-key, not shared across all users with that guardrail." (https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/features/guardrails)

Research detail: Guardrails assigned to members, keys or workspaces enforce USD budgets that reset daily, weekly or monthly, model and provider allowlists, ZDR per model group, allowed data regions, regex content filters, prompt injection detection and sensitive information redaction or blocking. Workspace guardrails can only tighten account policy.
