## Answer

Not covered. No governance, access control or infrastructure controls for data stores. Batch screening of documents only sanitises content.

Research detail: Static documents for RAG can be screened at upload or in batch for poisoning and sensitive data, and retrieved content can be screened at runtime. This covers sanitisation of content, not governance or infrastructure of data stores.
