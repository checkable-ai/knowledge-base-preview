## Answer

Not covered. No routing, quota, cost or rate limit enforcement. Guard screens prompts but is called by a gateway rather than acting as one.

Research detail: Guard screens prompts and outputs for prompt injection, jailbreaks, data leakage, harmful content and malicious links, with policy set per project. The integration guide describes an AI gateway pattern for central screening across applications. Lakera does not route model traffic or enforce quotas, cost or rates.
