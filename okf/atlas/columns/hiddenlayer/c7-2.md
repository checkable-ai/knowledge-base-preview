## Answer

Covered. Session correlation needs the application to propagate the session ID; Sentinel receives detections only.

Source statement: "Runtime detections will be sent from the HiddenLayer Console to an Azure Log Analytics Workspace for ingestion by Sentinel." (https://docs.hiddenlayer.ai/docs/integrations/overview)

Research detail: Runtime Policy Outcomes carry tenant, correlation, session, interaction, requester and project IDs, the policy ruleset version and highest threat level, and are delivered to webhook, Splunk or Azure Sentinel. Reports map detections to MITRE ATLAS tactics, PII can be redacted before analysis, and projects record an owner and escalation contact.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Runtime Policy Outcomes, Splunk, Azure Sentinel and webhook integrations, Projects

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Session correlation depends on the application propagating the session ID. Sentinel receives detections, not the audit log.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Instrument the session ID in every call and test end-to-end correlation in the SIEM.
