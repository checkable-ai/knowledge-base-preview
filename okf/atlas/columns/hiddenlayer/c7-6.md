## Answer

Not covered. No scanning of tools, plugins, skills or registry entries for look-alike names, poisoned descriptions or compromised packages. Model Scanner covers models only.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The scanner covers third-party model sources such as Hugging Face, with a repository sideloading detection category, and the JFrog Artifactory plugin blocks downloads flagged as malicious. Agent Harness Security flags malicious dependency installs at runtime.
