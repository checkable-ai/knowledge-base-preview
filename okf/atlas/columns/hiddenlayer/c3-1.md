## Answer

Research detail: Runtime Security sits in front of model traffic through gateway integrations (Azure APIM, TrueFoundry, LiteLLM) or framework guardrails, screening for prompt injection, PII, code, URLs and oversized inputs, with a per-project policy selected by the HL-Project-Id header.
