## Answer

Not covered. No routing, quotas, cost or rate limits. Nearby feature: gateway integrations (Azure APIM, LiteLLM, TrueFoundry) that screen traffic for injection.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Runtime Security sits in front of model traffic through gateway integrations (Azure APIM, TrueFoundry, LiteLLM) or framework guardrails, screening for prompt injection, PII, code, URLs and oversized inputs, with a per-project policy selected by the HL-Project-Id header. A token-count threshold can block oversized inputs.
