## Answer

Covered. Connected AWS, Azure and Databricks accounts only.

Source statement: "Assets are discovered every 5 minutes." (https://docs.hiddenlayer.ai/docs/products/console/asset_discovery_discovered_assets)

Research detail: AI Discovery connects to AWS (Bedrock, AgentCore, SageMaker, ECR), Azure (Foundry, Azure ML) and Databricks, inventories models, agents, endpoints, catalogs and schemas every 5 minutes, and automatically scans discovered models.
