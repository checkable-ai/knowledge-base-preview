## Answer

Not covered. HiddenLayer documents runtime sessions, agentic investigation and a Console audit log, but does not document write-once or integrity-validated storage, hash chaining, or an export of agent action records to such storage. Nearby features fall short of tamper evidence.

Research detail: The Console audit log records administrative actions with timestamp, user email, role and description, retained 365 days and forwardable to Splunk or a webhook. Runtime sessions record each turn's input, output, tool calls and policy outcome per session and requester.
