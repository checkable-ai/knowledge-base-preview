## Answer

Covered, with conditions. AI Attack Simulation is only available as a SaaS offering from HiddenLayer. The target must have a publicly exposed interface (endpoint, widget, etc.). The application must be able to return a response to the testing endpoint.

Research detail: AI Attack Simulation runs automated red team evaluations with built-in and custom objectives, attacker guidance and reports of success rates and interactions, against foundation model providers or custom applications via the SDK.
