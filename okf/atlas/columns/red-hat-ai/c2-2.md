## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Models-as-a-Service in OpenShift AI Self-Managed 3.5. Token limits are set per model in a subscription. Some features, such as external models and multi-tenancy, are Technology Preview. Some llm-d platforms are also Technology Preview.

Source statement: "Subscriptions give groups quota for specific models with token rate limits." (https://docs.redhat.com/en/documentation/red_hat_openshift_ai_self-managed/3.5/html/govern_llm_access_with_models-as-a-service/deploy-and-manage-models-as-a-service)

Last checked 13 November 2026. This platform is checked on demand, so this answer has not been rechecked since.

Research detail: Red Hat AI Inference serves models with vLLM, and Distributed Inference with llm-d adds prefix-cache aware routing and disaggregated serving on OpenShift, AKS and CoreWeave. Controlled deployment runs model versions side by side with weighted traffic splitting. Models-as-a-Service sets token limits per subscription.
