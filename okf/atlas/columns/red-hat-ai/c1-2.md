## Answer

Not covered. OGX ABAC and MCP gateway AuthPolicy (both Technology Preview) do not provide per-agent action scopes in connected systems.

Research detail: OGX applies route-level authorisation and resource-level ABAC, where users can read system resources and only owners can change their own resources. The MCP gateway uses Connectivity Link AuthPolicy and rate limiting resources. Both are Technology Preview and per-tool authorisation is not described.
