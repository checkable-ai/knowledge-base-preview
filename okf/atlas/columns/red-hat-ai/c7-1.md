## Answer

Not covered. No tamper-evident store and no per-action record of inputs, outputs and reasoning by user, agent and session. OGX authorisation decisions log only at DEBUG, which falls short.

Last checked 13 November 2026.

Research detail: OGX logs authentication and authorisation decisions, including route and ABAC decisions with user, resource and action, without logging tokens. OpenShift audit records show changes to the OpenShift AI Operator configuration. Prompts, outputs and reasoning are not recorded in an audit ledger.

## Products

OGX audit logging, OpenShift audit records

## Gaps
