## Answer

Not covered. The Models-as-a-Service dashboard gives token, request and rate-limit data per subscription, exportable for showback. It does not cover infrastructure cost, staff time or what agents deliver. Red Hat positions it as showback, not billing.

Research detail: The Models-as-a-Service dashboard shows token consumption, request counts and rate limit violations per subscription, and usage data can be exported for cost attribution. Red Hat says it is for showback, not billing. External metering with cost tracking is Developer Preview.
