## Answer

Not covered. EvalHub with garak and automated risk assessment (both Technology Preview) tests models. Attack testing of retrieval, tools and runtime is not a documented supported feature. MCP action testing is only on the roadmap.

Research detail: EvalHub runs the garak scanner, which Red Hat says is infused with Chatterbox Labs techniques, against OWASP LLM top 10, AVID and CWE benchmarks. Automated risk assessment, a Technology Preview, generates prompts per harm category, applies increasingly aggressive attack strategies and reports which techniques succeed, with results in MLflow. Red Hat describes the Chatterbox Labs technology as active probing for prompt injection, jailbreaking and data leakage, with agent response and MCP action testing on its roadmap.

## Products

EvalHub with garak, automated risk assessment, Chatterbox Labs AI assurance technology

## Gaps
