## Answer

Not covered. Red Hat certifications cover managed services; no IRAP, ISO 42001 or red-team attestation for self-managed Red Hat AI.

Research detail: Red Hat lists Red Hat OpenShift AI among the products certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and its 2025 consolidated commercial audit, covering SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, 27017, 27018 and PCI DSS, records first-time certifications for OpenShift AI with zero findings. These cover the Red Hat managed services. The self-managed platform in this column runs in the customer's environment, so its operational assurance and any IRAP assessment belong to the customer deployment. No IRAP assessment, ISO 42001 certificate or AI red-team attestation for Red Hat AI was found.
