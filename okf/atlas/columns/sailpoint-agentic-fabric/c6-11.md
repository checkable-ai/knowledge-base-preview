## Answer

Covered. Evidence reflects only data in SailPoint's platform. Customer compliance, legal or audit teams must supplement it.

Source statement: "Exporting a framework generates all evidence reports associated with the framework." (https://documentation.sailpoint.com/saas/help/agentic_fabric/audit_compliance.html)

Research detail: Built-in and custom frameworks group evidence reports (inventories, credential exposure, governance action log, MCP clients, tools). Reports export as zip files on demand or on schedules from daily to semi-annual, with an export history.
