## Answer

Not covered. The Govern page documents credential grant and revoke, expiration, rotation and just-in-time access for agents. It does not say that secrets are kept from the model, or that agents get brokered short-lived scoped credentials. Prompt redaction is a separate data-loss feature and does not broker credentials.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: The Credentials page inventories IAM access keys, PATs, client secrets, SAML certificates, service account keys and API keys with owner and status (Active, Expired, Inactive). Just-In-Time entitlements provision access only for an activation period on direct-connect sources, and non-human accounts can be disabled. Secret rotation appears only in product page claims.

## Products

Agentic Fabric Credentials, Privilege on Demand Just-In-Time access, Non-Human Accounts

## Gaps

No documented rotation, vaulting or brokered delivery that keeps secrets out of model context. The Credentials page is view and filter only.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pair with a secrets manager or credential broker. Ask SailPoint for documentation of secret rotation and Entro remediation.
