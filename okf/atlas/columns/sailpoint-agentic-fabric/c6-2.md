## Answer

Not covered. No submission and approval workflow for new agents, models or tools. Sanction classification is only a record and is not enforced. Business app classification falls short of registration and approval.

Research detail: Administrators classify business apps as Sanctioned, Unsanctioned or Unknown, and agents inherit that status. Activation requests can pass through approval chains. There is no submission workflow for registering a new agent, model or tool.
