## Answer

Covered. Endpoint agent needs supported macOS or Windows; sanction status is not enforced; browser extension captures personal activity.

Source statement: "discover AI agent software that is installed and running on your organization's managed laptops and desktops" (https://documentation.sailpoint.com/saas/help/agentic_fabric/onboarding/sensors/endpoint_sensor.html)

Research detail: Endpoint agents on macOS and Windows discover installed AI agent software, MCP clients and credentials. A browser extension detects sign-ins to sanctioned and unsanctioned web apps and logs uploads and pastes. Cloud connectors (AWS Bedrock, Microsoft Entra) and EDR or SIEM feeds add agents, and business apps are classified Sanctioned, Unsanctioned or Unknown.

## Products

Endpoint Agent Security, Browser Agent Security, AWS and Entra connectors, Business Apps

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Sanction status is not enforced. The endpoint agent needs macOS 26 or Windows 11. The extension captures personal activity, which raises notice and consent duties.
