## Answer

Covered. Cloud agents appear when registered in Agent 365. Local agents need Defender for Endpoint Plan 2. Inventory is discovered, not registered.

Source statement: "Microsoft Defender provides a centralized inventory of AI agents in your organization and assesses their security posture." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-xdr/security-for-ai/ai-agent-inventory)

Research detail: Defender keeps an inventory of AI agents (cloud and local), AI models and MCP servers, queryable in the AgentsInfo table with tools, knowledge sources and authentication. Defender CSPM builds an AI BOM, and the Security Dashboard for AI combines agents, models, MCP servers and other AI apps.
