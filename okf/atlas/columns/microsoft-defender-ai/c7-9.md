## Answer

Covered. Defender for Endpoint Plan 2, commercial cloud only. macOS discovery is preview. Blocking unsanctioned apps uses Defender for Endpoint with network protection.

Source statement: "Microsoft Defender can discover local AI agents and their configured MCP servers after it observes agent activity on onboarded devices, then surfaces them in the Microsoft Defender portal." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/local-agent-discovery-overview)

Research detail: Defender for Endpoint discovers local AI agents such as Claude Code, Cursor, ChatGPT Desktop and OpenClaw, and their MCP servers, on Windows and macOS with Plan 2. Defender for Cloud Apps flags untagged generative AI apps and blocks unsanctioned apps through Defender for Endpoint. Defender CSPM discovers AI workloads across Azure, Bedrock and Vertex AI.
