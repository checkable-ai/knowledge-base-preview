## Answer

Not covered. No credential issuance, short-lived scoping, rotation or revocation. Credential theft alerts and secret exfiltration detection are detection only.

Research detail: Defender does not broker or rotate credentials. It detects credential exposure instead: a credential theft alert fires when credentials appear in model responses, agent detections cover secret and credential leakage, and AI model scanning looks for exposed secrets in model files.
