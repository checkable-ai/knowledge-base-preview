## Answer

Not covered. Owners are for remediation tasks, not accountable owners of every agent, model and tool, and no process keeps them current. Nearby feature: AgentsInfo ownership field.

Research detail: Defender for Cloud governance rules assign an owner and due date to recommendations by tag or email, with weekly notices and a governance report. The Security Dashboard for AI assigns recommendations to users or groups with due dates, and AgentsInfo records agent ownership.
