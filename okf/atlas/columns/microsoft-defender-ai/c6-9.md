## Answer

Covered. Owner or Security Admin role on the scope. Exemptions mark Mitigated or Risk accepted with description and optional expiry. Preview recommendations may not support exemptions. No periodic review workflow.

Source statement: "Your organization might decide to accept the risks related to the specific resource or recommendation." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/defender-for-cloud/exempt-resource)

Research detail: Defender for Cloud exemptions mark a recommendation or resource as Mitigated or Risk accepted (waiver) with a description and optional expiry date, limited to Owner or Security Admin roles. The Security Dashboard for AI lets users skip recommendations.
