## Answer

Covered. Local agent discovery needs Defender for Endpoint Plan 2 (macOS is preview). Risk levels need Microsoft 365 E7 or Agent 365. Reach is inferred from identity permissions and configuration.

Source statement: "Visual relationships between local AI agents, devices, identities, and the resources those identities can access, to help assess potential impact." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/defender-endpoint/local-agent-discovery-overview)

Research detail: The agent page shows an attack surface map of agents, devices, identities and resources related to each agent, and the exposure graph tables let analysts query what a local agent can reach through its user's permissions. Defender CSPM attack path analysis covers AI workloads and grounding data across Azure, AWS and GCP.
