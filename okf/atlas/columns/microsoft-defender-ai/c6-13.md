## Answer

Not covered. Vendor pages give Azure-level IRAP only. Defender AI features are not stated to be in scope, and no ISO 42001 statement for Defender.

Research detail: Microsoft's IRAP page lists Azure and Office 365 among the platforms assessed at PROTECTED, and Azure's IRAP page covers in-scope Azure services across the four Australian regions, with the service list in the Service Trust Portal. Defender for Endpoint stores data in Azure data centres including Australia. Defender-specific SOC, ISO or IRAP scope is not stated on the pages read.
