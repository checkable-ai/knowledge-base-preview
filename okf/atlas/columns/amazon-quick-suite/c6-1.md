## Answer

Partly covered. No consolidated register of models, agents and tools with their components. Models are not listed, and the asset console is documented for finding and transferring assets owned by users.

Source statement: "Use the asset management console to find and transfer assets that a departed user owned." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/user-lifecycle-data-handling.html)

Research detail: The Agent Registry integration surfaces registered MCP servers as connectors, and the asset management console lets administrators find and transfer assets owned by users. Chat agents, spaces and connectors are managed in the console.
