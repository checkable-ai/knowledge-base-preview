## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Custom permissions are available in Enterprise Edition only.

Source statement: "In Enterprise edition, you can use custom permissions profiles to restrict the capabilities and features that people can access in AWS Quick." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/custom-permissions.html)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Custom permissions profiles (Enterprise) scope down capabilities by account, role and user, and cover creating and sharing chat agents, flows, apps, spaces and skills. Deny by Default blocks new AI capabilities until allowed. Service control policies can deny the API operations that create agents, flows, spaces and knowledge bases, and sign-up can be restricted.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Custom permissions, Deny by Default, service control policies, Quick Automate automation groups

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Custom permissions can only restrict and an account administrator can change them. Enterprise subscription needed.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Apply Deny by Default to the AI category and use SCPs for organisation-wide limits.
