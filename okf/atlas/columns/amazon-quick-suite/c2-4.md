## Answer

Covered.

Source statement: "AWS Quick stores conversations for up to 90 days, and you can access them in the left navigation pane." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/using-quick-chat.html)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Chat conversations are kept per user for 90 days of history, and documents uploaded in chat are deleted with the conversation after 90 days of inactivity. Retrieval uses the signed-in person's permissions. Desktop code runs keep variables across runs within a conversation.

## Products

## Gaps
