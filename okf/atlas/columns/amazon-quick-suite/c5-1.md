## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The human-in-the-loop task center is part of Quick Automate, available only for accounts provisioned through the AWS Management Console, not Free or Plus accounts.

Source statement: "Human-in-the-loop (HITL) tasks enable you to incorporate human judgment at critical points in your automated processes." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/hitl-task-center.html)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The human-in-the-loop task center lists tasks needing review with status, severity, due date and assignee. Users or groups can be assigned, tasks can be chat or form tasks and unresolvable tasks archived as exceptions. Quick Automate can create these tasks when human oversight is needed.
