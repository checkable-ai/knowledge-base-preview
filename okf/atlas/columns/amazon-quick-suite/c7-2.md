## Answer

Partly covered. No built-in detections, no real-time alerting (the guide says the service does not include it) and no named owner for response. Session and user correlation is left to the customer's own log queries.

Source statement: "CloudTrail events route to Amazon EventBridge on a best-effort basis." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/incident-response-logging-and-monitoring.html)

Research detail: CloudTrail events route to EventBridge on a best-effort basis so rules can send them to Lambda, SNS or SQS for automated response. Vended logs go to CloudWatch, S3 or Firehose, and CloudWatch masking policies can mask sensitive data.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

EventBridge, CloudWatch Logs, masking policies

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No built-in detections or response ownership. Session and actor correlation is by the customer's own log joins.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Stream logs to the SIEM and write detections for admin and connector changes.
