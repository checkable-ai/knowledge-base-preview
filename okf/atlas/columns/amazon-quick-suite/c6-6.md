## Answer

Source statement: "Owners can edit, share, and delete the chat agent." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/working-with-agents.html)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Administrators assign owners to the system chat agent, apps and extensions have owners, and orphaned assets can be transferred to another user. The Inactive users list flags removed directory users so their assets can be reassigned.
