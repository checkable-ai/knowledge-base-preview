## Answer

Escalated for a person to settle. The agent checks did not agree on this finding.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: For knowledge bases with document-level ACLs, Quick enforces synchronised document permissions on each retrieval and returns nothing if permissions cannot be evaluated. Row and column level security applies to BI data and embedding. Guidance points to Macie and CloudWatch masking for sensitive data.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

ACL-aware knowledge bases, RLS and CLS, CloudWatch Logs masking

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

ACLs are permanent and fixed at creation, sync every 24 hours by default and are not compatible with Research. No prompt or output DLP is documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Create knowledge bases with ACLs and add DLP outside Quick.
