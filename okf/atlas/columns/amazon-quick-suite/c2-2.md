## Answer

Partly covered. Model names and versions are not listed or pinned per agent, routing cannot be configured, and CloudWatch and CloudTrail logs do not record the Region where inference ran. Bedrock controls such as service control policies and inference-profile permissions are not available.

Source statement: "Cross-Region inference requests stay within the AWS Regions in your geography." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/regions.html)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: AI features use cross-Region inference within a geography, and Australia is a geography covering Sydney and Melbourne. Stored data stays in the primary Region, while prompts and results might move to another Region in the geography. Quick manages the routing and customers cannot configure it. Customer data is not used to train the underlying models.
