## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Audit reports are obtained through AWS Artifact, a separate AWS service. FedRAMP authorised Regions must be confirmed on the linked AWS pages. In-scope status does not make a customer's workload compliant.

Source statement: "Third-party auditors assess the security and compliance of AWS Quick as part of multiple AWS compliance programs." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/sec-compliance.html)

Research detail: The compliance page lists FedRAMP, HIPAA eligibility, PCI DSS, SOC 1, SOC 2 and SOC 3, ISO 9001, 27001, 27018 and 27019, BSI C5 and HITRUST as programs that include Quick, with reports through AWS Artifact. Cross-Region inference has an Australia geography with Sydney and Melbourne.
