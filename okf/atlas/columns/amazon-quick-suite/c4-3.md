## Answer

Covered.

Source statement: "They authenticate with the individual user's personal credentials and act as the invoking user." (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/sec-agentic-security.html)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: On-demand actions authenticate with the individual user's own credentials and act as that user, bounded by that user's connector permissions. Per-user OAuth keeps the initiating identity on downstream calls. Automated workflows instead use service-level authentication.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

On-demand actions with 3LO, Default OAuth app

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Scheduled and event-triggered workflows are not bounded by any one user's permissions and can reach whatever the service credential permits.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use on-demand actions for user-specific work and review the reach of service-level credentials before approving automations.
