## Answer

Escalated for a person to settle. The agent checks did not agree on this finding.

Research detail: Quick encrypts data at rest by default and supports customer managed KMS keys on Enterprise. Knowledge bases can enforce source document ACLs, BI data supports row and column level security, stored data stays in the primary Region and unsubscribing permanently deletes all data including the index.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Customer managed KMS keys, ACL-aware knowledge bases, RLS and CLS

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Key and ACL choices are permanent and must be made before creating resources. User feedback is not encrypted with the customer key.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Register the customer managed key and choose ACL-aware knowledge bases before onboarding.
