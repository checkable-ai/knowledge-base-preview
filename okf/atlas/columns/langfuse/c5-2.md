## Answer

Covered, with conditions. On Langfuse Cloud, evaluators run in Langfuse's infrastructure. On self-hosted deployments, the worker container needs network access to the LLM provider or gateway named in the LLM connection. Each evaluation incurs judge model costs. On Langfuse Cloud, the alert limit per organisation is 2 on Hobby, 20 on Core, 50 on Pro and 100 on Enterprise. Self-hosted alerts need Langfuse v4 or later, and self-hosted v4 has no alert limit.

Source statement: "Evaluate live production traffic to monitor your LLM application performance in real-time." (https://langfuse.com/docs/evaluation/evaluation-methods/llm-as-a-judge)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: LLM-as-a-Judge evaluators run continuously on matching production observations with filters and sampling. Datasets and experiments support offline validation against versioned test sets. Alerts fire on threshold breaches of scores, cost or latency over a time window.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

LLM-as-a-Judge, code evaluators, Datasets and Experiments, Alerts

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Anomaly detection is threshold-based only. No statistical baselining is documented. Trace-level evaluators are deprecated on Cloud from 16 November 2026.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Build evaluators per risk, calibrate against human labels, and route alerts to Slack or signed webhooks.
