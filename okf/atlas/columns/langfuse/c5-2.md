## Answer

Covered. Live evaluators (LLM-as-a-judge) run on production observations; LLM judge costs apply; sampling is available. Anomaly detection is threshold-based.

Source statement: "When an incoming observation matches a rule's filters, the rule triggers its attached evaluators." (https://langfuse.com/docs/evaluation/evaluation-methods/llm-as-a-judge)

Research detail: LLM-as-a-Judge evaluators run continuously on matching production observations with filters and sampling. Datasets and experiments support offline validation against versioned test sets. Alerts fire on threshold breaches of scores, cost or latency over a time window.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

LLM-as-a-Judge, code evaluators, Datasets and Experiments, Alerts

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Anomaly detection is threshold-based only. No statistical baselining is documented. Trace-level evaluators are deprecated on Cloud from 16 November 2026.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Build evaluators per risk, calibrate against human labels, and route alerts to Slack or signed webhooks.
