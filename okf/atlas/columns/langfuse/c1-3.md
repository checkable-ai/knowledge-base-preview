## Answer

Not covered. No pre-execution approval gate for customer agent actions; annotation queues are retrospective and Halo approval covers only Halo.

Research detail: Annotation queues let humans score outputs after the fact, but there is no pre-execution approval bound to a proposed agent action, with denial or replay handling.
