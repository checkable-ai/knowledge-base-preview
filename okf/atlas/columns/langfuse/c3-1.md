## Answer

Not covered. The Langfuse AI Gateway (public beta) is a central entry point with keys and approved models, but documents no quotas, rate limits or enforced cost caps per agent, user or workload (hard spend caps are roadmap only), and no prompt-injection screening. Spend is tracked on a dashboard, not limited.

Research detail: Langfuse does not route or rate-limit model traffic. Its documentation points to gateways such as LiteLLM, OpenRouter or Portkey, and its cost data can feed external rate limiting through the Metrics API.
