## Answer

Not covered. Prompt registry and model price definitions are not a register of models, agents and tools.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Langfuse keeps a central prompt registry with versions and labels, and a list of model definitions for pricing. It does not register agents or tools, schemas or software components.
