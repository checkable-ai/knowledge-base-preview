## Answer

Not covered. Agent graphs show executed paths in observed traces, not reachable systems or permissions.

Research detail: Agent graphs render a trace's agent, tool and retriever steps as a graph, aggregated or expanded. This shows what an agent did in observed runs, not its full reach or potential action scopes against downstream systems.
