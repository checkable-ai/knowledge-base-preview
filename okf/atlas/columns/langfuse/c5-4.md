## Answer

Not covered. Only application-level latency and counts; no host or container load or infrastructure anomaly monitoring.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Dashboards show application latency including P95 and P99, request counts and throughput, and alerts can fire on latency thresholds. Langfuse does not monitor host or container load.
