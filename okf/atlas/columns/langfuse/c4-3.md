## Answer

Not covered. No propagation of a person's identity or permissions to downstream systems; userId on traces is an unauthenticated label.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Langfuse can attach a userId attribute to traces for analytics, but it does not propagate user identity or delegated authority to downstream services.
