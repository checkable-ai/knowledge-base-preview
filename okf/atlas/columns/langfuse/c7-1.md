## Answer

Not covered. Audit logs cover administrative changes only. Traces can be deleted, and no write-once or integrity-validated storage is documented.

Research detail: Traces record inputs, outputs, tool calls and user and session identifiers. Enterprise audit logs record administrative actions by user or API key with before and after state, and are described as immutable. Trace data can be deleted by users with permission and through retention.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Tracing, Audit Logs (Enterprise or self-hosted EE)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Immutability of audit logs is asserted but the mechanism is not documented. Trace records are deletable, so they are not a tamper-proof ledger. Audit log retention period is not stated.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Export traces and audit logs to write-once blob storage with object lock, and restrict traces:delete scope.
