## Answer

Not covered. Langfuse sessions group traces for observability. They are not memory or resumable state for customer agents. Saved Halo conversations are only the built-in assistant's chat history.

Research detail: Langfuse sessions group traces from one conversation for replay and scoring. They are an observability record, not agent session state the agent reads or resumes from.
