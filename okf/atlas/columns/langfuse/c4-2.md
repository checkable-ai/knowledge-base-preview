## Answer

Not covered. Langfuse does not issue, rotate or revoke short-lived scoped credentials for the agents a customer runs, and it does not keep secrets away from those agents' models. Project API keys only authenticate to Langfuse. The run-specific MCP API keys are internal to Halo, Langfuse's own assistant. The self-hosting Halo page mentions their revocation only as a step in conversation cleanup. It does not describe how they are issued, what they can reach or how long they last.

Research detail: Langfuse stores LLM provider credentials, encrypted with ENCRYPTION_KEY, only for its own playground and evaluator calls. It does not broker credentials to agents or their tools.

## Options
