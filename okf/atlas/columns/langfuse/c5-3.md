## Answer

Not covered. Tracks token usage and model cost only. No infrastructure cost, staff time or value delivered measured against cost.

Research detail: Langfuse calculates token usage and USD cost per generation from ingested values or model price definitions, with pricing tiers, dashboards by model, tag or user, and cost alerts. It does not cover infrastructure or labour cost, savings or output yield.

## Products

## Gaps
