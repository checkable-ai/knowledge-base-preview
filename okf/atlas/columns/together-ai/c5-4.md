## Answer

Covered. Dedicated endpoints only; Prometheus metrics endpoint is beta; serverless not covered; no alerting described.

Source statement: "Dedicated model inference records latency, throughput, and utilization metrics for every endpoint and deployment." (https://docs.together.ai/docs/dedicated-endpoints/monitoring.md)

Research detail: Dedicated endpoints report latency, throughput, utilisation, time to first token and KV-cache metrics by edge, router and worker stage, with an events feed and a beta Prometheus endpoint for Grafana or Datadog.
