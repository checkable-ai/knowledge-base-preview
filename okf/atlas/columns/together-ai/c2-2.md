## Answer

Covered. Serverless model IDs may be redirected to upgraded versions; serverless has no region selection; ZDR must be enabled by an admin; dedicated endpoints (including EU regions) give fixed model and hardware.

Source statement: "Requests to the original model ID are automatically routed to the upgraded version" (https://docs.together.ai/docs/deprecations.md)

Research detail: Serverless, batch and dedicated inference serve named models. Dedicated endpoints run on reserved hardware with replica autoscaling, and the deprecation page sets notice periods and redirect rules. Privacy settings control prompt storage and ZDR, and serverless rate limits are best-effort with provisioned throughput under SLA.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Serverless inference, Batch API, Dedicated endpoints, Zero Data Retention

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Serverless model IDs can be redirected to upgraded versions after 3 days notice. Serverless has no region selection.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use dedicated endpoints for version and region control, and enable ZDR at organisation level.
