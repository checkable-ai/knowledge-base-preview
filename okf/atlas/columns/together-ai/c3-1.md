## Answer

Not covered. Only organisation-level prepaid cut-off; API keys have no spend or rate limits, and no routing or injection screening.

Research detail: Together enforces organisation-level prepaid cut-offs and contracted rate limits, and dedicated endpoints can split traffic by weight. Cost analytics attributes usage to projects and API keys in beta. There is no per-agent or per-key quota, routing policy or injection screening.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Spend controls, Rate limits, Dedicated endpoint traffic split

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

API keys cannot have spend caps or rate limits. Project budgets and alerts are planned. The cut-off does not guarantee an exact cap.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Place an external model gateway in front of Together for per-agent quotas and screening.
