## Answer

Covered. Prompt storage is on by default; an admin must turn it off in Privacy settings; ZDR is not retroactive; uploaded files persist until deleted; residency via dedicated endpoints including EU regions, by arrangement.

Source statement: "ZDR applies only to requests sent after you enable it." (https://docs.together.ai/docs/zero-data-retention.md)

Research detail: By default Together stores prompts and responses and may use them for product improvement. Admins can enable organisation-level ZDR, training use is opt-in, and stored data can be deleted. Fine-tuning and batch files are retained until deleted, while metadata and account data remain. EU and VPC deployments are by arrangement.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Zero Data Retention, Privacy settings, Enterprise data residency

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Storage is on by default. ZDR is not retroactive. Serverless has no region choice. The batch page says files are kept 7 days, which conflicts with the ZDR page. Sandbox retention is not stated.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable ZDR before first use, delete uploaded files after jobs and contract for residency.
