## Answer

Not covered. Sandbox snapshots and interpreter sessions keep state, but no session access rules, resume policy or retention after expiry are documented.

Research detail: Code interpreter sessions keep state across calls, belong to the caller and carry an expiry timestamp. Sandboxes keep state until terminated and can be snapshotted. Chat inference is stateless, with history held by the client.
