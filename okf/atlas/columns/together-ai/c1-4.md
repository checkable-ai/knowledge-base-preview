## Answer

Not covered. No agent provisioning, change or retirement process. Project, member and key management and SSO (Scale and Enterprise only) manage people and keys, not agents. SCIM is not available.

Research detail: Org admins create projects and manage members, project admins manage collaborators, and admins and editors create and revoke project API keys. SSO adds and removes members from the identity provider. Role-based controls for fine-tuning, endpoints and serverless inference are still rolling out and all collaborators currently have full access.
