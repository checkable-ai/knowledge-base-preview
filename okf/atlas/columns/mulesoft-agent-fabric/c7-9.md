## Answer

Covered. Only platforms with a configured provider scanner and credentials. Unmanaged endpoints and devices are not discovered.

Source statement: "Scheduled or on-demand scans pick up releases and configuration drift according to the options your administrator allows." (https://docs.mulesoft.com/general/exp-scanners-add-from-providers)

Research detail: Provider scanners register agents, APIs and MCP servers found on supported platforms such as Microsoft Copilot Studio and Kong, on a schedule or on demand, to catch new releases and configuration drift.
