## Answer

Not covered. External vault integration keeps secret values out of MuleSoft, but MuleSoft does not issue short-lived scoped credentials. Rotation, expiry and revocation sit with the vault.

Research detail: External vault integration keeps secret values in AWS Secrets Manager, Azure Key Vault or HashiCorp Vault, and MuleSoft stores only metadata and never stores, displays or logs secret values. A model proxy can take its provider credential From Vault and resolve it at runtime, so callers do not hold the provider key. Rotation of the vault connection credential is done under Settings.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

External vaults, Model Proxy connections

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Documented use is the model proxy. Rotation, expiry and revocation of the stored secrets are left to the vault. Connector credentials (MCP, A2A, AI Chain) are not tied to vaults in the pages read.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use Assume Role or a certificate service principal for the vault link, rotate in the vault, and test runtime behaviour when a secret is revoked.
