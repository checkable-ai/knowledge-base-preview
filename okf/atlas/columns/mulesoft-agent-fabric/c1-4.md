## Answer

Not covered. Catalogue lifecycle management, agent network projects and quarantine exist, but agent identities and permissions are created in the external IdP and retirement of discovered agents is not described.

Research detail: Agent network projects declare assets and policies in agent-network.yaml and publish them to Exchange, and brokers deploy to CloudHub 2.0 or Runtime Fabric. Assets are managed through their lifecycle from the catalogue page. A flagged agent can be quarantined and later restored.
