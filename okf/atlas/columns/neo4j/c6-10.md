## Answer

Not covered. No documented configurable rules for retention or deletion of agent inputs, outputs or memory. Aura Agent documents its location (Belgium) and that it stores no conversation history, but those are residency facts, not retention and deletion rules. Model provider retention is not stated.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Aura Agent runs in Belgium and does not store conversation history. Aura offers customer managed keys for data at rest. Deleting a NAMS workspace removes its managed database, and NAMS points to bring-your-own database for residency and retention needs.
