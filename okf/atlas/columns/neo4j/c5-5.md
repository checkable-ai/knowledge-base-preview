## Answer

Not covered. SHOW PRIVILEGES lists privileges inside Neo4j only. It gives no view of downstream systems an agent can reach.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: SHOW PRIVILEGES lists the privileges held by a role or user, and can output them as Cypher commands, which shows what an agent account can read or change in the graph.
