## Answer

Not covered. Only MCP servers have a disabled-by-default enable gate by an admin. No documented approval step for agents, no reviewer records, and models are not handled.

Last checked 13 November 2026.

Research detail: MCP servers registered by a Secure AI Agents builder are disabled by default and must be enabled by a Secure AI Agents admin before agents can connect, which acts as an approval gate. Agent registration has no documented approval step and models are out of scope.
