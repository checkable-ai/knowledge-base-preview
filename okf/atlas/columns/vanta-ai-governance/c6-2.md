## Answer

Not covered. AI use cases record how AI is used, and AI impact assessments can be approved. There is no documented flow to submit, register and approve a new model, agent or tool itself. Vendor intake in third-party risk management is vendor-focused, not a model, agent or tool approval flow.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Vanta has no documented intake and approval workflow specific to models, agents or tools. Third Party Risk Management offers an intake form for requesting new vendors, and risk assessments can go through up to five approval steps, which can be applied to AI use cases.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Vanta Third Party Risk Management (Intake form), Vanta Risk Management (approval steps)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No AI use case registration form or approval gate tied to the AI inventory is documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use vendor intake for external AI services and a dedicated risk register with approvals for internal AI use cases.
