## Answer

Covered. AI Governance inventory is early access. Component-level records are not described.

Source statement: "visibility into the MCPs and tools they can access" (https://www.vanta.com/products/ai-governance)

Research detail: The AI Governance page shows an AI inventory listing agents with their models, risk levels, status and number of employees with access, plus the MCP servers and tools each can reach. ISO 42001 support lets teams define which AI systems and people are in scope. The general Vanta inventory does not list AI systems or models as an asset type.
