## Answer

Covered. Built-in risk scoring is in Core Risk Management. Custom scoring needs the Advanced Risk Management add-on. Agent-level ranking sits in the early access AI Governance product.

Source statement: "Risk scoring determines how each risk is rated, scored, and grouped into a risk level." (https://help.vanta.com/en/articles/15089318-risk-scoring)

Research detail: Vanta offers prebuilt AI risk scenarios with owners and linked controls, configurable scoring rubrics for inherent and residual risk, EU AI Act role and risk tier classification, and impact assessments aligned to ISO 42001 and the EU AI Act. AI Governance adds contextual risk ranking per agent.
