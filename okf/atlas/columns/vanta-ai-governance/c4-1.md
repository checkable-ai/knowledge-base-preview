## Answer

Not covered. No per-agent identity bound to owner, runtime and release. The AI inventory maps agents to owners but is discovery context, not an identity.

Research detail: Vanta AI Governance lists discovered agents in an AI inventory with model, risk level and status, but it does not issue, bind or attest runtime agent or workload identities. Checked the AI Governance product page, launch blog and ISO 42001 page.
