## Answer

Not covered. No documented mechanism that limits what an agent may do in connected systems. Vanta's policies and boundary detection (early access) record and alert on activity but enforcement and supported platforms are undocumented.

Research detail: The AI Governance page says teams can set custom risk tiers and policies that restrict agents to the data, tools and permissions they need and control who can use them. The launch blog says Vanta detects when an agent operates outside defined boundaries and gives a repository access example. The product is early access and the enforcement mechanism is not documented.
