## Answer

Not covered. Policy and detection only. No sandboxing, egress limits, kill switch or credential revocation.

Research detail: The AI Governance product page says teams can define what agents may do, restrict access to the data, tools and permissions an agent needs, apply risk tiers and policies, and detect activity outside approved boundaries. It does not describe sandboxing, egress control or stopping an agent.
