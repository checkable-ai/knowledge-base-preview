## Answer

Covered. Confirmation prompts GA on Serverless and Stack 9.4+; custom tool confirmation setting planned for Stack.

Source statement: "Select **Allow** to proceed or **Deny** to cancel." (https://www.elastic.co/docs/explore-analyze/ai-features/agent-builder/chat)

Research detail: LLM observability traces let a reviewer inspect the exact prompt and response of a request. Agent Builder traces record agent, model and tool call spans, and when an administrator opts in to content capture they hold prompts, responses, system prompts and tool arguments and results. There is no review queue or sign-off workflow for outputs.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Elastic Observability LLM and agent monitoring, Agent Builder traces

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Content capture is off by default. Review is ad hoc in Discover or dashboards.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable content capture for reviewed agents and build a review dashboard or case workflow on the trace data streams.
