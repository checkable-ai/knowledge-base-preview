## Answer

Covered. IRAP scope is Elastic Cloud Hosted in Australian regions; ISO 42001 not listed.

Source statement: "We have completed our assessment against the Information Security Registered Assessors Program (IRAP) at the Protected Level!" (https://www.elastic.co/blog/achieving-irap-protected-level-assessment-elastic-cloud)

Research detail: Elastic states an IRAP assessment at the PROTECTED level for Elastic Cloud Hosted in the AWS ap-southeast-2, Azure australiaeast and GCP australia-southeast1 regions, announced on 21 January 2025. The Trust Center lists SOC 2, SOC 3, ISO 27001, 27017 and 27018, CSA STAR, FedRAMP High and Moderate and others, with reports on assurance.elastic.co. Serverless has SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, 27017, 27018, PCI DSS, HIPAA and CSA STAR.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Elastic Cloud Hosted, Elastic Cloud Serverless

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

IRAP scope is Elastic Cloud Hosted in three Australian regions, and Serverless is not stated as in scope. The Trust Center labels the IRAP badge Protected B, which conflicts with the blog's PROTECTED wording. Assessment date and assessor are not stated. ISO 42001 is not listed.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Request the IRAP report and confirm whether Agent Builder and Serverless are in scope. Treat the Protected B label as an error until Elastic confirms.
