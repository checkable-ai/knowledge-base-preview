## Answer

Not covered. Audit logs and traces are not documented as write-once or integrity-validated; traces omit content unless opted in.

Research detail: Elasticsearch and Kibana audit logs record authentication, authorisation decisions and other security events on certain subscription levels. Agent Builder traces record each agent, model and tool call per round, on by default from 9.5, in regular data streams. Elastic's own guidance says traces do not record who approved an action and recommends a separate append-only data stream with a write-only role.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Elasticsearch audit logging, Agent Builder traces

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No tamper protection is described for audit logs or traces. Traces hold structure only unless content capture is enabled. A superuser can still alter cluster data, so the blog advises shipping records off the cluster.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Ship audit and trace data to a separate write-once store and record approval decisions in an append-only stream.
