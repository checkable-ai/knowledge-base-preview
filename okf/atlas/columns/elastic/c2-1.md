## Answer

Covered. Elastic Workflows; GA on Stack from 9.4; subscription and version requirements apply.

Source statement: "The workflow fails when all retries are exhausted, unless paired with `fallback` or `continue`." (https://www.elastic.co/docs/explore-analyze/workflows/authoring-techniques/pass-data-handle-errors)

Research detail: Elastic Workflows run ordered YAML steps from manual, scheduled or alert triggers. Workflows can call agents through the ai.agent step, and agents can call workflows as tools or as pre-execution workflows that can cancel a run. Retry, cancellation and recovery behaviour were not found on the overview pages.
