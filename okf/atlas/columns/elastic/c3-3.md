## Answer

Covered. Connector DLS is beta and not on all subscriptions. Aggregations can reveal restricted data. Anonymisation only in Security AI features.

Source statement: "Document level security restricts the documents that users have read access to." (https://www.elastic.co/docs/deploy-manage/users-roles/cluster-or-deployment-auth/controlling-access-at-document-field-level)

Research detail: Elasticsearch DLS limits readable documents per role query, and templated role queries filter on the current user's attributes. FLS limits readable fields. Content connectors for SharePoint, Confluence, Google Drive and others sync source permissions for DLS. The Security AI Assistant can anonymise or block fields before sending them to the LLM.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Elasticsearch DLS and FLS, connector DLS, AI Assistant anonymization

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Connector DLS is beta and not on all subscriptions. Aggregations can reveal restricted data. Field anonymisation is limited to Security AI features, with no general DLP.
