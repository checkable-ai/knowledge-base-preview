## Answer

Not covered. ILM and data stream lifecycle delete index data, but no documented retention, deletion or residency rules for agent inputs, outputs and memory.

Research detail: ILM moves indices through phases and its delete phase removes data after a set period. On Serverless, data stream lifecycle sets retention. Conversation retention and residency controls were not found in fetched pages.
