## Answer

Not covered. Conversations are private by default and shareable, but no retention or expiry and no session resume are documented.

Research detail: Agent Builder conversations belong to one Kibana space, are private by default and can be shared without granting extra privileges. The Security AI Assistant saves per-user chat history with Private, Shared or Restricted visibility. Conversation retention settings were not found.
