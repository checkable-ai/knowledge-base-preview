## Answer

Not covered. No accountable owner field; creator permissions are access control only.

Research detail: Creators of experiments, registered models and gateway resources get MANAGE permission, and roles assign access to teams. Descriptions and tags can record owners. There is no dedicated accountable owner field or ownership review. Unity Catalog on Databricks has object ownership that can be granted to a group.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Basic auth RBAC, registry tags, Unity Catalog ownership (Databricks)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Ownership is permission-based, not accountability-based.

## Options
