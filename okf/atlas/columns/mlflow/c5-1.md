## Answer

Covered. Review queues are experimental (MLflow 3.14.0 or later). Assigning reviewers by user name needs the tracking server run with authentication enabled.

Source statement: "Review queues are experimental (added in MLflow 3.14.0) and may change in a future release." (https://mlflow.org/docs/latest/genai/assessments/review-queues/)

Research detail: Review Queues (experimental, MLflow 3.14.0) let teams flag traces, assign reviewers, define label schemas and track pending, complete and declined status. Reviewers can also add feedback in the trace UI, attributed to their user, and override automated judge feedback while the original is kept for audit.
