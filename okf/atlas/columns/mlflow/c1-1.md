## Answer

Out of scope. MLflow traces, evaluates and serves agents that users build. It does not run those agents on a host or decide what they may do there. The permission settings on the MLflow AI Assistant page (https://mlflow.org/docs/latest/genai/getting-started/try-assistant/) apply only to MLflow's own beta helper. They are not a control over the agents a customer governs with MLflow, so host-level permitted actions do not apply to this product.
