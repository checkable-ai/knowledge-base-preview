## Answer

Not covered. No residency or permitted-use controls; trace archival is not deletion, and manual deletion and masking do not make a retention rule.

Research detail: Trace archival applies retention at server, workspace and experiment level and moves old span payloads to an archive location. Traces can be deleted by timestamp or ID. Client-side span processors redact sensitive data before it leaves the app. Self-hosting lets the operator choose where data lives. No permitted-use controls are documented.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Trace archival, trace deletion, trace masking

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Archival is not deletion, deletion is manual, no residency or permitted-use controls.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Schedule deletion jobs and set archive locations per workspace.
