## Answer

Covered. Covers Google's own SOC agents (Triage and Investigation Agent, Detection Engineering Agent), not customer agents.

Source statement: "Each investigation opens in a detailed view that summarizes Gemini's analysis, its reasoning, and the supporting data it used." (https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/secops/triage-investigation-agent)

Research detail: Analysts review each Triage and Investigation Agent run through its timeline, summary, reasoning and supporting data, give thumbs up or down feedback on the verdict, and can re-run it. Playbooks can send low or medium confidence false positive verdicts to analyst review. Draft rules from the Detection Engineering Agent are not uploaded automatically and must be reviewed and created manually.
