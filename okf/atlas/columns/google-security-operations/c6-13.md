## Answer

Covered. Google Security Operations SIEM and SOAR are listed in Google Cloud's IRAP scope. Triage and Investigation Agent is not FedRAMP compliant. Gemini features' IRAP scope not stated.

Source statement: "Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP)" (https://docs.cloud.google.com/chronicle/docs/secops/compliance)

Research detail: Google Cloud's IRAP page states an independent assessor found Google Cloud strongly aligned with PROTECTED controls, lists Google Security Operations SIEM and SOAR in scope, and offers reports through Compliance Reports Manager. The SecOps compliance page lists FedRAMP High and Moderate, DoD IL2, IL4 and IL5, HIPAA and PCI DSS, and an Australia data boundary through Assured Workloads.

## Gaps

The IRAP page gives no assessment date. The Triage and Investigation Agent is stated as not FedRAMP compliant, and neither page says whether Gemini features are in IRAP scope. SOC 2 and ISO 27001 are not listed on the SecOps compliance page.
