## Answer

Partly covered. No configurable retention or deletion for Copilot prompts, generated outputs or memory. Data that already moved while the setting was on cannot be reversed.

Source statement: "When you allow data movement across regions, your inputs (prompts) and outputs (results) might move outside of your region to the location where the generative AI feature is hosted." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/geographical-availability-copilot)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Environments are bound to a geography, admins decide whether generative AI data may move across regions, and customer data is not used to train models unless the tenant opts in. Backup retention is an environment group rule and flow runs are kept for 30 days. Retention and deletion of Copilot prompts and generated content are not described.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Environment geography, data movement setting, backup retention rule, flow run retention

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Memory and prompt retention are not configurable. Australian processing may be in region or United States.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set environment geography and the data movement setting before enabling Copilot features.
