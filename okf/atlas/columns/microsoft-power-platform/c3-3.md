## Answer

Covered.

Source statement: "Create data policies that act as guardrails to help reduce the risk of users unintentionally exposing organizational data." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/wp-data-loss-prevention)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Data policies stop data flowing between Business and Non-business connectors, and managed environments add masking rules. Copilot answers only from data the user can access. These are connector level controls, not content inspection of prompts or outputs.
