## Answer

Not covered. Purview audit logs and the Office 365 Management API record admin, flow lifecycle, connector and Dataverse activity with user, time and result, but the documentation does not claim write-once or integrity-validated storage, and does not describe an export of agent action records to such storage. Run-level detail needs Application Insights or Dataverse auditing.

Research detail: Admin activity in Microsoft Purview is collected by default and cannot be disabled. It records environment lifecycle, settings, group rules, licensing and lockbox events with a common schema. Flow lifecycle and permission events are logged, but prompts, outputs and reasoning are not, and tamper proof storage is not claimed.
