## Answer

Partly covered. Model-driven apps have no owner. For cloud flows and agent flows the Owner column shows the creator and does not update when the owner changes. No accountable owner is recorded for models.

Source statement: "Proactively find resources owned by departing users to allow seamless ownership transfers and ensure business continuity." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/power-platform-inventory)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Inventory shows who owns each resource and finds those owned by departing users. Cloud flow owners can add co-owners, and the admin center supports ownership transfer. Accountability for the decisions an app makes is not modelled.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Power Platform inventory, flow owners and co-owners

## Gaps

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Review the departing owner list monthly and assign a service owner to every production flow.
