## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Approvals are a flow design choice added by the maker, not a platform-enforced gate. Approvals running longer than 30 days need to be stored in Microsoft Dataverse. A licence with standard connector rights (Power Automate, Office 365, or a Dynamics 365 licence with built-in Power Automate) is required to create approval flows. Power Automate US Government plans cannot automatically assign approvals to users outside the environment.

Source statement: "To create an approval workflow, add the Approvals - Start and wait for an approval action to any flow." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/modern-approvals)

Research detail: Power Automate has an Approvals action that pauses a flow for a named approver, who answers by email, the approvals centre or the app. Pipelines add approval based delegated deployments for production. Neither binds an approval to the exact action an AI proposes, and Copilot-built flows need the maker to confirm the design.

## Gaps

Replay and denial handling are not described. Approvals are a flow design choice, not a platform enforced gate for agent actions.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Require an approval step before any flow action that writes to a system of record, and require delegated deployments for production.
