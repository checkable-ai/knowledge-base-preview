## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Advanced connector policies apply to certified connectors only; custom and HTTP connectors stay under classic data policies. Design-time enforcement is rolling out portal by portal (Power Automate first, then Copilot Studio, then Power Apps), and until then ACP is runtime-only for that workload. In single-environment mode ACP works on managed and non-managed environments, but on non-managed environments nonblockable connectors stay nonblockable. Policies apply per environment or through environment groups. Blocking is at MCP server level, not per MCP tool. Sharing limits need managed environments.

Source statement: "Default-deny posture: All connectors and actions are blocked unless explicitly allowed." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/advanced-connector-policies)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Data policies classify connectors as Business, Non-business or Blocked, and advanced connector policies allow or block at connector and action level, including MCP servers. Policies apply per environment, environment group or tenant and take effect at design time and runtime. Sharing limits control who can share apps, flows and agents.

## Gaps
