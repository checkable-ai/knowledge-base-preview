## Answer

Not covered. No customer-facing hardening or scanning of the containers, agent software or hosts that run models and tools is documented. Nearby features (customer-managed key, Customer Lockbox, compliance reports) protect data and access, not the runtime hosts.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Power Apps and Power Automate are Microsoft operated services, so no customer facing assurance of inference containers, harness or hosts is offered beyond compliance reports. Related data protections include customer-managed keys and Customer Lockbox.
