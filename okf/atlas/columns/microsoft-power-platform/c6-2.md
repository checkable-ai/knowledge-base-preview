## Answer

Partly covered. No single intake form or workflow to submit and register a new agent, model or tool. Approval is split between connector allowlists (advanced connector policies), pipeline delegated deployments and sharing limits.

Source statement: "Secure production environments with approval based delegated deployments." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/alm/pipelines)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins approve connectors by allowlisting them in advanced connector policies, and pipelines add approval based delegated deployments for production. Sharing limits restrict who can distribute an app, flow or agent. There is no single intake form for registering a new agent or model.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Advanced connector policies, pipelines delegated deployments, sharing limits

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Approval is spread across tools and not a unified workflow.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Define the intake path in pipelines with approvals and tie connector allowlisting to it.
