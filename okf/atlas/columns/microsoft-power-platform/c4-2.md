## Answer

Not covered. No documented short-lived, scoped agent credentials with automatic rotation, expiry or revocation, and no documented isolation of secrets from the model. Connections are described only as saved credentials stored in the environment; customer-managed key revocation protects data at rest and is not a credential revocation control.

Research detail: Connectors use saved connections stored in the environment, and run-only users can supply their own connections. Pipelines can deploy with service principal connections where a connector supports them. Customer-managed keys can be revoked to lock an environment. Credential rotation, expiry and model-context isolation are not documented as features.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Connections and connection references, run-only user connections, service principal connections in pipelines

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No documented scoped credential delivery with automatic rotation or expiry. Revoking a customer-managed key protects data at rest and is not a credential revocation control for flows.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use service principal connections for production flows, rotate their secrets under the identity team, and keep maker-owned connections out of production environments.
