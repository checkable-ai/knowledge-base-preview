## Answer

Partly covered. No scheduled reassessment or retirement workflow for external suppliers. Custom and HTTP connectors are outside advanced connector policies and must be governed with classic data policies.

Source statement: "All connectors and actions are blocked unless explicitly allowed." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/advanced-connector-policies)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins can approve or block connectors, MCP servers and custom connectors through data policies and advanced connector policies, and new connectors are blocked by default under ACP. Periodic reassessment and retirement of third parties are not workflow features.

## Gaps
