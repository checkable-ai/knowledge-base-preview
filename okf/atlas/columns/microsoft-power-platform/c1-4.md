## Answer

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins control how apps, flows and environments are created and changed. Environment groups lock rules for member environments, managed environments gate premium controls, security roles decide who can create resources, and pipelines govern deployment between stages. Agent identities are not a provisioned object in these products.

## Products

## Gaps

No documented provisioning or retirement of agent identities. Retirement tooling sits in usage insights and inventory rather than a lifecycle workflow.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options
