## Answer

Partly covered. No proposal or intake stage and no retirement process for AI use cases. Pipelines do not carry Dataverse data and cannot deploy to a different tenant.

Source statement: "The same solution artifact must pass through pipeline stages in sequential order." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/alm/pipelines)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Pipelines move solutions through development, test and production stages with prevalidation, stored backups and required stage order. Environment types and environment groups separate dev from production. Proposal intake and retirement of AI use cases are not covered.
