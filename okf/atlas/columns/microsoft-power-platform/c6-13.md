## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The ISO/IEC 27001 page lists Power Apps and Power Automate as in scope in the commercial cloud and GCC and GCC High. The DoD listing does not include them. Audit reports and certificates are obtained from the Service Trust Portal. Downloading SOC audit reports from the Service Trust Portal requires an existing subscription or free trial account.

Source statement: "Power Apps cloud service either as a standalone service or as included in an Office 365 or Dynamics 365 branded plan or suite" (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/regulatory/offering-iso-27001)

Research detail: Microsoft says Power Platform services appear in its Azure compliance audit scope and lists ISO/IEC, SOC and Australia IRAP among the offerings. The pages read point to appendices and the Service Trust Portal for exact scope, so IRAP level and Power Platform coverage were not confirmed. No privacy impact or pen test material is published in the pages read.
