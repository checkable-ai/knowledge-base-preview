## Answer

Partly covered. No instant per-agent kill switch. No sandbox or egress policy for AI-generated code is documented. Generally available Copilot in cloud flows can only be turned off through Microsoft Support.

Source statement: "If a violation occurs, put the app, flow, or chatbot in to a suspended or quarantine state so that it can't operate." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/wp-data-loss-prevention)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: When a data policy changes, apps and flows that break it are put in a suspended or quarantine state and blocked connections fail at runtime. Admins can turn Copilot off per environment or tenant for preview features, and the CMK can be revoked to lock an environment. Full enforcement can take up to 24 hours.

## Products

Data policy quarantine, Copilot preview toggles, customer-managed key lock

## Gaps
