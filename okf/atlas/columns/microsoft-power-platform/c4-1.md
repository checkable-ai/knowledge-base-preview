## Answer

Not covered. No stable identity for an agent tied to an owner, runtime and release. Nearest features are flow owners and connections, and the Power Platform inventory, which record owners but do not issue an agent identity.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Power Apps and Power Automate document no stable agent or workload identity tied to an owner, runtime and release. Flows run through the connections their owners configure, and the inventory records owners. Agent identity work sits in the separately researched Copilot Studio and Dynamics agents.
