## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Viewing inventory needs a Microsoft Entra role: Global administrator, Power Platform administrator, Dynamics 365 administrator or Global reader see everything, while AI administrator and AI reader see AI resources only. Connector inventory is not available in GCC, GCC High or DoD. Inventory is not available in 21Vianet (China) or air-gapped environments.

Source statement: "Power Platform inventory captures the connectors and connector operations used by each resource." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/power-platform-inventory)

Research detail: Power Platform inventory gives admins a searchable view of agents, apps, flows and connectors across the tenant, shows which connectors and operations each resource uses, and exports to CSV. Resources appear within 15 minutes of change.
