## Answer

Covered.

Source statement: "A retry policy helps your workflows recover from transient failures that occur because of temporary or intermittent problems with a network or service." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/guidance/coding-guidelines/error-handling)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Power Automate is a workflow engine with triggers, actions, approvals that wait for people, default retry policies and run limits. Deleting a flow cancels pending runs and turning it off stops new runs. Delegation between agents and structured recovery for AI generated plans are not documented for these products.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Cloud flows, approvals, retry policies, flow run limits

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Plans in Power Apps generate solutions but are not a runtime orchestrator. Admin-side cancellation of one running flow is not described.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set retry policies deliberately and add approval or error branches to flows that Copilot generates.
