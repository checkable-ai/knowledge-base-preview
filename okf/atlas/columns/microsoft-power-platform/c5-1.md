## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Approvals are added by the flow maker, and review of Copilot output by the acting maker is advised, not enforced. Approvals running longer than 30 days need storage in Microsoft Dataverse. A licence with standard connector rights (Power Automate, Office 365, or a Dynamics 365 licence with built-in Power Automate) is required to create approval flows.

Source statement: "With Power Automate, you can manage the approval of documents or processes across several services, including SharePoint, Dynamics 365, Salesforce, OneDrive for work or school, Zendesk, or WordPress." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-automate/modern-approvals)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Copilot output is a draft the maker reviews before saving, and flow approvals put a person in the loop for a business decision. The FAQ tells users to check Copilot output before sharing it. There is no reviewer queue or sampling process.
