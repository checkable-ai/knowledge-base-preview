Research detail: Copilot in Power Apps and Power Automate runs on Azure OpenAI Service. Admins choose whether data may move across regions for generative AI, and customer data is not used for model training unless the tenant opts in. Model versions and per feature endpoints are not named in the pages read, and usage limits and throttling apply.
