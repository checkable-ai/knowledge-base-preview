## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Viewing inventory needs a Microsoft Entra role such as Global administrator, Power Platform administrator, Dynamics 365 administrator or Global reader (AI administrator and AI reader see AI resources only). Connector inventory is not available in GCC, GCC High or DoD. Inventory is not available in 21Vianet (China) or air-gapped environments. Power Pages sites in inventory are in preview.

Source statement: "Power Platform inventory gives tenant administrators a unified view of all agents, apps, flows, and sites built on Power Platform across their organization." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/admin/power-platform-inventory)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Power Platform inventory is a tenant wide register of agents, apps, flows, connectors, environments and environment groups, with operations used by each resource, owner and region. Data can be exported to CSV.
