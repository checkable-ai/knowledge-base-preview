## Answer

Covered. ZDR is for enterprise customers via account manager and excludes third-party vendor models; default retention up to 30 days.

Source statement: "By default, user's prompts and responses from model inference calls are retained for no longer than 30 days" (https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/data-retention-policy.md)

Research detail: Models are called by explicit model IDs through region-specific workspace endpoints, and the service deployment scope sets where inference runs. Prompts and responses are kept no longer than 30 days by default, with zero data retention available per workspace for listed models. Rate limits can be read and set per model, and model retirements are announced.
