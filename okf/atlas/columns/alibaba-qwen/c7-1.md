## Answer

Not covered. No documented per-agent record of tool calls, data access and changes keyed to agent, user or session, and no write-once or integrity-validated storage. Model Studio audit logs (ActionTrail) cover management operations without content, and inference logs with content are limited to the customer's own store in Beijing and Singapore with no tamper evidence stated.

Research detail: Audit logs are on by default and record request ID, time, model, tokens, latency and status for each call, queryable for 30 days. Inference logs add full prompts, responses and intermediate steps and are written to the customer's own Simple Log Service store, in Beijing and Singapore only. QwenCloud request logs are kept 14 days.
