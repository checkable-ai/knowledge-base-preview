## Answer

Not covered. Workspaces can exclude third-party models, but no approval, reassessment or retirement workflow.

Research detail: Third-party models offered directly by vendors fall outside ZDR and follow the vendor's terms, and sub-workspaces can exclude them through model permissions. No reassessment or retirement workflow for third parties is offered.
