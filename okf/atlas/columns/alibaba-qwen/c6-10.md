## Answer

Covered. ZDR via enterprise arrangement, excludes applications and third-party models; flagged content may be kept longer.

Source statement: "Stateful features (conversation storage, files) are retained until deleted by users." (https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/data-retention-policy.md)

Research detail: Prompts and responses are kept no longer than 30 days by default, and ZDR workspaces keep them only in a cache of up to 24 hours. Stateful data is kept until deleted, or 48 hours under ZDR. Region and deployment scope set where data is stored and processed, and Alibaba states customer data is not used for training without consent.

## Products

Data retention policy, ZDR, regions and deployment scopes

## Gaps

Flagged content may be kept up to 2 years. Applications and third-party models are outside ZDR.
