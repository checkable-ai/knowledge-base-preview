## Answer

Partly covered. No detections, response owner or alerting to a security operations team is documented. The analytics export covers aggregated metrics only, and complete logs go through a separate export. Redaction applies to model requests through guardrails, not to exported telemetry.

Source statement: "Integrate with existing SIEM infrastructure" (https://portkey.ai/docs/product/enterprise-offering/otel/analytics)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Enterprise can export analytics and complete request and response logs to OpenTelemetry endpoints, and exports logs to files or data lakes. Logs carry user, workspace and trace identifiers for correlation, and a metrics-only mode avoids storing body content.
