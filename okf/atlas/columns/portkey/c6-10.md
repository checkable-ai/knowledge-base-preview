## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The retention, zero-retention, PII scrubbing and region statements are given for the SaaS Enterprise deployment. In the Hybrid deployment, logs stay in the customer's own S3 or S3-compatible store under the customer's lifecycle policies, and metrics go to a ClickHouse database that can be hosted in the customer's region. Request logging modes are a Portkey Enterprise plan feature.

Source statement: "Default retention is 90 days (logs) and 365 days (metrics)." (https://portkey.ai/docs/enterprise/security)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Enterprise owners choose full logging or metrics-only mode for all requests, with an optional workspace override, and set custom retention periods. Hybrid deployment keeps LLM data in the customer's environment and bring-your-own-key encryption is available on AWS KMS. Portkey can also be configured not to store request and response bodies on request.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Request logging modes, custom retention, hybrid deployment, KMS integration

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Switching to metrics-only does not remove earlier logs and a purge needs support. No data subject deletion tool, regional residency options or agent memory governance are documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Choose metrics-only for sensitive workloads and set retention to policy.
