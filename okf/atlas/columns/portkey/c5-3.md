## Answer

Partly covered. Labour cost, savings and returns are not calculated. Only model usage cost is tracked.

Source statement: "provides useful data such as the timestamp, request type, LLM used, tokens generated, thinking tokens and cost." (https://portkey.ai/docs/product/observability/logs.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Each request is logged with team, project, model, tokens and exact cost, and budget policies cap spend per user, team or workspace. The open source Portkey Models pricing database feeds the cost figures. Enterprise has executive dashboards for adoption and ROI.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Logs and metadata, budget limits, usage policies, Portkey Models, executive reporting

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Labour cost, savings, output gains and profit are not calculated from the documentation. Detailed policies are Enterprise self-hosted only.
