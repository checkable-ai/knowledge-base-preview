## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The Registry API for the MCP Registry is in beta. Some Agent Registry access controls are listed as coming soon. Registers only what is connected through Portkey.

Source statement: "A single pane to view and manage every AI provider and model in your organization." (https://portkey.ai/docs/product/model-catalog.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: The Model Catalog lists every provider and model in the organisation and the MCP Registry is the central catalog of MCP servers with their tools, resources and prompts. The Agent Registry holds registered agents and their skills.
