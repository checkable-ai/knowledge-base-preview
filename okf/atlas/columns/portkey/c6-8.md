## Answer

Partly covered. No approval step before publishing. Versioning and restore cover prompts only, not model, tool, permission or knowledge changes.

Source statement: "Each version is immutable once created - to make changes, you must create a new version." (https://portkey.ai/docs/product/prompt-engineering-studio/prompt-versioning.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Prompt edits are saved as versions that can be compared, restored and published, and canary routing releases new models or prompts to a share of traffic. Audit logs record changes to configs, prompts, guardrails and integrations.
