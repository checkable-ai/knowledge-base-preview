## Answer

Not covered. The AI usage log and optional input/output log record executions, but no tamper-proof record is documented, logs are kept 7 days and capped at 100 MB, and the input/output log is off by default.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: The AI usage log records every AI execution with user, object, model and AI actions. An optional AI input/output log records prompts and responses with correlation IDs, and the Monitor tab keeps each run's reasoning and tool calls. MCP tool calls appear in both.

## Products

AI usage log, AI input/output log, Monitor tab

## Gaps

Logs are kept 7 days and capped at 100 MB. The input/output log is off by default and does not mask PII. No tamper-proof ledger is described.

## Options

Enable input/output logging where allowed and stream logs to write-once storage.
