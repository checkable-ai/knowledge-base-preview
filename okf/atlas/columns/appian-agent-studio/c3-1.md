## Answer

Not covered. Admins control enabled providers, models and regions, guardrails screen content, and custom providers can point to a customer gateway, but Appian has no per-agent or per-user quota, cost or rate limit.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Administrators control which providers, models and regions are enabled, can disable model families, and can route all AI traffic through an enterprise gateway such as Portkey, LiteLLM or Kong. AI guardrails screen inputs and outputs for prompt injection, PII and toxic content.

## Products

AI Services, custom provider, AI guardrails

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No per-agent or per-user quota or rate limit inside Appian. Guardrails are off by default and limited to 10 per environment.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use the custom provider route to an AI gateway for quotas and enable guardrails before production.
