## Answer

Not covered. Guardrails are environment-wide policies, but no ownership, versioning, publication or review cycle for AI policies is documented.

Research detail: AI guardrails are environment-wide content policies defined once by administrators from templates or custom definitions, with block or mask actions and set scope. They apply to every generative AI execution once enabled.
