## Answer

Not covered. AI Actions consumed are logged per run, but cost in tokens, currency or infrastructure and staff time, and return delivered, are not measured.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Each run shows AI actions consumed in the Monitor tab, and the AI usage log records AI actions per execution with usage groups for cost attribution. The Execute AI Agent smart service returns AI actions consumed.
