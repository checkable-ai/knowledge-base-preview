## Answer

Not covered. Residency and no-training are documented, but there are no documented retention or deletion rules for agent chat history or memory, and AI logs are fixed at 7 days. Document deletion rules apply only to document chat and smart search.

Research detail: Data stays in the site region unless cross-region processing is selected, is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is not used for training. Bedrock is stateless except for possible 30 day abuse review on some models. AI logs are kept 7 days.

## Gaps

Chat history cannot be cleared by users and its retention is not stated. No retention settings for agent memory.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Confirm chat history retention with Appian and choose processing regions to match residency rules.
