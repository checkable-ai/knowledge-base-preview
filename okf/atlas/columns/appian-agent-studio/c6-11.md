## Answer

Not covered. Appian provides an AI Usage Audit Log (kept for 7 days at most, capped at 100 MB) and inherits its platform certifications (SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001 and others). It documents no feature that collects control evidence and links it to the requirements or audits it supports. The customer has to export the logs and do that mapping in another tool.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Appian states its AI features inherit SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS and FedRAMP coverage, and AI audit logs plus Admin Console audit entries give evidence for compliance reviews.

## Gaps
