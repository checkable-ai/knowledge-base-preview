## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The MCSB standard is enabled by default when Defender for Cloud is enabled. Adding non-default standards needs at least one paid Microsoft Defender for Cloud plan. Viewing the data needs at least the Resource Policy Contributor and Security Admin roles; the Reader role gives access to policy compliance data.
