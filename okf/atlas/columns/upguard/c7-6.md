## Answer

Not covered. Threat monitoring flags registry entries referencing the customer's brand but does not scan tools, plugins or skills the customer uses for poisoning or compromise.

Research detail: Threat Monitoring surfaces MCP server definitions in public registries and marketplaces that reference the customer organisation. That can reveal lookalike or unofficial servers using the organisation's name.
