## Answer

Not covered. Vendor onboarding approval and app access policies cover external vendors and AI web apps. There is no submission, registration and approval flow for models or agents.

Research detail: Vendor onboarding requests, including an inbound portal on Professional plans and above, collect internal details so a vendor can be tiered and then approved or rejected. Users can request access to an app from the User Risk extension, routed to admins through Risk Automations, and admins approve or block apps.
