## Answer

Not covered. No register of models, agents and tools with components. The User Risk Applications list of detected SaaS and AI apps falls short.

Research detail: UpGuard keeps inventories of monitored vendors, detected products on internet-facing assets and, in User Risk, the SaaS and AI apps in use, with labels, attributes and a Shadow AI flag. Apps can be added manually before anyone uses them.

## Products

## Gaps

No register of models, agents or tools with schemas or software component records is documented. Manually added apps cannot be removed.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use a separate AI asset registry for internal models and agents and treat User Risk as the external AI app inventory.
