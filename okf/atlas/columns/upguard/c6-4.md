## Answer

Not covered. App usage policies are access rules, not authored AI policy documents with owners, versions and review.

Last checked 9 November 2026.

Research detail: User Risk app usage policies set an organisation default, a base rule per app and custom rules by team, role or user, with an optional change note and an app timeline. These are enforced in the browser.

## Products

## Gaps

These are app access rules, not authored AI policy documents with owners, versions, publication and review. The AI Policy Generator is a free website tool and is not documented as part of the platform.

## Options
