## Answer

Covered. Vendor Risk workflow; covers vendors' security posture, not model behaviour.

Source statement: "Approve or reject the vendor." (https://help.upguard.com/en/vendor-onboarding-process)

Research detail: Vendor Risk covers intake through onboarding requests, tier-based assessment, a reassessment date on each risk assessment, continuous monitoring and removal of a vendor when the relationship ends. A documented automation flags vendors never assessed or not assessed in 12 months. A Detected vendors tab lists the vendors behind apps that User Risk has seen in use but that are not yet monitored.

## Products

UpGuard Vendor Risk, User Risk, Risk Automations

## Gaps

Assessment is of vendor security posture, not the behaviour of external models or tools. The AI Risk Check for vetting AI tools is marked coming soon. AI-specific questionnaires in the library were not checked.
