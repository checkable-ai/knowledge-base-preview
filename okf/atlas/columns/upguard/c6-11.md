## Answer

Covered. Vendor Risk; evidence is checked against security control templates, not AI framework templates.

Source statement: "Citations are the specific passages, from uploaded evidence, that the AI used to decide a check's result." (https://help.upguard.com/en/vendor-risk-work-with-evidence-citations)

Research detail: Security Profile evaluates evidence against control templates (ISO 27001, NIST CSF 2.0, NIST SP 800-53, CSA CCM and others) and links each passed or failed check to citations, the exact passage from the evidence. Users can exclude or restore citations, and published assessments keep version history. Trust Exchange can run the same Security Profile as a self-assessment.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

UpGuard Vendor Risk Security Profile, Trust Exchange

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Evidence is for vendor and self security assessments. No AI framework such as ISO/IEC 42001 or NIST AI RMF appears in the control template library.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Map AI control requirements to UpGuard's check library as a custom template, or keep AI control evidence in a separate tool.
