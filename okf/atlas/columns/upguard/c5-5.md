## Answer

Not covered. Permissions catalogue shows OAuth grants by users to apps, not what each agent can reach and do downstream.

Research detail: User Risk has a Permissions catalogue of the OAuth permissions users have granted to apps, grouped by category, and the shadow AI monitoring page says it flags apps that access email, files or other sensitive data. This gives a readable view of what third-party apps, including AI apps, can reach.
