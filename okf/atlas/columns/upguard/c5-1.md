## Answer

Not covered. UpGuard documents no way for people to review what the customer's own AI agents produce. The closest feature is the Trust Exchange AI Analyst: it drafts questionnaire answers for the team to review, but that review covers only UpGuard's built-in suggestions inside Trust Exchange. It is not a review step for agent output in general.

Research detail: UpGuard does not offer human review of customer agent output. Its own AI outputs are reviewed by people: autofill suggestions carry confidence ratings and must be accepted, rejected or edited, and AI risk assessments are edited before publishing.
