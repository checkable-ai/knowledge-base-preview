## Answer

Covered. Applies to risks UpGuard detects (Vendor Risk, Breach Risk). Expiry date is optional and approval is configurable by an administrator.

Source statement: "Enter a justification, set an expiry date if applicable, then select whether the waiver requires approval." (https://help.upguard.com/en/articles/4862688-how-to-use-vendor-risk-waivers)

Research detail: Risk waivers in Vendor Risk and Breach Risk take a justification, an optional expiry date and an optional approval step with nominated approvers. Expired waivers lapse and the risk returns, with a reminder sent before expiry. User Risk waivers are tracked in the audit log.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

UpGuard risk waivers (Vendor Risk, Breach Risk, User Risk)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Waivers apply to risks UpGuard detects. Exceptions to internal AI policies outside UpGuard are not covered. User Risk waiver workflow detail was not found in the help pages read.
