## Answer

Not covered. Reports cover vendors, attack surface and employee app use, not AI control coverage, breaches or governance status.

Research detail: UpGuard reports vendor, attack surface and workforce posture: a Security Profile shows the share of controls implemented per vendor, compliance reports map vendors to ISO 27001 or NIST CSF, and the User Risk executive summary shows Shadow AI apps, policy distribution and risk ratings. Reports can be scheduled weekly to yearly.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

UpGuard Reporting, Vendor Risk Security Profile, User Risk Executive Summary

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Posture covers third parties, the external estate and employee app use. It does not report control coverage of internal AI systems or agents.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use the scheduled reports for third-party and Shadow AI posture and a GRC or AI governance tool for internal AI controls.
