## Answer

Not covered. Waiver approvals and automation checkpoints do not bind approval to a specific agent action.

Research detail: UpGuard does not offer human approval of agent actions. The Risk Automations product page says customers can add human-in-the-loop checkpoints to UpGuard's own automations, and risk waivers can require a nominated approver, but neither binds a decision to a proposed agent action.
