## Answer

Covered. Applies to Codex cloud tasks; local history retention is configured separately.

Source statement: "By default, a task's saved VM state is recoverable for up to seven days after you last start a turn or resume the task." (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/environments/cloud-environments)

Research detail: Local session transcripts are saved and can be capped or disabled. Cloud task VM state is recoverable for up to seven days after the last turn.

## Products

## Gaps
