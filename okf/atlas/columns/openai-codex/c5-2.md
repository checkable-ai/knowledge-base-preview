## Answer

Not covered. Training agents on their tasks and continuous customer-defined validation of behaviour. Safety monitoring is vendor-side and flags unsafe model behaviour during a task. It does not replace sandboxing or permissions, and Auto-review is not behaviour monitoring.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Monitoring can pause tasks for unsafe behaviour, and Auto-review evaluates boundary-crossing actions against a published policy that enterprises can customise.
