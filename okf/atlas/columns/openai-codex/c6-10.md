## Answer

Covered. Residency and EKM cover only eligible content, supported workloads, regions and configurations in eligible workspaces. Work Cloud local computer access does not provide strict zero data retention.

Source statement: "Data residency and inference residency cover only eligible content and supported workloads, regions, and configurations." (https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/admin-setup)

Research detail: Docs cover data residency for eligible content, Enterprise Key Management, an enforce_residency safeguard, local history caps and seven-day cloud task state.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Data residency, EKM, history settings

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Local computer access with Work Cloud has no strict zero data retention, and deleting a conversation does not remove every related copy.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Do not enable local computer access where ZDR is required.
