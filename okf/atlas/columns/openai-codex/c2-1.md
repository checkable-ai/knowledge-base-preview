## Answer

Not covered. No documented retry or recovery policy for the steps of an agent task. Subagent steering and stopping cover ordering and cancelling only. Retry of failed cloud environment setup falls short of recovering task steps.

Last checked 18 November 2026.

Research detail: Codex spawns subagents, routes follow-up instructions, waits for results and closes threads. Users can steer or stop subagents in the app and CLI, and long-running goals can be steered and run in parallel.
