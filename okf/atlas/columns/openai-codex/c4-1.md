## Answer

Not covered. Service accounts (pay-as-you-go plans, Codex CLI 0.142.0 or later for access tokens) give a non-human identity per automated workload such as a CI runner, scheduled job or integration, with runs attributed to it. They are not per-agent identities. There is no binding to the release or version, or to where the agent runs. Interactive sessions, subagents and cloud tasks have no identity of their own. The vendor page does not say the feature covers part of this outcome.

Research detail: Service accounts give each CI runner, scheduled job or shared integration a non-human ChatGPT workspace identity with roles, groups, plugins and SCIM provisioning, and runs are attributed to that account. Personal access tokens tie local runs to the creating member.

Service accounts, Codex access tokens

## Gaps

Identity is per workspace account, not per agent, and is not linked to a runtime or release. Service accounts need a pay-as-you-go plan. Subagents and cloud tasks have no separate identity.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Create one service account per automated workflow and record its runtime and release in an external registry.
