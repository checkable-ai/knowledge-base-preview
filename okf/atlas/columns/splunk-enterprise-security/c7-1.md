## Answer

Not covered. No documented tamper-evident record of agent actions with inputs, outputs and reasoning. Audit indexes and traces are not described as integrity-protected agent records.

Research detail: Splunk writes audit events for searches, logins and capability execution to the _audit index, ES records findings review activity as incident_review audit events, and ES 8.7 adds change history in Activity logs. The triage agent shows a timeline of its steps, and agent conversation data can be stored in a Splunk events index through HEC.
