## Answer

Not covered. No documented controlled process in Enterprise Security for setting up, changing and retiring an agent: no approval step, change history or versioning, and no retirement process. ES offers only its built-in agents. The nearby ES features are the AI SOC analyst settings (allowed actions, connectors, detections, with the agent turned on through a Splunk representative), the Triage agent on or off setting and the organisation-wide AI availability switch, which turn agents on or off rather than retire them. The nearest lifecycle feature is Agent Launchpad, which has create, edit, deactivate and delete, but it belongs to the separate AI Toolkit app, not ES, and its page documents no approval or change control.

Research detail: ES has no lifecycle for creating, changing or retiring agent identities and permissions. Its own agents are switched on by an administrator, and some 8.7 agents require contacting a Splunk representative.
