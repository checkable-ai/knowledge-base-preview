## Answer

Not covered. No short-lived scoped credential brokering, rotation or revocation for agents. MCP encrypted tokens only grant access to Splunk itself.

Research detail: ES does not broker credentials for agents. The Splunk MCP Server uses encrypted tokens that let an MCP client reach Splunk itself, and token authentication must be enabled on the deployment.

## Products

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

MCP tokens are credentials for Splunk access only. No delivery, rotation or revocation of credentials for downstream systems.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Use a secrets manager or credential broker outside Splunk and send its audit logs to ES.
