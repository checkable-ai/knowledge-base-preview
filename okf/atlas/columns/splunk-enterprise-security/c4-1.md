## Answer

Not covered. No identity issued or registered for customer agents, no owner, location or release linkage. UEBA covers users and assets only.

Research detail: ES does not issue or register identities for customer agents. Its UEBA monitors users and assets, and its own AI agents are features switched on by an administrator or a Splunk representative.
