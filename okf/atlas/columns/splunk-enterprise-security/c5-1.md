## Answer

Covered. Applies to Splunk's Triage agent in Enterprise Security, which must be set up and turned on. Does not cover customer-built agents.

Source statement: "Every step is documented inline so analysts can follow the agent's reasoning and act with confidence." (https://help.splunk.com/en/splunk-enterprise-security-8/administer/8.6/ai-assistant-in-security-and-agentic-capabilities/setting-up-the-triage-agent-in-splunk-enterprise-security)

Research detail: The triage agent analyses findings before an analyst opens them and shows a disposition recommendation, summary and reasoning, the tools and evidence used, and a step-by-step timeline. The agent never changes finding fields, the analyst keeps the final disposition, and analysts give thumbs up or down feedback with a reason.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

ES triage agent, AI analysis side panel

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Applies to ES's own agent output, not to customer agents. The AI analysis page does not describe an audit trail of analyst decisions on AI recommendations.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Track analyst overrides of agent dispositions through incident_review audit events.
