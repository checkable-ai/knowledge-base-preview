## Answer

Not covered. Training agents on their tasks and anomaly detection on agent behaviour. The nearby feature is sampled quality scores in AI Agent Monitoring, with detectors that alert on them.

Research detail: ES UEBA baselines normal activity for users and assets and scores deviations into entity risk. AI Agent Monitoring scores agent spans for bias, hallucination, relevance, sentiment and toxicity and flags an agent when fewer than 80 percent of evaluations pass.

## Products
