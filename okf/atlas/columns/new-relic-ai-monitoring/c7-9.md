## Answer

Partly covered. Detection covers only outbound calls to Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude and Amazon Bedrock, and the vendor states that calls to other providers aren't reported as GenAI telemetry. It does not cover employee use of endpoint or SaaS AI tools, unapproved agents that call other providers, or hosts running a language APM agent.

Source statement: "eBPF AI monitoring currently detects and reports on outbound calls to these LLM providers:" (https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/ebpf/ai-monitoring/)

Research detail: eBPF AI monitoring detects outbound calls to Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic and Amazon Bedrock at the kernel level with no code change. The docs say it helps spot GenAI workloads you did not know existed and build an inventory of services calling LLM providers. It covers four providers on Linux and Kubernetes, and is off by default.
