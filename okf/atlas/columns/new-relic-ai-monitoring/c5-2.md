## Answer

Not covered. No feature for training agents on their tasks. AI Evaluation (public preview) scores sampled prompts and completions asynchronously for safety and accuracy, and alert anomaly detection is generic. Neither is documented as validating agent behaviour continuously, and nothing in the vendor's own words says it covers part of this outcome.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: AI Evaluation, in public preview since October 2026, scores sampled interactions for prompt injection, data leakage, toxicity, faithfulness and relevancy, with pass or fail thresholds attached to traces. Anomaly thresholds on alert conditions can flag shifts in AI metrics such as token use or errors. No task training is offered.
