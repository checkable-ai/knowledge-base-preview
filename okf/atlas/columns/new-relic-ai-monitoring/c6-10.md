## Answer

Not covered. Controls cover only New Relic's telemetry copy (record_content, drop filters, retention). No deletion on request, residency rules or governance of agent inputs, outputs and memory.

Research detail: For AI telemetry stored in New Relic, record_content can be disabled, drop filters discard matching attributes before storage, and the AI Monitoring namespace has 30 day default retention (120 days with Data Plus). Reported data cannot be deleted and expires with retention. These controls cover New Relic's copy only, not the agent's inputs, outputs or memory.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

record_content setting, drop filters, data retention settings

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No deletion on request. No governance of data outside New Relic. New Relic does not guarantee drop filters fully address disclosure.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Disable content capture by default and test drop filters against seeded sensitive data.
