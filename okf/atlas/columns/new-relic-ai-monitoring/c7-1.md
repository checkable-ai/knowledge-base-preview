## Answer

Not covered. No documented write-once or integrity-validated store of agent actions, and no documented export of an agent action record to such storage. Nearby features fall short: AI Monitoring traces and Agentic AI Monitoring tool and agent spans are sampled telemetry with optional content capture and limited default retention, and the platform's data immutability statement is not a tamper-evident per-action ledger.

Research detail: Traces record each LLM call, tool call and agent step with inputs and outputs when content capture is on. New Relic says reported data cannot be edited or deleted and expires with retention. It is a sampled telemetry store, not a ledger, and content is often disabled or dropped for privacy.
