## Answer

Covered. Requires the Teams / Service Architecture Intelligence entity ownership feature to be enabled and a team tag on the agent configuration, or manual assignment. Ownership is at team level. Models that appear only in the model inventory may not be separate entities. The page does not state preview status.

Source statement: "Once enabled, New Relic will automatically assign any entity with a matching team tag to the corresponding team." (https://docs.newrelic.com/docs/service-architecture-intelligence/teams/team-ownership/)

Last checked 12 November 2026.
