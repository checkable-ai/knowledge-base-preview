## Answer

Covered. Covers agent identities and blueprints. Models and tools have no owner record. Sponsor lifecycle workflows (transfer to the sponsor's manager, notifications) need ID Governance licensing.

Source statement: "Sponsors are required for agent identity blueprints and agent identities, ensuring every agent has a designated business owner." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/agent-id/agent-owners-sponsors-managers)

Research detail: Every agent identity and blueprint requires at least one sponsor accountable for purpose and lifecycle, with optional technical owners and managers. Sponsorship transfers automatically to the sponsor's manager when the sponsor leaves, and lifecycle workflow templates notify cosponsors and managers.
