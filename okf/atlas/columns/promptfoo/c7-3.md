## Answer

Not covered. No model lineage, origin tracking or compliance attestation. ModelAudit scans model files statically and can emit a CycloneDX SBOM with licence data, which falls short of the full outcome.

Research detail: ModelAudit statically scans model files in many formats, including PyTorch, ONNX, SafeTensors, GGUF and pickle, for malicious code, embedded secrets, licence issues and weight anomalies, from local or remote sources such as Hugging Face and S3. The --sbom flag emits a CycloneDX SBOM with licence data. Lineage and compliance attestation are not covered.
