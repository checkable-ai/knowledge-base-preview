## Answer

Not covered. Enterprise audit logging records only administrative control-plane events (logins, user, role, team, permission and service account changes). It does not record agent actions or tool calls, and no write-once, integrity-validated storage or tamper-evident export is documented.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Enterprise audit logs record administrative actions on users, teams, roles, permissions and service accounts with actor, target and timestamp, retrievable through GET /api/v1/audit-logs. Eval runs are tracked separately. Tamper protection and retention are not described.
