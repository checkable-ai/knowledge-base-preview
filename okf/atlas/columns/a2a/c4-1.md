## Answer

Not covered. The Agent Card (name, version, optional provider, optional JWS signature) identifies the agent and can prove the card came from the claimed provider. It does not bind the agent to an accountable owner or to where it runs, and release identity is only a version string.

Research detail: A2A servers MUST publish an Agent Card with a name, description and a required version, and may name the provider. Cards MAY be signed with JWS over a JCS canonical form so clients can verify origin and integrity. The card does not bind the agent to an accountable owner or runtime.
