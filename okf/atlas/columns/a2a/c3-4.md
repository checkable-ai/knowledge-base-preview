## Answer

Not covered. The enterprise guide recommends API management in front of agents, but no gateway or allow-list of agent pairs is defined.

Research detail: The enterprise guide highly recommends API management in front of externally exposed agents for central authentication, authorisation, rate limits and quotas. The A2A-Version and A2A-Extensions headers let a gateway enforce versions. Allowed agent pairs are not defined.
