## Answer

Not covered. No short-lived scoped credential issuance. Rotation and revocation are SHOULD-level guidance, and keeping secrets out of model context is not addressed.

Research detail: Clients obtain credentials out of band and send them in headers on every request. Credentials MUST be treated as secrets, SHOULD be rotated, and agents SHOULD implement revocation. For in-task authorisation, agents SHOULD receive credentials out of band so they are not exposed to each agent in a chain. Keeping secrets out of model context is not addressed.
