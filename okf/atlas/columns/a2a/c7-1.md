## Answer

Not covered. A2A is a communication protocol specification. It recommends audit logging of task and agent interactions in its enterprise guidance, but defines no write-once or integrity-validated storage, no tamper-evidence, and no documented export of the record to such storage.

Research detail: Agents SHOULD provide audit trails for sensitive operations, and logs MUST NOT hold credentials or personal data unless protected. The enterprise guide recommends logging taskId, correlation IDs and trace context. No tamper-evident ledger is defined.
