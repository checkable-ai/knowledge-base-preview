## Answer

Not covered. The enterprise guide recommends per-skill access and least privilege, but the protocol defines no permission model for what an agent does in connected systems.

Research detail: Servers authorise each request on the authenticated identity and their own policy, which MAY consider skills, actions, data policies and OAuth scopes. The enterprise guide recommends per-skill authorisation and least privilege when the agent acts on backend systems. The protocol prescribes no permission model.
