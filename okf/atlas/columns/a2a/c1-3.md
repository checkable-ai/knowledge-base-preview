## Answer

Not covered. The auth-required task state can carry a human approval step, but the spec does not bind approval to the exact action, and does not define handling of refusals or repeat attempts. Implementers or extensions must build these.

Research detail: The specification names human approval before a destructive action as a use of the auth-required task state, and agents MUST NOT treat that state by itself as authorisation. How the approved operation is identified and checked is left to the implementation or an extension.
