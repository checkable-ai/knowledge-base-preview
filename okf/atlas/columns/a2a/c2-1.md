## Answer

Covered. Cancellation is best effort and not guaranteed.

Source statement: "The operation attempts to cancel the specified task and returns its updated state." (https://a2a-protocol.org/latest/specification/)

Research detail: Work runs as Tasks with defined states, including terminal states (completed, canceled, rejected, failed) and interrupted states (input-required, auth-required). The specification defines CancelTask, task subscription, push notifications with retry and backoff guidance, and contextId to group related tasks. Cancellation success is not guaranteed.
