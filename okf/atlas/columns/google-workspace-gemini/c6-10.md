<!-- chunk: answer -->
## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Automatic deletion periods apply to Gemini conversations (90, 540 or 1080 days, or off). Data regions advanced settings are supported on Frontline Plus, Enterprise Plus and Enterprise Essentials Plus, and changing settings per organisational unit or group needs one of those or the Data Regions add-on subscription. Gemini features that are not regionalised stop working when they are turned off under data regions. Gemini Notebook prompts and responses are not retained after the session ends.

Source statement: "Conversations are automatically deleted 90 days after the last interaction." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/workspace-with-gemini/manage-gemini-in-workspace-conversation-history-settings)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins set deletion periods for Gemini conversations and can use Vault retention for Workspace data. Data regions control storage and processing location, Gemini content is not used for training, and data can be exported.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Conversation history settings, Vault, data regions, Data Export tool

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Regions named for Gemini are EU and US, with no Australian region in the pages read. Features that process data globally are switched off when advanced regions settings are off. Notebook and Chrome are outside enterprise compliance coverage.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Check whether data regions cover the Gemini features in use and set history retention to policy.
