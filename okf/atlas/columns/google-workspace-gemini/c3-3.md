## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Supported on Frontline Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, Education Fundamentals, Standard and Plus, and Enterprise Essentials Plus. Google Chat and Gmail support is beta. Gemini Enterprise and Gemini in Chrome are not subject to the Gemini DLP rule.

Source statement: "Using data protection rules, you can restrict Gemini from accessing sensitive data and reduce AI-related security risks." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/security/about-dlp-for-gemini)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: DLP for Gemini lets admins block or audit Gemini's use of Drive content (and Chat and Gmail in beta) based on labels, data types or text patterns. DLP also evaluates Gemini output inserted into Gmail or Drive, and IRM and client-side encryption keep protected files from Gemini.

## Products

DLP for Gemini, DLP for Studio, IRM, client-side encryption
