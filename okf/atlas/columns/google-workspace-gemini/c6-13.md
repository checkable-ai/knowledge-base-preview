## Answer

Covered. The certifications are stated for Gemini in Workspace apps and the Gemini app.

Source statement: "Gemini has attained SOC 1/2/3, ISO 9001, ISO/IEC 27001, 27701, 27017, 27018, and 42001 certifications." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub)

Research detail: Google states Gemini has SOC 1/2/3, ISO 27001, 27017, 27018, 27701 and 42001 certifications and FedRAMP High, with BSI C5 listed. Google's IRAP page says Google Workspace was assessed against OFFICIAL and PROTECTED controls and reports can be requested. A DPIA support guide is published.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Privacy Hub, IRAP page, Compliance Reports Manager, DPIA support guide

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The IRAP page lists Google Workspace but the Gemini features are not named in the scope list read, so Gemini coverage is unconfirmed. Gemini Notebook and Gemini in Chrome are not covered by ISO, SOC or FedRAMP. No red-team or penetration test attestation specific to Gemini was found. An IRAP assessment is not an authority-to-operate decision.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Request the IRAP report and confirm Gemini scope with Google before relying on it.
