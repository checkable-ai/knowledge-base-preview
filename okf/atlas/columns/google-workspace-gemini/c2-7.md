## Answer

Partly covered. No sandbox, file, process or network egress limits are documented for Gemini features. Immediate single-flow stop is not self-service.

Source statement: "Blocks the flow's access to Google data and stops any in-progress or future runs." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/studio/manage-ws-flows-access-to-data)

Research detail: For Studio flows an admin can pause data access to stop in-progress and future runs, move a user to an organisational unit with Studio off to stop all their flows at once, and Studio halts a flow on detected injection drift.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent access management, Studio emergency stop, runtime classifiers

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Studio only. Stopping a single flow immediately needs Google support or the whole-user stop. No sandbox, filesystem or egress policy is documented for Gemini features.
