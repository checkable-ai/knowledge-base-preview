## Answer

Partly covered. No tamper-evident, write-once or integrity-validated storage is documented, and no documented export to such storage. Vault covers Gemini app prompts and responses, not agent action records.

Source statement: "You can also track how users create, edit, share, and run skills, and which steps flows complete on behalf of their owners." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/reports/workspace-studio-log-events)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Gemini for Workspace log events record the actor, action, app, feature, whether an AI agent acted, and agent ID, with search, export and alert rules. Vault can search and export Gemini app prompts and responses.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini for Workspace log events, Reports API, Vault

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Log events record actions and not prompt, output or reasoning text for Gemini in apps. Tamper-proofing is not documented. Export is capped at 100,000 rows (30 million with the security investigation tool).

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Send logs to a SIEM and use Vault for Gemini app conversations.
