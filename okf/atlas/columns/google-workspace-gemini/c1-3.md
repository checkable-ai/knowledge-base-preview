## Answer

Partly covered. Binding of approval to the exact action, handling of refusals and repeat attempts are not documented. Approval is by the flow user, and Gemini in Gmail, Docs and the Gemini app have no equivalent approval gate.

Source statement: "Pauses the run and requires the user to review and approve the step on the Workspace Studio Approvals page." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/security/about-dlp-for-studio)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Studio admin settings can enforce end-user confirmation before a flow shares data externally, and a DLP rule can block a step or require user approval before the flow continues. Log events record whether a user approved an agent action.

## Products

Studio approvals hub, DLP for Studio, log event User approved

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Studio only. Binding of an approval to the exact action, and denial and replay handling, are not documented. Approval is by the flow owner.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Turn on the external-sharing confirmation setting and DLP require-approval rules for Studio.
