## Answer

Partly covered. Retry, recovery and hand-off between agents are not documented. Gemini in Workspace apps is assistance, not orchestration.

Source statement: "Blocks the flow's access to Google data and stops any in-progress or future runs." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/studio/manage-ws-flows-access-to-data)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Studio flows run multi-step automations on user's behalf with step limits. An admin can pause a flow's data access, which stops in-progress and future runs, and DLP can cancel a run at a step.
