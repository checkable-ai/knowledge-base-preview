## Answer

Partly covered. Token lifetime, automatic rotation and expiry are not documented, and the pages do not state that secrets are kept out of model context. Gemini features in Workspace apps use the user's session.

Source statement: "Studio generates a dedicated OAuth Client ID that is restricted to a least privileged subset of the owner's permissions." (https://workspace.google.com/blog/identity-and-security/defend-against-agentic-risks-with-multi-layered-protections-in-google-workspace-studio)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Studio generates a dedicated OAuth client for each flow limited to the scopes its tasks need, instead of using the owner's full credentials. Admins can suspend a flow or revoke targeted OAuth scopes from Agent access management.
