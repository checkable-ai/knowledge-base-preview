## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The Gemini app keeps chats for the period admins set (3, 18 or 36 months). With history off, new chats are saved in user accounts for up to 72 hours. Gemini in Workspace apps has a separate setting with 90 day, 18 month and 3 year options, supported on Business Starter, Standard and Plus, Enterprise and eligible Education editions.

Source statement: "Admins can choose to automatically delete conversations after 3, 18, or 36 months." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub)

Research detail: The Gemini app keeps history for the signed-in user for 3, 18 or 36 months as set by admins, with 18 months as the default. Admins can turn history off, in which case chats are held for up to 72 hours.
