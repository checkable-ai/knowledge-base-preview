## Answer

Not covered. The product does not assess use cases, assign risk levels or match controls. The DPIA support guide only helps customers prepare their own assessment.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Google publishes a DPIA support guide for Gemini in Workspace and the Gemini app, and the Studio guide describes risks of poorly configured flows. There is no built-in risk classification tool.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

DPIA support guide, Studio admin guide

## Gaps

The guide supports the customer's own assessment. Risk levels and control selection are not assigned by the product. The guide targets EU privacy law.
