## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The Gemini in Workspace history settings are supported on Business Starter, Standard and Plus, Enterprise, Education Plus, Teaching & Learning add-on and Google AI Pro for Education editions. Gemini in Workspace apps uses options of 90 days, 18 months or 3 years.

Source statement: "Admins can choose to automatically delete conversations after 3, 18, or 36 months." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/generative-ai/generative-ai-in-google-workspace-privacy-hub)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Gemini in Workspace keeps a conversation history in the side panel that the user can resume. History stays within the app and is visible only to the user, and admins set deletion and retention.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini in Workspace conversation history

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Per-app history only. Content is not shared across users. Retention applies by admin setting.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Set the auto-deletion period to match records policy.
