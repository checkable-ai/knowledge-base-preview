## Answer

Covered, with conditions. DLP for Gemini needs a supported edition (for example Frontline Standard and Plus, Enterprise Standard and Plus, plus certain Education editions and Enterprise Essentials Plus). Gmail and Chat coverage is labelled beta; Drive is not. The AI control center needs Enterprise Standard or Enterprise Plus. AI classification labelling for Drive is included with Frontline Plus and Enterprise Plus, or with the Gemini Enterprise-Legacy, Google AI Pro for Education and AI Security add-ons.

Source statement: "With data loss prevention (DLP) for Gemini, you control what users can access in Gemini." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/security/about-dlp-for-gemini)

Research detail: Gemini is limited to content the user can access. Admins can turn each source (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Chat) on or off for Workspace Intelligence and use labels, DLP and trust rules to govern the data Gemini reaches.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Workspace Intelligence source controls, classification labels, DLP

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No sanitisation of stored data is documented. Controls govern what Gemini reads, not data stores themselves.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Classify and label sensitive Drive content before enabling Gemini.
