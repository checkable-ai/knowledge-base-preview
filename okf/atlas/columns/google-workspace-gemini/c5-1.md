## Answer

Partly covered. Review is by the acting user at a step. No separate reviewer queue or review of Gemini output in Workspace apps.

Source statement: "Pauses the run and requires the user to review and approve the step on the Workspace Studio Approvals page." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/security/about-dlp-for-studio)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Studio shows approval requests to the flow owner in an approvals hub before sensitive steps run, and DLP can require user approval. Google describes end-user warnings to check Gemini output.
