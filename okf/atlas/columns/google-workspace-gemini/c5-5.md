## Answer

Partly covered. Reach is shown only for Google data and Workspace OAuth scopes. There is no map of non-Google downstream systems or of what an agent can do in them.

Source statement: "As an administrator, you determine how much access Google Workspace work agents have to Google data." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/studio/manage-work-agent-access-to-data)

Research detail: Agent access management lists flows and their data access scopes, and Drive inventory reporting shows how data is classified and who can access it, which bounds what Gemini can retrieve.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent access management, Drive inventory reporting

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No single human-readable map of agent network reach. Studio only for agents.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Review flow scopes monthly and use Drive inventory reports before enabling Gemini.
