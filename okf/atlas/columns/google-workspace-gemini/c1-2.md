## Answer

Covered, with conditions. The Gemini Feature access panel is available only with Enterprise Standard and Plus, Teaching and Learning add-on, Education Plus and Google AI Pro for Education editions. Settings apply by organisational unit or configuration group, and group settings override organisational units. Studio setting changes can take up to 24 hours.

Source statement: "As an administrator, you can control which steps users can add to their flows." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/studio/manage-ws-flows-access-to-data)

Research detail: Admins turn Gemini features on or off per Workspace service (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Meet, Chat, Studio), choose which Workspace apps the Gemini app may use, and can block or restrict a Studio flow to certain data scopes. Settings apply by organisational unit or group.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Gemini feature access, Gemini app Workspace apps setting, Studio Agent access management

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Gemini feature controls need an eligible edition. Turning off a service does not stop Gemini in another app from using a file the user links. Per-action allow lists are not documented for Gemini in apps.

## Options

Set defaults per organisational unit and test the cross-app behaviour described on the page.
