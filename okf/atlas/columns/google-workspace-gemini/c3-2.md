## Answer

Partly covered. No input schemas, per-tool rate limits or generic egress filtering are documented.

Source statement: "As an administrator, you can control which steps users can add to their flows." (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/studio/manage-ws-flows-access-to-data)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins allow or block Studio step types, connectors and webhooks, and choose whether Gemini steps may use Workspace content or Google web search. DLP can block or require approval before a step runs.
