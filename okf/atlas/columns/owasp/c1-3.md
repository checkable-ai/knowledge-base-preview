Source statement: "Utilise human-in-the-loop control to require a human to approve high-impact actions before they are taken." (https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/)

Research detail: LLM01:2026 requires explicit human confirmation before privileged, irreversible or externally visible actions, showing the exact rendered action rather than a summary. LLM03:2026 sets graduated audit, warn, block and escalate policies, ASI02 adds dry-run diffs before approval, and ACS defines an ask disposition that routes a hook to an approver.
