## Answer

Covered. Guidance only, no conformance test.

Source statement: "Grant temporary credentials or API tokens that expire immediately after use." (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GenAI-Security-Project/GenAI-Security-Corpus/main/corpus/_extracted/agentic-top10/2026-final/OWASP-Top-10-for-Agentic-Applications-2026-v1.0.pdf.txt)

Research detail: The guidance sets specific mechanisms: just-in-time credentials that expire after use and are bound to a user session, short-lived task-scoped tokens, and keys that are never directly available to agents, with orchestrators mediating signing through HSM or KMS backed keys.
