## Answer

Not covered. ASI03 recommends per-agent identities with short-lived credentials and ACS envelopes carry an agent_id, but nothing ties an agent identity to an accountable owner, runtime location or release version.

Research detail: The Agentic Top 10 recommends per-agent identities with short-lived credentials such as mTLS certificates, per-agent cryptographic identity attestation and signed behavioural manifests, and suggests managing agents as non-human identities with lifecycle controls. ACS hook envelopes carry an agent_id. Linking identity to an accountable owner and release is not specified.
