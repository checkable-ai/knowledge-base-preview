## Answer

Not covered. No defined process to create an agent, change its permissions or configuration, and retire it. ASI03 lifecycle controls and ASI10 reintegration gates are general and fall short.

Research detail: ASI03 suggests identity platforms that manage agents with lifecycle controls, and ASI10 requires fresh attestation, dependency verification and human approval before a quarantined agent returns to production. A full provisioning process is not described.
