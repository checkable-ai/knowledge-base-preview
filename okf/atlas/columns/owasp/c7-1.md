## Answer

Not covered. OWASP GenAI guidance and the Agent Control Standard v0.1.0 are specifications, not a product with a configurable feature. The ACS audit chain is a requirement, and the reference Guardian logs as plain file appends with no signing or write-once/integrity-validated storage. Nearby ACS signing requirements fall short of a tamper-evident record in the product.

Research detail: ASI10 asks for immutable, signed audit logs of all agent actions, tool calls and inter-agent communication, and ASI08 for tamper-evident, time-stamped logs bound to cryptographic agent identities. ACS-Core makes an audit chain part of the mandatory baseline.
