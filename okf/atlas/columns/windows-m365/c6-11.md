## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Evidence upload is limited to users with editing rights in Compliance Manager; read-only users can only view it. Uses Microsoft Purview Compliance Manager within the assessed platform scope. Compliance Manager requires an Office 365 or Microsoft 365 licence, including Business Premium, and is also available to GCC, GCC High and DoD customers. Premium regulatory templates require purchase unless included in the licensing agreement. A5/E5/G5 subscriptions include three premium regulations.

Source statement: "You can also store evidence, add notes, and record status updates within the action." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/compliance-manager-improvement-actions)
