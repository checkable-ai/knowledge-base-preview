## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Needs Microsoft Purview sensitivity labels and Data Loss Prevention policies to be configured. Overshared SharePoint and OneDrive permissions still expose content. Copilot and agents can't access Double Key Encryption content and don't recognise labels on Teams meetings and chats. Blocking sensitive information types in prompts is in preview and subject to tenant rollout. Excluding external email is also in preview.

Source statement: "A DLP policy for Copilot can keep Copilot from summarizing labeled files and emails." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/plan/architecture-data-protection-auditing)
