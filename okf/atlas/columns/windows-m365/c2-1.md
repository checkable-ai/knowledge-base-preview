## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Behaviour differs by harness: the Microsoft Copilot harness retries and finds alternative paths, the Customer Conversation harness escalates to a human representative, and the standard harness follows the paths the maker built. The Microsoft Copilot harness and Customer Conversation harness use Copilot Credits, which is usage-based billing. The standard harness is licensed separately under Copilot Studio licensing.

Source statement: "Instead of following only a fixed script, it can take a goal, break it into steps, call the right tools across connectors, knowledge, MCP, and connected agents, and adjust when a step fails or a request changes."
