## Answer

Covered. Needs a Purview retention policy configured for the Microsoft Copilot experiences location. Stored-data residency is Australian but inference may run outside Australia until in-country processing ships. Files and connector destinations have separate lifecycles.

Source statement: "You can use a retention policy to retain data from messages in Copilot or another AI app, and delete those messages." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/retention-policies-copilot)

Research detail: Purview retention policies now have a separate Microsoft 365 Copilot location for prompts and responses. Stored content of Copilot interactions falls under Microsoft 365 data residency commitments, and Australia is a Local Region Geography with Advanced Data Residency available. Inference location is separate from storage: today Copilot can process outside Australia, and Microsoft has announced Australian in-country inference by the end of 2026.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Microsoft Purview retention, Microsoft 365 data residency, Advanced Data Residency, Microsoft 365 Copilot in-country data processing for Australia

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Files, Pages and connector destinations have their own lifecycles. Anthropic models are excluded from the EU Data Boundary, and their processing location for Australian tenants is not stated.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Map each Copilot data copy to a retention policy, and recheck the in-country processing release before making an Australian processing claim.
