## Answer

Covered. Applies to Copilot Studio agents on the standard harness, not Microsoft 365 Copilot built-in experiences. Default GPT Chat Latest takes snapshot updates within a major version; pick a specific model for a fixed version. Preview and experimental models are not for production; cross-geo models may process data outside the region.

Source statement: "Copilot Studio models vary by intended use, release status, and region." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot-studio/authoring-select-agent-model)

Research detail: Microsoft 365 Copilot sends prompts grounded in Microsoft Graph data to LLMs, including Microsoft-hosted models and OpenAI and Anthropic models as subprocessors that admins can choose to allow. Calls go to the closest data centres in the region but can use other regions under high load, and customers outside the EU may have queries processed in the US, EU or other regions. Microsoft has announced in-country inference for Microsoft 365 Copilot in Australia, expected by the end of 2026 under its April 2026 update.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Microsoft 365 Copilot, Microsoft 365 Copilot in-country data processing for Australia

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Model version per interaction is not exposed to the tenant. Australian in-country inference was a commitment, not a shipped setting, at the last dated source.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Check the Microsoft 365 admin center and Message Center for the Australian in-country processing release before relying on it, and decide whether to allow Anthropic models.
