## Answer

Covered. Reports are in the Service Trust Portal. ISO/IEC 42001 scope includes Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Studio. Check the IRAP and SOC 2 reports for coverage of agents built in Copilot Studio.

Source statement: "Microsoft AI Systems undergo regular independent third-party audits for ISO/IEC 42001 compliance." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/regulatory/offering-iso-42001)

Research detail: Microsoft says an Australian Government PROTECTED IRAP assessment of Microsoft 365 includes Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Chat, available from the Service Trust Portal, and that agencies can use Microsoft's IRAP assessments in their own approval to operate. Microsoft Copilot and Copilot Studio are in scope of Microsoft's ISO/IEC 42001 certification. Office 365 and Copilot Studio are in SOC 2 Type 2 scope.
