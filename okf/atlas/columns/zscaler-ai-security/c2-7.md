## Answer

Not covered. No sandbox for agent files, processes or network, and no documented kill switch for a running agent. Per-user egress allow/block to AI apps falls short.

Research detail: Zscaler controls network egress to AI apps per user and group with allow, block, isolate and caution actions, and AI Guard enforces policy at the moment of interaction with automatic remediation of detected violations. The June 2026 AI Broker for Agents announcement describes an Agent Registry and fine-grained access across agents. No documented sandbox for agent code or filesystem, and no documented control to stop a running agent or revoke its access at once.
