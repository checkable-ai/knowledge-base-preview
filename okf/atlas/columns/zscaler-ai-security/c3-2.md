## Answer

Not covered. No documented allowed-tool lists, input format checks, limits or blocks on dangerous tool actions. AI Broker's inline per-action least-privilege on MCP and A2A is the nearby feature, but it falls short of that.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Zscaler governs egress to AI apps per user and group with allow, block, isolate and caution actions. AI Guard flags malicious URLs in responses. AI Broker for Agents is announced to broker MCP traffic, which would place a gateway in front of agent tool calls.
