## Answer

Covered. Data handling differs by deployment type; zero data retention needs approval; Model Vault region is fixed at creation; production limits for newer models may need sales contact.

Source statement: "Every model in the vault runs in that region, and your prompts and responses are processed there." (https://docs.cohere.com/docs/model-vault/regions)

Research detail: Models are called by dated IDs such as command-a-03-2025, with documented lifecycle stages and email notice of deprecation. Rate limits are published per model and key type. Data handling is set out per deployment type, and Model Vault offers dedicated isolated inference with confidential computing.
