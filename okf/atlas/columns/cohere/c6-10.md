## Answer

Covered. 30-day automatic deletion applies to the SaaS platform; zero data retention needs approval; training opt-out is set in dashboard Data Controls; Model Vault allows region choice for residency. North agent memory retention is not documented.

Source statement: "If you have been approved for zero data retention, Cohere does not log any customer prompts or generations." (https://cohere.com/enterprise-data-commitments)

Research detail: SaaS prompts and generations are deleted after 30 days, ZDR is available to approved enterprise customers, training use can be opted out, and account deletion is processed within 7 days. Private and third-party cloud deployments send no prompts to Cohere. Retention of North agent memory is not documented.
