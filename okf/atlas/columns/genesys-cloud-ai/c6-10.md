## Answer

Not covered. No documented retention, deletion or residency rules for prompts, tool logs, guardrail logs or agent memory. Nearby feature: retention for recordings, transcripts and AI summaries.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Organisations can set a maximum interaction data retention time, with transcripts following recording retention. Customer data is not used for AI training without consent and the MSA opts customers out by default.
