## Answer

Not covered. No attribution of cost across model use, infrastructure and staff time, and no view of cost against return per agent. Usage reports show AI token consumption for named users and resources, and the VA Performance dashboard shows cost and latency trends. Neither attributes agent cost or calculates return.

Research detail: AI usage converts to AI Experience tokens per virtual agent session or minute and per Copilot user, viewable in billing usage reports against the fair use allowance. Containment metrics show how many sessions finish without a human.
