## Answer

Covered. Applies to Genesys Cloud Copilot and its AI agents acting for the signed-in user. Customer-facing agentic virtual agents use integration credentials, not a delegated end-customer identity.

Source statement: "Genesys Cloud Copilot and its AI Agents operate strictly within the permissions of the user." (https://help.genesys.cloud/faqs/how-does-genesys-cloud-copilot-ensure-trust-and-control/)

Research detail: Genesys Cloud Copilot and its AI agents operate strictly within the permissions of the signed-in user and call platform APIs on that user's behalf. Customer-facing agentic virtual agents act through data action integration credentials, not a propagated end-user identity.

## Products

## Gaps
