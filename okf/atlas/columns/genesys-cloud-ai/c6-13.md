## Answer

Covered. Vendor-level certifications; IRAP scope for AI features not confirmed.

Source statement: "ISO 42001:2023 certification establishes an Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS)" (https://help.genesys.cloud/articles/supported-security-standards/)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Genesys states that Genesys Cloud CX can be operated to IRAP PROTECTED level, announced on 13 December 2023 after an independent IRAP assessment, and offers the assessment through its IRAP page. Its standards page lists SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001:2022, ISO 27017, ISO 27018, ISO/IEC 42001:2023, PCI DSS and CSA CAIQ, with C5 and HIPAA for specific regions. Genesys is not on the Hosting Certification Framework list.

## Products

IRAP assessment, SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, ISO 42001

## Gaps

The IRAP statement is dated December 2023, more than 24 months before this check, and the assessment letter was not read, so whether AI Studio, Copilot and the agentic virtual agent are in scope is not established. No penetration test attestation or privacy impact material was found on the pages read.
