## Answer

Not covered. Audit devices (file, syslog, socket) emit logs with correlation IDs, but Vault provides no agent detections, SIEM integration content or response ownership.

Research detail: Audit logs carry actor identity and, by default, the Correlation-Id and X-Correlation-Id request headers, with sensitive strings HMAC hashed. Syslog and socket devices can send them to a SIEM. Vault has no detections or response ownership.
