## Answer

Not covered. No ownership, versioning, publication or review of organisational AI policies. Vault ACL and Sentinel policies are machine access-control rules, which is a different outcome.

Research detail: Vault manages access policies as ACL and Sentinel policies, with Sentinel enforcement levels of advisory, soft-mandatory and hard-mandatory, and parent namespaces can enforce policies on child tenants. These are machine access policies, not AI policy documents with ownership and review.

## Gaps

## Options
