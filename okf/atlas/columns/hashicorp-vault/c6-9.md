## Answer

Not covered. Sentinel overrides are only logged, with no exception register, approval, expiry or review.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Operators can override soft-mandatory Sentinel policies with a policy-override flag or header, and Vault records overrides in the audit log and server logs. There is no exception register with approval, expiry or review.
