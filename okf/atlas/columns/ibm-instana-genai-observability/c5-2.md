## Answer

Covered. Requires configuring AI evaluation definitions; scores are advisory. Smart Alerts add error and slow-call detection against historical baselines.

Source statement: "Define filter criteria once and continuously evaluate new traces as they arrive." (https://www.ibm.com/docs/en/instana-observability/saas?topic=features-ai-evaluation-in-instana)

Research detail: AI evaluation uses an LLM as judge to score outputs against templates for accuracy, relevance, completeness, consistency and clarity, or custom evaluators, with pass thresholds. Evaluations run continuously on new traces, on a daily or weekly schedule, or on demand. Smart alerts flag error rates above normal levels and slow calls against historical data.
