## Answer

Not covered. Instana cost calculation covers model token cost only (with Smart Alerts on token cost). It does not cover infrastructure cost, staff time, savings or delivered value, and has no budgets.

Research detail: Cost is calculated per model from input and output token prices, with predefined and custom pricing, and grouped by application or service and by hour or day. Smart alerts can fire on input, output and total token cost. Labour costs, savings and output yield are not covered.
