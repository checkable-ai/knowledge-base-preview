## Answer

Covered. Maestro instance management. Cancel is irreversible.

Source statement: "Performing the retry action on an instance restarts all the current activities in the workflow from the last completed step." (https://docs.uipath.com/maestro/automation-cloud/latest/user-guide/process-overview-homepage)

Research detail: Maestro orchestrates agents, robots and people in BPMN processes. Instance management offers pause, resume, retry from the last completed step, cancel, go to and migrate to another process version. An action history records who performed each action, plus system events such as throttling.
