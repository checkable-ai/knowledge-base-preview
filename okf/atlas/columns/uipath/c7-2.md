## Answer

Not covered. Audit and trace export to a SIEM through OpenTelemetry is documented. UiPath does not document native detections, a named owner for the response, or redaction of sensitive data in the exported telemetry. Export alone leaves the monitoring and response to the customer.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: UiPath describes a unified schema-based audit system that can feed Splunk or Microsoft Sentinel. AI Trust Layer audit can be exported on Enterprise plans, and Orchestrator audit exports to CSV and monthly archives.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Unified audit, AI Trust Layer audit export, Orchestrator audit

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No documented native detections or response ownership. AI Trust Layer exports with prompts are limited to four per month. The streaming mechanism for unified audit was not found in the fetched docs.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pull audit logs through the API on a schedule into the SIEM and write detections there.
