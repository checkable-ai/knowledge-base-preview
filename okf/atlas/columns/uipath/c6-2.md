## Answer

Not covered. Automation Hub approval is not linked to publishing and there is no registration approval for tools or models.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Automation Hub has an Agentic AI idea flow: an employee submits an assessment, an Idea Approver approves or rejects, the Process Owner completes a detailed assessment, and a Program Manager decides in the Decision Pipeline. Publishing is gated by Studio Web design policies.
