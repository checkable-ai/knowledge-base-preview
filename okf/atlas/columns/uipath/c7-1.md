## Answer

Not covered. UiPath documents audit logs and log export (UI, API, script, and robot log export to Azure Blob, S3 or GCS), but does not document write-once or integrity-validated storage, hash chaining or signing, and does not describe a documented export of an agent action record to such storage. Audit logs and traces fall short of a tamper-evident record of agent actions.

Research detail: The AI Trust Layer audit log records date, product, feature, tenant, user, model, model location, status and, when enabled, full prompts and outputs. Orchestrator audit records actions by user and component. Agent traces hold tool inputs, outputs and decisions. Maestro records an action history with who acted.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Trust Layer audit, Orchestrator audit, agent traces, Maestro action history

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Documentation does not state that logs are tamper-proof (only the product page claims tamper-evident). No agent field in the AI Trust Layer audit. Prompt saving can be turned off, and trace TTL can be 1 day.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Export audit data to a write-once store. Keep input and output saving on for audited agents.
