## Answer

Not covered. Context Grounding has tenant scoping, RBAC, encryption and cascading deletion, but there is no documented cleaning, sanitisation or content screening of ingested data, and index health monitoring does not govern data.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: Context Grounding indexes are created in Orchestrator, and the Agents hub shows index status, health and ingestion progress. The AI Trust Layer policy can disable Context Grounding at tenant level.
