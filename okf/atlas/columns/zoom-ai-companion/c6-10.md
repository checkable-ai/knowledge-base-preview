## Answer

Covered. Custom retention periods for certain content; zero data retention for meeting summary is admin-set; EU and AU residency uses ZM+ or ZMO. Retention of agent memory and conversation history is not specified.

Source statement: "Customers may also set custom retention periods for certain content." (https://library.zoom.com/ai/ai-whitepaper/models-processing-storage-and-usage.md)

Research detail: Retention follows the associated service with custom retention for some content. Admins can enforce zero data retention for summary transcripts. Third-party model providers have zero data retention, customer communications content is not used for training, and EU and AU accounts use ZM+ for residency.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Transcript retention settings, ZM+ and ZMO, CMK

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Memory and conversation history retention are not specified, and participants or third parties may keep their own transcripts.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Disable and lock transcripts where needed and confirm memory retention with Zoom.
