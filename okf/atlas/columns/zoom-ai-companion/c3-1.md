## Answer

Not covered. No customer-facing gateway for model calls. Model routing is internal to Zoom, and the documentation shows no customer-set quotas, per-agent rate limits or prompt injection screening. AI Credits are a licensing measure, not a control point.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Zoom routes each request to a model internally under the federated approach. Advanced Mode and agentic workflows consume AI credits, and feature access depends on licence, credit availability and usage limits.
