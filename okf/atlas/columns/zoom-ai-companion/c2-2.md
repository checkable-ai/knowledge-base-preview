## Answer

Not covered. Admins cannot choose model version or endpoint, versions are not disclosed, and models in the federation can change at any time. ZM+/ZMO and region handling are nearby features but do not give per-agent model control.

Research detail: Zoom uses a federated approach that routes requests across Zoom-hosted models and Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini and other providers. Customers can move to Zoom-Hosted Models Plus (isolated Bedrock instances) or Zoom-Hosted Models Only through Zoom support. Third-party providers operate under zero data retention.

## Products

Federated approach, ZM+, ZMO

## Gaps

Admins cannot pick a model directly, model versions are not disclosed and Zoom states models can change at any time.

## Options

Choose ZM+ or ZMO where third-party processing is unacceptable, and ask Zoom for model change notice terms.
