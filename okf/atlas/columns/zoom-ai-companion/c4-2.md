## Answer

Not covered. Short-lived rotated agent credentials and isolation of secrets from model context are not documented. OAuth for inbound MCP access covers only access to Zoom.

Research detail: External MCP clients reach Zoom MCP servers through a standard OAuth flow on a Marketplace app, with scopes chosen per app and PKCE, and send the access token as a bearer token. Client registration is manual. How ZoomMate stores, rotates or revokes credentials for its own connectors, and whether they reach model context, is not documented.

## Gaps
