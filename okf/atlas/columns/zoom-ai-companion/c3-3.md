## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Custom AI guardrails are announced and expected to be available in Q1 2027; they are not yet generally available. Access to third-party data source apps requires a ZoomMate license.

Source statement: "Admins can set rules to exclude specific topics or data from AI processing, and set limits on what data AI agents can output." (https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/zoomtopia2026-ai-governance-zoom-workplace/?cms_guid=false)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Admins choose which repositories and folders are indexed and retrieval respects source permissions. Admins can redact predefined personal data entities and block custom regular expressions from meeting summaries, and can stop persistent transcripts.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

ZoomMate indexing scope, Personal Data Redaction Service, Block Sensitive Information

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Redaction and regex blocking apply to meeting summaries and need a support ticket. No DLP on agent prompts, tool outputs or connector data is documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable summary redaction and pair Zoom with a DLP product for connected data.
