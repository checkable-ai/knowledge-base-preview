## Answer

Not covered. Only third-party apps in Zoom meetings are attributable. No wider shadow AI discovery.

Research detail: Third-party apps that access meeting content outside their own account must now attribute to a user, so they are visible to admins and participants. Marketplace Admin App Management lets admins disable or remove installed apps.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Meeting SDK attribution, Marketplace Admin App Management

## Gaps

Covers third-party AI apps in Zoom meetings only, not wider shadow AI. External guests can still bring note takers subject to host controls.

## Options

Combine Marketplace pre-approval with meeting entry controls, and use a CASB for other shadow AI.
