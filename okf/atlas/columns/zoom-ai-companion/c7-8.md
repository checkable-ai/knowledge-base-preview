## Answer

Not covered. Zoom documents admin-managed connections and permission-preserving access to source data, but nothing that governs, cleans or secures the data stores themselves (index retention, sanitisation, deletion controls). The vendor does not say it covers part of this outcome.

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: Account-level sources are indexed into a customer-specific index with permission metadata, scoped to admin-selected repositories. Knowledge collections feed custom agents. Customer Managed Key is available for Enterprise or as an add-on.
