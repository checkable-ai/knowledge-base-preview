## Answer

Not covered. Zoom assesses sub-processors annually, but customers cannot approve, reassess or retire individual model providers. Marketplace app controls cover apps, not external models.

Research detail: Sub-processors including model providers must meet DPA-equivalent obligations and are assessed at least annually. Customers can avoid third-party model providers with ZM+ or ZMO. Marketplace admins can approve, disable or remove third-party apps.
