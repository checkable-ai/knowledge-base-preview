## Answer

Not covered. No documented stable agent identity tied to an owner, runtime or release. Name, avatar and description in AI Studio fall short.

Research detail: Custom agents built in AI Studio have a name, avatar and description, and AI agents in Team Chat are listed separately in a channel profile card with an 'AI agents present' notice. Third-party apps joining meetings outside their account must now attribute to a user through an On Behalf token, ZAK token or RTMS. No documented stable agent identity linked to an owner, runtime or release.

## Products

AI Studio custom agents, ZoomMate AI agents in Team Chat, Meeting SDK On Behalf token

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Custom agents have no documented owner field or version identity. ZoomMate itself runs as the invoking user.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Keep an external register mapping each custom agent to an owner and published state, and require OBF or RTMS attribution for third-party meeting apps.
