## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Agentic workflows for ZoomMate are available to paid ZoomMate and Custom AI Companion users in the US. Agentic workflows need a Zoom Workplace Pro, Pro Plus, Business, Business Plus, Enterprise, Enterprise Plus or Enterprise Bundle account. Free users get up to 10 runs per month. ZoomMate may not be available for select industry verticals and regional customers. Tool sets default to always ask for approval, but users can choose to always allow actions without confirmation. An admin can disable automatic allow and force approval for sensitive write operations.

Source statement: "Agentic workflows for ZoomMate are available to paid ZoomMate and Custom AI Companion users in the US." (https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0085380)

Last checked 16 November 2026.

Research detail: ZoomMate Actions can run automatically or pause for review or approval before final execution, which the page says is especially relevant for communications and external system changes. AI-generated Zoom Tasks are shown awaiting acceptance.
