## Answer

Covered. Account owners and admins; AI Studio custom agents (Custom AI Companion add-on). Features can be toggled and locked at account or group level.

Source statement: "admins can monitor activity and usage patterns, manage approval workflows, and control agent availability to users." (https://www.zoom.com/en/products/ai-assistant/features/agent-builder/)

Research detail: Only account owners and admins can create, edit, publish, unpublish and delete custom agents. Custom AI Companion features are toggled and locked at account or group level.

## Products

AI Studio custom agents, Admin Center settings and locks

## Gaps
