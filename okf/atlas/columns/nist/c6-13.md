## Answer

Not covered. No IRAP, SOC 2 or ISO attestation; NIST runs no certification for the RMF.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: MEASURE 1.3 asks that internal experts who were not front-line developers or independent assessors take part in regular assessments. The RMF is voluntary and NIST runs no certification or attestation scheme for it. It does not cover IRAP, SOC 2 or ISO certification.
