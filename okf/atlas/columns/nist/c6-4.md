## Answer

Not covered. No policy ownership, versioning or publication mechanism.

Last checked 12 November 2026.

Research detail: GOVERN 1.1 to 1.4 ask that legal requirements are understood and that trustworthy AI characteristics and risk management are built into transparent policies, and GOVERN 1.5 asks for periodic review with defined frequency. GV-4.2-001 suggests terms of use and GV-6.1-010 suggests updating acceptable use policies. Policy versioning and publication are not specified.
