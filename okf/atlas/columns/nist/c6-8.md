## Answer

Not covered. No approval, versioning or rollback of prompts, tools and permissions.

Research detail: MANAGE 4.1 includes change management in post-deployment plans, and MG-1.3-001 suggests staged release approaches. MP-4.1-007 and MG-3.1-003 suggest re-evaluating models after fine-tuning or retrieval augmentation, and MS-2.8-003 mentions version control across the lifecycle. Approval and rollback of prompt, tool and permission changes are not covered.
