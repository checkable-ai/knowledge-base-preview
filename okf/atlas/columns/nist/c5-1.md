Research detail: MS-2.6-004 suggests reviewing generative AI outputs for validity and safety, including reviewing generated code, and MAP 2.2 asks for documentation of how system output may be overseen by humans. MEASURE 2.9 asks that AI output is interpreted within its context. The documents do not specify how review is recorded or enforced.
