## Answer

Not covered. MAP 3.5 and GOVERN 3.2 require human oversight roles and processes in general. Nothing requires approval of a specific proposed action before it runs, or handling of refusals and repeat attempts.

Research detail: GOVERN 3.2 asks for policies that define roles for human-AI configurations and oversight of AI systems, and MAP 3.5 asks for processes for human oversight to be defined, assessed and documented. MS-4.2-004 in the Generative AI Profile asks organisations to monitor and document cases where human operators override the system's decisions. None of this binds an approval to a specific proposed action.
