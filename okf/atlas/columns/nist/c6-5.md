## Answer

Covered. Voluntary guidance; agents and use cases not treated separately from AI systems.

Source statement: "Processes and procedures are in place for decommissioning and phasing out of AI systems safely" (https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/govern/)

Research detail: The RMF frames risk management across the AI lifecycle. MAP gives an initial go or no-go decision, MEASURE asks for testing before deployment and in operation, MANAGE 4.1 asks for post-deployment monitoring plans covering change management and decommissioning, and GOVERN 1.7 asks for procedures to decommission AI systems safely. GV-1.7-002 lists decommissioning factors such as data retention and dependencies.
