## Answer

Not covered. No tamper-proof action ledger. MS-2.8-003 provenance history is for content, not actions.

Last checked 12 November 2026. This platform is checked on demand, so this answer has not been rechecked since.

Research detail: MS-2.8-003 suggests digital content transparency solutions that document each instance where content is generated, modified or shared, giving a tamper-proof history, and notes version control can track changes across the lifecycle. GV-1.5-003 suggests a document retention policy for TEVV history. Neither covers a ledger of actions by user, agent and session.
