## Answer

Not covered. No telemetry, correlation, redaction or SOC hand-off.

Research detail: GOVERN 4.3 asks for practices that enable identification of incidents and information sharing, and MANAGE 4.3 asks for processes to track, respond to and recover from incidents and errors. MS-2.7-004 suggests security metrics such as the number of unauthorised access attempts, and GV-2.1-002 suggests engaging teams for generative AI incident response. Telemetry content, correlation and redaction are not specified.
