## Answer

Not covered. GOVERN 6.1, GOVERN 6.2 and MANAGE 3.1 cover third-party risk policy, contingency and regular monitoring. Retirement of third-party services is not addressed, and no reassessment schedule is set.

Research detail: GOVERN 6.1 and 6.2 ask for policies on third-party AI risks and contingency processes for third-party failures, and MANAGE 3.1 asks for third-party risks to be monitored regularly. The Generative AI Profile adds specific actions: approved provider lists (GV-6.1-007), contract clauses allowing evaluation of third-party processes (GV-6.1-006), third-party incident response plans (GV-6.2-003) and continuous monitoring of third-party systems (GV-6.2-004).
