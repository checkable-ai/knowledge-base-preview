## Answer

Covered. Voluntary guidance; ownership of individual agents and tools not addressed.

Source statement: "Roles and responsibilities and lines of communication related to mapping, measuring, and managing AI risks" (https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/govern/)

Research detail: GOVERN 2.1 asks that roles, responsibilities and lines of communication for AI risk are documented, and GOVERN 2.3 asks executive leadership to take responsibility for decisions about AI risks. MANAGE 2.4 asks for responsibilities to deactivate systems to be assigned. GV-1.6-003 adds human oversight roles to the inventory record.
