## Answer

Not covered. MANAGE 2.4 asks for mechanisms to supersede, disengage or deactivate AI systems, which is a stop process only. There is no guidance on sandboxing, file or process limits, network egress, or how fast a stop must work.

Research detail: MANAGE 2.4 asks for mechanisms, with assigned responsibilities, to supersede, disengage or deactivate AI systems whose outcomes are inconsistent with intended use. GV-1.7-001 asks for protocols so that generative AI systems can be deactivated when necessary, and MG-2.4-002 and MG-2.4-004 ask for escalation procedures and criteria for deactivation. The documents do not cover sandboxing, process policy or network egress.
