## Answer

Not covered. MEASURE 2.4 asks for monitoring of system behaviour in production. It gives no anomaly detection method or baseline, and nothing on training agents on their tasks.

Research detail: MEASURE 2.4 asks for the functionality and behaviour of the AI system to be monitored in production, and MEASURE 3.1 asks for approaches to track existing, unanticipated and emergent risks. MS-2.6-005 suggests verifying that the architecture can monitor outputs and performance and handle security anomalies. These are outcomes without a detection method.
