## Answer

Not covered. No governance of agent data stores or sanitisation.

Research detail: MP-4.1-005 suggests policies for collection, retention and minimum quality of data, and MS-2.5-005 suggests verifying training data and TEVV data provenance and that fine-tuning or retrieval data is grounded. MG-3.2-003 suggests documenting sources and types of training data. Store infrastructure is not covered.
