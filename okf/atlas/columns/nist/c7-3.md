## Answer

Not covered. No model file inspection or AI BoM. GV-1.6-003 inventory fields fall short.

Research detail: GV-1.6-003 suggests inventory entries record data provenance (source, signatures, versioning, watermarks) and underlying foundation models and versions. MG-3.2-002 suggests documenting how pre-trained models were adapted, and MANAGE 3.2 asks that pre-trained models are monitored. Model file inspection and an AI bill of materials are not covered.
