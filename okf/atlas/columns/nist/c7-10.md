## Answer

Not covered. Red-teaming suggested, but no automated attack testing of tools and runtime.

Research detail: MS-2.7-007 suggests AI red-teaming to assess resilience against named attacks, including prompt injection, data poisoning, membership inference, model extraction and sponge examples. MP-5.1-005 suggests adversarial role-playing, red-teaming or chaos testing, and MS-1.3-002 suggests internal and external red-teaming. Automation and tool or runtime boundaries are not specified.
