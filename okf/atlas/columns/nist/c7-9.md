## Answer

Not covered. No discovery method for unsanctioned AI. Inventory (GOVERN 1.6) is not detection.

Research detail: GV-1.6-001 suggests enumerating organisational generative AI systems into the AI inventory, and GV-1.6-002 asks for inventory exemptions for AI embedded in application software to be defined in policy. GV-6.1-009 asks procurement to address solutions that rely on embedded generative AI. There is no discovery method for unsanctioned use.
