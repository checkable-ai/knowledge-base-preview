## Answer

Covered. Voluntary guidance; agents and tools are not named inventory items.

Source statement: "Mechanisms are in place to inventory AI systems and are resourced according to organizational risk priorities." (https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/govern/)

Research detail: GOVERN 1.6 asks for mechanisms to inventory AI systems. GV-1.6-003 lists inventory fields for generative AI systems: data provenance, known issues from bug tracking or incident databases, human oversight roles, intellectual property and sensitive data considerations, and underlying foundation models, versions and access modes. Agents and tools are not named as inventory items.
