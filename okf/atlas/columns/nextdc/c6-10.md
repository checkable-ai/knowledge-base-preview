## Answer

Not covered. No retention, deletion or permitted-use rules for agent inputs, outputs or memory. S7 sovereign residency is a planned claim and Certified Strategic status covers government hosting, not retention rules.

Research detail: NEXTDC describes itself as a DTA Certified Strategic hosting provider and its certifications page lists Certified Strategic under the Hosting Certification Framework. The S7 page says the campus is intended to deliver sovereign data residency, and the MoU announcement says S7 will be aligned to the SOCI framework. These cover where data is physically held. Retention, deletion and permitted use sit with the customer.
