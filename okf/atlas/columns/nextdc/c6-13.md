## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Facility-level assessments only: ISO 27001:2022 for all NEXTDC data centres and head office, plus SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type 2 reports. No IRAP, ISO 42001 or penetration test attestation is published. S7 is not yet built.

Source statement: "This certification has been achieved for all NEXTDC data centres and Head Office." (https://www.nextdc.com/about-us/certifications)

Last checked 19 October 2026.

Research detail: NEXTDC lists ISO 27001:2022 for all data centres and head office, SOC 1 Type 2 and SOC 2 Type 2 reports, PCI DSS for physical security and availability, Certified Strategic under the Hosting Certification Framework, DISP membership, SCEC designed and built facilities, and Uptime Institute Tier III and IV. The certifications page does not mention IRAP or ISO 42001.
