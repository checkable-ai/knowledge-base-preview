## Answer

Not covered. Change history and approvals are recorded, but OneTrust does not version, release or roll back models, prompts, tools or permissions.

Research detail: Record-level change history captures prompt updates, model swaps, retrieval source changes and new agent actions, with who approved them and whether reassessment is required. OneTrust does not version, release or roll back the artefacts themselves.
