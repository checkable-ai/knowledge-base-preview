## Answer

Not covered. No documented approval of the exact action by an authorised person before it runs, and no refusal or repeat-attempt handling. CORIE flagging a tool call for human review falls short of an approval gate. Approval gates at intake and deployment are not per-action approvals.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: CORIE can route a flagged tool call for human review, and the AI Control Plane is described as deciding whether an action proceeds, is blocked or needs human approval. Governance workflows also include approval gates at intake, deployment and material change.
