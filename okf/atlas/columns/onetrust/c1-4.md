## Answer

Not covered. Registry records and governance workflows do not set up, change or retire the agent itself. Deleting a record leaves the underlying agent untouched.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: The registry governs agent records through lifecycle states from proposed to retired, with change history recording what changed and who approved it. It does not create or retire the agent identities or permissions in the runtime.
