## Answer

Not covered. AI Guard and AI Policy Engine detect and block sensitive data, but no per-agent or per-user retrieval filtering gateway is documented.

Research detail: AI Guard classifies prompts and responses with 300+ classifiers and applies allow, redact or block actions, including on streaming responses. The AI Policy Engine can block a model's access to a sensitive dataset, and CORIE can deny a data request before the model sees it.
