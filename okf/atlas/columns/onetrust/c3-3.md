## Answer

Not covered. No documented per-user retrieval filtering. CORIE tool-call enforcement and AI Guard redaction of prompts and responses are nearby features, but they are not a data-access gateway filtering what each agent and user can retrieve.

Research detail: AI Guard classifies prompts and responses with 300+ classifiers and applies allow, redact or block actions, including on streaming responses. The AI Policy Engine can block a model's access to a sensitive dataset, and CORIE can deny a data request before the model sees it.

## Products

AI Guard SDK and Light Worker Node, AI Policy Engine, CORIE

## Gaps
