## Answer

Not covered. No sandboxing or agent-wide stop or access revocation. CORIE and AI Guard act on individual calls only, which falls short of an emergency stop.

Research detail: OneTrust does not host or sandbox agent code execution and documents no filesystem, process or network egress policy for agents. The AI Policy Engine can block or restrict a model's access to a sensitive dataset, AI Guard can allow, redact or block prompts and outputs, and CORIE (announced 29 September 2026) can allow, stop or flag an individual tool call. None of these is described as halting a running agent or revoking its access as a whole, and per-action blocking sits under the tool and data gateway controls.
