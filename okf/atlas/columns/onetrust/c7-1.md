## Answer

Not covered. No documented tamper protection and no per-action record by user, agent and session with inputs, outputs and reasoning. The CORIE Evidence Ledger records decisions, controls and actions but does not document integrity protection.

Research detail: CORIE records the agent, request, purpose, outcome and governing policy for each decision, and the Evidence Ledger records what was considered, which control applied and what action was taken. The governance FAQ describes a continuous audit trail from intake through monitoring.
