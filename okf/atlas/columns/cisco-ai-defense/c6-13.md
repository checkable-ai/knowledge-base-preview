## Answer

Not covered. No public IRAP, ISO 27001 or ISO/IEC 42001 attestation for AI Defense. The SOC 2 Type 2 statement sits on a securitydocs page that could not be fetched, and the Cisco Trust Portal needs a browser and NDA. Event logs are stored in the US, and points of presence are in the US, Frankfurt and Tokyo only.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Cisco states that AI Defense storage is tenant-isolated and encrypted and was tested under SOC 2 Type 2 confidentiality controls with no exceptions noted. The Hybrid model keeps runtime inspection content in the customer's environment, which helps an agency risk assessment. No IRAP assessment is stated.
