## Answer

Covered. Needs Splunk or ServiceNow integration or syslog/OpenTelemetry export from hybrid deployments configured. User correlation needs the application to pass user IDs. Response ownership is assigned by the customer.

Source statement: "Integrate with AI security operations and broader SecOps through security console integrations, such as Splunk." (https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/products/collateral/security/ai-defense/ai-defense-ds.html)

Research detail: Runtime events with OWASP and MITRE ATLAS mappings export to Splunk, to ServiceNow SecOps and DLP modules, and from hybrid deployments to any syslog SIEM through OpenTelemetry. Sensitive values are redacted from events by default.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Splunk integration, ServiceNow integration, OpenTelemetry syslog export

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Actor correlation needs the app to pass user IDs. Session correlation is not documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Pass user and session IDs in requests and correlate in the SIEM.
