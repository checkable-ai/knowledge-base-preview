## Answer

Not covered. No rules governing retention, deletion or residency of customer agent inputs, outputs or memory. Only AI Defense's own event retention is documented.

Research detail: AI Defense documents how it retains and redacts its own data, such as one-year event retention and a logging opt-out. It does not govern retention or residency of customer AI inputs, outputs or memory.
