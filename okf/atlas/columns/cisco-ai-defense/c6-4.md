## Answer

Not covered. Policy Studio versions and publishes runtime guardrail policies only. No organisational AI policy ownership, authoring workflow or review cycle.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Runtime policies are defined from guardrails, DLP and MCP profiles. Policy Studio keeps draft and published states, version history and a change log, and only admins can edit policies.
