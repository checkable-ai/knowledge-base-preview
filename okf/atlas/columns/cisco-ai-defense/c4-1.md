## Answer

Not covered. No issuance of agent identities or binding to owner, runtime and release. Inventory records are discovery data only.

Research detail: AI Defense does not issue or manage agent or workload identities. It inventories agents found in AWS and GitHub, with provider ARN, owner and source commit, but these are discovery records. OAuth with a cluster JWKS only authenticates AI Defense hybrid workloads to AI Defense.
