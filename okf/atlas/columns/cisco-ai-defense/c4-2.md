## Answer

Not covered. No short-lived scoped credential issuance, rotation or revocation for agents. Stored credentials are only for AI Defense's own use.

Research detail: AI Defense does not deliver or rotate credentials for agents. It stores credentials it needs itself, such as red teaming target credentials in tenant secrets and MCP server OAuth client details for scanning.
