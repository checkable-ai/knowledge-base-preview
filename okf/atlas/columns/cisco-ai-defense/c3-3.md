## Answer

Not covered. No filtering of what each agent or user may retrieve. DLP and privacy guardrails only detect, block or redact sensitive content in prompts and responses.

Research detail: Privacy guardrails detect PII, PHI and PCI in prompts and responses and can block or redact at the gateway. DLP Profiles add keyword and RE2 regex detectors with monitor or block actions. Secure Access integration applies DLP to third-party AI apps.
