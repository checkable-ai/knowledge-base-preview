## Answer

Covered. Risk details need an E7 or Agent 365 licence. Counts can lag security portals by up to an hour.

Source statement: "Risk signals in the Agent Registry are a consolidated set of agent-related security detections that give IT administrators a single view of agent risks across their tenant." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-agent-365/admin/agent-registry-overview)

Research detail: The registry shows risk signals from Entra, Defender and Purview per agent, plus counts of agents at risk, ownerless and unmanaged. Defender gives risk levels and posture recommendations, and Purview DSPM AI observability ranks agents by risk.
