## Answer

Covered. Owner reassignment applies only to Agent Builder and Copilot Studio agents.

Source statement: "If the sponsor is leaving the organization, sponsorship of the agent identities is automatically transferred to their manager." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/id-governance/agent-id-governance-overview)

Research detail: Each agent identity and blueprint must have at least one sponsor, and sponsorship moves to the sponsor's manager when they leave, with lifecycle workflow notifications. The registry flags agents without owners, and admins can assign or add owners.
