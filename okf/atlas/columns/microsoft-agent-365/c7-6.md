## Answer

Not covered. No scanning for look-alike names, poisoned tool descriptions or compromised packages. Publisher-category restriction and request approval govern availability only.

Research detail: Admins can restrict plugins by publisher category, including certified external publishers, and approve or reject access requests. The Certification tab shows publisher attestation and Microsoft 365 Certification status. Defender detects indirect prompt injection in tool responses.

## Products

Tools registry, Certification tab, Defender

## Gaps

No scanning for squatting, tool metadata poisoning or package compromise. Third-party MCP servers are used at the customer's own risk under the registry notice.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options
