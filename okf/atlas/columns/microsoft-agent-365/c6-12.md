## Answer

Not covered. No scheduled reassessment workflow. Approval, blocking and publisher controls exist, but periodic review and retirement are left to the customer.

Research detail: Third-party agent platforms are connected and synced with shared admin responsibilities for credentials. Admins can allow or block certified external publishers, review Microsoft 365 Certification valid for 12 months, and approve or delete third-party MCP servers.
