## Answer

Covered. Needs Purview eDiscovery or Communication Compliance licensing and configured policies. Applies to agent-to-human and human-to-agent interactions in Teams and email; review happens after delivery.

Source statement: "You can review and export information directly from the review set." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/ai-agent-365)

Research detail: Purview Communication Compliance detects policy violations in agent-to-human and human-to-agent interactions in Teams and email for reviewer triage. eDiscovery lets reviewers search and export AI interactions.

## Products

## Gaps

## Options
