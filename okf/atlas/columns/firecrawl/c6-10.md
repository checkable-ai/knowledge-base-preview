<!-- chunk: answer -->
## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Zero data retention is available on Enterprise plans and must be enabled per team. Agent ZDR requires both Scrape ZDR and Search ZDR enabled for the team. ZDR agent runs are billed at 2x AI credits and do not support follow-up turns. Firecrawl processes all data in the United States and does not offer EU data residency.

Source statement: "In a ZDR run, Firecrawl does not keep the prompt, the result or the content of the agent's steps in logs or storage." (https://docs.firecrawl.dev/features/agent.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Zero data retention is available on Enterprise for Scrape, Interact, Search and Agent, so page content and results are not persisted past the request. All data is processed in the United States with no EU option. Account data is kept until the customer asks for deletion.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Zero data retention, PII redaction, Trust Center deletion monitoring

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

ZDR is Enterprise only and must be enabled for the team. No regional choice. Retention of non-ZDR content and the 2 day default cache are not otherwise configurable beyond storeInCache.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Enable ZDR for the team and confirm Agent results are retrieved within 24 hours.
