## Answer

Not covered. No recording, approval, review or expiry of policy exceptions or accepted risks. The Threat Protection whitelist overrides other rules but has no approver, expiry or review.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Threat Protection has a custom whitelist that overrides every other rule, an option to disable request overrides and an enterprise-only switch to ignore robots.txt on crawls. These can record exceptions to policy, but approval, expiry and review of them are not documented.
