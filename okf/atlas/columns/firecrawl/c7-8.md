## Answer

With our team for a decision. The agent checks did not agree on this finding.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Content can be sanitised with PII redaction and not retained with zero data retention. Scrape caching defaults to two days and storeInCache false skips it, while change tracking snapshots persist. Firecrawl feeds data stores but does not govern them.

## Products

redactPII, zero data retention, cache controls

## Gaps

Governance of the downstream store is the customer's. Default retention of non-ZDR content is not stated.
