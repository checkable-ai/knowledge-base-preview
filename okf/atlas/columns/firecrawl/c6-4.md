## Answer

Not covered. No authoring, ownership, versioning, publication or review of AI policies. Threat Protection is an enterprise-gated URL fetch policy that admins can edit, with no version history or review cycle.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Threat Protection is a written organisation policy with mode, risk threshold, blacklist, whitelist, blocked TLDs and failure policy. Admins define it, it can be enforced so requests cannot weaken it, and others see a read-only view. No versioning or review cycle is documented.
