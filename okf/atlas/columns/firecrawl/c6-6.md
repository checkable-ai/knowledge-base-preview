## Answer

Not covered. No accountable owner field or review for agents, models or tools. API key owners and team admins manage keys, which is not ownership of agents.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Key owners and team Admins can rename keys, set limits and manage them, and SIEM events attribute fetches to a key name. This gives a weak link from activity to a person or team. No accountable owner field for agents or decisions exists.
