## Answer

With our team for a decision. The agent checks did not agree on this finding.

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: In Agent chat mode, requireApproval stops the turn before any paid provider call and returns a pendingApproval listing each call with its provider, input and credit estimate for the caller to approve or decline. It covers paid provider calls only, not every scrape or browser action.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Agent requireApproval and pendingApproval

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Not documented for ordinary scrape, crawl or Interact actions. Replay handling and an approver separate from the caller are not described.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Build approval in the calling agent for write-capable steps and use requireApproval where paid providers are enabled.
