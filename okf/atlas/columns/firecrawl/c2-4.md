## Answer

Partly covered. Who can read a session's memory is not separately controlled (sessions are tied to the owning team), and retention for non-ZDR sessions beyond the timeouts is not documented.

Source statement: "Sessions also expire automatically based on TTL (default: 10 minutes) or inactivity timeout (default: 5 minutes)." (https://docs.firecrawl.dev/features/interact.md)

Last checked 17 November 2026.

Research detail: Interact keeps a browser session open across calls and shares its state between them until it is stopped. Agent threads let a follow-up turn continue earlier work. Sessions are tied to the scrape ID and the owning team.

## Products

Interact sessions, Agent threads

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Session timeouts, per-user access to a session and retention for non-ZDR sessions are not documented.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Stop sessions explicitly and use ZDR where session content is sensitive.
