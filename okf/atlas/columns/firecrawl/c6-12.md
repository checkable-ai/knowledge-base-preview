## Answer

Not covered. No approval, scheduled reassessment or retirement process for a customer's external models, tools and services. The published subprocessor list and DPA cover Firecrawl as a supplier only.

Research detail: The subprocessor list is published in the Trust Center with advance notice to subscribers before a new subprocessor handles customer personal data. Enterprise customers can sign a DPA and run vendor security reviews. This covers Firecrawl as a vendor, not a customer's approval flow for other tools.
