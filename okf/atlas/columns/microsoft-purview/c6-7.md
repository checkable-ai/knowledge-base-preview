## Answer

Not covered. No feature assesses each AI use case, gives it a risk level and matches it to required controls. Compliance Manager AI regulation templates assess compliance against a regulation and do not rate individual use cases.

Research detail: Compliance Manager provides premium assessment templates for the EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 23894, ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF that map controls and improvement actions. Insider Risk Management assigns risk levels to agents shown in AI observability.

## Products

Compliance Manager AI regulation templates, Insider Risk Management

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Templates assess compliance, not per use case risk tiering. AI templates count against premium licences, with six months free for Copilot or Agent licences.

## Options

Use the EU AI Act template per AI system as a proxy for use case assessment.
