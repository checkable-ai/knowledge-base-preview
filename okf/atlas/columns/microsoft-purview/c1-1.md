## Answer

Not covered. No documented control over what an agent may do on its host (files, commands, local tools). Endpoint DLP restricted apps and app groups govern how listed apps handle protected files, which is data-loss prevention and not scoping of an agent's permitted local actions.

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Checked endpoint DLP and Agent 365 pages. Endpoint DLP warns or blocks users pasting sensitive data into third-party AI sites in a browser, but there is no control over what an agent may do on a local host.
