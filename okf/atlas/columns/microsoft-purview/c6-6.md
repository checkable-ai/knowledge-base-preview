## Answer

Not covered. Compliance Manager owners are assigned to improvement actions, not to agents, models or tools. Agent owners are shown from Entra, not maintained in Purview.

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Compliance Manager improvement actions can be assigned to an owner who is notified by email, and assessors validate the work. AI observability shows the owner recorded for each Agent 365 instance.
