## Answer

Covered. Needs Compliance Manager roles. Evidence upload needs edit rights. AI regulation templates are premium. Automated testing covers only eligible improvement actions.

Source statement: "You can upload evidence related to implementation and testing work, in the form of files or links, directly to the Evidence tab of an improvement action's details page." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/compliance-manager-improvement-actions)

Research detail: Compliance Manager stores evidence files and links on improvement actions, locks evidence once tested, exports testing history and assessment reports for auditors, and syncs Foundry evaluation results for AI models and agents into assessments.
