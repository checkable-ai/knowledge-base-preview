## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Needs a Compliance Manager role. Only users with editing rights can upload, download and delete evidence. Compliance Manager requires an Office 365 or Microsoft 365 licence. AI regulatory templates use premium regulation licences.

Source statement: "This environment is a secure, centralized repository to help you demonstrate satisfaction of controls to meet compliance standards and regulations." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/compliance-manager-improvement-actions)

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Compliance Manager stores evidence files and links on improvement actions, locks evidence once tested, exports testing history and assessment reports for auditors, and syncs Foundry evaluation results for AI models and agents into assessments.
