## Answer

Covered. Data risk assessments for Fabric need one-time Entra app setup.

Source statement: "Data Security Posture Management continuously scans your environment to identify sensitive data, assess risk, and recommend actions to reduce exposure." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/data-security-posture-management-learn-about)

Research detail: Unified Catalog governs data assets with governance domains, data products, access policies and data quality rules. DSPM data risk assessments scan Fabric workspaces and SharePoint sites for overshared sensitive data and recommend labelling and DLP remediation.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Unified Catalog, DSPM data risk assessments

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No feature store specific controls or sanitisation of training or retrieval data. Fabric assessments need a one-time Entra app setup.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Register retrieval sources in Unified Catalog and run a custom data risk assessment before connecting them to agents.
