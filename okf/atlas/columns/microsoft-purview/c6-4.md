## Answer

Not covered. No documented workflow to author, own, version, publish or review an organisation's AI policies. Compliance Manager assessments and improvement actions track compliance but do not manage a policy lifecycle.

Research detail: Compliance Manager supplies AI regulatory templates, supports custom assessments with added controls, and versions templates through updates that admins review before accepting. Nontechnical documentation improvement actions track policy work.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Compliance Manager regulatory templates, custom assessments, assessment updates

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No authoring, publication or periodic review workflow for an organisation's own AI policies. Custom assessments are preview.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Hold AI policies in a document system and track them as documentation actions in Compliance Manager.
