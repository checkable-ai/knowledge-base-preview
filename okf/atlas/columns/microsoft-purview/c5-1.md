## Answer

Covered. Needs eDiscovery and Communication Compliance licensing and roles. AI interaction content must be collected (non-Microsoft apps need a collection policy). Review happens after the fact through policies and review sets, not as an approval gate before output is used.

Source statement: "You can review and export information directly from the review set." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/ai-microsoft-purview)

Research detail: Communication Compliance policies detect and route AI prompts and responses for reviewer triage, and eDiscovery review sets let reviewers examine and export AI interactions. Activity explorer shows prompt and response text to users with the content viewer role.
