## Answer

Not covered. Purview Audit records AI events, but Microsoft does not document write-once or integrity-validated storage of the audit log that makes alteration or deletion detectable. Retention policies and the audit log listing fall short of tamper-evidence.

Research detail: The unified audit log captures AI interactions by user and agent with prompt and response message IDs, accessed resources, plugins, model provider and policy outcomes. Agent 365 audit covers agent-to-human, agent-to-tool and agent-to-agent interactions. Audit Premium retains records up to one year, or ten years with an add-on.

## Products

Purview Audit (Standard and Premium), DSPM activity explorer

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Documentation does not claim tamper-proof storage. Fabric data agent records exclude internal orchestration and reasoning. Third-party AI app audit needs pay-as-you-go billing.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Export via the Management Activity API to immutable storage and test record completeness for a sample agent.
