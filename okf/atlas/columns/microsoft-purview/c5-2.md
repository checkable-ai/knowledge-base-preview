## Answer

Partly covered. I fetched the page. It documents the Risky Agents (preview) template, which continuously watches what Copilot Studio and Foundry agents do. It detects risky prompts, sensitive responses, access to sensitive or priority SharePoint files, access to risky websites and external sharing. It also flags activity above the agent's baseline ('Activity is above agent's usual activity for the day'). That meets the core of the control, monitoring agent behaviour: continuous validation and anomaly detection. The adversary's point stands that Purview does not train agents on their tasks. But a monitoring product does not train agents, and the vendor's page never says the feature covers only part of the outcome, so partial does not apply under the rules. Preview status and the limit to Copilot Studio and Foundry agents go in conditions, not the verdict.
