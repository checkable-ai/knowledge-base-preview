## Answer

Covered. Risky Agents template is in preview. Covers Copilot Studio and Microsoft Foundry agents only. Part of Insider Risk Management. Documented as applied by default for all organizations. Purview does not train agents on tasks.

Source statement: "This policy detects risky prompts, agents generating sensitive responses, agents accessing sensitive or priority SharePoint files, agents accessing risky websites, and agents sharing SharePoint files with people outside of the organization." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/insider-risk-management-policy-templates)

Research detail: The Risky Agents Insider Risk template, in preview, detects risky prompts, sensitive responses, risky website access and external sharing by Copilot Studio and Foundry agents, and tracks activity above an agent's baseline. Compliance Manager syncs 15 automated Foundry evaluation actions such as coherence and fluency.
