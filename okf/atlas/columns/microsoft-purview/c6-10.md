## Answer

Partly covered. Copilot memory (in preview) is not covered: Purview retention policies and labels do not apply to it and Microsoft says there are no admin controls to enforce retention rules for it. No data residency rules for agent inputs, outputs or memory are documented.

Source statement: "Use retention policies to automatically retain or delete user prompts and responses for AI apps." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/ai-microsoft-purview)

Research detail: Retention policies retain, delete or both for prompts and responses across Microsoft Copilot experiences, enterprise AI apps including Foundry and ChatGPT Enterprise, and other AI apps. eDiscovery can search, hold and delete AI interaction data.

## Products

Data Lifecycle Management retention policies, eDiscovery

## Gaps

Deletion can take up to about 16 days. Non-Microsoft app content needs a collection policy. Residency is not addressed on these pages.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Create a retention policy for the Enterprise AI apps location and verify deletion through eDiscovery.
