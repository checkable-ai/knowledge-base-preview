## Answer

Partly covered. Purview retention policies and labels do not apply to Copilot memory, and there are no admin controls to enforce retention rules for it. Admins cannot restrict what is added to memory, and memory actions do not generate audit log entries. Saved memories are kept until the user deletes them.

Source statement: "Retention policies and retention labels configured in Purview by organization admins don't apply to Copilot memory." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot/manage/copilot-personalization-memory)

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: Retention policies retain, delete or both for prompts and responses across Microsoft Copilot experiences, enterprise AI apps including Foundry and ChatGPT Enterprise, and other AI apps. eDiscovery can search, hold and delete AI interaction data.
