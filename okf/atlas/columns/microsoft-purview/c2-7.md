## Answer

Not covered. No sandboxing of agent files, processes or network, and no way to stop a running agent or cut its access. DLP for Copilot is a data control and policy updates can take up to four hours.

Research detail: Purview controls what data Copilot and agents may process. DLP for the Microsoft 365 Copilot location can stop Copilot responding to prompts with sensitive information types, block external web search for those prompts and exclude labelled files and external email from grounding. These are data controls. Purview does not sandbox agent code, set process or network egress policy, or offer a way to stop a running agent or revoke its access.

## Products

None for execution containment. DLP for Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Chat is the nearest data-side control.

## Gaps

DLP policy updates can take up to four hours to reach Copilot, so it cannot act as an immediate stop.
