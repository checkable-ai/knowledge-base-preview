## Answer

Covered. Risky AI usage needs the Microsoft Insider risk or Purview browser extension on user devices. Correlation and response ownership sit in Defender XDR or Sentinel. Users are pseudonymised by default in Insider Risk Management. Audit records can be exported to a SIEM.

Source statement: "Insights from these signals are integrated into Microsoft Defender XDR to provide a comprehensive view of AI-related risks." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/ai-microsoft-purview)

Research detail: Audit records flag jailbreak and cross prompt injection detections and can be sent to a SIEM through the Office 365 Management Activity API. Insider Risk Risky AI usage detects prompt injection and protected material access, feeds Defender XDR, and pseudonymises users by default.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Purview Audit, Insider Risk Management, Defender XDR integration

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

Purview is not a SIEM. Detection fields depend on the AI app populating them, and correlation and response ownership sit in Defender or Sentinel.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Stream audit to Microsoft Sentinel and test that detections reach an owned incident queue.
