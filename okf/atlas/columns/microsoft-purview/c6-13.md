## Answer

Covered. The public ISO 27001 page lists Compliance Manager and the Microsoft Purview portal as in-scope Office 365 services (Commercial, GCC, GCC High and DoD environments). Certificates and audit reports are on the Service Trust Portal. Current IRAP, SOC 2 and ISO 42001 scope for Purview and its newer agent features is not confirmed on public pages.

Source statement: "Auditors review Office 365 cloud services at least annually against the ISO 27001:2013 standard." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/compliance/regulatory/offering-iso-27001)

Research detail: Microsoft states that Azure in-scope services were IRAP assessed up to PROTECTED across its four Australian regions, and that Office 365 commercial services are also IRAP assessed. Azure, Dynamics 365 and Microsoft 365 services are covered by SOC 2 Type 2 reports issued semi-annually. Microsoft states ISO/IEC 42001 certification for its AI services, including Copilot, Copilot Studio, Foundry and Security Copilot, but Purview is not in that list. The service lists for IRAP and SOC 2 sit in reports on the Service Trust Portal, so the public pages do not confirm that Microsoft Purview is in scope.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

Microsoft IRAP assessments (Azure, Office 365), Azure SOC 2 Type 2 report, Microsoft ISO/IEC 42001 certification. Purview Compliance Manager also carries an IRAP assessment template, which is evidence tooling rather than an attestation of Purview.

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

The public IRAP pages list assessment milestones only to December 2020, which is older than 24 months, and name no service list. Whether Microsoft Purview and its agent features are in the current IRAP and SOC 2 scope needs the Service Trust Portal reports. No product-specific privacy impact or red-team material was found on these pages.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Download the current Azure and Office 365 IRAP reports and SOC 2 bridge letter from the Service Trust Portal and check the in-scope service list for Microsoft Purview. Use the reports in the agency's own IRAP assessment and authority to operate.
