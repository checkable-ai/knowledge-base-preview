## Answer

Covered. Needs device onboarding and the Purview browser extension (or network data security through a SASE partner). Limited to the supported third-party AI sites list. Documented on the DSPM for AI (classic) page.

Source statement: "One-click policies for collecting information about users visiting third-party generative AI sites and sending sensitive information to them." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/dspm-for-ai)

Research detail: DSPM detects user visits and prompts to third-party generative AI sites categorised in the Defender for Cloud Apps catalog, such as ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek, through the Purview browser extension and device onboarding. Network data security extends detection to apps and APIs through SASE integration.
