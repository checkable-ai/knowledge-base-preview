## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Needs an account in the Entra Compliance Administrator role or equivalent. Allow a day or so after first use before data appears. Compliance Manager requires an Office 365 or Microsoft 365 licence. AI regulatory templates count towards premium regulation licences. A5/E5/G5 subscriptions include a choice of three premium regulations.

Source statement: "Enhanced reporting provides advanced filtering and customizable views, supporting granular analysis of sensitive data usage, DLP activity, and posture trends." (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/data-security-posture-management-learn-about)

Last checked 14 November 2026.

Research detail: DSPM's Posture page shows key metrics, objectives and a 30-day posture trend, with reports on sensitive data use, policy usage and risky behaviour of users and agents. Compliance Manager shows assessment progress, control status by family and a compliance score.
