## Answer

Covered. Review is after the fact; queues hold up to 1,000 records.

Source statement: "Annotation Queues provide a structured workflow for human review of LLM traces." (https://docs.datadoghq.com/llm_observability/investigate/annotation_queues)

Research detail: Annotation Queues give reviewers the span tree, tool calls and evaluation results beside a label schema with pass or fail and reasoning fields. Traces can be added manually or sampled by automation rules, and owners control reviewers and assignments. End-user feedback can also be submitted.
