## Answer

Covered. AI Guard is in Preview (Datadog must enable a backend feature flag per organisation). Blocking is off by default and must be enabled in the blocking policy. Applies only to SDK-instrumented apps. Changing tool blocking needs the AI Guard Write permission.

Source statement: "You can configure AI Guard to block requests for specific tools, for specific services and environments." (https://docs.datadoghq.com/security/ai_guard/setup/)

Research detail: AI Guard policies include a tool blocklist that blocks specific tools per service and environment, and Tool Protection can block tool calls judged malicious. This restricts some actions against connected systems for instrumented apps. AI Guard is in Preview and blocking is off by default.

<!-- chunk: products -->
## Products

AI Guard (Tool Protection, tool blocklist)

<!-- chunk: gaps -->
## Gaps

No allow-list of scopes per downstream system, no parameter-level permissions, Preview only, depends on SDK integration.

<!-- chunk: options -->
## Options

Combine AI Guard blocklists with least-privilege credentials on the downstream systems. Test blocking in the AI Guard Playground.
