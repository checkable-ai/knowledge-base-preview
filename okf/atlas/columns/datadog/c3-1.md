## Answer

Not covered. No gateway for routing, quotas, cost or rate limits per agent, user or workload. AI Guard evaluates instrumented calls inline and screens prompts, which is only the optional part of the control.

Research detail: AI Guard sits inline with an AI app or agent and evaluates prompts for injection and jailbreak, with blocking configurable at organisation, environment and service level. Automatic integrations exist for LangChain, OpenAI and Anthropic SDKs, and a manual LiteLLM Proxy integration. It does not route traffic or enforce quotas or rates.

## Gaps

## Options
