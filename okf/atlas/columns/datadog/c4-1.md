## Answer

Not covered. No agent identity issuance tied to owner, runtime and release. ml_app, service and env tags are self-asserted labels.

Research detail: Datadog groups LLM telemetry by the ml_app tag and service and environment tags, which label where spans come from. It does not issue or manage agent identities linked to an owner and release. This sits outside an observability product.
