## Answer

Not covered. No issuing of short-lived scoped credentials to agents for the tools and systems they call, and no rotation. Service access tokens (scoped, expiring, revocable) only authenticate to Datadog APIs and AI Gateway, not agent credentials generally. AI Guard redaction hides secrets in messages but does not manage a credential lifecycle.

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Checked LLM Observability, AI Guard and Agent Console docs. None broker, rotate or revoke credentials for agents. AI Guard and Sensitive Data Scanner can detect secrets in prompts and outputs, which is detection, not brokerage.
