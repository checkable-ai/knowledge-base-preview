## Answer

Covered. Few managed evaluations exist, so most quality checks need custom LLM-as-a-judge evaluators. Experiments compare changes offline. No task training of agents.

Source statement: "To monitor the quality of your live LLM responses, run evaluators in production." (https://docs.datadoghq.com/llm_observability/investigate/evaluations/evaluation_developer_guide/)

Research detail: Evaluations run managed checks, custom LLM-as-a-judge criteria and externally submitted results across traces. Experiments compare prompts, models and providers against versioned datasets, and Patterns clusters production traffic into topics. Managed evaluations currently listed are Language Mismatch and Sensitive Data Scanning.
