## Answer

Covered, with conditions. Not supported on the Datadog US1-FED and US2-FED government sites (app.ddog-gov.com, us2.ddog-gov.com). Custom LLM-as-a-judge evaluations need a connected LLM provider account (OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock or a custom provider). Agent Observability does not support data residency for OpenAI. Evaluators published from the SDK stay as drafts until they are activated in the Datadog UI.

Source statement: "Datadog automatically runs them on production traces in real time, with no code changes required." (https://docs.datadoghq.com/llm_observability/investigate/evaluations/evaluation_developer_guide/)

Last checked 15 November 2026.

Research detail: Evaluations run managed checks, custom LLM-as-a-judge criteria and externally submitted results across traces. Experiments compare prompts, models and providers against versioned datasets, and Patterns clusters production traffic into topics. Managed evaluations currently listed are Language Mismatch and Sensitive Data Scanning.
