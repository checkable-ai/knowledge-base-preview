## Answer

Covered. Applies to LLM Observability telemetry (traces, spans, annotated interactions, prompt versions), not an agent's own memory stores. Default trace retention is 15 days, longer via add-ons arranged with the account team. Not available on the US government sites.

Source statement: "Traces and spans from your instrumented applications are retained for 15 days on all plans by default." (https://docs.datadoghq.com/llm_observability/data_governance)

Research detail: Datadog documents retention for each LLM Observability data type, Data Access Control by ml_app and project, span processor redaction in the app, and Sensitive Data Scanner redaction. This covers telemetry about agents, not the agents' own inputs, outputs and memory.
